pflanzen

to plant

📌 Grammatiknotiz

Regular verb. Weak conjugation. Often used in gardening or environmental contexts.

Beispielsatz

Wir pflanzen heute einen Baum.

We are planting a tree today.

gießen

to water (plants)

📌 Grammatiknotiz

Irregular (strong) verb. Vowel change in Präteritum and Partizip II.

Beispielsatz

Vergiss nicht, die Blumen zu gießen!

Don't forget to water the flowers!

wachsen

to grow

📌 Grammatiknotiz

Irregular (strong) verb. Uses 'sein' as auxiliary in Perfekt. Vowel change in Präteritum and Partizip II.

Beispielsatz

Die Bäume wachsen sehr schnell.

The trees are growing very fast.

blühen

to bloom / to flower

📌 Grammatiknotiz

Regular verb. Weak conjugation. Describes the flowering process of plants.

Beispielsatz

Die Rosen blühen wunderschön im Frühling.

The roses bloom beautifully in spring.

sammeln

to collect / to gather

📌 Grammatiknotiz

Regular verb. Weak conjugation. Can be used for collecting natural items (mushrooms, berries) or for collecting waste.

Beispielsatz

Wir sammeln Pilze im Wald.

We collect mushrooms in the forest.

schützen

to protect

📌 Grammatiknotiz

Regular verb. Weak conjugation. Often used in the context of environmental protection.

Beispielsatz

Wir müssen die Umwelt schützen.

We must protect the environment.

trennen

to separate

📌 Grammatiknotiz

Regular verb. Weak conjugation. Often used for waste separation (Müll trennen).

Beispielsatz

Wir müssen den Müll trennen.

We must separate the waste.

recyceln

to recycle

📌 Grammatiknotiz

Regular verb. Weak conjugation. A common verb in environmental discussions.

Beispielsatz

Wir recyceln Glas und Papier.

We recycle glass and paper.

sparen

to save (energy, water, money)

📌 Grammatiknotiz

Regular verb. Weak conjugation. Important for discussing resource conservation.

Beispielsatz

Wir sollten mehr Energie sparen.

We should save more energy.

verbrauchen

to consume / to use up

📌 Grammatiknotiz

Regular verb with inseparable prefix 'ver-'. Weak conjugation. Often used in discussions about resource consumption.

Beispielsatz

Wir verbrauchen zu viel Strom.

We consume too much electricity.

verschmutzen

to pollute / to contaminate

📌 Grammatiknotiz

Regular verb with inseparable prefix 'ver-'. Weak conjugation. Crucial for discussing environmental damage.

Beispielsatz

Fabriken verschmutzen oft die Luft.

Factories often pollute the air.

reinigen

to clean / to purify

📌 Grammatiknotiz

Regular verb. Weak conjugation. Often used for cleaning natural environments or water/air.

Beispielsatz

Wir müssen den Strand reinigen.

We must clean the beach.

wandern

to hike

📌 Grammatiknotiz

Regular verb. Uses 'sein' as auxiliary in Perfekt as it indicates a change of location.

Beispielsatz

Am Wochenende wandern wir in den Bergen.

On the weekend, we hike in the mountains.

baden

to bathe / to swim (in a lake/sea)

📌 Grammatiknotiz

Regular verb. Weak conjugation. Can use 'haben' or 'sein' in Perfekt depending on nuance (activity vs. movement to/from water). 'Haben' is more common for the general act.

Beispielsatz

Im Sommer baden wir oft im See.

In summer, we often bathe in the lake.

atmen

to breathe

📌 Grammatiknotiz

Regular verb. Weak conjugation. Essential for describing living beings and air quality.

Beispielsatz

In den Bergen kann man besser atmen.

In the mountains, one can breathe better.

scheinen

to shine (sun) / to seem

📌 Grammatiknotiz

Irregular (strong) verb. Vowel change in Präteritum and Partizip II. Often used impersonally with 'die Sonne'.

Beispielsatz

Die Sonne scheint heute den ganzen Tag.

The sun is shining all day today.

regnen

to rain

📌 Grammatiknotiz

Regular verb. Weak conjugation. Almost exclusively used impersonally with 'es'.

Beispielsatz

Es regnet heute den ganzen Tag.

It is raining all day today.

schneien

to snow

📌 Grammatiknotiz

Regular verb. Weak conjugation. Almost exclusively used impersonally with 'es'.

Beispielsatz

Es schneit schon seit Stunden.

It has been snowing for hours already.

wehen

to blow (wind)

📌 Grammatiknotiz

Regular verb. Weak conjugation. Often used with 'der Wind' or impersonally.

Beispielsatz

Der Wind weht stark vom Meer.

The wind is blowing strongly from the sea.

leben

to live

📌 Grammatiknotiz

Regular verb. Weak conjugation. Fundamental for discussing life in nature.

Beispielsatz

Viele Tiere leben im Wald.

Many animals live in the forest.

sterben

to die

📌 Grammatiknotiz

Irregular (strong) verb. Uses 'sein' as auxiliary in Perfekt. Vowel change in Präteritum and Partizip II.

Beispielsatz

Im Winter sterben viele Pflanzen, wenn es zu kalt ist.

In winter, many plants die if it's too cold.

fliegen

to fly

📌 Grammatiknotiz

Irregular (strong) verb. Uses 'sein' as auxiliary in Perfekt for movement. Vowel change in Präteritum and Partizip II.

Beispielsatz

Die Vögel fliegen in den Süden.

The birds fly to the south.

schwimmen

to swim

📌 Grammatiknotiz

Irregular (strong) verb. Uses 'sein' as auxiliary in Perfekt for movement. Vowel change in Präteritum and Partizip II.

Beispielsatz

Wir gehen im Sommer oft im See schwimmen.

We often go swimming in the lake in summer.

sich erholen

to relax / to recover (in nature)

📌 Grammatiknotiz

Reflexive verb. The prefix 'er-' is inseparable. Often used in the context of relaxing in natural surroundings.

Beispielsatz

Ich erhole mich gerne in der Natur.

I like to relax in nature.

beobachten

to observe / to watch

📌 Grammatiknotiz

Regular verb with inseparable prefix 'be-'. Weak conjugation. Used for observing animals, plants, or natural phenomena.

Beispielsatz

Wir beobachten oft Vögel im Garten.

We often observe birds in the garden.
