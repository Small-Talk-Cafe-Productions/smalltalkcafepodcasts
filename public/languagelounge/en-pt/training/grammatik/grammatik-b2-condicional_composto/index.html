Formação do Condicional Composto

Formation of the Compound Conditional

📌 Nota gramatical

The conditional composto is formed by combining the auxiliary verb 'ter' (or sometimes 'haver' in formal registers) in the conditional simple (teria, terias, teria, teríamos, teríeis, teriam) followed by the past participle of the main verb (-ado/-ido). It expresses an action that would have happened in the past under certain circumstances.

Exemplo

Frases Condicionais do Passado: 'Se' + Mais-que-perfeito Composto

Past Conditional Sentences: 'Se' + Compound Pluperfect Subjunctive

📌 Nota gramatical

To express an unreal condition in the past, use 'se' followed by the pretérito mais-que-perfeito composto do conjuntivo (tivesse + past participle) in the subordinate clause, paired with the condicional composto in the main clause.

Exemplo

Ações Futuras no Passado (Futuro do Pretérito Composto)

Future in the Past using Compound Conditional

📌 Nota gramatical

Just like the simple conditional expresses a future relative to a past starting point, the compound conditional can express an action that was future relative to a point in the past, but completed before a subsequent point.

Exemplo

Suposição ou Probabilidade no Passado

Supposition or Probability in the Past

📌 Nota gramatical

The conditional composto can express a conjecture, probability, or uncertainty regarding an action that occurred in the past, often translated as 'probably would have' or 'must have' in specific contexts.

Exemplo

Atitude Polida ou Suavizada sobre o Passado

Polite or Softened Regret regarding the Past

📌 Nota gramatical

Speakers use the conditional composto to soften criticisms, express regret, or make polite suggestions about past actions without sounding overly aggressive or definitive.

Exemplo

Colocação de Pronomes com o Condicional Composto

Pronoun Placement with the Compound Conditional

📌 Nota gramatical

In European Portuguese, clitic pronouns attach to the auxiliary verb in compound tenses (mesóclise or próclise depending on triggers), but standard proclisis applies if a word triggers it (like 'não', 'se', 'que'). Otherwise, use mesoclitic forms with the auxiliary or place before the auxiliary if a trigger exists.

Exemplo

Inversão da Frase: Resultado Primeiro

Sentence Inversion: Main Clause First

📌 Nota gramatical

The structure of unreal past conditions is flexible. You can place the main clause containing the conditional composto first, followed by the 'se' clause with the pluperfect subjunctive, without changing the meaning.

Exemplo

Omissão da Oração 'Se' (Contexto Implícito)

Omission of the 'If' Clause (Implied Context)

📌 Nota gramatical

At the B2 level, the conditional composto is frequently used on its own when the conditional context ('se...') is clearly understood from the previous conversation or situational context.

Exemplo

Construções com 'Caso' e o Passado

Constructions with 'Caso' and the Past

📌 Nota gramatical

While 'caso' usually triggers the subjunctive for future possibilities, unreal past conditions strictly rely on 'se' + pluperfect subjunctive, rather than 'caso' + past tenses.

Exemplo

Verbos Irregulares no Particípio Passado

Irregular Past Participles in the Compound Conditional

📌 Nota gramatical

Formation of the compound conditional relies heavily on correct past participles. Common irregulars include: fazer (feito), dizer (dito), ver (visto), pôr (posto), abrir (aberto), and escrever (escrito).

Exemplo

Diferença entre Condicional Simples e Composto

Difference between Simple and Compound Conditional

📌 Nota gramatical

The simple conditional expresses present or future hypothetical outcomes based on present/future conditions, whereas the compound conditional deals exclusively with unrealized actions and outcomes in the past.

Exemplo

Expressões Idiomáticas com o Condicional Composto

Idiomatic Expressions with the Compound Conditional

📌 Nota gramatical

In conversational European Portuguese, native speakers frequently use certain expressions with the conditional composto to express disbelief, irony, or dramatic emphasis regarding past events.

Exemplo

Configurar treino
