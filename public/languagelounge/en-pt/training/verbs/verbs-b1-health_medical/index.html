adoecer

to fall ill

📌 Nota gramatical

-er conjugation group, auxiliary verb 'ter'. Regular verb.

Exemplo

Costumo adoecer sempre que o inverno chega com muito frio.

I usually fall ill whenever winter arrives with lots of cold weather.

apanhar

to catch (a cold, virus)

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation group, auxiliary verb 'ter'. Regular verb.

Exemplo

Ele apanhou uma gripe forte porque saiu à chuva sem casaco.

He caught a bad flu because he went out in the rain without a coat.

aspirar

to inhale

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation group, auxiliary verb 'ter'. Regular verb.

Exemplo

O médico pediu-me para aspirar profundamente o ar pelo nariz.

The doctor asked me to inhale the air deeply through my nose.

atenuar

to alleviate / to soothe

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation group, auxiliary verb 'ter'. Regular verb.

Exemplo

Este xarope ajuda a atenuar a tosse irritante durante a noite.

This cough syrup helps to soothe the irritating cough during the night.

consultar

to consult / to see (a doctor)

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation group, auxiliary verb 'ter'. Regular verb.

Exemplo

Decidi consultar um cardiologista para verificar a minha tensão arterial.

I decided to see a cardiologist to check my blood pressure.

curar

to cure / to heal

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation group, auxiliary verb 'ter'. Regular verb.

Exemplo

Os antibióticos ajudam a curar a infeção bacteriana rapidamente.

Antibiotics help to cure the bacterial infection quickly.

desinfetar

to disinfect

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation group, auxiliary verb 'ter'. Regular verb.

Exemplo

É fundamental desinfetar a ferida com álcool antes de colocar o penso.

It is essential to disinfect the wound with alcohol before applying the plaster.

diagnosticar

to diagnose

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation group, auxiliary verb 'ter'. Regular verb.

Exemplo

O médico conseguiu diagnosticar a doença logo na primeira consulta.

The doctor managed to diagnose the illness right at the first appointment.

doar

to donate

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation group, auxiliary verb 'ter'. Regular verb.

Exemplo

Costumo doar sangue duas vezes por ano para ajudar no hospital local.

I usually donate blood twice a year to help at the local hospital.

engolir

to swallow

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir conjugation group, auxiliary verb 'ter'. Regular verb.

Exemplo

Tenho tanta dor de garganta que me custa muito engolir a comida.

I have such a sore throat that it is very hard for me to swallow food.

examinar

to examine

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation group, auxiliary verb 'ter'. Regular verb.

Exemplo

A pediatra começou por examinar os ouvidos e a garganta da criança.

The pediatrician started by examining the child's ears and throat.

inchar

to swell

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation group, auxiliary verb 'ter'. Regular verb.

Exemplo

Depois de torcer o tornozelo, o pé começou logo a inchar bastante.

After twisting my ankle, my foot immediately started to swell significantly.

medicar

to medicate / to prescribe medication

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation group, auxiliary verb 'ter'. Regular verb.

Exemplo

O clínico geral decidiu medicar o paciente com analgésicos fortes.

The general practitioner decided to prescribe strong painkillers to the patient.

operar

to operate (surgery)

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation group, auxiliary verb 'ter'. Regular verb.

Exemplo

O cirurgião vai operar o joelho do atleta na próxima terça-feira.

The surgeon is going to operate on the athlete's knee next Tuesday.

prevenir

to prevent

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir conjugation group, auxiliary verb 'ter'. Regular verb.

Exemplo

Tomar a vacina contra a gripe ajuda a prevenir doenças graves no inverno.

Taking the flu vaccine helps to prevent severe illnesses in the winter.

recuperar

to recover

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation group, auxiliary verb 'ter'. Regular verb.

Exemplo

Depois da cirurgia, levei um mês a recuperar totalmente as forças.

After the surgery, it took me a month to fully recover my strength.

respirar

to breathe

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation group, auxiliary verb 'ter'. Regular verb.

Exemplo

Com esta alergia grave, é muito difícil respirar sem medicação.

With this severe allergy, it is very difficult to breathe without medication.

tosse

to cough

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation group, auxiliary verb 'ter'. Regular verb. (Infinitive: tosse -> tosse is noun, wait! Infinitive is tossir!)

Exemplo

Comecei a tossir imenso durante a noite devido ao ar seco do quarto.

I started to cough a lot during the night due to the dry air in the bedroom.

tratar

to treat / to cure

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation group, auxiliary verb 'ter'. Regular verb.

Exemplo

O dermatologista conseguiu tratar a minha alergia de pele com sucesso.

The dermatologist managed to treat my skin allergy successfully.

vacinar

to vaccinate

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation group, auxiliary verb 'ter'. Regular verb.

Exemplo

Todos os anos devemos vacinar-nos contra a gripe antes do inverno.

Every year we should vaccinate ourselves against the flu before winter.
