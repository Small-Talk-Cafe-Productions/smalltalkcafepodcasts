cozinhar

to cook

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Eu gosto de cozinhar sopa ao domingo.

I like to cook soup on Sunday.

assar

to bake, to roast

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Elas vão assar o frango no forno.

They are going to roast the chicken in the oven.

ferver

to boil

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb, auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

A água já começa a ferver na panela.

The water is already starting to boil in the pot.

fritar

to fry

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Ele gosta de fritar batatas ao jantar.

He likes to fry potatoes for dinner.

cortar

to cut

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Cuidado ao cortar os tomates com a faca.

Careful when cutting the tomatoes with the knife.

descascar

to peel

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb with spelling change in 1st person singular of preterite (c -> qu), auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Eu ajudo a descascar as batatas para o almoço.

I help peel the potatoes for lunch.

provar

to taste, to try

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Queres provar este bolo de chocolate?

Do you want to taste this chocolate cake?

adicionar

to add

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

É preciso adicionar um pouco de sal.

It is necessary to add a little bit of salt.

mexer

to stir, to mix

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb, auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Deves mexer a sopa com uma colher de pau.

You must stir the soup with a wooden spoon.

temperar

to season

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Vou temperar a salada com azeite e vinagre.

I am going to season the salad with olive oil and vinegar.

beber

to drink

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb, auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Costumo beber um copo de sumo ao pequeno-almoço.

I usually drink a glass of juice at breakfast.

comer

to eat

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb, auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Nós gostamos de comer peixe fresco.

We like to eat fresh fish.

comprar

to buy

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Vou ao supermercado comprar fruta e legumes.

I'm going to the supermarket to buy fruit and vegetables.

preparar

to prepare

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Ela sabe preparar pratos tradicionais portugueses.

She knows how to prepare traditional Portuguese dishes.

servir

to serve

📌 Nota gramatical

Stem-changing -ir verb (e -> i in 1st person singular present), auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

O empregado vai servir o almoço agora.

The waiter is going to serve lunch now.

lavar

to wash (dishes/food)

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Tens de lavar a fruta antes de a comer.

You have to wash the fruit before eating it.

guardar

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

You must store the milk in the fridge.

gostar

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, takes preposition 'de', auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Nós gostamos muito de comida italiana.

We really like Italian food.

provar

to try (food)

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo
