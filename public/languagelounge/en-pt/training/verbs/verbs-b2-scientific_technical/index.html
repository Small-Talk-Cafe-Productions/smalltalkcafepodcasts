analisar

to analyze

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, auxiliary 'ter'.

Exemplo

O laboratório vai analisar as amostras de água recolhidas ontem.

The laboratory will analyze the water samples collected yesterday.

desenvolver

to develop

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb, auxiliary 'ter'.

Exemplo

A empresa conseguiu desenvolver um software inovador para a indústria.

The company managed to develop innovative software for the industry.

to calculate

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, auxiliary 'ter'.

Exemplo

We need to calculate the strength of the materials before construction.

to prove / to verify

📌 Nota gramatical

Exemplo
