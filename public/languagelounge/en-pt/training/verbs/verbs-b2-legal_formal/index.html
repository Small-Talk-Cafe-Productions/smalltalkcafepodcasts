ratificar

to ratify

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

O parlamento vai ratificar o acordo comercial na próxima sessão.

The parliament is going to ratify the trade agreement in the next session.

revogar

to revoke

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; spelling change (g to gu before e) in pretérito perfeito (eu revoguei).

Exemplo

O governo pode revogar a lei se houver protestos públicos.

The government can revoke the law if there are public protests.

estipular

to stipulate

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

O contrato vai estipular as condições exatas para a entrega.

The contract will stipulate the exact conditions for delivery.

alegar

to allege / to claim

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

O suspeito continua a alegar inocência perante o tribunal.

The suspect continues to allege innocence before the court.

cumprir

to comply with / to fulfill

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb; auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Todas as empresas devem cumprir as normas de segurança rigorosamente.

All companies must comply with safety regulations strictly.

violar

to violate / to breach

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

A empresa foi multada por violar as leis de proteção de dados.

The company was fined for violating data protection laws.

recorrer

to appeal / to resort to

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb; often used with preposition 'a' (recorrer a).

Exemplo

O advogado decidiu recorrer da decisão do juiz para o tribunal superior.

The lawyer decided to appeal the judge's decision to the higher court.

denunciar

to denounce / to report (a crime)

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

O funcionário resolveu denunciar a corrupção na empresa.

The employee decided to report the corruption in the company.

absolver

to acquit / to absolve

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb; auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

O júri decidiu absolver o réu por falta de provas concretas.

The jury decided to acquit the defendant due to a lack of concrete evidence.

condenar

to sentence / to condemn

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

O juiz vai condenar o criminoso a cinco anos de prisão efetiva.

The judge is going to sentence the criminal to five years of effective prison.

proibir

to prohibit / to ban

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb with slight stem vowel variation in present (proíbo, proíbes, proíbe, proíbem).

Exemplo

A nova regulamentação vai proibir o uso de plásticos descartáveis.

The new regulation will prohibit the use of disposable plastics.

contestar

to contest / to dispute

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Os herdeiros decidiram contestar o testamento em tribunal.

The heirs decided to contest the will in court.

garantir

to guarantee / to ensure

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb; stem vowel variation in present (garanto, garantes, garante, garantem).

Exemplo

O contrato deve garantir os direitos fundamentais dos trabalhadores.

The contract must guarantee the fundamental rights of workers.

notificar

to notify / to serve notice

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; spelling change (c to qu before e) in pretérito perfeito (eu notifiquei).

Exemplo

A empresa foi obrigada a notificar os clientes sobre a falha de segurança.

The company was forced to notify customers about the security breach.

submeter

to submit

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb; auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

O projeto deve ser submetido à aprovação da câmara municipal.

The project must be submitted for the approval of the city council.

promulgar

to promulgate

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; spelling change (g to gu before e) in pretérito perfeito (eu promulguei).

Exemplo

O Presidente da República vai promulgar a nova lei esta semana.

The President of the Republic is going to promulgate the new law this week.

desempenhar

to perform / to discharge (duties)

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

O novo diretor vai desempenhar funções de grande responsabilidade.

The new director will perform duties of great responsibility.

fiscalizar

to inspect / to oversee

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; spelling change (c to ç before a/o/u) in present (fiscalizo).

Exemplo

A inspeção do trabalho serve para fiscalizar as condições laborais.

Labor inspection serves to oversee working conditions.

delegar

to delegate

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; spelling change (g to gu before e) in pretérito perfeito (eu deleguei).

Exemplo

O diretor pode delegar algumas competências nos seus adjuntos.

The director can delegate some powers to his deputies.

ratificar

to validate / to confirm formally

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

A direção pretende ratificar o compromisso assumido perante os parceiros.

Management intends to validate the commitment made to partners.
