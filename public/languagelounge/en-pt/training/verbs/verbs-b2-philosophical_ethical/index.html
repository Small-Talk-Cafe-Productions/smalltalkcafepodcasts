argumentar

to argue

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar). Regular verb used to discuss logical reasoning and debate.

Exemplo

O filósofo tenta argumentar que a liberdade absoluta é uma ilusão.

The philosopher tries to argue that absolute freedom is an illusion.

refutar

to refute

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar). Regular verb used in intellectual debate to counter claims.

Exemplo

A teoria científica conseguiu refutar a hipótese anterior com dados sólidos.

The scientific theory managed to refute the previous hypothesis with solid data.

justificar

to justify

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar). Spelling change in preterite (c -> qu before e: justifiquei).

Exemplo

O fim nunca deve justificar os meios numa sociedade ética.

The end should never justify the means in an ethical society.

questionar

to question

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar). Regular verb expressing critical inquiry.

Exemplo

Um bom pensador deve sempre questionar as normas estabelecidas.

A good thinker must always question established norms.

condenar

to condemn

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar). Regular verb used in moral and ethical judgments.

Exemplo

A sociedade deve condenar firmemente qualquer forma de discriminação.

Society must firmly condemn any form of discrimination.

ponderar

to ponder / to weigh

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar). Regular verb expressing careful thought and reflection.

Exemplo

Precisamos de ponderar todas as consequências éticas antes de agir.

We need to ponder all ethical consequences before acting.

deduzir

to deduce

📌 Nota gramatical

Third conjugation (-ir). Stem-changing in present (eu deduzo).

Exemplo

Podemos deduzir a partir destas premissas que a verdade é relativa.

We can deduce from these premises that truth is relative.

inferir

to infer

📌 Nota gramatical

Third conjugation (-ir). Regular pattern with stem alternation in present (eu infiro).

Exemplo

É possível inferir princípios éticos universais através da razão pura?

Is it possible to infer universal ethical principles through pure reason?

assumir

to assume / to take on

📌 Nota gramatical

Third conjugation (-ir). Regular verb often used for taking responsibility.

Exemplo

Cada indivíduo deve assumir a responsabilidade pelas suas escolhas morais.

Every individual must assume responsibility for their moral choices.

contestar

to contest / to challenge

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar). Regular verb expressing opposition to authority or ideas.

Exemplo

Os cidadãos têm o direito de contestar leis que considerem injustas.

Citizens have the right to contest laws they consider unjust.

validar

to validate

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar). Regular verb used in epistemology and ethics.

Exemplo

A experiência empírica ajuda a validar teorias sobre a realidade.

Empirical experience helps to validate theories about reality.

discernir

to discern

📌 Nota gramatical

Third conjugation (-ir). Regular verb for making moral distinctions.

Exemplo

É preciso sabedoria para discernir entre o bem e o mal.

Wisdom is needed to discern between good and evil.

analisar

to analyze

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar). Regular verb essential for critical thinking.

Exemplo

O ensaio exige que o aluno consiga analisar o dilema moral proposto.

The essay requires the student to be able to analyze the proposed moral dilemma.

interpretar

to interpret

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar). Regular verb used in hermeneutics and philosophy.

Exemplo

Diferentes culturas podem interpretar o mesmo conceito ético de forma distinta.

Different cultures can interpret the same ethical concept in different ways.

postular

to postulate

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar). Regular verb used to state a foundational premise.

Exemplo

Immanuel Kant vem postular que a moralidade deriva da razão prática.

Immanuel Kant postulates that morality derives from practical reason.

teorizar

to theorize

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar). Regular verb used in abstract and academic discourse.

Exemplo

Os sociólogos passam muito tempo a teorizar sobre a justiça social.

Sociologists spend a lot of time theorizing about social justice.

transgredir

to transgress

📌 Nota gramatical

Third conjugation (-ir). Regular verb for breaking moral or legal boundaries.

Exemplo

Aquele que decide transgredir a lei moral deve arcar com as consequências.

He who decides to transgress the moral law must bear the consequences.

sustentar

to sustain / to uphold

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar). Regular verb used to uphold claims or viewpoints.

Exemplo

A tese principal vem sustentar que a empatia é inata ao ser humano.

The main thesis maintains that empathy is innate to human beings.

conceituar

to conceptualize

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar). Regular verb used to define abstract ideas.

Exemplo

É difícil conceituar a liberdade numa sociedade tão regulada.

It is difficult to conceptualize freedom in such a regulated society.

absolver

to absolve

📌 Nota gramatical

Second conjugation (-er). Regular verb used in moral and judicial contexts.

Exemplo

A consciência moral pode absolver ou condenar as nossas ações.

Moral conscience can absolve or condemn our actions.
