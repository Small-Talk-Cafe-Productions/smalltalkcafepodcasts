investir

to invest

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb; often used with the preposition 'em'.

Exemplo

Decidi investir as minhas poupanças num fundo de ações diversificado.

I decided to invest my savings in a diversified stock fund.

lucrar

to profit / to make a profit

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; frequently used in commercial and corporate contexts.

Exemplo

A nossa empresa conseguiu lucrar bastante no último trimestre fiscal.

Our company managed to profit significantly in the last fiscal quarter.

economizar

to save money / to economize

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; synonymous with 'poupar'.

Exemplo

Tivemos de economizar água e eletricidade para reduzir as despesas mensais.

We had to save water and electricity to reduce monthly expenses.

poupar

to save / to spare

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; very common in personal finance contexts.

Exemplo

A minha avó sempre me ensinou a poupar para imprevistos financeiros.

My grandmother always taught me to save for financial emergencies.

orçamentar

to budget / to estimate costs

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; relates to financial planning and budgets.

Exemplo

Precisamos de orçamentar o projeto antes de apresentar a proposta ao cliente.

We need to budget the project before presenting the proposal to the client.

faturar

to bill / to invoice / to turn over (revenue)

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; commonly used regarding company turnover and sales.

Exemplo

A pequena empresa começou a faturar mais após abrir uma loja online.

The small business started to bill more after opening an online store.

financiar

to finance / to fund

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; used for loans, mortgages, and backing projects.

Exemplo

O banco recusou-se a financiar a compra daquele terreno industrial.

The bank refused to finance the purchase of that industrial land.

desperdiçar

to waste

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; opposite of poupar/economizar in resource management.

Exemplo

Não podemos desperdiçar recursos públicos em obras desnecessárias.

We cannot waste public resources on unnecessary construction works.

desvalorizar

to devalue / to lose value / to depreciate

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; used for currency, assets, and properties.

Exemplo

A inflação alta fez desvalorizar a moeda nacional de forma drástica.

High inflation caused the national currency to devalue drastically.

valorizar

to appreciate / to increase in value

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; antonym of desvalorizar.

Exemplo

O setor imobiliário continua a valorizar nas grandes cidades portuguesas.

The real estate sector continues to appreciate in major Portuguese cities.

taxar

to tax / to levy a tax

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; used in fiscal and taxation discussions.

Exemplo

O governo estuda taxar as grandes fortunas para combater as desigualdades.

The government is considering taxing large fortunes to fight inequalities.

descontar

to deduct / to discount / to cash (a cheque)

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; common in salary deductions and banking.

Exemplo

O empregador vai descontar os dias de falta diretamente no meu salário.

The employer will deduct the days missed directly from my salary.

cobrar

to charge / to collect (payment)

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; used for prices, fees, and debt collection.

Exemplo

Muitos bancos começaram a cobrar comissões elevadas pelas transferências.

Many banks started to charge high fees for transfers.

pagar

to pay

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb with minor spelling adjustment in 1st person singular preterite (paguei).

Exemplo

Prefiro pagar todas as minhas contas por débito direto para evitar atrasos.

I prefer to pay all my bills by direct debit to avoid delays.

falir

to go bankrupt / to fail (business)

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb; used in commercial collapse and insolvency contexts.

Exemplo

Centenas de pequenas empresas ameaçam falir devido à crise energética.

Hundreds of small businesses threaten to go bankrupt due to the energy crisis.

resgatar

to redeem / to withdraw (funds) / to rescue

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; used for investments, funds, and financial bailouts.

Exemplo

Decidi resgatar o dinheiro do meu plano de poupança para comprar carro novo.

I decided to withdraw the money from my savings plan to buy a new car.

distribuir

to distribute / to allocate

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb with stem changes in present indicative (distribuo, distribuis...).

Exemplo

A empresa vai distribuir dividendos pelos acionistas no final do ano.

The company will distribute dividends to shareholders at the end of the year.

subsidiar

to subsidize

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; often used in discussions regarding government aid and energy.

Exemplo

O Estado continua a subsidiar os combustíveis para proteger as famílias.

The state continues to subsidize fuels to protect families.

acumular

to accumulate / to amass

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; frequently used regarding wealth, debts, or capital.

Exemplo

Com o passar dos anos, conseguiu acumular uma fortuna considerável.

Over the years, he managed to accumulate a considerable fortune.

gastar

to spend / to consume

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; standard term for spending money or resources.

Exemplo

Devemos evitar gastar mais do que aquilo que ganhamos todos os meses.

We must avoid spending more than what we earn every month.
