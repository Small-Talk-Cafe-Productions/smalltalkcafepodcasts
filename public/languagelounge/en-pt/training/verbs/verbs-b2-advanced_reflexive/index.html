abster-se

to abstain / to refrain from

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular reflexive verb conjugated like 'ter' ('abstenho-me, abstive-me'); takes 'de'.

Exemplo

Três deputados abstiveram-se na votação final do orçamento de Estado.

Three MPs abstained in the final vote on the state budget.

ater-se

to stick to / to abide by

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular reflexive verb conjugated like 'ter' ('atenho-me, ative-me'); takes 'a'.

Exemplo

Durante o depoimento, a testemunha ateve-se estritamente aos factos observados.

During the testimony, the witness stuck strictly to the observed facts.

conter-se

to restrain oneself / to hold back

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular reflexive verb conjugated like 'ter' ('contenho-me, contive-me').

Exemplo

Perante aquela injustiça flagrante, foi difícil conter-me e ficar calado.

In the face of that blatant injustice, it was hard to restrain myself and stay quiet.

opor-se

to oppose / to object to

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular reflexive verb conjugated like 'pôr' ('oponho-me, opus-me'); takes 'a'.

Exemplo

Os moradores opuseram-se firmemente ao abate das árvores centenárias da avenida.

The residents firmly opposed the felling of the century-old trees on the avenue.

comprometer-se

to commit oneself / to pledge

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er reflexive verb; takes the preposition 'a' + infinitive.

Exemplo

O governo comprometeu-se a reforçar o investimento no Serviço Nacional de Saúde.

The government committed itself to strengthening investment in the National Health Service.

empenhar-se

to strive / to dedicate oneself wholeheartedly

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar reflexive verb; takes the preposition 'em'.

Exemplo

Toda a equipa se empenhou ao máximo para garantir o sucesso do congresso.

The whole team dedicated themselves fully to ensuring the congress's success.

resignar-se

to resign oneself to

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar reflexive verb; takes the preposition 'a'.

Exemplo

Recuso-me a resignar-me perante a degradação do património histórico.

I refuse to resign myself to the degradation of historical heritage.

indignar-se

to become indignant / to be outraged

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar reflexive verb; takes 'com' or 'perante'.

Exemplo

A opinião pública indignou-se com a falta de transparência do processo.

Public opinion was outraged by the lack of transparency in the process.

vangloriar-se

to boast / to brag about

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar reflexive verb ('vanglorio-me'); takes the preposition 'de'.

Exemplo

Ele vangloria-se constantemente dos seus contactos no mundo financeiro.

He constantly boasts about his contacts in the financial world.

queixar-se

to complain

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar reflexive verb; takes the preposition 'de'.

Exemplo

Os utentes queixam-se frequentemente dos atrasos nos comboios suburbanos.

Users frequently complain about delays on suburban trains.

equivocar-se

to be mistaken

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar reflexive verb; spelling change 'equivoquei-me' in preterite.

Exemplo

Reconheço que me equivoquei na análise preliminar das estatísticas.

I acknowledge that I was mistaken in the preliminary analysis of the statistics.

adaptar-se

to adapt

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar reflexive verb; takes the preposition 'a'. Note silent or pronounced 'p' retained in PT-PT.

Exemplo

A empresa adaptou-se rapidamente às novas exigências do trabalho remoto.

The company adapted quickly to the new demands of remote work.

aperceber-se

to realize / to become aware of

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er reflexive verb; always takes the preposition 'de' ('aperceber-se de que...').

Exemplo

Só me apercebi de que tinha esquecido o portátil quando cheguei ao escritório.

I only realized that I had forgotten my laptop when I arrived at the office.

debruçar-se

to pore over / to examine closely / to lean over

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar reflexive verb ('debrucei-me'); figuratively takes 'sobre' ('debruçar-se sobre um tema').

Exemplo

A comissão parlamentar vai debruçar-se sobre a reforma da lei laboral.

The parliamentary committee will examine the labour law reform closely.

deparar-se

to come across / to be faced with

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar reflexive verb; takes the preposition 'com' ('deparar-se com um obstáculo').

Exemplo

Durante a investigação nos arquivos, deparámo-nos com cartas inéditas.

During the research in the archives, we came across unpublished letters.

retratar-se

to retract / to recant (a statement)

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar reflexive verb; non-reflexively means 'to portray', reflexively means 'to formally retract/apologize'.

Exemplo

O comentador viu-se obrigado a retratar-se publicamente após a polémica.

The commentator was forced to publicly retract his statement after the controversy.
