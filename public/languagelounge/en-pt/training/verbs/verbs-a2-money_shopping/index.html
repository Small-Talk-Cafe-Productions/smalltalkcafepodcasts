Money & Shopping

Dinheiro e Compras • A2 • 20 cartões

Estrutura

Frases de exemplo

Etiquetas:

← → Teclas de seta para navegar · A para ouvir

comprar

to buy

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation.

Exemplo

Eu quero comprar um casaco novo.

I want to buy a new coat.

pagar

to pay

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; orthographic change in 1st person singular (g -> gu) to keep hard sound.

Exemplo

Posso pagar com cartão?

Can I pay by card?

gastar

to spend

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation.

Exemplo

Não quero gastar muito dinheiro hoje.

I don't want to spend much money today.

poupar

to save (money)

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation.

Exemplo

Devias poupar dinheiro para as férias.

You should save money for the holidays.

custar

to cost

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; usually used in 3rd person singular or plural.

Exemplo

Quanto custa esta camisola?

How much does this sweater cost?

vender

to sell

📌 Nota gramatical

-er verb; regular conjugation.

Exemplo

Eles vendem fruta fresca no mercado.

They sell fresh fruit at the market.

procurar

to look for / to search

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation.

Exemplo

Estou a procurar umas calças pretas.

I am looking for some black trousers.

levar

to take / to carry

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation.

Exemplo

Vou levar este casaco, por favor.

I'll take this coat, please.

escolher

to choose

📌 Nota gramatical

-er verb; regular conjugation.

Exemplo

Podes escolher o presente que quiseres.

You can choose the gift you want.

experimentar

to try on / to try

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation.

Exemplo

Quero experimentar estas calças.

I want to try on these trousers.

trocar

to exchange / to change

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation.

Exemplo

Quero trocar esta camisola por um tamanho maior.

I want to exchange this sweater for a larger size.

oferecer

to offer / to give as a gift

📌 Nota gramatical

-er verb; irregular 1st person singular present (ofereço).

Exemplo

Vou oferecer um livro à minha mãe.

I am going to give a book as a gift to my mother.

levantar

to withdraw (money) / to lift

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation (distinct from reflexive levantar-se).

Exemplo

Preciso de levantar dinheiro na caixa multibanco.

I need to withdraw money at the ATM.

abranger

to include / to cover

📌 Nota gramatical

-er verb; regular conjugation.

Exemplo

O preço abrange todas as taxas.

The price includes all taxes.

arrumar

to tidy up / to pack

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation.

Exemplo

Vou arrumar as compras no armário.

I'm going to put away the shopping in the cupboard.

ficar

to fit (size/clothes) / to stay / to be located

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; orthographic change in 1st person singular (c -> qu).

Exemplo

Estas calças ficam-me muito bem.

These trousers fit me very well.

levar

to take (time/money)

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation.

Exemplo

Quanto tempo leva a viagem?

How long does the trip take?

descontar

to discount / to deduct

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation.

Exemplo

Eles descontam dez por cento no talão.

They deduct ten percent on the receipt.

servir

to serve / to fit

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir verb; stem-changing in present (sirvo, serves).

Exemplo

Este tamanho não me serve.

This size doesn't fit me.

assinar

to sign

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation.

Exemplo

Precisa de assinar o talão do cartão.

You need to sign the card receipt.
