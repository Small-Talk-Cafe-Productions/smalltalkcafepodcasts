Frases de exemplo

Etiquetas:

← → Teclas de seta para navegar · A para ouvir

aprender

to learn

📌 Nota gramatical

-er verb, auxiliary verb 'ter', regular conjugation pattern.

Exemplo

Eu quero aprender a falar português muito bem.

I want to learn to speak Portuguese very well.

ensinar

to teach

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter', regular conjugation pattern.

Exemplo

O professor ensina matemática aos alunos da escola.

The teacher teaches mathematics to the school students.

estudar

to study

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter', regular conjugation pattern.

Exemplo

Eu estudo na biblioteca todas as terças-feiras.

I study at the library every Tuesday.

ler

to read

📌 Nota gramatical

-er verb, auxiliary verb 'ter', irregular present singular forms (eu leio).

Exemplo

Gosto de ler livros de aventura no meu quarto.

I like to read adventure books in my bedroom.

escrever

to write

📌 Nota gramatical

-er verb, auxiliary verb 'ter', regular conjugation pattern.

Exemplo

Escrevo uma carta formal para o meu professor.

I am writing a formal letter to my teacher.

ouvir

to listen / to hear

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir verb, auxiliary verb 'ter', irregular present stem (ouço).

Exemplo

Os alunos ouvem o professor com muita atenção.

The students listen to the teacher with great attention.

falar

to speak

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter', regular conjugation pattern.

Exemplo

Falamos português durante a aula de línguas.

We speak Portuguese during the language class.

perguntar

to ask

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter', regular conjugation pattern.

Exemplo

Posso perguntar uma dúvida sobre o exercício?

Can I ask a question about the exercise?

responder

to answer / to reply

📌 Nota gramatical

-er verb, auxiliary verb 'ter', regular conjugation pattern.

Exemplo

Tu sabes responder a esta questão difícil?

Do you know how to answer this difficult question?

explicar

to explain

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb (c->qu spelling change in preterite eu form: expliquei), auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

A professora explica a regra gramatical no quadro.

The female teacher explains the grammar rule on the board.

entender

to understand

📌 Nota gramatical

-er verb, auxiliary verb 'ter', regular conjugation pattern.

Exemplo

Eu não entendo esta palavra nova.

I do not understand this new word.

praticar

to practice

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb (c->qu spelling change in preterite eu form: pratiquei), auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Pratico conversação com um amigo português.

I practice conversation with a Portuguese friend.

memorizar

to memorize

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter', regular conjugation pattern.

Exemplo

Tento memorizar os verbos irregulares da lista.

I try to memorize the irregular verbs on the list.

repetir

to repeat

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir verb, auxiliary verb 'ter', stem-changing in present (eu repito, tu repetes).

Exemplo

Podes repetir a frase, por favor?

Can you repeat the sentence, please?

corrigir

to correct

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir verb (g->j spelling change in present eu form: corrojo), auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

O professor corrige os erros no meu caderno.

The teacher corrects the mistakes in my notebook.

copiar

to copy

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter', regular conjugation pattern.

Exemplo

Copia os apontamentos do quadro para o caderno.

Copy the notes from the board into the notebook.

terminar

to finish

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter', regular conjugation pattern.

Exemplo

Terminei o meu trabalho de português agora.

I finished my Portuguese assignment just now.

começar

to start / to begin

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb (ç->c spelling change before 'e': comecei), auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

A aula de matemática começa às oito horas.

The math class starts at eight o'clock.

passar

to pass / to happen

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter', regular conjugation pattern.

Exemplo

Eu passei no exame final de francês.

I passed the final French exam.

faltar

to be absent / to miss

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter', used with preposition 'a' for missing class.

Exemplo

Faltei à aula de inglês ontem porque estava doente.

I missed the English class yesterday because I was sick.
