Frases de exemplo

Etiquetas:

← → Teclas de seta para navegar · A para ouvir

comer

to eat

📌 Nota gramatical

-er conjugation group. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Eu como uma maçã.

I eat an apple.

beber

to drink

📌 Nota gramatical

-er conjugation group. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Eu bebo água fresca.

I drink fresh water.

querer

to want

📌 Nota gramatical

-er conjugation group with stem change in present. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Eu quero um café.

I want a coffee.

gostar

to like

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation group. Requires preposition 'de'. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Eu gosto de peixe.

I like fish.

almoçar

to have lunch

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation group. Orthographic change ç before a/o. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Eu almoço às doze.

I have lunch at twelve.

jantar

to have dinner

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation group. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Eu janto cedo.

I have dinner early.

cozinhar

to cook

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation group. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Eu cozinho bem.

I cook well.

comprar

to buy

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation group. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Eu compro fruta.

I buy fruit.

pagar

to pay

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation group. Orthographic change gu before e (peguei). Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Eu pago o jantar.

I pay for dinner.

pedir

to ask for / to order

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir conjugation group with stem change in present (eu peço). Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Eu peço um bife.

I order a steak.

levar

to take / to bring

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation group. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Eu levo a conta.

I'll take the check.

trazer

to bring

📌 Nota gramatical

-er irregular verb (eu trago). Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Eu trago o menu.

I bring the menu.

provar

to taste / to try

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation group. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Eu provo o vinho.

I taste the wine.

cortar

to cut

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation group. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Eu corto o pão.

I cut the bread.

servir

to serve

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir conjugation group with stem change in present (eu sirvo). Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Eu sirvo o almoço.

I serve lunch.

tomar

to take / to have (food/drink)

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation group. Used in 'tomar pequeno-almoço' (to have breakfast). Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Eu tomo café.

I have coffee.

fazer

to make / to do

📌 Nota gramatical

-er irregular verb (eu faço). Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Eu faço o jantar.

I make dinner.

preparar

to prepare

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation group. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Eu preparo a salada.

I prepare the salad.

adorar

to love / to adore

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation group. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Eu adoro chocolate.

I love chocolate.

detestar

to detest / to hate

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation group. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Eu detesto sopa.

I hate soup.
