estar

to be (temporary states/feelings)

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -ar verb used for temporary states, moods, and emotions. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Eu estou feliz hoje.

I am happy today.

sentir-se

to feel

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular reflexive -ir verb with stem change in present (sinto, sentes). Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Eu sinto-me bem.

I feel good.

sorrir

to smile

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb expressing a positive emotion/action. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Elas sorriem sempre.

They always smile.

chorar

to cry

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb expressing sadness or pain. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

O bebé chora muito.

The baby cries a lot.

gostar

to like

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, takes the preposition 'de' (gostar de). Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Eu gosto de ti.

I like you.

amar

to love

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb expressing deep affection. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Eu amo a minha família.

I love my family.

odiar

to hate

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb expressing strong dislike. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Eu odeio o frio.

I hate the cold.

temer

to fear

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb expressing fear or worry. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Eu temo o escuro.

I fear the dark.

irritar

to annoy / to irritate

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb describing annoyance. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Isso irrita-me muito.

That annoys me a lot.

preocupar-se

to worry

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular reflexive -ar verb, takes preposition 'com' (preocupar-se com). Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Eu não me preocupo.

I don't worry.

divertir-se

to have fun / to enjoy oneself

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular reflexive -ir verb with stem change in present (divirto). Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Eu divirto-me na festa.

I have fun at the party.

assustar

to scare / to frighten

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

O cão assusta-me.

The dog scares me.

agradar

to please

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Isso agrada-me muito.

That pleases me a lot.

agoniar

to distress / to nauseate

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

O calor agonia-me.

The heat distresses me.

acalmar

to calm down

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Eu preciso de acalmar.

I need to calm down.

ofender

to offend

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Eu não quero ofender-te.

I don't want to offend you.

perdoar

to forgive

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Eu perdoo-te.

I forgive you.

agradecer

to thank / to be grateful

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -cer verb (c becomes ç before o/a). Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Eu agradeço a ajuda.

I thank you for the help.

desesperar

to despair

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Eu não quero desesperar.

I don't want to despair.

admirar

to admire

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb expressing admiration. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Eu admiro a tua coragem.

I admire your courage.
