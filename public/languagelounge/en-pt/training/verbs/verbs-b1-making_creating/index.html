fazer

to make / to do

📌 Nota gramatical

-er verb group with highly irregular stems in the present and past tenses; auxiliary verb ter.

Exemplo

Costumo fazer um bolo de chocolate sempre que os meus amigos vêm jantar.

I usually make a chocolate cake whenever my friends come over for dinner.

criar

to create

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, fully regular conjugation pattern; auxiliary verb ter.

Exemplo

O artista conseguiu criar uma escultura maravilhosa com materiais reciclados.

The artist managed to create a wonderful sculpture using recycled materials.

construir

to build / to construct

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir verb with stem vowel alternation in the present indicative (u/i); auxiliary verb ter.

Exemplo

Eles vão construir uma casa nova no campo para passarem lá o verão.

They are going to build a new house in the countryside to spend the summer there.

produzir

to produce

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir verb with a -z- in certain present forms (produzo); auxiliary verb ter.

Exemplo

Esta fábrica local pretende produzir energia limpa através de painéis solares.

This local factory aims to produce clean energy through solar panels.

inventar

to invent

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, fully regular conjugation; auxiliary verb ter.

Exemplo

As crianças adoram inventar histórias fantásticas antes de adormecerem.

Children love to invent fantastic stories before falling asleep.

desenhar

to draw / to design

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, regular pattern; auxiliary verb ter.

Exemplo

O arquiteto passou semanas a desenhar os planos para o novo museu.

The architect spent weeks drawing the plans for the new museum.

preparar

to prepare

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, regular conjugation; auxiliary verb ter.

Exemplo

Comecei a preparar a apresentação importante que vou dar na próxima terça-feira.

I started to prepare the important presentation that I will give next Tuesday.

organizar

to organize

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group with orthographic change in 1st person singular preterite (-g- to -gu-); auxiliary verb ter.

Exemplo

Conseguimos organizar um evento solidário que ajudou centenas de famílias carenciadas.

We managed to organize a charity event that helped hundreds of needy families.

desenvolver

to develop

📌 Nota gramatical

-er verb group, regular conjugation; auxiliary verb ter.

Exemplo

A empresa investiu muito dinheiro para desenvolver uma aplicação móvel inovadora.

The company invested a lot of money to develop an innovative mobile application.

formar

to form / to train / to educate

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, regular pattern; auxiliary verb ter.

Exemplo

A universidade pretende formar profissionais qualificados para o mercado digital moderno.

The university aims to train qualified professionals for the modern digital market.

causar

to cause

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, regular conjugation; auxiliary verb ter.

Exemplo

A forte tempestade de ontem à noite chegou a causar vários estragos na cidade.

Last night's severe storm ended up causing various damages in the city.

compor

to compose / to make up

📌 Nota gramatical

-or verb group (derived from poner), highly irregular conjugation; auxiliary verb ter.

Exemplo

O músico passou meses a compor canções inspiradas nas suas viagens por Portugal.

The musician spent months composing songs inspired by his travels through Portugal.

estabelecer

to establish

📌 Nota gramatical

-er verb group with standard -cer to -ço pattern in present 1st person; auxiliary verb ter.

Exemplo

A nova empresa conseguiu estabelecer parcerias importantes com marcas internacionais.

The new company managed to establish important partnerships with international brands.

gerar

to generate

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, regular pattern; auxiliary verb ter.

Exemplo

O projeto inovador vai gerar dezenas de novos postos de trabalho na região.

The innovative project will generate dozens of new jobs in the region.

montar

to assemble / to set up / to mount

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, regular conjugation; auxiliary verb ter.

Exemplo

Demorámos duas horas a montar todos os móveis novos que comprámos na loja.

It took us two hours to assemble all the new furniture we bought at the store.

fabricar

to manufacture

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group with orthographic change in 1st person singular preterite (-c- to -qu-); auxiliary verb ter.

Exemplo

Esta pequena empresa familiar continua a fabricar calçado artesanal de alta qualidade.

This small family business continues to manufacture high-quality handmade footwear.

fundar

to found / to establish

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, regular pattern; auxiliary verb ter.

Exemplo

O meu avô ajudou a fundar esta instituição de caridade há mais de quarenta anos.

My grandfather helped found this charity institution over forty years ago.

realizar

to carry out / to realize / to hold (an event)

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group with orthographic change in 1st person singular preterite (-z-); auxiliary verb ter.

Exemplo

A associação vai realizar um concerto beneficente para angariar fundos este fim de semana.

The association is going to hold a benefit concert to raise funds this weekend.

provocar

to provoke / to cause

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group with orthographic change in 1st person singular preterite (-c- to -qu-); auxiliary verb ter.

Exemplo

O novo imposto pode provocar grandes protestos por parte dos proprietários de carros.

The new tax may cause major protests by car owners.

cometer

to commit (a mistake/crime)

📌 Nota gramatical

-er verb group, regular conjugation pattern; auxiliary verb ter.

Exemplo

É normal cometer erros quando estamos a aprender uma nova língua estrangeira.

It is normal to commit mistakes when we are learning a new foreign language.
