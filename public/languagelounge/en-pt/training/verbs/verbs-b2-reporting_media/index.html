noticiar

to report news

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter', takes direct object.

Exemplo

O telejornal de ontem decidiu noticiar o escândalo financeiro em primeira mão.

Yesterday's TV news decided to report the financial scandal firsthand.

divulgar

to disclose, to release

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb with spelling change in 1st person singular preterite (divulguei), auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

A polícia vai divulgar novos dados sobre a investigação no jornal da noite.

The police will release new data about the investigation on the evening news.

censurar

to censor

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

O governo tentou censurar o artigo crítico publicado na imprensa independente.

The government tried to censor the critical article published in the independent press.

transmitir

to broadcast, to transmit

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb, auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

A estação de rádio vai transmitir o debate político em direto esta noite.

The radio station is going to broadcast the political debate live tonight.

publicar

to publish

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb with spelling change in 1st person singular preterite (publiquei), auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

O jornal de investigação decidiu publicar a entrevista exclusiva com o ministro.

The investigative newspaper decided to publish the exclusive interview with the minister.

esclarecer

to clarify, to clear up

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb, auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

O porta-voz convocou uma conferência para esclarecer as dúvidas sobre a nova lei.

The spokesperson called a conference to clarify doubts about the new law.

desmentir

to deny, to refute

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb (follows mentir model in stems where applicable, but standard -ir here), auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

A administração teve de desmentir rapidamente os rumores falsos na imprensa.

The administration had to quickly deny the false rumors in the press.

enfatizar

to emphasize

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb with spelling change in 1st person singular preterite (enfatizei), auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

O editorial de hoje faz questão de enfatizar a importância da liberdade de imprensa.

Today's editorial makes a point of emphasizing the importance of freedom of the press.

entrevistar

to interview

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

A jornalista vai entrevistar o escritor premiado no programa de amanhã.

The journalist is going to interview the award-winning writer on tomorrow's show.

comentar

to comment on

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Os analistas políticos recusaram-se a comentar os resultados parciais das eleições.

The political analysts refused to comment on the partial election results.

generalizar

to generalize

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb with spelling change in 1st person singular preterite (generalizei), auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Não se deve generalizar a partir de um único caso relatado pela imprensa.

One should not generalize from a single case reported by the press.

garantir

to guarantee, to assure

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb with stem vowel alternation in present (garanto, garantes), auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

A fonte anónima garantiu que a informação era totalmente fidedigna.

The anonymous source assured that the information was completely reliable.

apresentar

to present, to host

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Ela foi convidada para apresentar o principal telejornal da estação pública.

She was invited to host the public station's main television news.

acusar

to accuse

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

O jornal acusou o político de manipular dados estatísticos para ganhar votos.

The newspaper accused the politician of manipulating statistical data to win votes.

demonstrar

to demonstrate, to show

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

A investigação jornalística conseguiu demonstrar a veracidade das acusações.

The journalistic investigation managed to demonstrate the truth of the accusations.

verificar

to verify, to check

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb with spelling change in 1st person singular preterite (verifiquei), auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

É fundamental verificar as fontes antes de publicar qualquer notícia online.

It is essential to verify sources before publishing any news online.

revelar

to reveal

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Um jornal de referência prometeu revelar novos documentos secretos amanhã.

A newspaper of record promised to reveal new secret documents tomorrow.

criticar

to criticize

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb with spelling change in 1st person singular preterite (critiquei), auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Vários críticos de televisão aproveitaram para criticar a falta de originalidade.

Several television critics took the opportunity to criticize the lack of originality.

alegar

to claim, to allege

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

A defesa do jornal alegou que a publicação estava protegida pela lei da imprensa.

The newspaper's defense claimed that the publication was protected by press law.

insinuar

to insinuate

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

O comentador parecia insinuar que houve corrupção na adjudicação do contrato.

The commentator seemed to insinuate that there was corruption in awarding the contract.
