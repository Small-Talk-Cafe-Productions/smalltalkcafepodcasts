passear

to stroll / to go for a walk/outing

📌 Nota gramatical

Verb ending in -ear: inserts 'i' in singular and 3rd plural present ('passeio, passeias, passeia, passeiam').

Exemplo

Ao domingo à tarde gostamos de passear à beira-mar em Cascais.

On Sunday afternoons we like to stroll by the sea in Cascais.

jogar

to play (games/sports)

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; spelling change 'joguei' in preterite. Used for sports and board/video games.

Exemplo

Os meus amigos jogam futebol todas as quartas-feiras à noite.

My friends play football every Wednesday evening.

tocar

to play (musical instruments) / to touch

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; spelling change 'toquei' in preterite. Used for musical instruments.

Exemplo

O meu irmão toca guitarra portuguesa numa casa de fado.

My brother plays Portuguese guitar in a fado house.

pintar

to paint

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb.

Exemplo

Nos tempos livres, ela pinta aguarelas de paisagens alentejanas.

In her spare time, she paints watercolours of Alentejo landscapes.

desenhar

to draw / to sketch

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb.

Exemplo

O Miguel desenha retratos a carvão com imenso talento.

Miguel draws charcoal portraits with immense talent.

fotografar

to photograph / to take photos

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb.

Exemplo

Adoro fotografar os elétricos antigos nas colinas de Lisboa.

I love photographing the old trams on the hills of Lisbon.

dançar

to dance

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; spelling change 'dancei' in preterite.

Exemplo

Eles dançaram a noite toda nas festas de Santo António.

They danced all night at the Saint Anthony festivities.

cantar

to sing

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb.

Exemplo

A fadista cantou em silêncio absoluto perante o público.

The fado singer sang in absolute silence before the audience.

nadar

to swim

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb.

Exemplo

No verão nadamos no mar, embora a água seja fresca.

In summer we swim in the sea, although the water is chilly.

acampar

to camp

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb.

Exemplo

No mês de agosto costumamos acampar na Costa Vicentina.

In August we usually camp on the Vicentine Coast.

pescar

to fish

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; spelling change 'pesquei' in preterite.

Exemplo

O meu tio gosta de pescar à linha nas rochas da Ericeira.

My uncle likes line-fishing on the rocks in Ericeira.

colecionar

to collect (as a hobby)

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; spelled with single 'c' in modern PT-PT ('colecionar').

Exemplo

O meu avô coleciona selos antigos e moedas raras.

My grandfather collects old stamps and rare coins.

jardinar

to garden

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb.

Exemplo

A minha mãe adora jardinar e cuidar das roseiras ao sábado.

My mother loves gardening and tending the rose bushes on Saturdays.

pedalar

to cycle / to pedal

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb.

Exemplo

Pedalámos ao longo da ciclovia desde Belém até ao Cais do Sodré.

We cycled along the bike path from Belém to Cais do Sodré.

divertir-se

to have fun / to enjoy oneself

📌 Nota gramatical

Stem-changing -ir reflexive verb ('divirto-me, divertes-te, diverte-se').

Exemplo

Nós divertimo-nos imenso no concerto de ontem à noite.

We had a great time at last night's concert.

brincar

to play (children's play / to joke)

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; spelling change 'brinquei'. Used for free play, not structured sports.

Exemplo

Os miúdos estão a brincar às escondidas no jardim.

The kids are playing hide-and-seek in the garden.
