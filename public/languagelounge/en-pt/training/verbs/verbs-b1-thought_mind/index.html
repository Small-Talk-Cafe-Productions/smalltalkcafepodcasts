pensar

to think

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation; often followed by the preposition 'em' (e.g., pensar em alguém).

Exemplo

Eu preciso de pensar bem antes de tomar uma decisão importante.

I need to think carefully before making an important decision.

acreditar

to believe

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation; generally followed by the preposition 'em' (acreditar em).

Exemplo

Eu acredito que a educação é fundamental para mudar a sociedade.

I believe that education is essential to change society.

lembrar-se

to remember

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar reflexive verb; uses enclitic pronoun placement in European Portuguese; followed by 'de'.

Exemplo

Eu não me lembro onde é que deixei as chaves do carro.

I don't remember where I left the car keys.

esquecer-se

to forget

📌 Nota gramatical

-er reflexive verb; uses enclitic pronoun placement in European Portuguese; followed by 'de'.

Exemplo

Eu esqueci-me completamente de comprar o pão para o pequeno-almoço.

I completely forgot to buy the bread for breakfast.

imaginar

to imagine

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation pattern.

Exemplo

Eu consigo imaginar como seria viver numa ilha deserta.

I can imagine what it would be like to live on a deserted island.

compreender

to understand

📌 Nota gramatical

-er verb; regular conjugation pattern.

Exemplo

Eu compreendo perfeitamente os teus motivos para mudar de emprego.

I perfectly understand your reasons for changing jobs.

entender

to understand / to figure out

📌 Nota gramatical

-er verb; regular conjugation pattern, synonymous with compreender.

Exemplo

Eu não entendo porque é que ele se chateou com aquela brincadeira.

I don't understand why he got upset with that joke.

achar

to think / to find (opinion)

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation; very common for expressing personal opinions.

Exemplo

Eu acho que este filme é demasiado longo e um pouco aborrecido.

I think this movie is too long and a bit boring.

duvidar

to doubt

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation; followed by the preposition 'de'.

Exemplo

Eu duvido que ele consiga acabar aquele relatório até amanhã.

I doubt he can finish that report by tomorrow.

supor

to suppose / to guess

📌 Nota gramatical

-or verb (derived from pôr); irregular conjugation pattern.

Exemplo

Eu suponho que eles já tenham chegado ao hotel em Lisboa.

I suppose they have already arrived at the hotel in Lisbon.

considerar

to consider

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation pattern.

Exemplo

Eu considero esta proposta muito interessante para o futuro da empresa.

I consider this proposal very interesting for the company's future.

decidir

to decide

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir verb; regular conjugation pattern.

Exemplo

Eu decidi tirar férias em setembro para viajar para o Algarve.

I decided to take a vacation in September to travel to the Algarve.

reparar

to notice / to realize

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation; often followed by 'em'.

Exemplo

Eu não reparei que tinhas cortado o cabelo tão curto.

I didn't notice that you had cut your hair so short.

notar

to notice

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation pattern.

Exemplo

Eu notei uma grande diferença no comportamento dele esta semana.

I noticed a big difference in his behavior this week.

desconfiar

to distrust / to suspect

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation; followed by 'de'.

Exemplo

Eu desconfio que há qualquer coisa escondida nesta história.

I suspect there is something hidden in this story.

desculpar

to excuse / to forgive

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation; often used reflexively as 'desculpar-se'.

Exemplo

Eu espero que ele me desculpe pelo atraso na reunião.

I hope he excuses me for being late to the meeting.

resolver

to solve / to decide

📌 Nota gramatical

-er verb; regular conjugation pattern in European Portuguese.

Exemplo

Eu resolvi estudar mais para conseguir passar no exame de condução.

I decided to study more to manage to pass the driving test.

adivinhar

to guess

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation pattern.

Exemplo

Eu tentei adivinhar a resposta certa, mas estava completamente errado.

I tried to guess the right answer, but I was completely wrong.

calcular

to calculate / to figure

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation pattern.

Exemplo

Eu calculo que demoremos cerca de duas horas a chegar ao Porto de autocarro.

I figure it will take us about two hours to get to Porto by bus.

estranhar

to find strange / to be unaccustomed to

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation pattern.

Exemplo

Eu estranhei o silêncio na casa quando cheguei do trabalho.

I found the silence in the house strange when I arrived from work.
