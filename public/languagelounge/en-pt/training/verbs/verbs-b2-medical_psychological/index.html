diagnosticar

to diagnose

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

O médico conseguiu diagnosticar a doença rara logo na primeira consulta de rotina.

The doctor managed to diagnose the rare illness right in the first routine appointment.

desmaiar

to faint

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Auxiliary verb: ter / ser.

Exemplo

A paciente esteve tanto tempo em pé na sala de espera que acabou por desmaiar.

The female patient stood for so long in the waiting room that she ended up fainting.

receitar

to prescribe

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

O especialista decidiu receitar um analgésico mais forte para aliviar a dor crónica.

The specialist decided to prescribe a stronger painkiller to alleviate the chronic pain.

melhorar

to improve / to recover

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Depois de iniciar a fisioterapia, o paciente começou a melhorar significativamente a mobilidade.

After starting physiotherapy, the patient began to significantly improve his mobility.

internar

to hospitalize / to admit

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Os médicos decidiram internar o homem devido a complicações respiratórias graves.

The doctors decided to hospitalize the man due to severe respiratory complications.

curar

to cure / to heal

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

A medicina moderna ainda procura curar certas doenças genéticas incuráveis.

Modern medicine still seeks to cure certain incurable genetic diseases.

examinar

to examine / to check up

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

A pediatra quis examinar a garganta da criança para ver se havia infeção.

The pediatrician wanted to examine the child's throat to see if there was an infection.

operar

to operate (surgery)

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

A equipa cirúrgica vai operar o joelho do atleta já na próxima segunda-feira.

The surgical team is going to operate on the athlete's knee as early as next Monday.

acalmar

to calm down / to soothe

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

A enfermeira tentou acalmar o paciente que estava muito assustado com a operação.

The nurse tried to calm down the patient who was very frightened about the surgery.

engolir

to swallow

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Devido à forte dor de garganta, custava-lhe muito engolir qualquer tipo de alimento.

Due to the severe sore throat, it was very hard for him to swallow any kind of food.

esquecer-se

to forget

📌 Nota gramatical

Reflexive -er verb requiring the preposition 'de'. European placement: 'eu esqueci-me', 'tu esqueceste-te', etc. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

O paciente costuma esquecer-se de tomar a medicação às horas certas.

The patient usually forgets to take his medication at the right times.

traumatizar

to traumatize

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Um acidente de viação grave pode traumatizar profundamente uma pessoa.

A serious car accident can deeply traumatize a person.

enfrentar

to face / to confront

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Com a ajuda da psicoterapia, ela aprendeu a enfrentar os seus medos mais profundos.

With the help of psychotherapy, she learned to face her deepest fears.

suportar

to endure / to bear

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

A dor nas costas era tão intensa que ele mal conseguia suportar o desconforto.

The back pain was so intense that he could barely endure the discomfort.

sangrar

to bleed

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

O corte no braço era profundo e começou logo a sangrar abundantemente.

The cut on his arm was deep and immediately began to bleed heavily.

recuperar

to recover / to convalesce

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Após a cirurgia cardíaca, o paciente vai recuperar no quarto durante uma semana.

Following heart surgery, the patient will recover in the room for a week.

inchar

to swell

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

O tornozelo começou logo a inchar depois da torção durante o jogo de futebol.

The ankle immediately began to swell after the twist during the football match.

bloquear

to block / to freeze (psychologically)

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

O medo do fracasso pode bloquear a criatividade de qualquer profissional sob pressão.

Fear of failure can block the creativity of any professional under pressure.

desabafar

to vent / to confide

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

É muito importante desabafar com um terapeuta quando nos sentimos sobrecarregados.

It is very important to vent to a therapist when we feel overwhelmed.

envenenar

to poison

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Os testes revelaram que o animal foi acidentalmente envenenado com pesticida.

The tests revealed that the animal was accidentally poisoned with pesticide.
