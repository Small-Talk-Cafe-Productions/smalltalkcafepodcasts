achar

to think, to find (have an opinion)

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, regular conjugation, auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Eu acho que este filme é demasiado longo para ser interessante.

I think that this movie is too long to be interesting.

acreditar

to believe

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, regular conjugation, usually followed by 'em' or 'que'.

Exemplo

Eu acredito que a educação pode mudar completamente a sociedade moderna.

I believe that education can completely change modern society.

pensar

to think, to reflect

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, regular conjugation, used for mental processes and opinions.

Exemplo

Nós precisamos de pensar bem antes de tomar uma decisão difícil.

We need to think carefully before making a difficult decision.

defender

to defend, to advocate

📌 Nota gramatical

-er verb group, regular conjugation, used in arguments and debates.

Exemplo

Ela defende sempre os direitos dos trabalhadores nas reuniões sindicais.

She always defends workers' rights in union meetings.

concordar

to agree

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, regular conjugation, typically takes the preposition 'com'.

Exemplo

Eu concordo totalmente com a tua proposta para melhorar o projeto.

I completely agree with your proposal to improve the project.

discordar

to disagree

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, regular conjugation, typically takes the preposition 'de'.

Exemplo

Lamento muito, mas eu discordo completamente da tua perspetiva.

I am very sorry, but I completely disagree with your perspective.

argumentar

to argue, to reason

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, regular conjugation, used to support an argument.

Exemplo

O advogado argumentou que o cliente não estava no local do crime.

The lawyer argued that the client was not at the crime scene.

provar

to prove, to taste

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, regular conjugation, used for proving a point or tasting food.

Exemplo

Os cientistas conseguiram provar a eficácia da nova vacina.

The scientists managed to prove the effectiveness of the new vaccine.

justificar

to justify

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, spelling change in 1st person singular preterite (c -> qu: justifiquei).

Exemplo

Como é que tu podes justificar uma atitude tão irresponsável?

How can you justify such an irresponsible attitude?

demonstrar

to demonstrate, to show

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, regular conjugation, used to demonstrate a point or emotion.

Exemplo

O relatório demonstra claramente que o investimento foi rentável.

The report clearly demonstrates that the investment was profitable.

duvidar

to doubt

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, regular conjugation, usually takes the preposition 'de'.

Exemplo

Eu duvido muito que ele consiga terminar o trabalho a tempo.

I very much doubt that he can finish the work on time.

convencer

to convince

📌 Nota gramatical

-er verb group, regular conjugation, used in persuasive arguments.

Exemplo

O vendedor tentou convencer-me a comprar o carro novo.

The salesman tried to convince me to buy the new car.

admitir

to admit

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir verb group, regular conjugation, used when conceding a point.

Exemplo

Ele teve de admitir que cometeu um erro grave no projeto.

He had to admit that he made a serious mistake in the project.

negar

to deny

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, regular conjugation, used to refute accusations or facts.

Exemplo

O suspeito continua a negar todas as acusações feitas pela polícia.

The suspect continues to deny all accusations made by the police.

sustentar

to sustain, to uphold (an argument)

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, regular conjugation, used to maintain a stance or theory.

Exemplo

O investigador sustenta que a teoria antiga está completamente errada.

The researcher maintains that the old theory is completely wrong.

criticar

to criticize

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, spelling change in 1st person singular preterite (c -> qu: critiquei).

Exemplo

Muitas pessoas criticam o governo pelas novas medidas económicas.

Many people criticize the government for the new economic measures.

apoiar

to support, to back

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, regular conjugation, used for supporting causes or opinions.

Exemplo

Nós apoiamos a iniciativa local para proteger o ambiente natural.

We support the local initiative to protect the natural environment.

rejeitar

to reject

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, regular conjugation, used when turning down ideas or proposals.

Exemplo

O parlamento decidiu rejeitar a proposta de lei apresentada ontem.

Parliament decided to reject the bill presented yesterday.

reconhecer

to recognize, to acknowledge

📌 Nota gramatical

-er verb group, regular conjugation, used when acknowledging facts or truths.

Exemplo

É importante reconhecer que cometemos falhas no processo de planeamento.

It is important to acknowledge that we made mistakes in the planning process.

concluir

to conclude

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir verb group, stem changes in present indicative (eu concluo, tu concluis, ele conclui).

Exemplo

Com base nestes dados, podemos concluir que o mercado está a mudar.

Based on this data, we can conclude that the market is changing.
