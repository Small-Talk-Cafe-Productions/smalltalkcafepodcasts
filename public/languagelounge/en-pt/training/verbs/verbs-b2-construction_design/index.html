construir

to build / to construct

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -uir verb ('construo, constróis, constrói, construímos, constroem').

Exemplo

A câmara decidiu construir uma nova ponte pedonal sobre o rio.

The town hall decided to build a new pedestrian bridge over the river.

reconstruir

to rebuild / to reconstruct

📌 Nota gramatical

Conjugated like 'construir' ('reconstruo, reconstróis, reconstrói').

Exemplo

O Marquês de Pombal mandou reconstruir a Baixa de Lisboa após o terramoto de 1755.

The Marquis of Pombal ordered the rebuilding of downtown Lisbon after the 1755 earthquake.

demolir

to demolish

📌 Nota gramatical

Stem-changing -ir verb ('o' becomes 'u' in 1st person singular present: 'demulo, demoles, demole').

Exemplo

As autoridades decidiram demolir o edifício devoluto por razões de segurança.

The authorities decided to demolish the derelict building for safety reasons.

projetar

to design / to project / to plan

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; spelled 'projetar' (without silent 'c') in modern European Portuguese.

Exemplo

O arquiteto Álvaro Siza Vieira projetou o Pavilhão de Portugal para a Expo 98.

The architect Álvaro Siza Vieira designed the Pavilion of Portugal for Expo 98.

planear

to plan

📌 Nota gramatical

-ear verb ('planeio, planeias, planeia, planeamos, planeiam'); standard PT-PT verb (vs BR 'planejar').

Exemplo

Os urbanistas planeiam alargar as zonas pedonais no centro histórico.

Urban planners plan to expand the pedestrian zones in the historic centre.

edificar

to erect / to build (formal)

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; spelling change 'edifiquei' in 1st person singular preterite.

Exemplo

Os romanos edificaram templos e aquedutos que ainda hoje perduram na Península Ibérica.

The Romans erected temples and aqueducts that still endure today in the Iberian Peninsula.

erguer

to raise / to erect

📌 Nota gramatical

-guer verb: drops 'u' before 'o' in 1st person singular present ('ergo, ergues, ergue').

Exemplo

Em poucos meses ergueram a estrutura metálica do novo pavilhão desportivo.

In a few months they erected the metal structure of the new sports pavilion.

remodelar

to remodel / to refurbish

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb.

Exemplo

Comprámos um apartamento antigo e vamos remodelar a cozinha e a casa de banho.

We bought an old flat and are going to refurbish the kitchen and bathroom.

reabilitar

to rehabilitate / to renovate (buildings/urban areas)

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; key urban planning verb in Portugal ('reabilitação urbana').

Exemplo

O programa municipal incentiva os proprietários a reabilitar prédios degradados.

The municipal programme encourages owners to renovate rundown buildings.

isolar

to insulate / to isolate

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb.

Exemplo

É fundamental isolar termicamente as paredes e o telhado para poupar energia no inverno.

It is essential to thermally insulate the walls and roof to save energy in winter.

pavimentar

to pave

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb.

Exemplo

Os calceteiros pavimentaram a praça com calçada portuguesa tradicional.

The master pavers paved the square with traditional Portuguese cobblestone.

estruturar

to structure

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb.

Exemplo

Os engenheiros civis estruturaram as fundações para resistir a abalos sísmicos.

The civil engineers structured the foundations to withstand seismic tremors.

esboçar

to sketch / to outline

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; spelling change 'esbocei' in 1st person singular preterite.

Exemplo

A designer esboçou as primeiras linhas do mobiliário num caderno a carvão.

The designer sketched the first lines of the furniture in a charcoal notebook.

arquitetar

to design / to architect / to devise

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; spelled 'arquitetar' without silent 'c' in PT-PT.

Exemplo

Eles arquitetaram uma solução engenhosa para ligar os dois jardins desnivelados.

They devised an ingenious solution to connect the two split-level gardens.

revestir

to clad / to coat / to cover (surfaces)

📌 Nota gramatical

Stem-changing -ir verb like 'vestir' ('revisto, revestes, reveste, revestimos, revestem').

Exemplo

Decidimos revestir a fachada exterior com azulejos artesanais azuis e brancos.

We decided to clad the exterior façade with handmade blue and white tiles.

urbanizar

to urbanize / to develop (land)

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb.

Exemplo

A câmara aprovou o plano para urbanizar a antiga zona industrial ribeirinha.

The town hall approved the plan to redevelop the former riverside industrial zone.
