Emotion & Feeling

emocionar-se

to be moved / to get emotional

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar reflexive verb; takes 'com' ('emocionar-se com algo').

Exemplo

O público emocionou-se com a interpretação da fadista.

The audience was moved by the fado singer's performance.

assustar-se

to get scared / to be frightened

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar reflexive verb; takes 'com'.

Exemplo

O cão assustou-se com o barulho dos trovões durante a tempestade.

The dog got scared by the sound of thunder during the storm.

preocupar-se

to worry

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar reflexive verb; takes the preposition 'com'.

Exemplo

Os pais preocupam-se com o futuro profissional dos filhos.

Parents worry about their children's professional future.

arrepender-se

to regret / to repent

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er reflexive verb; always takes the preposition 'de' ('arrepender-se de algo').

Exemplo

Não me arrependo de ter mudado de carreira aos trinta anos.

I don't regret having changed careers at thirty.

orgulhar-se

to be proud of

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar reflexive verb; takes the preposition 'de'.

Exemplo

Orgulhamo-nos muito dos resultados alcançados pela nossa equipa.

We are very proud of the results achieved by our team.

desiludir

to disappoint / to let down

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb; very common in European Portuguese (also reflexive 'desiludir-se').

Exemplo

O final do filme desiludiu a maioria dos espectadores.

The ending of the film disappointed most of the spectators.

comover

to move / to touch (emotionally)

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb.

Exemplo

A história de solidariedade daquela aldeia comoveu o país inteiro.

That village's story of solidarity moved the entire country.

admirar

to admire

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb.

Exemplo

Admiro profundamente a paciência e a dedicação dos professores.

I deeply admire the patience and dedication of teachers.

invejar

to envy

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb.

Exemplo

Confesso que invejo a calma com que resolves problemas difíceis.

I confess that I envy the calmness with which you solve difficult problems.

sofrer

to suffer

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb; takes 'de' for illnesses or 'com' for situations.

Exemplo

A agricultura no sul do país sofre com a falta de chuva.

Agriculture in the south of the country suffers from the lack of rain.

chorar

to cry / to weep

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb.

Exemplo

A criança chorou porque perdeu o seu brinquedo preferido no parque.

The child cried because he lost his favourite toy in the park.

sorrir

to smile

📌 Nota gramatical

Conjugated like 'rir' ('sorrio, sorris, sorri, sorrimos, sorriem').

Exemplo

Ela sorriu maliciosamente quando ouviu a nossa pergunta.

She smiled mischievously when she heard our question.

recear

to fear / to be afraid of

📌 Nota gramatical

-ear verb: inserts 'i' in singular and 3rd plural present ('receio, receias, receia, receiam').

Exemplo

Receio que o comboio das oito já tenha partido.

I fear that the eight o'clock train has already departed.

odiar

to hate

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -iar verb (conjugates like -ear in the present: 'odeio, odeias, odeia, odiamos, odeiam').

Exemplo

Odeio ficar preso no trânsito na Ponte 25 de Abril.

I hate getting stuck in traffic on the 25 de Abril Bridge.

entusiasmar-se

to get excited / to become enthusiastic

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar reflexive verb; takes 'com'.

Exemplo

Os alunos entusiasmaram-se imenso com a visita de estudo ao oceanário.

The students got very excited about the school trip to the oceanarium.

encantar

to enchant / to delight

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb.

Exemplo

A hospitalidade das pessoas do Minho encantou os visitantes estrangeiros.

The hospitality of the people of Minho delighted the foreign visitors.
