visitar

to visit

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation. Takes direct object without a preposition.

Exemplo

Sempre que vou a Lisboa, gosto de visitar os monumentos históricos.

Whenever I go to Lisbon, I like to visit the historical monuments.

viajar

to travel

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation. Often used with prepositions 'para' or 'por'.

Exemplo

Viajar de comboio pela Europa permite ver paisagens fantásticas.

Traveling by train through Europe allows you to see fantastic landscapes.

explorar

to explore

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation. Used for discovering new places or ideas.

Exemplo

Adoramos explorar as ruas antigas do centro histórico a pé.

We love exploring the old streets of the historical center on foot.

alugar

to rent

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation. Often used when renting cars or holiday apartments.

Exemplo

Decidimos alugar um carro para visitar as praias mais isoladas.

We decided to rent a car to visit the most isolated beaches.

reservar

to book / to reserve

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation. Essential for hotels, tickets, and restaurants.

Exemplo

É aconselhável reservar os bilhetes com antecedência na época alta.

It is advisable to book tickets in advance during the high season.

conhecer

to know (people/places) / to meet / to experience

📌 Nota gramatical

-er verb; regular conjugation. Used for getting to know places or people.

Exemplo

Quero muito conhecer a gastronomia tradicional do norte de Portugal.

I really want to experience the traditional gastronomy of northern Portugal.

descobrir

to discover

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir verb; stem vowel changes in present singular (descubro). Regular otherwise.

Exemplo

Caminhar sem destino é a melhor forma de descobrir recantos secretos.

Walking without a destination is the best way to discover secret corners.

apreciar

to appreciate / to enjoy

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation. Commonly used for art, views, and culture.

Exemplo

Gosto de sentar-me num café ao ar livre e apreciar a paisagem urbana.

I like sitting in an outdoor café and appreciating the urban landscape.

fotografar

to photograph

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation. Used when capturing travel memories.

Exemplo

Costumo fotografar os monumentos mais bonitos para recordar mais tarde.

I usually photograph the most beautiful monuments to remember later.

provar

to taste / to try (food)

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation. Used for local culinary experiences.

Exemplo

Quando vais ao Porto, deves provar os pratos típicos da região.

When you go to Porto, you must try the typical dishes of the region.

comprar

to buy

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation. Used for purchasing souvenirs and tickets.

Exemplo

Comprei algumas recordações artesanais para oferecer aos meus amigos.

I bought some artisanal souvenirs to offer to my friends.

celebrar

to celebrate

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation. Useful for cultural festivals and events.

Exemplo

As pessoas saem à rua para celebrar as festas populares com muita música.

People go out into the street to celebrate popular festivals with lots of music.

participar

to participate / to take part

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation. Takes preposition 'em' (participar em).

Exemplo

Os turistas podem participar nas oficinas de olaria tradicional.

Tourists can participate in traditional pottery workshops.

observar

to observe / to watch

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation. Used for sightseeing and nature watching.

Exemplo

Subimos ao miradouro para observar o pôr do sol sobre o oceano.

We went up to the viewpoint to watch the sunset over the ocean.

relaxar

to relax

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation. Frequently used when talking about holidays.

Exemplo

Depois de um dia intenso a caminhar, apetece-me relaxar na praia.

After an intense day of walking, I feel like relaxing on the beach.

informar-se

to find out / to get informed

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar reflexive verb with European pronoun placement (eu informo-me). Takes preposition 'sobre'.

Exemplo

É importante informar-se sobre os costumes locais antes de viajar.

It is important to find out about local customs before traveling.

perder-se

to get lost

📌 Nota gramatical

-er reflexive verb (perder) with stem change in present (perco-me). European placement.

Exemplo

Às vezes, perder-se nas ruas de uma cidade desconhecida é uma grande aventura.

Sometimes, getting lost in the streets of an unknown city is a great adventure.

comunicar

to communicate

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation. Useful when discussing language barriers while traveling.

Exemplo

Mesmo sem falar bem a língua, consegui comunicar com os locais.

Even without speaking the language well, I managed to communicate with the locals.

durar

to last

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; regular conjugation. Used for describing the length of trips or events.

Exemplo

A nossa viagem de interrail vai durar exatamente três semanas.

Our interrail trip is going to last exactly three weeks.

costumar

to be used to / to usually do

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb; modal auxiliary followed by an infinitive to express habits.

Exemplo

Costumamos planear o itinerário antes de partir para um novo país.

We usually plan the itinerary before leaving for a new country.
