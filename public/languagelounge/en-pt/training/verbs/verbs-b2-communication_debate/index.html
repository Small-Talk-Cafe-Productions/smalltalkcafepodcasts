argumentar

to argue

📌 Nota gramatical

regular -ar verb; takes preposition 'com' or 'contra'.

Exemplo

O advogado soube argumentar perante o júri de forma muito convincente.

The lawyer knew how to argue before the jury in a very convincing way.

esclarecer

to clarify

📌 Nota gramatical

regular -er verb; often used in formal or professional contexts.

Exemplo

Gostaria de esclarecer um mal-entendido que surgiu na última reunião.

I would like to clarify a misunderstanding that arose at the last meeting.

persuadir

to persuade

📌 Nota gramatical

regular -ir verb; takes direct object or reflexive.

Exemplo

A campanha publicitária conseguiu persuadir muitos consumidores a mudar de marca.

The advertising campaign managed to persuade many consumers to change brands.

contestar

to contest / to challenge

📌 Nota gramatical

regular -ar verb; implies challenging a decision or statement.

Exemplo

Os sindicatos decidiram contestar as novas medidas laborais apresentadas pelo governo.

The unions decided to contest the new labor measures presented by the government.

enfatizar

to emphasize

📌 Nota gramatical

regular -ar verb; spelling change (c to ç) before 'a', 'o', 'u'.

Exemplo

O orador quis enfatizar a importância da cooperação internacional no projeto.

The speaker wanted to emphasize the importance of international cooperation in the project.

refutar

to refute

📌 Nota gramatical

regular -ar verb; used in formal debate and academic discourse.

Exemplo

O cientista apresentou provas concretas para refutar a teoria anterior.

The scientist presented concrete evidence to refute the previous theory.

comunicar

to communicate

📌 Nota gramatical

regular -ar verb; takes preposition 'com' (with someone) or 'por' (via).

Exemplo

As empresas modernas precisam de comunicar eficazmente através das redes sociais.

Modern companies need to communicate effectively through social media.

sugerir

to suggest

📌 Nota gramatical

irregular -ir verb; stem change in 1st person singular present ('sugiro').

Exemplo

Posso sugerir uma abordagem diferente para resolver este problema complexo?

May I suggest a different approach to solve this complex problem?

exprimir

to express

📌 Nota gramatical

regular -ir verb; often used synonymously with 'expressar'.

Exemplo

A arte é uma forma excelente de exprimir emoções profundas e complexas.

Art is an excellent way to express deep and complex emotions.

divergir

to diverge / to disagree

📌 Nota gramatical

irregular -ir verb (similar pattern to sugerir); takes preposition 'de'.

Exemplo

Os peritos começaram a divergir quanto à interpretação dos resultados financeiros.

The experts began to diverge regarding the interpretation of the financial results.

convencer

to convince

📌 Nota gramatical

regular -er verb; c changes to ç in 1st person singular present ('convenço').

Exemplo

Foi preciso muito esforço para convencer a direção a aprovar o orçamento.

It took a lot of effort to convince management to approve the budget.

demonstrar

to demonstrate / to show

📌 Nota gramatical

regular -ar verb; very common in formal presentations and debates.

Exemplo

O relatório veio demonstrar que a empresa precisa de cortar custos urgentes.

The report demonstrated that the company needs to cut costs urgently.

intervir

to intervene

📌 Nota gramatical

irregular -ir verb; derived from 'vir', follows its conjugation pattern.

Exemplo

O moderador teve de intervir para acalmar os ânimos no debate aceso.

The moderator had to intervene to calm tempers in the heated debate.

retrucar

to retort / to snap back

📌 Nota gramatical

regular -ar verb; spelling change (c to qu) before 'e'.

Exemplo

Em vez de se calar, ela decidiu retrucar com uma frase muito espirituosa.

Instead of staying quiet, she decided to retort with a very witty phrase.

pontuar

to punctuate / to highlight

📌 Nota gramatical

regular -ar verb; often used metaphorically in speech.

Exemplo

O orador soube pontuar o seu discurso com momentos de humor inteligente.

The speaker knew how to punctuate his speech with moments of intelligent humor.

aludir

to allude / to mention

📌 Nota gramatical

regular -ir verb; takes preposition 'a'.

Exemplo

No seu discurso, o político quis aludir aos problemas económicos recentes.

In his speech, the politician wanted to allude to the recent economic problems.

concordar

to agree

📌 Nota gramatical

regular -ar verb; takes preposition 'com' (with someone/something).

Exemplo

A maioria dos deputados acabou por concordar com a nova proposta de lei.

Most deputies ended up agreeing with the new law proposal.

ponderar

to weigh / to consider carefully

📌 Nota gramatical

regular -ar verb; used when evaluating different communication options.

Exemplo

Precisamos de ponderar bem todos os prós e contras antes de responder.

We need to carefully weigh all the pros and cons before responding.

propugnar

to advocate / to champion

📌 Nota gramatical

regular -ar verb; formal vocabulary typical of political or philosophical debate.

Exemplo

O partido continua a propugnar reformas profundas no sistema educativo nacional.

The party continues to advocate deep reforms in the national education system.

retorquir

to retort / to talk back

📌 Nota gramatical

regular -ir verb; synonym of retrucar, frequent in written and spoken debate.

Exemplo

Sem perder a calma, ela soube retorquir com um argumento demolidor.

Without losing her cool, she knew how to retort with a devastating argument.
