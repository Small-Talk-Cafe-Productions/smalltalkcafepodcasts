ser

to be

📌 Nota gramatical

Third-conjugation irregular verb (-ir). Auxiliary verb for passive voice; uses ter in compound tenses.

Exemplo

Eu sou português e moro em Lisboa.

I am Portuguese and I live in Lisbon.

estar

to be

📌 Nota gramatical

First-conjugation irregular verb (-ar). Used for temporary states, locations, and progressive actions (estar a + infinitive).

Exemplo

Nós estamos a jantar na cozinha.

We are having dinner in the kitchen.

ter

to have

📌 Nota gramatical

Second-conjugation irregular verb (-er). Auxiliary verb for compound tenses; also used for age and physical sensations.

Exemplo

Eu tenho vinte anos de idade.

I am twenty years old.

ir

to go

📌 Nota gramatical

Third-conjugation irregular verb (-ir). Highly irregular; shares some past forms with 'ser'.

Exemplo

Nós vamos ao supermercado comprar pão.

We are going to the supermarket to buy bread.

vir

to come

📌 Nota gramatical

Third-conjugation irregular verb (-ir). Stem changes in present and other tenses.

Exemplo

Tu vens à festa de aniversário?

Are you coming to the birthday party?

fazer

to do, to make

📌 Nota gramatical

Second-conjugation irregular verb (-er). Irregular first-person singular present ('faço') and unique past stem.

Exemplo

Eu faço os meus trabalhos de casa.

I do my homework.

dizer

to say, to tell

📌 Nota gramatical

Second-conjugation irregular verb (-er). Irregular first-person singular present ('digo').

Exemplo

O que é que tu dizes?

What are you saying?

poder

to be able to, can

📌 Nota gramatical

Second-conjugation irregular modal verb (-er). Stem vowel changes in present tense.

Exemplo

Posso usar o teu telemóvel?

Can I use your mobile phone?

querer

to want

📌 Nota gramatical

Second-conjugation irregular verb (-er). Stem changes in present tense ('quero').

Exemplo

Eu quero beber um café com leite.

I want to drink a white coffee.

saber

to know (facts/information)

📌 Nota gramatical

Second-conjugation irregular verb (-er). Irregular first-person singular present ('sei').

Exemplo

Eu sei falar um pouco de francês.

I know how to speak a little French.

ver

to see

📌 Nota gramatical

Second-conjugation irregular verb (-er). Shortened forms in present and other tenses.

Exemplo

Nós vemos um filme todos os sábados.

We watch a film every Saturday.

trazer

to bring

📌 Nota gramatical

Second-conjugation irregular verb (-er). Irregular first-person present ('trago') and past stem.

Exemplo

Eu trago sempre o pequeno-almoço para a cama.

I always bring breakfast to bed.

dar

to give

📌 Nota gramatical

First-conjugation irregular verb (-ar). Irregular first-person present ('dou') and past tense.

Exemplo

Eu dou um presente ao meu amigo.

I give a gift to my friend.

ler

to read

📌 Nota gramatical

Second-conjugation irregular verb (-er). Orthographic adjustments in present 3rd person.

Exemplo

Eu leio o jornal todas as manhãs.

I read the newspaper every morning.

ouvir

to hear, to listen

📌 Nota gramatical

Third-conjugation irregular verb (-ir). Stem vowel change in present tense ('ouço').

Exemplo

Eu ouço música no meu quarto.

I listen to music in my bedroom.

pedir

to ask for, to request

📌 Nota gramatical

Third-conjugation irregular verb (-ir). Stem vowel change in present first person ('peço').

Exemplo

Eu peço sempre um sumo de laranja.

I always ask for an orange juice.

perder

to lose

📌 Nota gramatical

Second-conjugation irregular verb (-er). Stem vowel change in present first person ('perco').

Exemplo

Eu nunca perco as minhas chaves.

I never lose my keys.

pôr

to put, to place

📌 Nota gramatical

Old second-conjugation irregular verb (-or). Derived from Latin ponere; irregular in all tenses.

Exemplo

Eu ponho o livro em cima da mesa.

I put the book on top of the table.

dormir

to sleep

📌 Nota gramatical

Third-conjugation stem-changing verb (-ir). Changes 'o' to 'u' in the first-person present ('durmo').

Exemplo

Eu durmo oito horas por noite.

I sleep eight hours per night.

preferir

to prefer

📌 Nota gramatical

Third-conjugation stem-changing verb (-ir). Changes 'e' to 'i' in the first-person present ('prefiro').

Exemplo

Eu prefiro chá verde a café.

I prefer green tea to coffee.

Configure a sua sessão de treino.
