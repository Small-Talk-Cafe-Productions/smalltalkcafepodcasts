acordar

to wake up

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb.

Exemplo

Eu acordo cedo todos os dias.

I wake up early every day.

levantar-se

to get up

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar reflexive verb with European enclitic pronoun placement.

Exemplo

Eu levanto-me depressa.

I get up quickly.

tomar

to have (a meal/coffee)

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, used for meals like breakfast.

Exemplo

Eu tomo o pequeno-almoço às oito.

I have breakfast at eight.

começar

to start, to begin

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb with spelling change in 1st person singular (ç -> c before e).

Exemplo

A aula começa às nove.

The class starts at nine.

terminar

to finish, to end

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb.

Exemplo

O dia de trabalho termina às cinco.

The work day ends at five.

trabalhar

to work

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb.

Exemplo

Eu trabalho todo o dia.

I work all day.

almoçar

to have lunch

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb with spelling change (ç).

Exemplo

Eu almoço à uma da tarde.

I have lunch at one in the afternoon.

jantar

to have dinner

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb.

Exemplo

Eu janto às oito da noite.

I have dinner at eight in the evening.

chegar

to arrive

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb with spelling change in 1st person singular (gu before e).

Exemplo

O autocarro chega às dez.

The bus arrives at ten.

sair

to leave, to go out

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -ir verb (stem vowel change in present eu).

Exemplo

Eu saio de casa às oito.

I leave home at eight.

voltar

to return, to go back

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb.

Exemplo

Eu volto para casa às seis.

I return home at six.

dormir

to sleep

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -ir verb (stem vowel alternation o -> u in present tu/ele).

Exemplo

Eu durmo oito horas por noite.

I sleep eight hours per night.

jantar

to have supper / dinner

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb.

Exemplo

Nós jantamos às nove.

We have dinner at nine.

passar

to spend (time), to pass

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb.

Exemplo

Eu passo o dia em casa.

I spend the day at home.

demorar

to take (time), to be long

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb.

Exemplo

Eu demoro vinte minutos.

I take twenty minutes.

esperar

to wait

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb.

Exemplo

Eu espero pelo autocarro.

I wait for the bus.

ficar

to stay, to remain

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb with spelling change in 1st person singular (qu before e).

Exemplo

Eu fico em casa à noite.

I stay at home at night.

almoçar

to eat lunch

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb with ç spelling change.

Exemplo

Eu almoço ao meio-dia.

I eat lunch at noon.

deitar-se

to go to bed, to lie down

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar reflexive verb with European enclitic pronouns.

Exemplo

Eu deito-me cedo.

I go to bed early.

I have classes at nine.
