falar

to speak / to talk

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Costumo falar com os meus colegas de trabalho todos os dias.

I usually talk to my work colleagues every day.

dizer

to say / to tell

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -er verb; auxiliary verb 'ter'; stem changes in present 'digo'.

Exemplo

O diretor disse-me que o projeto foi aprovado ontem.

The director told me that the project was approved yesterday.

perguntar

to ask / to inquire

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Decidi perguntar ao professor se podia mudar de horário.

I decided to ask the teacher if I could change my schedule.

perguntar

to ask / to inquire

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Decidi perguntar ao professor se podia mudar de horário.

I decided to ask the teacher if I could change my schedule.

responder

to answer / to reply

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb; auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Demorei muito tempo a responder ao email do cliente.

It took me a long time to reply to the client's email.

explicar

to explain

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb with spelling change in preterite (c -> qu); auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

O professor explicou a matéria de forma muito clara.

The teacher explained the subject matter very clearly.

conversar

to converse / to chat

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Adoro conversar com os meus amigos enquanto tomamos café.

I love chatting with my friends while we drink coffee.

contar

to tell (a story) / to count

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

O avô gosta de contar histórias antigas aos netos.

Grandpa likes to tell old stories to his grandchildren.

ouvir

to listen / to hear

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -ir verb; stem changes in present 'ouço'; auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Gosto de ouvir música portuguesa enquanto viajo de autocarro.

I like listening to Portuguese music while travelling by bus.

escutar

to listen attentively

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Para resolver o problema, primeiro deves escutar os outros.

To solve the problem, you must first listen to others.

telefonar

to phone / to call

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; takes preposition 'a' before indirect object; auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Vou telefonar à minha mãe para saber se está tudo bem.

I am going to call my mother to find out if everything is okay.

chamar

to call / to name

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Chamo-me Ana e estou a aprender português no instituto.

My name is Ana and I am learning Portuguese at the institute.

gritar

to shout / to scream

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Não precisas de gritar comigo, consigo ouvir-te perfeitamente.

You don't need to shout at me, I can hear you perfectly.

sussurrar

to whisper

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Ele sussurrou-me um segredo ao ouvido durante a aula.

He whispered a secret in my ear during class.

anunciar

to announce

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

A empresa vai anunciar os novos resultados financeiros amanhã.

The company is going to announce the new financial results tomorrow.

prometer

to promise

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb; auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Prometo que te ligo assim que chegar a casa.

I promise that I will call you as soon as I get home.

discutir

to discuss / to argue

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb; auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Vamos discutir este assunto na próxima reunião de equipa.

We are going to discuss this matter in the next team meeting.

elogiar

to praise / to compliment

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

O chefe elogiou o meu trabalho no relatório final.

The boss praised my work on the final report.

insultar

to insult

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Nunca deves insultar ninguém, mesmo quando estás furioso.

You must never insult anyone, even when you are furious.

agradecer

to thank / to express gratitude

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -er verb with ç before o/a (agradeço); auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Gostaria de agradecer toda a ajuda que me deram.

I would like to thank you for all the help you gave me.
