proteger

to protect

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Devemos proteger a floresta local.

We must protect the local forest.

poluir

to pollute

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb with stem vowel alternation in the present singular. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

As fábricas poluem o ar da cidade.

Factories pollute the city air.

reciclar

to recycle

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Eu reciclo o papel e o plástico.

I recycle paper and plastic.

plantar

to plant

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Nós vamos plantar árvores no parque.

We are going to plant trees in the park.

poupar

to save (resources/energy)

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Devemos poupar água em casa.

We must save water at home.

chover

to rain

📌 Nota gramatical

Impersonal regular -er verb (used mostly in 3rd person singular). Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Vai chover muito esta tarde.

It is going to rain a lot this afternoon.

ventar

to be windy / to blow

📌 Nota gramatical

Impersonal regular -ar verb (used mostly in 3rd person singular). Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Está a ventar muito na costa.

It is very windy on the coast.

queimar

to burn

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Os fogos queimam muitas florestas.

Fires burn many forests.

destruir

to destroy

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -ir verb (changes i to oi in 1st person singular present). Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

A poluição destrói a natureza.

Pollution destroys nature.

nascer

to be born / to rise (sun/plants)

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -er verb (-cer becomes -ço in 1st person singular). Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

As plantas novas começam a nascer na primavera.

New plants begin to grow in the spring.

morrer

to die

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -er verb (stem vowel changes o to u in some forms). Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Muitos peixes morrem devido à poluição.

Many fish die due to pollution.

respirar

to breathe

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Precisamos de respirar ar puro na montanha.

We need to breathe pure air in the mountain.

observar

to observe / to watch

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Gosto de observar os pássaros na árvore.

I like to watch the birds in the tree.

explorar

to explore

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Vamos explorar a trilha na floresta.

Let's explore the trail in the forest.

limpar

to clean

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Hoje vamos limpar a praia juntos.

Today we are going to clean the beach together.

reduzir

to reduce

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb with -z- in present 1st person. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Devemos reduzir o uso de plástico.

We must reduce the use of plastic.

reutilizar

to reuse

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Eu gosto de reutilizar as garrafas de vidro.

I like to reuse glass bottles.

brilhar

to shine

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

O sol brilha intensamente no verão.

The sun shines intensely in the summer.

subir

to climb / to go up

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb with stem vowel alternation (u/o). Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Nós gostamos de subir a montanha.

We like to climb the mountain.

coar

to filter / to strain

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

As árvores ajudam a coar a luz do sol.

Trees help to filter the sunlight.
