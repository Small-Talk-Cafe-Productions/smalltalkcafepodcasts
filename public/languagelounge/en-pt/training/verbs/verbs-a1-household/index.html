limpar

to clean

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb.

Exemplo

Ao sábado de manhã, limpamos a casa toda.

On Saturday mornings, we clean the whole house.

arrumar

to tidy up / to put away

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; essential everyday PT-PT verb for tidying a room or parking a car.

Exemplo

Arruma o teu quarto antes de ires brincar!

Tidy up your bedroom before going to play!

cozinhar

to cook

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb.

Exemplo

O meu pai cozinha muito bem pratos tradicionais.

My father cooks traditional dishes very well.

lavar

to wash

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb.

Exemplo

Quem é que lava a loiça depois do almoço?

Who washes the dishes after lunch?

varrer

to sweep

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb.

Exemplo

Ela varre o chão da cozinha com uma vassoura.

She sweeps the kitchen floor with a broom.

aspirar

to vacuum / to hoover

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb.

Exemplo

Eu aspiro o tapete da sala duas vezes por semana.

I vacuum the living room rug twice a week.

engomar

to iron

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; in PT-PT both 'engomar' and 'passar a ferro' are standard.

Exemplo

Preciso de engomar esta camisa branca para amanhã.

I need to iron this white shirt for tomorrow.

arranjar

to fix / to repair / to arrange

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; very common in PT-PT for repairing household items ('arranjar a torneira').

Exemplo

O canalizador veio cá a casa arranjar o esquentador.

The plumber came to our house to fix the water heater.

abrir

to open

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular conjugation in indicative tenses, but irregular past participle 'aberto'.

Exemplo

Abro a janela logo de manhã para arejar o quarto.

I open the window first thing in the morning to air the bedroom.

fechar

to close / to shut

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; also used with 'à chave' ('fechar à chave' = to lock).

Exemplo

Não te esqueças de fechar a porta da rua à chave.

Don't forget to lock the front door.

ligar

to turn on / to connect

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; spelling change 'liguei' in preterite. Used for appliances and lights.

Exemplo

Está frio na sala; vou ligar o aquecedor.

It is cold in the living room; I'm going to turn on the heater.

desligar

to turn off / to switch off

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; spelling change 'desliguei'.

Exemplo

Desliga a televisão antes de ires para a cama.

Turn off the television before going to bed.

aquecer

to heat up / to warm

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb; spelling change 'aqueço' in 1st person singular present.

Exemplo

Vou aquecer a sopa no fogão para o jantar.

I am going to heat up the soup on the stove for dinner.

arrefecer

to cool down / to get cold

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb; 'arrefeço'. Typical PT-PT verb (vs BR 'esfriar').

Exemplo

Come o arroz depressa antes que arrefeça!

Eat the rice quickly before it gets cold!

guardar

to put away / to keep / to store

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb.

Exemplo

Guarda o leite e a manteiga no frigorífico, se faz favor.

Put the milk and butter away in the fridge, please.

decorar

to decorate

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb.

Exemplo

Eles decoraram a sala nova com quadros e plantas.

They decorated the new living room with paintings and plants.

Configurar treino

Configure a sua sessão de treino.
