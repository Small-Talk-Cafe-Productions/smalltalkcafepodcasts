carregar

to charge (a battery) / to upload

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, regular conjugation with auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Costumo carregar o telemóvel todas as noites antes de dormir.

I usually charge my mobile phone every night before sleeping.

descarregar

to download / to discharge

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, regular conjugation with auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Consegui descarregar uma aplicação fantástica para aprender línguas.

I managed to download a fantastic application to learn languages.

guardar

to save (files/data)

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, regular conjugation with auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Deves guardar sempre o teu documento antes de fechar o programa.

You must always save your document before closing the program.

apagar

to delete / to erase

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, spelling change in preterite (gu > gué) to maintain sound.

Exemplo

Sem querer, acabei de apagar um ficheiro muito importante.

Unintentionally, I just deleted a very important file.

clicar

to click

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, spelling change in preterite (c > qu) to maintain sound.

Exemplo

Clica naquele link azul para abrir a página web.

Click on that blue link to open the web page.

ligar

to turn on / to connect / to call

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, regular conjugation with auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Não consigo ligar a televisão porque falta eletricidade.

I can't turn on the television because there is no electricity.

desligar

to turn off / to disconnect

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, regular conjugation with auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Lembra-te de desligar o telemóvel antes de entrar no cinema.

Remember to turn off your mobile phone before entering the cinema.

enviar

to send

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, regular conjugation with auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Enviei o relatório final por correio eletrónico esta manhã.

I sent the final report by email this morning.

partilhar

to share

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, regular conjugation with auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Gosto de partilhar notícias interessantes nas minhas redes sociais.

I like to share interesting news on my social networks.

funcionar

to work / to function

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, regular conjugation with auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

A minha impressora nova não quer funcionar hoje.

My new printer doesn't want to work today.

pesquisar

to search / to research

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, regular conjugation with auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Vou pesquisar na internet sobre a história dos computadores.

I'm going to search on the internet about the history of computers.

imprimir

to print

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir verb group, regular conjugation with auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Preciso de imprimir o meu bilhete de comboio antes de viajar.

I need to print my train ticket before traveling.

conectar

to connect

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, regular conjugation with auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

O telemóvel não consegue conectar-se à rede Wi-Fi da biblioteca.

The mobile phone cannot connect to the library's Wi-Fi network.

atualizar

to update

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, regular conjugation with auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

É importante atualizar o sistema operativo regularmente por segurança.

It is important to update the operating system regularly for security.

bloquear

to block / to lock

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, regular conjugation with auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Esqueci-me do código PIN e acabei por bloquear o cartão SIM.

I forgot the PIN code and ended up blocking the SIM card.

instalar

to install

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, regular conjugation with auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Ajudei o meu avô a instalar o WhatsApp no telemóvel novo.

I helped my grandfather to install WhatsApp on his new mobile phone.

navegar

to surf / to navigate (the web)

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, regular conjugation with auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Passo muito tempo a navegar na internet à procura de inspiração.

I spend a lot of time surfing the internet looking for inspiration.

to scan

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb group, regular conjugation with auxiliary verb 'ter' (also commonly 'digitalizar' in Portugal).

Exemplo

Preciso de escanear este contrato para o enviar ao meu advogado.

I need to scan this contract to send it to my lawyer.

-ar verb group, regular conjugation with auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Quero gravar um podcast sobre tecnologia e inovação digital.

I want to record a podcast about technology and digital innovation.
