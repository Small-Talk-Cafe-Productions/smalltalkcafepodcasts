planear

to plan

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Nós vamos planear as férias de verão.

We are going to plan the summer holidays.

organizar

to organize

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Ela organiza os documentos no escritório.

She organizes the documents in the office.

marcar

to book / to schedule

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Eu preciso de marcar uma consulta médica.

I need to schedule a doctor's appointment.

cancelar

to cancel

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Temos de cancelar a viagem devido à chuva.

We have to cancel the trip due to the rain.

mudar

to change / to move

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Nós vamos mudar de casa no próximo mês.

We are going to move house next month.

preparar

to prepare

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Elas preparam a apresentação para a conferência.

They are preparing the presentation for the conference.

arrumar

to tidy up / to arrange

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Tu deves arrumar o teu quarto hoje.

You must tidy up your bedroom today.

limpar

to clean

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Nós limpamos a casa ao fim de semana.

We clean the house at the weekend.

decidir

to decide

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir conjugation verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Ainda não decidi o destino das férias.

I haven't decided the holiday destination yet.

escolher

to choose

📌 Nota gramatical

-er conjugation verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Podes escolher o filme para hoje.

You can choose the film for today.

começar

to begin / to start

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation verb with spelling change in 1st person singular (ç -> c). Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

A reunião vai começar às dez horas.

The meeting is going to start at ten o'clock.

terminar

to finish / to end

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

O projeto termina na próxima sexta-feira.

The project finishes next Friday.

lembrar

to remember / to remind

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation verb. Often used reflexively as 'lembrar-se'. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Tens de lembrar o João da reunião.

You have to remind João about the meeting.

esquecer

to forget

📌 Nota gramatical

-er conjugation verb. Often used reflexively as 'esquecer-se'. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Não te esqueças dos bilhetes.

Don't forget the tickets.

confirmar

to confirm

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Preciso de confirmar a minha reserva.

I need to confirm my reservation.

avisar

to warn / to notify / to let know

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Vou avisar os colegas sobre a mudança.

I am going to notify the colleagues about the change.

convidar

to invite

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Quero convidar os meus amigos para jantar.

I want to invite my friends for dinner.

participar

to participate / to take part

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation verb. Takes preposition 'em'. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Nós vamos participar no workshop de amanhã.

We are going to take part in tomorrow's workshop.

adiar

to postpone / to delay

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar conjugation verb. Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Temos de adiar a reunião para a próxima semana.

We have to postpone the meeting until next week.

prever

to foresee / to predict

📌 Nota gramatical

-er conjugation verb, irregular (compound of 'ver'). Auxiliary verb: ter.

Exemplo

Conseguimos prever os problemas com antecedência.

We managed to foresee the problems in advance.
