Verbs for Hypotheses & Wishes

desejar

to wish / to desire

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar) regular verb, taking direct objects or subordinate clauses.

Exemplo

Eu desejo que tu tenhas muito sucesso no teu novo trabalho.

I wish that you have a lot of success in your new job.

imaginar

to imagine

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar) regular verb, frequently used in hypothetical scenarios.

Exemplo

Consegues imaginar como seria viver numa ilha deserta?

Can you imagine what it would be like to live on a deserted island?

supor

to suppose / to guess

📌 Nota gramatical

Second conjugation (-or) irregular verb, conjugated similarly to 'por'.

Exemplo

Suponho que o autocarro vá atrasar devido ao trânsito intenso.

I suppose that the bus is going to be late due to the heavy traffic.

esperar

to hope / to wait

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar) regular verb used to express hopes and expectations.

Exemplo

Espero que o tempo melhore no próximo fim de semana.

I hope that the weather improves next weekend.

duvidar

to doubt

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar) regular verb, often followed by the subjunctive for doubt.

Exemplo

Duvido que ele consiga acabar o relatório a tempo.

I doubt that he manages to finish the report on time.

acreditar

to believe

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar) regular verb requiring preposition 'em'.

Exemplo

Acredito que tudo vai correr bem no exame de amanhã.

I believe that everything will go well in tomorrow's exam.

pretender

to intend / to aim

📌 Nota gramatical

Second conjugation (-er) regular verb used for expressing plans and wishes.

Exemplo

Pretendo comprar um telemóvel novo assim que receber o subsídio.

I intend to buy a new mobile phone as soon as I receive the allowance.

garantir

to guarantee

📌 Nota gramatical

Third conjugation (-ir) stem-changing verb in the present tense (eu garanto).

Exemplo

Garantimos que o trabalho estará pronto antes do jantar.

We guarantee that the work will be ready before dinner.

arriscar

to risk

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar) regular verb with spelling change (-car to -quei in preterite).

Exemplo

Não devemos arriscar investir dinheiro sem consultar um especialista.

We must not risk investing money without consulting an expert.

suspeitar

to suspect

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar) regular verb, often used in hypotheses.

Exemplo

Suspeito que alguém tenha mexido nos meus documentos.

I suspect that someone has touched my documents.

imaginar-se

to imagine oneself

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation reflexive verb with European enclitic pronoun placement.

Exemplo

Eu imagino-me a viver no campo quando for reformado.

I imagine myself living in the countryside when I retire.

almejar

to yearn for / to aspire to

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar) regular verb used for strong wishes and career/life goals.

Exemplo

Todos nós almejamos ter uma vida tranquila e sem preocupações.

We all yearn to have a peaceful life without worries.

conjeturar

to conjecture / to guess

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar) regular verb used in formal or intellectual hypotheses.

Exemplo

Os cientistas começam a conjeturar sobre a origem deste fenómeno.

Scientists are beginning to conjecture about the origin of this phenomenon.

julgar

to judge / to reckon

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar) regular verb used to express opinions and assumptions.

Exemplo

Julgo que esta solução é a melhor para o nosso problema.

I reckon that this solution is the best for our problem.

estipular

to stipulate

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar) regular verb used in conditions and contracts.

Exemplo

O contrato estipula que o pagamento deve ser feito até ao dia dez.

The contract stipulates that payment must be made by the tenth.

prever

to foresee / to predict

📌 Nota gramatical

Second conjugation (-er) irregular verb, compound of 'ver'.

Exemplo

Prevejo que teremos muitas dificuldades se chover durante o evento.

I foresee that we will have many difficulties if it rains during the event.

suspirar

to sigh

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar) regular verb expressing longing or relief.

Exemplo

Ela suspira sempre que pensa nas férias de verão.

She sighs whenever she thinks about the summer holidays.

ansiar

to yearn / to long for

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar) regular verb, often used with preposition 'por'.

Exemplo

Ansio por ver o resultado final do nosso projeto de arquitetura.

I yearn to see the final result of our architecture project.

especular

to speculate

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar) regular verb used when forming hypotheses without firm evidence.

Exemplo

Os jornais continuam a especular sobre os motivos da demissão.

The newspapers continue to speculate about the reasons for the resignation.

acalentar

to cherish / to harbor (a dream/hope)

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar) regular verb used figuratively for nurturing hopes or wishes.

Exemplo

Acalento o sonho de abrir o meu próprio negócio um dia.

I cherish the dream of opening my own business one day.
