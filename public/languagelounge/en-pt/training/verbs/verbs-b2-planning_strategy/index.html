planear

to plan

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

Precisamos de planear todas as etapas do projeto antes de avançarmos.

We need to plan all the stages of the project before we move forward.

organizar

to organize

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb (spelling change z->c before e: organizei); auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

É fundamental organizar a nossa agenda semanal para cumprirmos os prazos.

It is fundamental to organize our weekly schedule to meet the deadlines.

to define

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb; auxiliary verb 'ter'.

Exemplo

We need to define clear objectives before starting this campaign.
