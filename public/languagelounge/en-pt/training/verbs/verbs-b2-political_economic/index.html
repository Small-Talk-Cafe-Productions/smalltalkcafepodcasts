desenvolver

to develop

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb, auxiliary verb 'ter', used frequently in economic and industrial contexts.

Exemplo

O governo pretende desenvolver novas estratégias para combater o desemprego jovem.

The government intends to develop new strategies to combat youth unemployment.

financiar

to finance

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter', essential for banking, state budgets, and campaigns.

Exemplo

O banco recusou-se a financiar o novo projeto de infraestruturas públicas.

The bank refused to finance the new public infrastructure project.

negociar

to negotiate

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter', used in diplomatic and labor union contexts.

Exemplo

Os sindicatos continuam a negociar melhores condições salariais com a patronal.

The trade unions continue to negotiate better salary conditions with management.

aprovar

to approve / to pass (legislation)

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter', used in parliamentary and corporate votes.

Exemplo

O parlamento acabou por aprovar o orçamento de estado após longos debates.

Parliament ended up approving the state budget after long debates.

regulamentar

to regulate

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter', standard regulatory terminology.

Exemplo

A União Europeia quer regulamentar o uso da inteligência artificial nas empresas.

The European Union wants to regulate the use of artificial intelligence in companies.

investir

to invest

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb (stem vowel alternates in present singular), auxiliary verb 'ter', governs preposition 'em'.

Exemplo

O Estado deve investir mais na educação pública e na saúde.

The State must invest more in public education and healthcare.

promover

to promote

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb, auxiliary verb 'ter', frequent in public campaigns and economic growth agendas.

Exemplo

O governo criou um programa para promover a igualdade de género no trabalho.

The government created a program to promote gender equality at work.

reeleger

to re-elect

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb, auxiliary verb 'ter', used in electoral and political contexts.

Exemplo

Os cidadãos decidiram reeleger o presidente devido às suas reformas económicas.

The citizens decided to re-elect the president due to his economic reforms.

decretar

to decree / to order officially

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter', used for executive branches and state emergencies.

Exemplo

O primeiro-ministro decidiu decretar o estado de emergência devido à crise sanitária.

The prime minister decided to decree a state of emergency due to the health crisis.

ratificar

to ratify

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb (-car changes to -quei in preterite), auxiliary verb 'ter', used in treaties.

Exemplo

Todos os estados membros devem ratificar o tratado internacional até ao final do ano.

All member states must ratify the international treaty by the end of the year.

enfraquecer

to weaken

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb, auxiliary verb 'ter', common in political and economic commentary.

Exemplo

A alta inflação tende a enfraquecer o poder de compra das famílias.

High inflation tends to weaken the purchasing power of families.

privatizar

to privatize

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter', standard economic reform term.

Exemplo

O governo planeia privatizar a companhia aérea nacional para reduzir a dívida pública.

The government plans to privatize the national airline to reduce public debt.

subsidiar

to subsidize

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter', common in debates on agriculture and energy.

Exemplo

A União Europeia continua a subsidiar a agricultura para proteger os produtores locais.

The European Union continues to subsidize agriculture to protect local producers.

recolher

to collect / to gather (taxes, data)

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb, auxiliary verb 'ter', used for taxes, revenue, and statistical data.

Exemplo

A autoridade tributária conseguiu recolher mais impostos este ano fiscal.

The tax authority managed to collect more taxes this fiscal year.

estabilizar

to stabilize

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter', core economic policy vocabulary.

Exemplo

O Banco Central tomou medidas urgentes para estabilizar a moeda nacional.

The Central Bank took urgent measures to stabilize the national currency.

sancionar

to sanction / to penalize

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter', used in international relations and legal enforcement.

Exemplo

A comunidade internacional decidiu sancionar o país devido à violação dos direitos humanos.

The international community decided to sanction the country due to human rights violations.

reestruturar

to restructure

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter', common in corporate finance and public administration.

Exemplo

A multinacional teve de reestruturar a sua dívida para evitar a falência.

The multinational had to restructure its debt to avoid bankruptcy.

candidatar-se

to run for office / to apply

📌 Nota gramatical

Reflexive -ar verb, auxiliary verb 'ter', European enclitic pronoun placement in conjugation.

Exemplo

Vários políticos importantes anunciaram que vão candidatar-se às próximas eleições.

Several prominent politicians announced they will run for office in the next elections.

divergir

to diverge / to differ

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb (with stem change in present singular), auxiliary verb 'ter', used in policy disputes.

Exemplo

Os economistas continuam a divergir sobre o impacto real desta reforma fiscal.

Economists continue to diverge on the real impact of this tax reform.

combater

to combat / to fight against

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb, auxiliary verb 'ter', ubiquitous in political manifestos and economic policies.

Exemplo

O novo plano estratégico visa combater a corrupção nas instituições públicas.

The new strategic plan aims to combat corruption in public institutions.
