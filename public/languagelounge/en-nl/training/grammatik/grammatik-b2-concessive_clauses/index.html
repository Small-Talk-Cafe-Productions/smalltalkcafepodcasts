Hoewel (although / though)

Expressing a contrast with a subordinate clause using 'hoewel'.

📌 Grammaticanotitie

'Hoewel' is a subordinating conjunction meaning 'although' or 'though'. Because it introduces a subordinate clause (bijzin), the conjugated verb must be sent directly to the very end of that clause. ⚠️ Placing the verb directly after 'hoewel' as in main clauses (Hoewel het regende hard...), forgetting that subordinate clauses require the verb-final rule.

Voorbeeld

Hoewel het hard regende, gingen we toch een stuk wandelen in het bos.

Although it was raining hard, we still went for a walk in the forest.

Ondanks (despite / in spite of)

Expressing concession using the preposition 'ondanks'.

📌 Grammaticanotitie

'Ondanks' is a preposition, not a conjunction. Therefore, it is followed directly by a noun phrase (noun + modifiers) rather than a full clause with a conjugated verb. ⚠️ Treating 'ondanks' like a conjunction and putting a full sentence behind it (Ondanks dat het regende... which is grammatically different, see card 003).

Voorbeeld

Ondanks de zware regenval besloten we om buiten te lunchen.

Despite the heavy rainfall, we decided to have lunch outside.

Ondanks dat (despite the fact that)

Using 'ondanks dat' to connect a prepositional meaning with a full clause.

📌 Grammaticanotitie

While purists sometimes prefer 'ondanks het feit dat', spoken and written Dutch frequently use 'ondanks dat' to mean 'despite the fact that'. It functions as a conjunction and requires the verb at the end. ⚠️ Confusing 'ondanks dat' with 'hoewel'. Both express concession, but 'ondanks dat' specifically emphasizes 'despite the fact that'.

Voorbeeld

Ondanks dat de trein vertraging had, arriveerde ik op tijd op mijn werk.

Despite the fact that the train was delayed, I arrived at work on time.

Al / Al is het... (even if / although)

Using 'al' to introduce a concessive clause or condition.

📌 Grammaticanotitie

'Al' can be used similarly to 'hoewel' (although). Furthermore, 'al' is often used in the pattern 'al is het...' (even if it is...) to emphasize that a condition does not alter the outcome. ⚠️ Mistaking 'al' for 'already' (al) in these specific clause structures where it functions as a concessive conjunction meaning 'although' or 'even if'.

Voorbeeld

Al begrijp ik je standpunt, ik ben het er nog steeds niet mee eens.

Although I understand your point of view, I still do not agree with it.

Alhoewel (although - formal alternative)

Using the slightly more formal synonym 'alhoewel'.

📌 Grammaticanotitie

'Alhoewel' is synonymous with 'hoewel' and follows the exact same grammatical rules (subordinate clause, verb to the end). It tends to appear slightly more frequently in formal written Dutch. ⚠️ Thinking 'alhoewel' behaves differently from 'hoewel' regarding word order; both require the verb at the end.

Voorbeeld

Alhoewel de economische situatie verbetert, blijven consumenten erg voorzichtig.

Although the economic situation is improving, consumers remain very cautious.

Zelfs als / Ook al (even if)

Expressing hypothetical concession with 'zelfs als' and 'ook al'.

📌 Grammaticanotitie

To express 'even if' (hypothetical or real condition that does not change the result), Dutch uses 'zelfs als' (even if) or 'ook al' (even though / even if). They take standard subordinate clause word order (verb at the end). ⚠️ Using English literal translations like zelfs wanneer where 'zelfs als' or 'ook al' are much more natural idiomatic choices in Dutch.

Voorbeeld

Zelfs als het morgen sneeuwt, gaat de buitentraining gewoon door.

Even if it snows tomorrow, the outdoor training session will still take place.

Niettemin / Desondanks (nevertheless / nonetheless)

Using concessive adverbial connectors between sentences.

📌 Grammaticanotitie

'Niettemin' and 'desondanks' are adverbial connectors (bijwoordelijke aansluitingen), not subordinating conjunctions. When placed in the first position of a main clause, inversion occurs (verb before subject). ⚠️ Failing to apply subject-verb inversion after 'niettemin' or 'desondanks' when starting a new sentence (Desondanks de directie keurde... instead of ...desondanks keurde de directie...).

Voorbeeld

De plannen waren erg duur; desondanks keurde de directie ze goed.

The plans were very expensive; nonetheless, the board approved them.

Hoe... hoe / Wat... ook (however much / no matter what)

Expressing emphatic concession with 'hoe... ook' structures.

📌 Grammaticanotitie

The structure 'hoe [adjective/adverb] [subject] ook [verb]' means 'however much / no matter how'. It is a powerful B2-level concessive construction expressing that a degree or effort makes no difference. ⚠️ Placing 'ook' in the wrong position or forgetting it entirely in 'hoe... ook' clauses (Hoe hard hij probeerde...).

Voorbeeld

Hoe hard hij ook probeerde, hij kon de fout in de code niet vinden.

No matter how hard he tried, he couldn't find the error in the code.

Toegegeven dat / Weliswaar... maar (admittedly / admittedly... but)

Making a concession before introducing a counter-argument.

📌 Grammaticanotitie

'Weliswaar' (admittedly / true) is used in the first clause to grant a point, usually paired with 'maar' (but) in the second clause to introduce the main contrast or restriction. ⚠️ Using 'weliswaar' without balancing it with a contrasting clause containing 'maar' or 'toch'.

Voorbeeld

Het project was weliswaar erg duur, maar het leverde direct veel op.

Admittedly, the project was very expensive, but it yielded a lot immediately.

In plaats van dat / In tegenstelling tot (instead of / in contrast to)

Concessive contrasts involving alternatives and differences.

📌 Grammaticanotitie

'In plaats van dat' introduces a clause representing a contrastive alternative (verb at the end). 'In tegenstelling tot' is a prepositional phrase meaning 'in contrast to', followed by a noun phrase. ⚠️ Mixing up 'in plaats van te' (with an infinitive) and 'in plaats van dat' (with a full subordinate clause).

Voorbeeld

In tegenstelling tot haar collega’s werkt zij het liefst in complete stilte.

In contrast to her colleagues, she prefers to work in complete silence.

Niettegenstaande (notwithstanding / despite - formal)

Using the highly formal preposition 'niettegenstaande'.

📌 Grammaticanotitie

'Niettegenstaande' is a formal preposition meaning 'notwithstanding' or 'despite'. It governs a noun phrase (usually in the genitive/accusative form without an article or with standard definite articles) and belongs to professional or academic Dutch. ⚠️ Using 'niettegenstaande' in informal, spoken contexts where 'ondanks' is far more natural and appropriate.

Voorbeeld

Niettegenstaande de economische crisis bleef het bedrijf winst maken.

Notwithstanding the economic crisis, the company continued to make a profit.

Integrated Concessive Clause Practice (B2 nuance)

Choosing the correct concessive connector based on syntax and style.

📌 Grammaticanotitie

At B2 level, mastery requires choosing between conjunctions ('hoewel'), prepositions ('ondanks'), and adverbs ('desondanks') based on whether you need a full clause, a noun phrase, or a sentence connector. ⚠️ Applying conjunction syntax (hoewel) to prepositional words (ondanks) or vice-versa, disrupting the sentence structure.

Voorbeeld

Ondanks dat het budget beperkt was, slaagden we erin het doel te bereiken.

Despite the fact that the budget was limited, we managed to achieve the goal.

Contrastregel en veelvoorkomende fouten: Toegevende bijzin (hoewel, ondanks, al)

Contrast rule and common errors: Concessive Clauses (hoewel, ondanks, al)

📌 Grammaticanotitie

Key contrast in Concessive Clauses (hoewel, ondanks, al): Pay special attention to distinguishing core forms (Hoewel, Ondanks, Ondanks dat, Al / Al is het...) from their false friends or overlapping structures. Primary pitfall: Placing the verb directly after 'hoewel' as in main clauses (Hoewel het regende hard...), forgetting that subordinate clauses require the verb-final rule. ⚠️ Avoid the common error: Placing the verb directly after 'hoewel' as in main clauses (Hoewel het regende hard...), forgetting that subordinate clauses require the verb-final rule.

Voorbeeld

Hoewel het hard regende, gingen we toch een stuk wandelen in het bos.

Although it was raining hard, we still went for a walk in the forest.

Woordvolgorde en zinsstructuur: Toegevende bijzin (hoewel, ondanks, al)

Word order and sentence structure: Concessive Clauses (hoewel, ondanks, al)

📌 Grammaticanotitie

Word order and sentence structure rules for Concessive Clauses (hoewel, ondanks, al) (B2): In main clauses, subordinate clauses, and with negation or adverbial phrases, ensure Hoewel, Ondanks, Ondanks dat, Al / Al is het... occupy their strict grammatical position. ⚠️ Misplacing elements relative to the conjugated verb, negation particle, or clause boundary.

Voorbeeld

Ondanks de zware regenval besloten we om buiten te lunchen.

Despite the heavy rainfall, we decided to have lunch outside.

Formeel register vs spreektaal: Toegevende bijzin (hoewel, ondanks, al)

Formal register vs. colloquial spoken usage: Concessive Clauses (hoewel, ondanks, al)

📌 Grammaticanotitie

Register and usage nuance (B2): While everyday spoken communication frequently uses conversational shortcuts, formal written texts and CEFR B2 exams require precise adherence to Concessive Clauses (hoewel, ondanks, al). ⚠️ Using overly colloquial shortcuts in formal written contexts or stiff bookish phrasing where natural flow is required.

Voorbeeld

Ondanks dat de trein vertraging had, arriveerde ik op tijd op mijn werk.

Despite the fact that the train was delayed, I arrived at work on time.

Beslisregel en praktische samenvatting: Toegevende bijzin (hoewel, ondanks, al)

Decision framework and practical summary: Concessive Clauses (hoewel, ondanks, al)

📌 Grammaticanotitie

Decision framework for Concessive Clauses (hoewel, ondanks, al) (B2): Before producing a sentence, verify 1) grammatical agreement (gender, number, person), 2) tense/mood triggers, and 3) exact word order. ⚠️ Producing clauses without checking case, mood triggers, or subject-verb agreement.

Voorbeeld

Al begrijp ik je standpunt, ik ben het er nog steeds niet mee eens.

Although I understand your point of view, I still do not agree with it.
