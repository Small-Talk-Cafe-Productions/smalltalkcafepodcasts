De basisvolgorde (SVO)

The basic word order (Subject-Verb-Object)

📌 Grammaticanotitie

In a standard Dutch declarative sentence, the subject comes first, followed immediately by the conjugated verb (the 'persoonsvorm'). This is the most common way to build a simple sentence. ⚠️ Learners sometimes put the verb at the end like in German subordinate clauses, but in simple Dutch main clauses, the verb must follow the subject.

Voorbeeld

Ik drink een kop koffie.

I drink a cup of coffee.

De V2-regel

The Verb-Second (V2) Rule

📌 Grammaticanotitie

The 'Golden Rule' of Dutch main clauses is that the finite verb is always the second element. It doesn't matter if the first element is the subject, a time expression, or a place; the verb stays in position two. ⚠️ Thinking 'second position' means 'second word'. It means the second grammatical element (e.g., 'De oude man' is one element).

Voorbeeld

Jan woont in Amsterdam.

Jan lives in Amsterdam.

Inversie met tijd

Inversion with time expressions

📌 Grammaticanotitie

If you start a sentence with a time expression (like 'vandaag' or 'nu'), the subject and verb must swap places (inversion) so the verb remains in the second position. ⚠️ Saying 'Vandaag ik werk' (Today I work). In Dutch, the verb must come before the subject if the sentence starts with time.

Voorbeeld

Vandaag werk ik niet.

Today I am not working.

Inversie met plaats

Inversion with place expressions

📌 Grammaticanotitie

When a sentence begins with a location or a word like 'hier' (here) or 'daar' (there), inversion occurs. The verb stays in position two, and the subject moves to position three. ⚠️ Using English order: 'Hier uw sleutel is' or 'Hier de sleutel is'. The verb must be second.

Voorbeeld

Hier is uw sleutel.

Here is your key.

Inversie met bijwoorden

Inversion with adverbs

📌 Grammaticanotitie

Adverbs like 'misschien' (maybe), 'soms' (sometimes), or 'vaak' (often) trigger inversion when they start a sentence. The verb remains the second element. ⚠️ Saying 'Soms ik drink thee'. Remember: if it's not the subject in front, the subject must follow the verb.

Voorbeeld

Soms drink ik thee.

Sometimes I drink tea.

Ja/Nee-vragen (V1)

Yes/No Questions (Verb-First)

📌 Grammaticanotitie

To ask a question that can be answered with 'yes' or 'no', the verb moves to the very first position, followed by the subject. This is the main exception to the V2 rule. ⚠️ Using 'do' like in English ('Doe je komen uit...?'). Dutch does not use an auxiliary 'do' for questions; just flip the verb and subject.

Voorbeeld

Kom je uit Nederland?

Are you from the Netherlands?

Vragen met een vraagwoord

Questions with a question word

📌 Grammaticanotitie

When using question words like 'wie' (who), 'wat' (what), or 'waar' (where), the question word is position one and the verb is position two. The subject follows the verb. ⚠️ Putting the subject before the verb in a question, e.g., 'Waar de leraar woont?'

Voorbeeld

Waar woont de leraar?

Where does the teacher live?

De plaats van 'niet'

The position of 'niet' (not)

📌 Grammaticanotitie

In simple sentences, 'niet' usually comes after the verb and the subject. If there is a direct object, 'niet' often comes after it, unless the object is a specific noun with 'de' or 'het'. ⚠️ Putting 'niet' before the verb like 'Ik niet begrijp'. The verb must stay in position two.

Voorbeeld

Ik begrijp het niet.

I do not understand it.

Modale hulpwerkwoorden

Modal auxiliary verbs

📌 Grammaticanotitie

When using a modal verb (can, must, want, will), the modal verb takes the second position and is conjugated. The second verb (the infinitive) moves to the very end of the sentence. ⚠️ Putting both verbs together in the middle. The second verb must go to the end.

Voorbeeld

Ik wil een biertje drinken.

I want to drink a beer.

Scheidbare werkwoorden

Separable verbs

📌 Grammaticanotitie

Many Dutch verbs have a prefix (like 'op', 'af', 'uit'). In a main clause, the main part of the verb stays in position two, but the prefix moves to the very end of the sentence. ⚠️ Keeping the verb together (e.g., 'Ik opbel mijn moeder'). You must separate them in main clauses.

Voorbeeld

Ik bel mijn moeder op.

I am calling my mother.

Meewerkend voorwerp

Indirect Object order

📌 Grammaticanotitie

In a standard V2 sentence, the indirect object (the person receiving something) usually comes before the direct object (the thing being given). ⚠️ Swapping the objects incorrectly. Usually, it is: Subject - Verb - Person - Thing.

Voorbeeld

Ik geef de hond eten.

I give the dog food.

Object-inversie voor nadruk

Object inversion for emphasis

📌 Grammaticanotitie

Sometimes the object is placed first to emphasize it. Because of the V2 rule, the verb remains in position two, and the subject must move to position three. ⚠️ Saying 'Dat boek zij leest'. Even when the object is first, the verb must come before the subject.

Voorbeeld

Dat boek leest zij graag.

That book, she likes to read.

Contrast rule and common errors: Main-Clause Word Order (Verb in Second Position)

📌 Grammaticanotitie

Key contrast in Main-Clause Word Order (Verb in Second Position): Pay special attention to distinguishing core forms (De basisvolgorde, De V2-regel, Inversie met tijd, Inversie met plaats) from their false friends or overlapping structures. Primary pitfall: Learners sometimes put the verb at the end like in German subordinate clauses, but in simple Dutch main clauses, the verb must follow the subject. ⚠️ Avoid the common error: Learners sometimes put the verb at the end like in German subordinate clauses, but in simple Dutch main clauses, the verb must follow the subject.

Voorbeeld

Ik drink een kop koffie.

I drink a cup of coffee.

Woordvolgorde en zinsstructuur: Woordvolgorde in de hoofdzin (V2-regel)

Word order and sentence structure: Main-Clause Word Order (Verb in Second Position)

📌 Grammaticanotitie

Word order and sentence structure rules for Main-Clause Word Order (Verb in Second Position) (A1): In main clauses, subordinate clauses, and with negation or adverbial phrases, ensure De basisvolgorde, De V2-regel, Inversie met tijd, Inversie met plaats occupy their strict grammatical position. ⚠️ Misplacing elements relative to the conjugated verb, negation particle, or clause boundary.

Voorbeeld

Jan woont in Amsterdam.

Jan lives in Amsterdam.

Formeel register vs spreektaal: Woordvolgorde in de hoofdzin (V2-regel)

Formal register vs. colloquial spoken usage: Main-Clause Word Order (Verb in Second Position)

📌 Grammaticanotitie

Register and usage nuance (A1): While everyday spoken communication frequently uses conversational shortcuts, formal written texts and CEFR A1 exams require precise adherence to Main-Clause Word Order (Verb in Second Position). ⚠️ Using overly colloquial shortcuts in formal written contexts or stiff bookish phrasing where natural flow is required.

Voorbeeld

Vandaag werk ik niet.

Today I am not working.

Beslisregel en praktische samenvatting: Woordvolgorde in de hoofdzin (V2-regel)

Decision framework and practical summary: Main-Clause Word Order (Verb in Second Position)

📌 Grammaticanotitie

Decision framework for Main-Clause Word Order (Verb in Second Position) (A1): Before producing a sentence, verify 1) grammatical agreement (gender, number, person), 2) tense/mood triggers, and 3) exact word order. ⚠️ Producing clauses without checking case, mood triggers, or subject-verb agreement.

Voorbeeld

Hier is uw sleutel.
