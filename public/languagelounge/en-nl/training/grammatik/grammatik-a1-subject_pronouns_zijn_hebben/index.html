Persoonlijke voornaamwoorden (enkelvoud)

Subject Pronouns (Singular)

📌 Grammaticanotitie

In Dutch, singular subject pronouns are: 'ik' (I), 'jij/je' (you - informal), 'u' (you - formal), 'hij' (he), 'zij/ze' (she), and 'het' (it). 'Je' and 'ze' are unstressed versions of 'jij' and 'zij'. Use 'u' for strangers, elders, or in professional settings. ⚠️ Using 'jij' for everyone. In the Netherlands, 'u' is still important for showing respect to older people or authorities.

Voorbeeld

Ik ben een cursist.

I am a student.

Persoonlijke voornaamwoorden (meervoud)

Subject Pronouns (Plural)

📌 Grammaticanotitie

The plural subject pronouns are: 'wij/we' (we), 'jullie' (you all), and 'zij/ze' (they). 'We' and 'ze' are the unstressed, more common forms in speech. 'Jullie' is used for both formal and informal groups. ⚠️ Confusing 'jullie' with 'u'. 'Jullie' is always plural, while 'u' is singular or plural but always formal.

Voorbeeld

Wij zijn in de klas.

We are in the classroom.

Jij vs. Je

Emphatic vs. Unstressed 'You'

📌 Grammaticanotitie

Dutch has two forms for 'you' (singular/informal). 'Jij' is used for emphasis or contrast. 'Je' is the neutral, high-frequency form used in most everyday sentences. ⚠️ Overusing 'jij' in every sentence. It can sound overly emphatic or aggressive if used where 'je' is more natural.

Voorbeeld

Ben je moe?

Are you tired?

Zijn: De basis (enkelvoud)

To Be: The Basics (Singular)

📌 Grammaticanotitie

The verb 'zijn' (to be) is irregular. Singular forms: Ik ben (I am), Jij bent (You are), U bent (You are - formal), Hij/Zij/Het is (He/She/It is). ⚠️ Saying 'Ik is' or 'Jij is'. Remember that 'is' is only for the third person singular (hij/zij/het).

Voorbeeld

Ik ben Jan.

I am Jan.

Zijn: De basis (meervoud)

To Be: The Basics (Plural)

📌 Grammaticanotitie

For all plural subjects (wij, jullie, zij), the verb 'zijn' becomes 'zijn'. This makes plural conjugation very simple in Dutch. ⚠️ Trying to conjugate 'jullie' differently. All plural pronouns use the infinitive form 'zijn'.

Voorbeeld

Wij zijn klaar.

We are ready.

Hebben: De basis (enkelvoud)

To Have: The Basics (Singular)

📌 Grammaticanotitie

The verb 'hebben' (to have) is slightly irregular. Singular forms: Ik heb (I have), Jij hebt (You have), U heeft/hebt (You have - formal), Hij/Zij/Het heeft (He/She/It has). ⚠️ Using 'heb' for 'hij'. It must be 'hij heeft'.

Voorbeeld

Ik heb een vraag.

I have a question.

Hebben: De basis (meervoud)

To Have: The Basics (Plural)

📌 Grammaticanotitie

Like 'zijn', the plural forms of 'hebben' are identical for all plural pronouns: Wij hebben, Jullie hebben, Zij hebben. ⚠️ Forgetting the 'n' at the end of 'hebben' when speaking about a group.

Voorbeeld

Wij hebben honger.

We are hungry (lit: We have hunger).

Inversie met 'jij'

Inversion with 'jij'

📌 Grammaticanotitie

A unique rule in Dutch: when 'jij' or 'je' follows the verb (usually in a question), the '-t' ending of the verb is dropped. This applies to 'hebben' (Heb je?) but NOT to 'zijn' (Ben je?). ⚠️ Saying 'Hebt je?'. The correct form is 'Heb je?'.

Voorbeeld

Heb je een hond?

Do you have a dog?

Zij (enkelvoud) vs. Zij (meervoud)

She vs. They

📌 Grammaticanotitie

The word 'zij' can mean 'she' or 'they'. You distinguish them by the verb conjugation. 'Zij is' = She is; 'Zij zijn' = They are. ⚠️ Confusing the meaning because the pronoun looks the same. Always look at the verb ending!

Voorbeeld

Zij heeft een kat.

She has a cat.

Het als onderwerp

'Het' as Subject

📌 Grammaticanotitie

'Het' is used for neuter nouns, weather, and time. It is also used as a dummy subject like 'it' in English. ⚠️ Using 'hij' for objects that are 'het-woorden'. If the noun is 'het boek', you must use 'het' to refer to it.

Voorbeeld

Het is acht uur.

It is eight o'clock.

U: De beleefdheidsvorm

U: The Polite Form

📌 Grammaticanotitie

'U' is the formal 'you'. It always takes a singular verb form ending in -t (except for 'zijn'). Note: 'U bent' and 'U heeft' (or 'U hebt'). ⚠️ Using 'u' with the plural verb 'zijn'. It is 'u bent', never 'u zijn'.

Voorbeeld

Bent u mevrouw Bakker?

Are you Mrs. Bakker?

Leeftijd met 'zijn'

Age with 'To Be'

📌 Grammaticanotitie

In Dutch, you use the verb 'zijn' (to be) to express age, just like in English. You do NOT use 'hebben' (to have). ⚠️ Translating from languages like French or Spanish and saying 'Ik heb 20 jaar'. In Dutch, it must be 'Ik ben 20'.

Voorbeeld

Ik ben dertig jaar.

I am thirty years old.
