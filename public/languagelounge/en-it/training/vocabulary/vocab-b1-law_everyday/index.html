Frasi di esempio

questura

police headquarters (immigration office)

📌 Nota grammaticale

In Italy, the 'questura' is specifically where you handle immigration and passport matters.

Esempio

documento

document / ID

📌 Nota grammaticale

Often used in the plural to mean 'identification papers'.

Esempio

modulo

form

📌 Nota grammaticale

Used for any official application form.

Esempio

compilare

to fill out / to complete (a form)

📌 Nota grammaticale

A regular -are verb. Used specifically for documents and forms.

Esempio

sportello

counter / window (office)

📌 Nota grammaticale

Uses 'lo' because it starts with 's' + consonant.

Esempio

appuntamento

appointment

📌 Nota grammaticale

The verb 'prendere un appuntamento' is the standard way to say 'to make an appointment'.

Esempio

permesso di soggiorno

residence permit

📌 Nota grammaticale

Essential term for foreigners living in Italy.

Esempio

rinnovare

to renew

📌 Nota grammaticale

Used for documents like passports, licenses, and permits.

Esempio

marca da bollo

revenue stamp

📌 Nota grammaticale

These physical stamps are required for many official Italian documents.

Esempio

avvocato

lawyer

📌 Nota grammaticale

The feminine form is 'l'avvocata' or 'l'avvocatessa', though 'l'avvocato' is often used for both.

Esempio

multa

fine / ticket

📌 Nota grammaticale

'Prendere una multa' is to get a ticket; 'fare una multa' is to issue one.

Esempio

contratto

contract

📌 Nota grammaticale

Common types include 'di lavoro' (work) or 'di affitto' (rental).

Esempio

firmare

to sign

📌 Nota grammaticale

A regular -are verb.

Esempio

firma

signature

📌 Nota grammaticale

Related to the verb 'firmare'.

Esempio

certificato

certificate

📌 Nota grammaticale

Commonly used for 'di residenza' (residence) or 'medico' (medical).

Esempio

scadere

to expire

📌 Nota grammaticale

An intransitive verb. The noun is 'la scadenza' (deadline/expiry).

Esempio

valido

valid

📌 Nota grammaticale

Adjective. Changes ending: valido, valida, validi, valide.

Esempio

denuncia

formal report / complaint

📌 Nota grammaticale

Often used with the verb 'fare' (to file/make).

Esempio

ufficio postale

post office

📌 Nota grammaticale

In Italy, the post office handles many banking and bureaucratic services.

Esempio

residenza

residence / legal address

📌 Nota grammaticale

Refers to your official legal address, different from 'domicilio' (where you temporarily live).

Esempio

richiedere

to request / to apply for

📌 Nota grammaticale

Irregular verb (past participle: richiesto).

Esempio

cittadinanza

citizenship

📌 Nota grammaticale

Ends in -anza, which is almost always feminine.

Esempio

legale

legal

📌 Nota grammaticale

Adjective. Used for both masculine and feminine singular.

Esempio

codice fiscale

tax code / fiscal code

📌 Nota grammaticale

An alphanumeric code essential for all bureaucratic life in Italy.

Esempio

autocertificazione

self-certification

📌 Nota grammaticale

A signed declaration that replaces official certificates in Italian law.
