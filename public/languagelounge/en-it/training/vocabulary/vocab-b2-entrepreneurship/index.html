startup

startup

📌 Nota grammaticale

Foreign invariable noun in Italian; the article and plural agreement indicate its gender and number.

Esempio

azienda

company / business

📌 Nota grammaticale

Feminine noun starting with a vowel, hence the apostrophe in the singular definite article.

Esempio

socio

business partner / associate

📌 Nota grammaticale

Standard masculine noun ending in -o; plural drops the -o and adds -i.

Esempio

capitale

capital / funds

📌 Nota grammaticale

Masculine noun in this financial context (la capitale means the capital city).

Esempio

investitore

investor

📌 Nota grammaticale

Masculine noun starting with a vowel, taking 'l' in the singular and 'gli' in the plural.

Esempio

finanziamento

funding / financing

📌 Nota grammaticale

Regular masculine noun derived from the verb finanziare.

Esempio

mercato

market

📌 Nota grammaticale

Common business noun; often used with prepositions like sul mercato (on the market).

Esempio

concorrenza

competition

📌 Nota grammaticale

Feminine noun; refers to competing companies or the state of competing.

Esempio

strategia

strategy

📌 Nota grammaticale

Feminine noun ending in -gia; plural drops the -i in spelling (strategie).

Esempio

obiettivo

goal / objective

📌 Nota grammaticale

Masculine noun starting with a vowel. Often used with verbs like raggiungere (to reach).

Esempio

fallimento

failure / bankruptcy

📌 Nota grammaticale

Regular masculine noun related to the verb fallire (to fail/go bankrupt).

Esempio

profitto

profit

📌 Nota grammaticale

Masculine noun; synonymous with utile in commercial contexts.

Esempio

rischio

risk

📌 Nota grammaticale

Masculine noun with plural ending in -chi (hard c sound maintained).

Esempio

innovazione

innovation

📌 Nota grammaticale

Feminine abstract noun ending in -zione.

Esempio

cliente

customer / client

📌 Nota grammaticale

Can be masculine (il cliente) or feminine (la cliente), though plural is always i clienti.

Esempio

fornitore

supplier / vendor

📌 Nota grammaticale

Regular masculine noun denoting a person or company providing goods.

Esempio

gestione

management / running (of a business)

📌 Nota grammaticale

Feminine noun; often combined with nouns like 'del personale' (HR management).

Esempio

assumere

to hire / to employ

📌 Nota grammaticale

Irregular third-conjugation verb (-ere); past participle is assunto.

Esempio

fondare

to found / to establish

📌 Nota grammaticale

Regular first-conjugation verb (-are).

Esempio

gestire

to manage / to run

📌 Nota grammaticale

Regular third-conjugation verb (-ire), taking -isc- forms (gestisco, gestisci).

Esempio

investire

to invest

📌 Nota grammaticale

Regular third-conjugation verb (-ire), follows standard -isco pattern (investisco).

Esempio

negoziare

to negotiate

📌 Nota grammaticale

Regular first-conjugation verb (-are).

Esempio

redditizio

profitable / lucrative

📌 Nota grammaticale

Adjective matching gender/number of the noun (redditizia f.s., redditizi m.pl., redditizie f.pl.).

Esempio

innovativo

innovative

📌 Nota grammaticale

Standard adjective ending in -o (innovativa f.s., innovativi m.pl., innovative f.pl.).

Esempio

to take the plunge / to make the big leap

📌 Nota grammaticale

Idiomatic expression commonly used when someone transitions from employment to entrepreneurship.
