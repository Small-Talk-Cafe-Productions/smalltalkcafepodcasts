secolo

century

📌 Nota grammaticale

Used with ordinal numbers (primo, secondo) to indicate specific centuries.

Esempio

epoca

era / epoch

📌 Nota grammaticale

Often used in the construction 'all'epoca di' (at the time of).

Esempio

impero

empire

📌 Nota grammaticale

Takes the article l' and the plural article gli because it starts with a vowel.

Esempio

patrimonio

heritage / asset

📌 Nota grammaticale

Often used in the phrase 'patrimonio dell'umanità' (World Heritage Site).

Esempio

usanza

custom / habit

📌 Nota grammaticale

Synonym of 'consuetudine' or 'tradizione'.

Esempio

monumento

monument

📌 Nota grammaticale

Standard masculine noun ending in -o.

Esempio

rovina

ruin

📌 Nota grammaticale

Commonly used in plural 'le rovine' when referring to archaeological sites.

Esempio

reperto

artifact / find

📌 Nota grammaticale

Refers specifically to objects found during research or excavations.

Esempio

scavo

excavation / dig

📌 Nota grammaticale

Uses 'lo' because the word starts with 's' followed by a consonant.

Esempio

rito

ritual / rite

📌 Nota grammaticale

Can refer to religious ceremonies or social traditions.

Esempio

leggenda

legend

📌 Nota grammaticale

Used with the preposition 'secondo' to mean 'according to'.

Esempio

mostra

exhibition / show

📌 Nota grammaticale

Comes from the verb 'mostrare' (to show).

Esempio

opera

work / opera

📌 Nota grammaticale

Can mean a single piece of art (opera d'arte) or the musical genre.

Esempio

costume

custom / traditional dress

📌 Nota grammaticale

In this context, it refers to historical clothing or social habits.

Esempio

cittadinanza

citizenship

📌 Nota grammaticale

Abstract noun ending in -anza, always feminine.

Esempio

testimonianza

testimony / evidence

📌 Nota grammaticale

Often used to describe how a building 'bears witness' to the past.

Esempio

eredità

inheritance / legacy

📌 Nota grammaticale

Nouns ending in an accented vowel are invariable in the plural.

Esempio

affresco

fresco

📌 Nota grammaticale

The plural ends in -chi to maintain the hard 'k' sound.

Esempio
