Introduzione al congiuntivo presente e i verbi in -are

Introduction to the present subjunctive and -are verbs

📌 Nota grammaticale

The present subjunctive is used to express subjectivity, doubt, uncertainty, or personal opinions. For regular verbs ending in -are (like parlare), the singular persons (io, tu, lui/lei) and the first-person plural (noi) all share the same ending (-i for singular, -iamo for noi). This can make subject pronouns necessary for clarity. ⚠️ Using the indicative form (e.g., penso che Marco studia) instead of the subjunctive (studi) after expressions of opinion.

Esempio

Penso che Marco studi molto per il suo esame di storia.

I think that Marco studies hard for his history exam.

I verbi regolari in -ere e -ire

Regular verbs in -ere and -ire

📌 Nota grammaticale

For regular verbs ending in -ere (like prendere) and -ire (like partire), the singular endings are all -a (io, tu, lui/lei). For verbs in -isc- (like finire), the -isc- infix is kept across all singular persons and the third-person plural in the present subjunctive. ⚠️ Applying -i endings to -ere and -ire verbs in the singular, confusing them with -are verbs.

Esempio

Credo che tu prenda la decisione giusta per il tuo futuro.

I believe that you are taking the right decision for your future.

La struttura della frase con il congiuntivo

Sentence structure with the subjunctive

📌 Nota grammaticale

A subjunctive sentence typically requires two different subjects and two clauses connected by the conjunction 'che'. The main clause contains a trigger verb (opinion, doubt, emotion), followed by 'che', and then the subordinate clause with the verb in the subjunctive mood. ⚠️ Omiting 'che' or attempting to use the subjunctive when the subject of both clauses is identical (which requires an infinitive with 'di').

Esempio

Spero che voi passiate una vacanza meravigliosa al mare.

I hope that you spend a wonderful vacation at the seaside.

Verbi irregolari comuni: essere e avere

Common irregular verbs: essere and avere

📌 Nota grammaticale

The auxiliary verbs 'essere' and 'avere' are highly irregular in the present subjunctive and must be memorized. Essere conjugates as: sia, sia, sia, siamo, siate, siano. Avere conjugates as: abbia, abbia, abbiamo, abbiate, abbiano (note the 'bbi' root). ⚠️ Using indicative forms like sei or hai inside subordinate clauses requiring the subjunctive.

Esempio

È fondamentale che tu sia puntuale alla riunione di lavoro.

It is fundamental that you are punctual for the work meeting.

Altri verbi irregolari chiave: andare, fare, dire, venire

Other key irregular verbs: andare, fare, dire, venire

📌 Nota grammaticale

Many irregular verbs derive their subjunctive stem from the first person singular of the present indicative ('io' form). For example, fare gives fac-, dire gives dic-, venire gives veng-, and andare gives vad- (with noi and voi following the regular -iamo/-iate patterns). ⚠️ Creating regular forms for irregular verbs, such as saying andisca instead of vada.

Esempio

Voglio che tu vada a comprare il pane fresco prima di cena.

I want you to go buy fresh bread before dinner.

Espressioni di opinione e credenza

Expressions of opinion and belief

📌 Nota grammaticale

Verbs expressing personal opinions, thoughts, and beliefs (pensare, credere, ritenere, immaginare, supporre) trigger the present subjunctive when you want to emphasize uncertainty or subjective viewpoint in the subordinate clause. ⚠️ Using the indicative mood after 'pensare che' because speakers translate literally from English 'I think that it is...'.

Esempio

Penso che questo libro sia molto interessante per i giovani.

I think that this book is very interesting for young people.

Espressioni di dubbio e incertezza

Expressions of doubt and uncertainty

📌 Nota grammaticale

When expressing doubt, disbelief, or uncertainty, Italian mandates the subjunctive mood. Common triggers include dubitare che, non essere sicuro che, and non sapere se/che. ⚠️ Using the future tense instead of the present subjunctive to express future uncertainty in subordinate clauses.

Esempio

Dubito che Paolo sappia la verità su quello che è successo.

I doubt that Paolo knows the truth about what happened.

Espressioni di emozione e sentimento

Expressions of emotion and feeling

📌 Nota grammaticale

Feelings and emotional reactions (temere, avere paura, essere felice, dispiacere, sperare) always trigger the subjunctive in the subordinate clause because emotions are strictly subjective experiences. ⚠️ Confusing sperare che (which frequently takes the subjunctive, though indicative is also heard colloquially) with verbs like essere felice che.

Esempio

Sono molto felice che tu passi il fine settimana con noi.

I am very happy that you are spending the weekend with us.

Espressioni impersonali

Impersonal expressions

📌 Nota grammaticale

Impersonal phrases formed with 'è' + adjective or noun (è importante che, è necessario che, è probabile che, è bello che) express necessity, probability, or value judgments and require the subjunctive. ⚠️ Using the infinitive directly after impersonal phrases when the subjects of the main and subordinate clauses are different.

Esempio

È necessario che tutti studino la grammatica con attenzione.

It is necessary that everyone studies grammar carefully.

Verbi di volontà, desiderio e comando

Verbs of will, desire, and command

📌 Nota grammaticale

Verbs expressing desire, preference, or a command directed at someone else (volere, desiderare, preferire, ordinare, pregare) require the subjunctive when the subordinate clause has a different subject. ⚠️ Using an infinitive with 'di' when the subjects of the main verb and the desired action are different.

Esempio

Il direttore vuole che i dipendenti arrivino puntualmente al lavoro.

The manager wants the employees to arrive punctually at work.

Congiunzioni che richiedono il congiuntivo

Conjunctions that require the subjunctive

📌 Nota grammaticale

Certain compound conjunctions inherently introduce subordinate clauses of purpose, condition, or concession and always require the subjunctive. Examples include: affinché (so that), prima che (before), a meno che (unless), benché / sebbene (although). ⚠️ Following conjunctions like prima che or benché with the indicative mood.

Esempio

Ti spiego la regola affinché tu capisca perfettamente l'esercizio.

I explain the rule to you so that you understand the exercise perfectly.

Quando NON usare il congiuntivo: lo stesso soggetto

When NOT to use the subjunctive: the same subject

📌 Nota grammaticale

The primary rule overriding all subjunctive triggers is identity of subject: if the subject of the main clause and the subordinate clause is the same person, Italian grammar prohibits the subjunctive and requires the preposition 'di' + infinitive. ⚠️ Using 'che' followed by a subjunctive verb even when the subject does not change (e.g., penso che io parta instead of penso di partire).

Esempio

Penso di partire per le vacanze la settimana prossima.

I think I am leaving for the holidays next week.

Regola di contrasto ed errori frequenti: Il congiuntivo presente

Contrast rule and common errors: The Congiuntivo presente

📌 Nota grammaticale

Key contrast in The Congiuntivo presente: Pay special attention to distinguishing core forms (I verbi regolari in -ere e -ire, Verbi irregolari comuni: essere e avere, Espressioni di opinione e credenza, Espressioni di dubbio e incertezza) from their false friends or overlapping structures. Primary pitfall: Using the indicative form (e.g., penso che Marco studia) instead of the subjunctive (studi) after expressions of opinion. ⚠️ Avoid the common error: Using the indicative form (e.g., penso che Marco studia) instead of the subjunctive (studi) after expressions of opinion.

Esempio

Penso che Marco studi molto per il suo esame di storia.

I think that Marco studies hard for his history exam.

Ordine delle parole e posizione sintattica: Il congiuntivo presente

Word order and sentence structure: The Congiuntivo presente

📌 Nota grammaticale

Word order and sentence structure rules for The Congiuntivo presente (B1): In main clauses, subordinate clauses, and with negation or adverbial phrases, ensure I verbi regolari in -ere e -ire, Verbi irregolari comuni: essere e avere, Espressioni di opinione e credenza, Espressioni di dubbio e incertezza occupy their strict grammatical position. ⚠️ Misplacing elements relative to the conjugated verb, negation particle, or clause boundary.

Esempio

Credo che tu prenda la decisione giusta per il tuo futuro.

I believe that you are taking the right decision for your future.

Registro formale vs uso colloquiale: Il congiuntivo presente

Formal register vs. colloquial spoken usage: The Congiuntivo presente

📌 Nota grammaticale

Register and usage nuance (B1): While everyday spoken communication frequently uses conversational shortcuts, formal written texts and CEFR B1 exams require precise adherence to The Congiuntivo presente. ⚠️ Using overly colloquial shortcuts in formal written contexts or stiff bookish phrasing where natural flow is required.

Esempio

Spero che voi passiate una vacanza meravigliosa al mare.

I hope that you spend a wonderful vacation at the seaside.

Regola di decisione e sintesi pratica: Il congiuntivo presente

Decision framework and practical summary: The Congiuntivo presente

📌 Nota grammaticale

Decision framework for The Congiuntivo presente (B1): Before producing a sentence, verify 1) grammatical agreement (gender, number, person), 2) tense/mood triggers, and 3) exact word order. ⚠️ Producing clauses without checking case, mood triggers, or subject-verb agreement.

Esempio

È fondamentale che tu sia puntuale alla riunione di lavoro.

It is fundamental that you are punctual for the work meeting.
