Ja/nej-frågor: Verb på plats 1 (Verb + Subjekt)

Yes/No questions: Start directly with the finite verb

📌 Grammatiknotering

To form a yes/no question in Swedish, simply invert the subject and the finite verb so the verb stands first (Position 1). Never use a helper verb like English 'do/does/did'. ⚠️ Using 'gör' to translate English 'do' in questions ('Gör du tala svenska?' is ungrammatical).

Exempel

Talar du svenska?

Do you speak Swedish?

Frågeordsfrågor: Frågeord + Verb + Subjekt

Question-word questions: Question word in slot 1, Verb in slot 2

📌 Grammatiknotering

When a question starts with a question word (vad, var, när, hur, varför, vem), the question word occupies Position 1 and the finite verb immediately follows in Position 2, before the subject. ⚠️ Keeping Subject + Verb after a question word ('Var du bor?' instead of 'Var bor du?').

Exempel

Var bor du?

Where do you live?

Grundläggande frågeord: vad, vem, när, varfӧr, hur

Core question words: what, who, when, why, how

📌 Grammatiknotering

The essential question words are: 'vad' (what), 'vem' (who - singular), 'vilka' (who - plural), 'när' (when), 'varför' (why), and 'hur' (how). ⚠️ Confusing 'vem' (who) with 'var' (where) or 'vad' (what).

Exempel

Vem ringer du och varför skrattar du?

Who are you calling and why are you laughing?

Plats vs. riktning: var, vart, varifrån

Location vs. direction: where (at), where (to), where (from)

📌 Grammatiknotering

Swedish distinguishes static location from movement: 'var' = where (at a place), 'vart' = where to (direction/destination), and 'varifrån' = where from (origin). ⚠️ Confusing 'var' (where) with the past tense 'var' (was/were)—they are spelled and pronounced identically!

Exempel

Var är du nu, och vart ska du gå?

Where are you now, and where are you going?

Vilken, vilket, vilka (which / what + noun)

Inflecting 'which / what' before a noun: vilken (en), vilket (ett), vilka (plural)

📌 Grammatiknotering

'Vilken/vilket/vilka' agrees with the gender and number of the noun it asks about: vilken dag (en-word), vilket språk (ett-word), vilka böcker (plural). ⚠️ Using 'vad' directly before a noun ('Vad tid är det?' or 'Vad buss tar du?' instead of 'Vilken tid/buss').

Exempel

Vilken buss tar du och vilket tåg går till Malmö?

Which bus do you take and which train goes to Malmö?

Frågor med hur + adjektiv/adverb: hur mycket, hur många, hur ofta

Compound question phrases with 'hur'

📌 Grammatiknotering

Combine 'hur' with adjectives or adverbs to ask about quantity, frequency, age, or duration: 'hur mycket' (uncountable), 'hur många' (plural countable), 'hur gammal' (how old), 'hur länge' (how long time), 'hur långt' (how far). ⚠️ Confusing 'hur länge' (duration in time) with 'hur långt' (physical distance) or 'hur lång' (tall/long).

Exempel

Hur mycket kostar kaffet och hur många bullar vill du ha?

How much does the coffee cost and how many buns do you want?

Vems (whose)

Asking about ownership: 'vems' (whose)

📌 Grammatiknotering

'Vems' means 'whose' and is always followed by an indefinite noun (never with a definite suffix). ⚠️ Adding the definite article after 'vems' ('Vems väskan är det?' instead of 'Vems väska är det?').

Exempel

Vems väska står här under bordet?

Whose bag is standing here under the table?

Ja vs. Jo vid svar på frågor

Answering 'ja' vs. 'jo' (contradicting a negative question)

📌 Grammatiknotering

Use 'ja' to answer yes to a positive question. Use 'jo' to answer 'yes, actually!' to a negative question (a question containing 'inte', 'ingen', or 'aldrig'). ⚠️ Answering 'ja' to a negative question; Swedish speakers use 'jo' to cancel the negation.

Exempel

– Kommer du inte ikväll? – Jo, jag kommer klockan åtta!

— Aren't you coming tonight? — Yes (I am), I'm coming at eight!

Kortsvar med hjälpverb: Det gör jag / Det är jag / Det har jag

Short answers: 'Ja, det är/har/kan/gör jag'

📌 Grammatiknotering

In polite short answers ('Yes, I do' / 'No, I don't'), Swedish uses 'Ja/Nej, det + verb + subject'. Repeat auxiliaries/modals (är, har, kan, ska, vill), and use 'gör' (presens) or 'gjorde' (preteritum) for all other verbs. ⚠️ Using 'gör' when the question has 'är', 'har', or a modal verb ('Är du redo? – Ja, det gör jag' -> must be 'Ja, det är jag').

Exempel

– Gillar du kaffe? – Ja, det gör jag! / Nej, det gör jag inte.

— Do you like coffee? — Yes, I do! / No, I don't.

Påhängsfrågor: eller hur? / visst?

Question tags: 'eller hur?' (right? / isn't it? / don't you?)

📌 Grammatiknotering

Instead of changing tag verbs like English ('isn't he?', 'don't you?', 'can't we?'), Swedish uses one universal tag phrase at the end of the sentence: ', eller hur?' (or ', va?' in casual speech). ⚠️ Trying to translate 'isn't it?' literally as 'är inte det?' at the end of a sentence.

Exempel

Du kommer från Göteborg, eller hur?

You come from Gothenburg, don't you?
