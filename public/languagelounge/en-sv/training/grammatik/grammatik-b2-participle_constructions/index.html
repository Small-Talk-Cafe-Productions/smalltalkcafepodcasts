Satsförkortning med presens particip efter rörelse- och positionsverb

Present participle after verbs of motion and posture (komma springande, bli sittande)

📌 Grammatiknotering

After verbs like 'komma' (come), 'gå' (go), 'stå' (stand), 'sitta' (sit), 'ligga' (lie), and 'bli' (remain), Swedish uses a present participle (-ande/-ende) to describe the manner of movement or a continuing state ('kom gående' = came walking, 'blev sittande' = remained sitting). ⚠️ Using an infinitive after 'komma' for manner of movement (kom springa — 'kom att tänka på' means 'happened to think of', whereas 'came running' is 'kom springande').

Exempel

Hunden kom glatt springande mot oss så fort vi öppnade trädgårdsgrinden.

The dog came happily running toward us as soon as we opened the garden gate.

Attributiva participfraser (Utbyggda framförställda attribut)

Extended pre-nominal participle phrases in formal written Swedish

📌 Grammatiknotering

In formal, legal, and journalistic Swedish, a participle preceded by adverbials or prepositional phrases can be placed BEFORE the noun ('den av regeringen tillsatta utredningen' = the inquiry appointed by the government). In everyday speech, this is unpacked into a relative clause ('utredningen som regeringen har tillsatt'). ⚠️ Forgetting double definiteness ('den/det/de' at the very start and the definite suffix on the noun at the end) in extended pre-nominal phrases.

Exempel

Det nyligen presenterade lagförslaget väckte en livlig debatt i riksdagen.

The recently presented bill sparked a lively debate in parliament.

Efterställda participfraser (Apposition som ersätter relativsats)

Post-nominal participle clauses (reduced relative clauses)

📌 Grammatiknotering

Instead of cramming modifiers before the noun, modern B2 prose often places a past or present participle phrase AFTER the noun as a reduced relative clause. Crucially, a post-nominal past participle MUST agree in gender and number with the noun it modifies! ⚠️ Leaving a post-nominal past participle uninflected (ett hus, byggd på 1800-talet — since 'hus' is an ett-word, it must be 'byggt'!).

Exempel

Rapporten, skriven av tre ledande klimatforskare, publicerades i går.

The report, written by three leading climate researchers, was published yesterday.

Fristående (adverbiella) participfraser i satsens början

Sentence-initial participial clauses modifying the main-clause subject

📌 Grammatiknotering

A sentence can begin with a participle phrase expressing cause, time, or state ('Trött efter resan gick han och lade sig'). In Position 1, this reduced clause occupies the Fundament and triggers V2 inversion in the main clause, and its logical subject MUST match the grammatical subject of the main clause! ⚠️ Creating a 'dangling participle' where the main-clause subject does not match the participle, or failing to inflect 'Imponerade' in the plural when the subject is plural.

Exempel

Inspirerad av föreläsningen bestämde hon sig för att byta karriärinriktning.

Inspired by the lecture, she decided to change her career direction.

Sammansatta particip (Substantiv/Adverb + particip som ett ord)

Compound participles: solbelyst, välskriven, hemmahörande, banbrytande

📌 Grammatiknotering

Swedish is extremely productive in forming single-word compound participles where an object, agent, or adverb is fused onto a present or past participle: 'ett solbelyst rum' (sunlit), 'en välskriven text' (well-written), 'en privatägd bank' (privately owned), 'ett vinstdrivande företag' (profit-making). ⚠️ Writing compound participles as two separate words (när producerade or väl skriven — in Swedish they are fused into 'närproducerade', 'välskriven').

Exempel

De presenterade en banbrytande metod i en välskriven vetenskaplig artikel.

They presented a groundbreaking method in a well-written scientific article.

Presens particip som adverb (förvånansvärt, strålande, uteslutande)

Present participles functioning as degree or manner adverbs

📌 Grammatiknotering

Present participles are frequently used as adverbs modifying adjectives or verbs without changing form: 'strålande glad' (radiantly happy), 'förbluffande enkel' (astonishingly simple), 'uteslutande' (exclusively), 'genomgående' (throughout / consistently). ⚠️ Trying to add '-t' to a present participle when using it as an adverb.

Exempel

Resultatet i undersökningen var genomgående positivt och övertygande.

The result of the survey was consistently positive and convincing.

Lexikaliserade particip som prepositioner och konjunktioner

Participles grammaticalized as prepositions: angående, beträffande, beroende på, oavsett

📌 Grammatiknotering

Several high-frequency B2 formal prepositions and connectors are originally present or past participles: 'angående' / 'rörande' / 'beträffande' (regarding/concerning), 'inkluderande' (including), 'beroende på' (depending on), 'förutsatt att' (provided that), and 'oavsett' (regardless of). ⚠️ Adding 'om' or 'till' after 'angående' or 'beträffande' (angående om... — they govern the noun phrase directly!).

Exempel

Angående er förfrågan vill vi meddela att leveransen sker på tisdag.

Regarding your inquiry, we would like to inform you that delivery will take place on Tuesday.

Particip efter 'få' och 'ha' (få något gjort / ha något sparat)

Causative and resultative 'få/ha + object + neuter participle'

📌 Grammatiknotering

To express 'get something done', Swedish uses 'få + object + past participle' (agreeing with the object: 'få bilen reparerad', 'få taket lagat') or 'få + supine'. Notice the subtle difference: 'Jag har sparat tiotusen kronor' (perfect action) vs. 'Jag har tiotusen kronor sparade' (possessive state). ⚠️ Failing to make the participle agree with the object in gender/number (få bilen reparerat instead of 'få bilen reparerad').

Exempel

Vi måste äntligen få det läckande taket reparerat innan höstregnen börjar.

We must finally get the leaking roof repaired before the autumn rains begin.

Absoluta participkonstruktioner med prepositionen 'med'

Absolute constructions with 'med + noun + participle' (with X done/doing)

📌 Grammatiknotering

Where English uses 'With his arms crossed' or 'With the contract signed', Swedish uses 'med + definite noun + past/present participle' (agreeing with the noun in gender and number!). ⚠️ Using a bare supine or uninflected participle in 'med'-phrases (med armarna korsad instead of plural 'korsade').

Exempel

Med kontraktet påskrivet och nycklarna i handen firade de sitt nya kontor.

With the contract signed and the keys in hand, they celebrated their new office.

Gerundivum / Presens particip med passiv modal betydelse (tillfredsställande)

Adjectival participles with 'o-' prefix expressing (im)possibility

📌 Grammatiknotering

Many Swedish participles prefixed with negative 'o-' express what cannot be done ('ett olösligt / oslagbart rekord', 'en oförglömlig kväll', 'oersättlig', 'oväntad', 'oavbruten'). Note that particle verbs place 'o-' BEFORE the particle ('oinbjuden', 'oavslutat')! ⚠️ Placing negative 'o-' between the particle and the verb stem (upp-oklarad instead of 'ouppklarad').

Exempel

Trots flera ouppklarade frågor kom parterna fram till en hållbar lösning.

Despite several unresolved issues, the parties arrived at a sustainable solution.

Word order and sentence structure: Participle Constructions & Reduced Clauses

📌 Grammatiknotering

Word order and sentence structure rules for Participle Constructions & Reduced Clauses (B2): In main clauses, subordinate clauses, and with negation or adverbial phrases, ensure Attributiva participfraser, Efterställda participfraser, Sammansatta particip, Presens particip som adverb occupy their strict grammatical position. ⚠️ Misplacing elements relative to the conjugated verb, negation particle, or clause boundary.

Exempel
