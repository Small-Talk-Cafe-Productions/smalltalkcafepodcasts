Grundregeln för dubbel bestämdhet

Core pattern: den/det/de + adjective-a + noun in definite form

📌 Grammatiknotering

When a definite noun in Swedish is preceded by an adjective, definiteness must be marked THREE times: 1) a freestanding definite article (den/det/de), 2) the weak adjective ending (-a), and 3) the suffixed definite article on the noun (-en/-et/-na). ⚠️ Forgetting the suffixed article on the noun (den stora bil) or omitting the freestanding article (stora bilen) in standard contexts.

Exempel

Den stora röda bilen står parkerad framför det vita huset.

The big red car is parked in front of the white house.

Fristående artikel: den, det, de (dom)

Choosing the freestanding article: den (en), det (ett), de (plural)

📌 Grammatiknotering

The freestanding article (adjektivets bestämda artikel) agrees with the noun: 'den' for singular en-words, 'det' for singular ett-words, and 'de' (pronounced 'dom' in speech) for all plurals regardless of gender. ⚠️ Using 'den' with an ett-word (den lilla företaget) or forgetting that plural ett-words also take 'de' + definite plural ending (-en/-na).

Exempel

Det lilla företaget lanserade de innovativa produkterna på den svenska marknaden.

The small company launched the innovative products on the Swedish market.

Adjektivets bestämda form på -a (och -e för maskulinum)

Weak adjective ending -a (and optional -e for male persons)

📌 Grammatiknotering

In definite noun phrases, the adjective always takes the weak ending '-a' across singular en-words, ett-words, and plurals. When referring to a singular male person, '-e' may optionally be used instead of '-a' (den gamle mannen). ⚠️ Adding '-t' to the adjective in definite neuter phrases (det stort huset instead of 'det stora huset').

Exempel

Den trevliga grannen hjälpte den gamle mannen med de tunga matkassarna.

The nice neighbor helped the old man with the heavy grocery bags.

Dubbel bestämdhet med 'den här / den där'

Double definiteness with colloquial demonstratives 'den här' and 'den där'

📌 Grammatiknotering

The demonstratives 'den här / det här / de här' (this/these) and 'den där / det där / de där' (that/those) ALWAYS require the noun to be in the definite form, both with and without an adjective. ⚠️ Using an indefinite noun after 'den här / den där' (den här ny dator instead of 'den här nya datorn').

Exempel

Den här bekväma fåtöljen passar perfekt i det där ljusa vardagsrummet.

This comfortable armchair fits perfectly in that bright living room.

Undantag 1: 'denna / detta / dessa' (Ingen dubbel bestämdhet!)

Exception 1: Formal demonstratives 'denna, detta, dessa' take INDEFINITE noun

📌 Grammatiknotering

Unlike 'den här', the formal/written demonstratives 'denna' (en), 'detta' (ett), and 'dessa' (plural) take the weak adjective (-a) followed by the INDEFINITE form of the noun (no suffixed article!). ⚠️ Adding a definite suffix to the noun after 'denna/detta/dessa' (denna stora bilen in standard written Swedish instead of 'denna stora bil').

Exempel

Denna komplicerade fråga kräver en noggrann utredning under detta år.

This complicated issue requires a thorough investigation during this year.

Undantag 2: Possessiva pronomen och genitiv-s

Exception 2: Possessives and s-genitives take weak adjective + INDEFINITE noun

📌 Grammatiknotering

After possessive pronouns (min, din, hans, sin, vår, er, deras) and nouns in the s-genitive (Annas, företagets), there is NO freestanding article and the noun MUST be in the indefinite form, while the adjective still takes '-a'. ⚠️ Putting a definite suffix on the noun after a possessive (min nya cykeln or Annas bilen).

Exempel

Min nya cykel står bredvid Annas gamla röda bil på gården.

My new bicycle is standing next to Anna's old red car in the courtyard.

Undantag 3: Samma, nästa, föregående, följande

Exception 3: Words like 'samma' and 'nästa' omit both articles

📌 Grammatiknotering

Determiners such as 'samma' (the same), 'nästa' (the next), 'föregående' (the previous), and 'följande' (the following) normally take NO freestanding article and an INDEFINITE noun, even though English uses 'the'. ⚠️ Saying den samma bilen or den nästa veckan by translating English 'the same / the next' literally.

Exempel

Vi tar nästa tåg till Göteborg och bor på samma mysiga hotell som sist.

We are taking the next train to Gothenburg and staying at the same cozy hotel as last time.

Speciellt mönster med 'hela' och 'halva'

Special pattern with 'hela' (the whole) and 'halva' (half the)

📌 Grammatiknotering

'Hela' and 'halva' take a definite noun (with suffixed article) directly WITHOUT the freestanding article 'den/det/de' unless another descriptive adjective intervenes. ⚠️ Saying den hela dagen instead of idiomatic 'hela dagen', or forgetting the definite suffix (hela dag).

Exempel

Vi promenerade genom hela staden och åt upp halva tårtan efteråt.

We walked through the whole city and ate up half the cake afterwards.

Superlativ och ordningstal i bestämd form

Superlatives and ordinal numbers with double definiteness

📌 Grammatiknotering

Superlatives (-aste / -sta) and ordinal numbers (första, andra, tredje) normally require double definiteness ('den bästa filmen', 'det tredje kapitlet'), though the freestanding article is sometimes dropped in fixed titles or expressions ('första gången'). ⚠️ Leaving the noun indefinite after a superlative (den bästa bok instead of 'den bästa boken').

Exempel

Det här är den högsta byggnaden och det äldsta torget i hela staden.

This is the tallest building and the oldest square in the whole city.

Fasta uttryck och egennamn utan fristående artikel

Fixed expressions, names, and lexicalized phrases without 'den/det/de'

📌 Grammatiknotering

In proper names, geographical names, and closely knit idiomatic concepts (e.g., Vita huset, Röda korset, svenska språket, gamla stan), the freestanding article is omitted while the definite suffix on the noun remains. ⚠️ Inserting 'det' or 'den' into fixed proper names (Det Vita huset, Den Gamla stan).

Exempel

Vi strosade runt i Gamla stan och diskuterade svenska folkhemmets historia.

We strolled around the Old Town and discussed the history of the Swedish welfare state.
