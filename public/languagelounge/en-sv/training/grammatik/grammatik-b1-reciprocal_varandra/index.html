Reciprokt pronomen: 'varandra' vs. reflexivt 'sig'

Reciprocal 'varandra' (each other / one another) vs. reflexive 'sig' (themselves)

📌 Grammatiknotering

'Varandra' expresses a mutual action between two or more subjects ('De hjälper varandra' = They help each other). By contrast, 'sig' (or 'oss/er') means each person acts upon themselves ('De tvättar sig' = They wash themselves). 'Varandra' is used for all persons in the plural (vi, ni, de). ⚠️ Using 'sig' or 'oss' when a reciprocal meaning is intended (Vi såg oss på caféet means 'We saw ourselves', whereas 'We saw each other' must be 'Vi såg varandra' or s-verb 'Vi sågs').

Exempel

Vi har känt varandra sedan gymnasiet och ringer varandra varje vecka.

We have known each other since high school and call each other every week.

Genitivformen 'varandras' (+ obestämt substantiv!)

Reciprocal possessive 'varandras' (each other's) + indefinite noun

📌 Grammatiknotering

Adding '-s' to 'varandra' forms the possessive 'varandras' (each other's). Just like after any other possessive or genitive in Swedish, the following noun MUST be in the indefinite form and any adjective takes the weak '-a' ending! ⚠️ Using a definite noun after 'varandras' (varandras bilarna instead of 'varandras bilar').

Exempel

De läste igenom varandras rapporter och gav konstruktiv återkoppling.

They read through each other's reports and gave constructive feedback.

Reciproka deponensverb på -s (träffas, ses, hörs, hjälpas åt)

Reciprocal s-verbs: träffas, ses, hörs, kramas, skiljas

📌 Grammatiknotering

Swedish has a group of very common verbs ending in '-s' that inherently carry a reciprocal meaning ('each other') without needing the word 'varandra': 'träffas' (meet each other), 'ses' (see each other), 'höras' (be in touch), 'kramas' (hug), 'hjälpas åt' (help each other). ⚠️ Adding 'varandra' redundantly after a reciprocal s-verb (Vi träffas varandra — either say 'Vi träffas' or 'Vi träffar varandra').

Exempel

Vi ses utanför biografen klockan sju, och vi hörs på telefon innan dess!

We'll see each other outside the cinema at seven, and we'll be in touch by phone before then!

Genitiv på -s: Grundregeln (Utan apostrof!)

Forming the s-genitive on indefinite, definite, and plural nouns (no apostrophe)

📌 Grammatiknotering

In Swedish, the genitive '-s' is attached directly to the end of the word—whether indefinite, definite singular, or definite plural—WITHOUT an apostrophe: en flickas bok, flickans bok, flickornas böcker, Sveriges huvudstad. ⚠️ Inserting an English-style apostrophe before '-s' (Stockholm's skärgård or företaget's vd).

Exempel

Barnens leksaker låg utspridda över hela vardagsrummets golv.

The children's toys lay scattered across the entire living room floor.

Ord på -s, -x och -z i genitiv (Ingen extra ändelse!)

Genitive of names and nouns already ending in -s, -x, or -z

📌 Grammatiknotering

When a word or proper name already ends in '-s', '-x', or '-z' (Lukas, Anders, Marx, ett hus, ett pris), no extra '-s' is added. In writing, an apostrophe is NOT used either—unless needed for clarity on bare indefinite nouns, where a preposition phrase is preferred instead ('taket på ett hus'). ⚠️ Writing Anders's or Anderses instead of the unchanged written form 'Anders' before an indefinite noun ('Anders bil').

Exempel

Anders nya lägenhet ligger alldeles i närheten av Johannes arbetsplats.

Anders's new apartment is located right near Johannes's workplace.

Substantiv och adjektiv efter genitiv-s (Ingen bestämd slutartikel!)

Noun phrases after s-genitive: weak adjective (-a) + INDEFINITE noun

📌 Grammatiknotering

Just like after possessive pronouns ('min', 'varandras'), a noun preceded by an s-genitive MUST stand in its bare indefinite form, while any intervening adjective takes the weak '-a' ending! ⚠️ Adding the definite suffix to a noun after an s-genitive (stadens kyrkan or Sveriges längsta älven).

Exempel

Sveriges längsta älv och stadens äldsta kyrka lockar många besökare.

Sweden's longest river and the city's oldest church attract many visitors.

Gruppgenitiv (Konungens av Danmark bröstkarameller -> kungen av Danmarks)

Group genitive: attaching '-s' to the end of a multi-word phrase

📌 Grammatiknotering

When a possessor consists of a name/noun phrase with a post-modifier or coordination ('Anna och Erik', 'chefen för avdelningen'), the '-s' is attached to the LAST word of the entire phrase ('Anna och Eriks hus' when owned jointly; 'Annas och Eriks bilar' when owned separately). ⚠️ Putting '-s' on 'någon' in 'someone else's' (någons annan) instead of the end of the phrase ('någon annans').

Exempel

Anna och Eriks gemensamma sommarhus ligger vid en vacker insjö.

Anna and Erik's joint summer house is located by a beautiful lake.

Prepositionsuttryck i stället för genitiv (på, till, för, av)

Prepositional alternatives to the s-genitive (taket på huset, chefen för...)

📌 Grammatiknotering

In everyday Swedish, especially for inanimate part-whole relationships or functional roles, prepositions often sound more natural than '-s': 'på' for parts of objects ('taket på huset', 'slutet på filmen'), 'till' for kinship/ownership ('föräldrarna till barnet', 'ägaren till bilen'), and 'för' for leadership ('chefen för bolaget'). ⚠️ Using 'av' everywhere as a literal translation of English 'of' (taket av huset, ägaren av bilen — Swedish uses 'på' and 'till' here!).

Exempel

Ägaren till den felparkerade bilen ombeds kontakta receptionen omedelbart.

The owner of the illegally parked car is requested to contact the reception immediately.

Mått- och tidsgenitiv på -s (en tvåtimmarsresa, ett treårsavtal)

Genitive of measure, time, and age with '-s'

📌 Grammatiknotering

Swedish uses the s-genitive extensively to indicate duration, measure, or value before a noun (often compounded or written before an adjective + noun): 'en treveckorssemester' / 'en tre veckors semester', 'ett fem minuters samtal', 'en tioårig flickas dröm'. ⚠️ Using a singular bare noun without '-s' in uncompounded measure phrases (en två timmar resa instead of 'en två timmars resa' or 'en tvåtimmarsresa').

Exempel

Efter en sex timmars tågresa genom landet kom vi äntligen fram till Umeå.

After a six-hour train journey through the country, we finally arrived in Umeå.

Äldre genitivuttryck efter 'till' (till sjöss, till fots, till bords)

Fossilized old genitives in fixed expressions after 'till'

📌 Grammatiknotering

Because the preposition 'till' governed the genitive case in Old Swedish, many high-frequency idiomatic phrases still preserve '-s' (or plural '-a') after 'till': 'till fots' (on foot), 'till sjöss' (at sea), 'till skogs' (into the woods), 'till bords' (at the table), 'till salu' (for sale), 'till hands' (at hand). ⚠️ Treating these '-s' forms as plurals or trying to use them outside fixed 'till'-idioms.

Exempel

Vi valde att ta oss upp till fjällstugan till fots i stället för att ta liften.

We chose to make our way up to the mountain cabin on foot instead of taking the lift.
