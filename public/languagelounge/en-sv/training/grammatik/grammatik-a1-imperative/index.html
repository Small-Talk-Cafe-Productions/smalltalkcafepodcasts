Imperativ = verbets stam!

The imperative form IS the verb stem (used to identify verb groups)

📌 Grammatiknotering

In Swedish, the imperative (command form) is identical to the verb stem—the base form from which all other tenses are built. There is only one imperative form whether addressing one person or many. ⚠️ Adding a subject pronoun ('Du kom här!')—standard imperatives have no subject.

Exempel

Lyssna noga, läs texten och skriv ett svar!

Listen carefully, read the text, and write an answer!

Grupp 1 (-ar-verb): Imperativ slutar på -a (samma som infinitiv)

Group 1 verbs: Imperative ends in -a (identical to the infinitive)

📌 Grammatiknotering

For Group 1 verbs (whose present tense ends in -ar), drop the '-r' from the present tense. Thus, the imperative ends in '-a', just like the infinitive: prat-ar -> Prata!, öppn-ar -> Öppna!, stann-ar -> Stanna! ⚠️ Dropping the '-a' from Group 1 verbs ('Öppn!' or 'Prat!'—Group 1 stems keep their '-a'!).

Exempel

Öppna dörren och titta här!

Open the door and look here!

Grupp 2 (-er-verb): Imperativ slutar på konsonant (utan -a/-er)

Group 2 verbs: Drop -er from the present (or -a from the infinitive)

📌 Grammatiknotering

For Group 2 verbs (present tense in -er), drop '-er' (or drop '-a' from the infinitive) so the imperative ends in a consonant: ring-er -> Ring!, stäng-er -> Stäng!, köp-er -> Köp!, läs-er -> Läs! ⚠️ Using the infinitive in '-a' as an imperative for Group 2 verbs ('Stänga fönstret!' instead of 'Stäng fönstret!').

Exempel

Stäng fönstret och ring mig imorgon!

Close the window and call me tomorrow!

Grupp 3 (kortverb): Imperativ slutar på lång vokal (samma som infinitiv)

Group 3 short verbs: Imperative is the one-syllable vowel stem

📌 Grammatiknotering

For Group 3 verbs (presens in -r after a long vowel), drop '-r'. The imperative is identical to the infinitive: tro-r -> Tro!, bo-r -> Bo!, klä-r -> Klä! ⚠️ Keeping the present-tense '-r' in the imperative.

Exempel

Tro på dig själv och klä på dig varmt!

Believe in yourself and dress warmly!

Grupp 4 (starka och oregelbundna verb): Ta bort -er eller -r

Group 4 strong/irregular verbs: Drop -er or -r from the present tense

📌 Grammatiknotering

Strong and irregular verbs also use their stem as the imperative: skriv-er -> Skriv!, drick-er -> Drick!, komm-er -> Kom! (spelled with one 'm'), gå-r -> Gå!, se-r -> Se!, ta-r -> Ta!, är -> Var! (be!). ⚠️ Writing 'Komm!' with double 'm' (Swedish never writes '-mm' at the end of a word: 'Kom!') or saying 'Är tyst!' instead of 'Var tyst!'.

Exempel

Ta en kaka, drick lite kaffe och var inte blyg!

Take a cookie, drink some coffee, and don't be shy!

Negativ imperativ: Imperativ + inte

Negative commands ('Don't...'): Imperative verb + 'inte'

📌 Grammatiknotering

To form a negative command ('Don't do that!'), place 'inte' (or 'aldrig') AFTER the imperative verb—never before it! ⚠️ Putting 'inte' before the imperative like English 'Don't forget' ('Inte glöm!' -> must be 'Glöm inte!').

Exempel

Glöm inte nycklarna och gå inte för långt!

Don't forget the keys and don't walk too far!

Ordföljd vid negations- och objektpronomen i imperativ

With unstressed object pronouns, the pronoun precedes 'inte' in imperatives

📌 Grammatiknotering

When a negative imperative has an unstressed object pronoun or reflexive pronoun (mig, dig, honom, den, det), the pronoun normally stands between the imperative verb and 'inte': 'Glöm det inte!', 'Oroa dig inte!'. ⚠️ Forgetting the reflexive pronoun 'dig' (singular) or 'er' (plural) with reflexive imperatives: 'Skynda dig!' (to one person), 'Skynda er!' (to several people).

Exempel

Oroa dig inte, och stör honom inte nu!

Don't worry, and don't disturb him now!

Artiga uppmaningar med gärna, tack eller är du snäll

Softening imperatives with 'gärna', 'tack', or 'är du snäll'

📌 Grammatiknotering

Swedish does not have a single universal word like 'please'. Instead, soften imperatives by adding 'gärna' (gladly/please) right after the verb, or 'tack' / 'är du snäll' at the end of the sentence. ⚠️ Overusing formal 'vänligen' (kindly) in casual speech where 'gärna' or 'tack' sounds much more natural.

Exempel

Sitt gärna ner och ta en kopp kaffe!

Please have a seat and take a cup of coffee!

Artiga frågor som uppmaning: Kan du...? / Skulle du kunna...?

Polite requests using modal questions instead of bare imperatives

📌 Grammatiknotering

In everyday Swedish, requests to strangers or colleagues are usually phrased as questions with 'Kan du...?' (Can you...?) or 'Skulle du kunna...?' (Could you...?) rather than bare imperatives. ⚠️ Using a blunt imperative with a waiter ('Ge mig en kaffe!') instead of 'Jag tar gärna en kaffe' or 'Kan jag få en kaffe, tack?'.

Exempel

Kan du öppna fönstret lite?

Can you open the window a little?

Uppmaningar i första person plural: Ska vi...? / Låt oss...

'Let's...': 'Ska vi + infinitive?' or formal 'Låt oss + infinitive!'

📌 Grammatiknotering

To say 'Let's...' in everyday Swedish, use 'Ska vi...?' ('Ska vi gå nu?') or 'Kom så går vi!'. 'Låt oss + infinitive' exists in formal or solemn style. ⚠️ Using 'Låt oss' in casual everyday chat where Swedes naturally say 'Ska vi...?' or 'Vi tar och...'.

Exempel

Ska vi ta en fikapaus nu? Kom så går vi!

Shall we / Let's take a coffee break now? Come on, let's go!

Contrast rule and common errors: The Imperative

Ordföljd och satsstruktur: Imperativ (uppmaningsform)

Word order and sentence structure: The Imperative

📌 Grammatiknotering

Word order and sentence structure rules for The Imperative (A1): In main clauses, subordinate clauses, and with negation or adverbial phrases, ensure Imperativ = verbets stam!, Grupp 1 : Imperativ slutar på -a, Grupp 2 : Imperativ slutar på konsonant, Grupp 4 : Ta bort -er eller -r occupy their strict grammatical position. ⚠️ Misplacing elements relative to the conjugated verb, negation particle, or clause boundary.

Exempel

Öppna dörren och titta här!

Open the door and look here!

Formellt register vs talspråk: Imperativ (uppmaningsform)

Formal register vs. colloquial spoken usage: The Imperative

📌 Grammatiknotering

Register and usage nuance (A1): While everyday spoken communication frequently uses conversational shortcuts, formal written texts and CEFR A1 exams require precise adherence to The Imperative. ⚠️ Using overly colloquial shortcuts in formal written contexts or stiff bookish phrasing where natural flow is required.

Exempel

Stäng fönstret och ring mig imorgon!

Close the window and call me tomorrow!
