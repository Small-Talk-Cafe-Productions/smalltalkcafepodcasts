Desde mi punto de vista, conviene destacar que...

From my point of view, it is worth pointing out that...

📌 Nota gramatical

Formal register. Uses 'conviene' followed by an infinitive to offer a constructive suggestion.

Ejemplo

Desde mi punto de vista, conviene destacar que los costes iniciales son elevados.

From my point of view, it is worth pointing out that the initial costs are high.

Partiendo de la base de que...

On the premise that... / Assuming that...

📌 Nota gramatical

Standard connector for setting a foundational assumption before building an argument.

Ejemplo

Partiendo de la base de que el presupuesto es limitado, debemos priorizar.

Assuming that the budget is limited, we must prioritize.

No se puede pasar por alto el hecho de que...

One cannot overlook the fact that...

📌 Nota gramatical

Impersonal structure ('se puede') used to introduce an undeniable counterpoint.

Ejemplo

No se puede pasar por alto el hecho de que las ventas han caído.

One cannot overlook the fact that sales have dropped.

Dicho de otro modo,...

In other words,...

📌 Nota gramatical

Useful transitional phrase for clarifying or simplifying a complex statement.

Ejemplo

Dicho de otro modo, necesitamos reestructurar el departamento entero.

In other words, we need to restructure the entire department.

A fin de cuentas, lo que importa es...

At the end of the day, what matters is...

📌 Nota gramatical

Colloquial yet professional idiom used to cut to the chase and summarize a core value.

Ejemplo

A fin de cuentas, lo que importa es la satisfacción del cliente.

At the end of the day, what matters is customer satisfaction.

Lejos de ser una solución, esto representa...

Far from being a solution, this represents...

📌 Nota gramatical

Rhetorical device using 'lejos de' + infinitive to strongly refute an opponent's proposal.

Ejemplo

Lejos de ser una solución, esto representa un grave problema añadido.

Far from being a solution, this represents a serious added problem.

Conviene señalar la contradicción entre...

It is worth pointing out the contradiction between...

📌 Nota gramatical

Analytical structure used to expose flaws or inconsistencies in an argument.

Ejemplo

Conviene señalar la contradicción entre tus palabras y tus actos.

It is worth pointing out the contradiction between your words and actions.

Por mucho que queramos ignorarlo,...

No matter how much we want to ignore it,...

📌 Nota gramatical

Uses 'por mucho que' followed by the subjunctive to dismiss wishful thinking.

Ejemplo

Por mucho que queramos ignorarlo, el mercado está cambiando.

No matter how much we want to ignore it, the market is changing.

En aras de la transparencia, debemos admitir que...

In the interest of transparency, we must admit that...

📌 Nota gramatical

Formal debate phrase ('en aras de') used to seize the moral high ground and confess a drawback.

Ejemplo

En aras de la transparencia, debemos admitir que hubo errores.

In the interest of transparency, we must admit that there were mistakes.

No nos equivoquemos; el verdadero problema radica en...

Make no mistake; the real problem lies in...

📌 Nota gramatical

Uses the imperative ('no nos equivoquemos') to redirect focus to the speaker's core thesis.

Ejemplo

No nos equivoquemos; el verdadero problema radica en la falta de fondos.

Make no mistake; the real problem lies in the lack of funds.

Huelga decir que...

Needless to say... / It goes without saying that...

📌 Nota gramatical

Advanced rhetorical phrase meaning something is completely obvious or understood.

Ejemplo

Huelga decir que la puntualidad es obligatoria en estas reuniones.

Needless to say, punctuality is mandatory in these meetings.

A mi modo de ver, se trata de una falacia.

The way I see it, this is a fallacy.

📌 Nota gramatical

Direct yet professional pushback against flawed logic.

Ejemplo

A mi modo de ver, se trata de una falacia argumental muy peligrosa.

The way I see it, this is a very dangerous argumentative fallacy.

En contraposición a lo que se suele afirmar,...

As opposed to what is usually claimed,...

📌 Nota gramatical

Used to challenge common assumptions with opposing evidence.

Ejemplo

En contraposición a lo que se suele afirmar, los costes son bajos.

As opposed to what is usually claimed, the costs are low.

Si bien es cierto que..., no es menos cierto que...

While it is true that..., it is equally true that...

📌 Nota gramatical

Classic concession structure balancing two competing truths.

Ejemplo

Si bien es cierto que es caro, no es menos cierto que es duradero.

While it is true that it's expensive, it is equally true that it's durable.

Todo apunta a que...

All signs point to... / Everything indicates that...

📌 Nota gramatical

Useful for drawing a strategic conclusion based on available evidence.

Ejemplo

Todo apunta a que deberíamos aplazar la decisión hasta el mes próximo.

All signs point to us postponing the decision until next month.

Aceptar esta premisa nos llevaría directamente a...

Accepting this premise would lead us straight to...

📌 Nota gramatical

Employs a conditional clause to demonstrate the logical consequences of an opponent's argument.

Ejemplo

Aceptar esta premisa nos llevaría directamente al fracaso absoluto.

Accepting this premise would lead us straight to absolute failure.

Sobra decir que...

It goes without saying that...

📌 Nota gramatical

A common equivalent to 'huelga decir', slightly more conversational.

Ejemplo

Sobra decir que la confidencialidad es nuestra máxima prioridad.

It goes without saying that confidentiality is our top priority.

Planteo la siguiente cuestión: ¿hasta qué punto...?

I raise the following question: to what extent...?

📌 Nota gramatical

Strategic rhetorical question to pivot the debate to a deeper level.

Ejemplo

Planteo la siguiente cuestión: ¿hasta qué punto nos beneficia este acuerdo?

I raise the following question: to what extent does this agreement benefit us?

Por consiguiente, se deduce que...

Therefore, it follows that...

📌 Nota gramatical

Formal logical connector used to deliver a definitive inference.

Ejemplo

Por consiguiente, se deduce que debemos cambiar de estrategia.

Therefore, it follows that we must change our strategy.

Para redondear el argumento, cabe añadir que...

To round off the argument, it is worth adding that...

📌 Nota gramatical

Polished formula for delivering a final supporting point or closing punchline.

Ejemplo

Para redondear el argumento, cabe añadir que el impacto será positivo.

To round off the argument, it is worth adding that the impact will be positive.
