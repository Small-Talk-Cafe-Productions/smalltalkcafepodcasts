dilema

dilemma

📌 Nota gramatical

Noun ending in -ma of Greek origin that takes the masculine article 'el'.

Ejemplo

conciencia

conscience / consciousness

📌 Nota gramatical

Feminine noun. Not to be confused with 'conocimiento' (knowledge).

Ejemplo

razonamiento

reasoning

📌 Nota gramatical

Derived from the verb 'razonar'. Commonly used in academic discussions.

Ejemplo

sesgo

bias

📌 Nota gramatical

Often used in philosophical and sociological critiques regarding objectivity.

Ejemplo

premisa

premise

📌 Nota gramatical

Fundamental term in formal logic and argumentation.

Ejemplo

postura

stance / position

📌 Nota gramatical

Used both for physical posture and abstract ideological or philosophical positions.

Ejemplo

juicio

judgment

📌 Nota gramatical

Commonly paired with verbs like 'emitir' (to issue) or 'suspender' (to suspend).

Ejemplo

libertad

freedom / liberty

📌 Nota gramatical

Abstract noun that can appear in the plural ('libertades') when referring to civil liberties.

Ejemplo

justicia

justice

📌 Nota gramatical

Uncountable noun in this abstract philosophical sense.

Ejemplo

valor

value / courage

📌 Nota gramatical

Has a dual meaning: moral value or courage, depending on context.

Ejemplo

deber

duty / obligation

📌 Nota gramatical

Can function as a noun (duty) or as an auxiliary verb (to have to).

Ejemplo

verdad

truth

📌 Nota gramatical

Often contrasted with 'mentira' (lie) or 'apariencia' (appearance).

Ejemplo

cuestionar

to question / to call into question

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Takes direct object without a preposition.

Ejemplo

reflexionar

to reflect / to ponder

📌 Nota gramatical

Usually followed by the preposition 'sobre'.

Ejemplo

argumentar

to argue / to reason

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb used heavily in academic and philosophical discourse.

Ejemplo

coherente

coherent / consistent

📌 Nota gramatical

Adjective ending in -e; does not change for gender, only adds -s for plural.

Ejemplo

subjetivo

subjective

📌 Nota gramatical

Adjective. Feminine form is 'subjetiva'.

Ejemplo

objetivo

objective

📌 Nota gramatical

Can also function as a noun meaning 'goal' or 'objective'.

Ejemplo

ético

ethical

📌 Nota gramatical

Adjective with accent mark. Feminine form is 'ética'.

Ejemplo

contradictorio

contradictory

📌 Nota gramatical

Adjective. Feminine form is 'contradictoria'.

Ejemplo

desde mi punto de vista

from my point of view

📌 Nota gramatical

Standard B2 expression used to introduce personal opinions politely.

Ejemplo

poner en duda

to call into question / to doubt

📌 Nota gramatical

Common idiomatic collocation in formal discussions.

Ejemplo

llegar a la conclusión

to reach the conclusion

📌 Nota gramatical

Followed by 'de que' + clause when stating the specific conclusion.

Ejemplo

carecer de sentido

to make no sense / to lack meaning

📌 Nota gramatical

The verb 'carecer' always requires the preposition 'de'.

Ejemplo

to take into account

Extremely common collocation used in both everyday and academic Spanish.
