ley

law

📌 Nota gramatical

Notice that the plural form 'leyes' requires an 'es' ending, which is standard for nouns ending in a consonant.

Ejemplo

multa

fine

📌 Nota gramatical

Commonly used with verbs like 'poner' (to give/issue) or 'pagar' (to pay).

Ejemplo

juicio

trial, court case

📌 Nota gramatical

Can also mean 'judgment' or 'opinion' in a broader psychological or philosophical sense.

Ejemplo

abogado

lawyer, attorney

📌 Nota gramatical

The feminine form is 'la abogada' (plural: 'las abogadas').

Ejemplo

contrato

contract

📌 Nota gramatical

Often used with verbs like 'firmar' (to sign) and 'cancelar' (to cancel).

Ejemplo

denuncia

complaint, police report

📌 Nota gramatical

Collocates strongly with the verb 'presentar' or 'poner' when dealing with legal or police procedures.

Ejemplo

requisito

requirement

📌 Nota gramatical

Often followed by the preposition 'para' to state what the requirement is needed for.

Ejemplo

trámite

administrative procedure, paperwork

📌 Nota gramatical

Essential bureaucratic noun. 'Hacer los trámites' means to go through the necessary procedures.

Ejemplo

documento

document

📌 Nota gramatical

A fundamental word in any bureaucratic or legal context.

Ejemplo

firma

signature

📌 Nota gramatical

Do not confuse with the verb form 'firma' (he/you formal signs), though they share the same root.

Ejemplo

formulario

form, questionnaire

📌 Nota gramatical

In Spain, 'rellenar un formulario' is commonly used; in Latin America, 'llenar un formulario' is equally frequent.

Ejemplo

cita

appointment

📌 Nota gramatical

Can also mean a date (romantic) or a literary quote depending on the context.

Ejemplo

multar

to fine

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb directly related to the noun 'multa'.

Ejemplo

firmar

to sign

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb used heavily in administrative and commercial contexts.

Ejemplo

renovar

to renew

📌 Nota gramatical

Stem-changing verb (o > ue) in present tense: renuevo, renuevas, renueva...

Ejemplo

denunciar

to report (to the police), to denounce

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb used when reporting crimes or illegal acts.

Ejemplo

cumplir

to comply with, to fulfill

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb. Often followed by the preposition 'con' when meaning 'to comply with'.

Ejemplo

prohibido

prohibited, forbidden

📌 Nota gramatical

Past participle used as an adjective; changes form for gender and number (prohibited/prohibiteds).

Ejemplo

legal

legal

📌 Nota gramatical

Adjective ending in a consonant, so the singular form applies to both masculine and feminine nouns.

Ejemplo

obligatorio

mandatory, compulsory

📌 Nota gramatical

Adjective that agrees with the noun it modifies (obligatorio/obligatoria).

Ejemplo

taxa

fee, tax (regional/official)

📌 Nota gramatical

Often refers to administrative or official public service fees.

Ejemplo

demanda

lawsuit, demand

📌 Nota gramatical

Can mean a legal lawsuit or economic demand/market request depending on context.

Ejemplo

sanción

sanction, penalty

📌 Nota gramatical

Plural changes from 'sanción' to 'sanciones', dropping the written accent on the 'o'.

Ejemplo

reclamación

claim, complaint

📌 Nota gramatical

Useful expression: 'hoja de reclamaciones' (official complaint form in shops and restaurants).

Ejemplo

prohibición

ban, prohibition

📌 Nota gramatical

Abstract noun derived from the verb 'prohibir'. Plural shifts accent: prohibición -> prohibiciones.
