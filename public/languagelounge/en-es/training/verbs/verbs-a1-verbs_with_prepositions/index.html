Verbs with Prepositions

Frases de ejemplo

Etiquetas:

← → Flechas para navegar · A para escuchar

pensar

to think about

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb, stem-changing e>ie, takes preposition 'en'.

Ejemplo

Pienso en mi familia.

I think about my family.

empezar

to start to

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb, stem-changing e>ie, takes preposition 'a' before an infinitive.

Ejemplo

Empiezo a estudiar español.

I start to study Spanish.

aprender

to learn to

📌 Nota gramatical

-er verb, takes preposition 'a' before an infinitive.

Ejemplo

Aprendo a hablar español.

I learn to speak Spanish.

ayudar

to help to

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb, takes preposition 'a' before an infinitive.

Ejemplo

Ayudo a limpiar la casa.

I help to clean the house.

ir

to go to

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir irregular verb, takes preposition 'a' for destinations.

Ejemplo

Voy a la escuela.

I go to school.

venir

to come from

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir irregular verb, takes preposition 'de' for origin.

Ejemplo

Vengo de España.

I come from Spain.

salir

to leave from / to go out of

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir irregular verb (yo salgo), takes preposition 'de'.

Ejemplo

Salgo de casa ahora.

I leave the house now.

entrar

to enter into

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb, takes preposition 'en' for entering a place.

Ejemplo

Entro en la tienda.

I enter the store.

vivir

to live in

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir verb, takes preposition 'en' for places.

Ejemplo

Vivo en Madrid.

I live in Madrid.

viajar

to travel to

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb, takes preposition 'a' for destinations.

Ejemplo

Viajo a México.

I travel to Mexico.

jugar

to play with

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb, stem-changing o>ue, takes preposition 'con' for instruments/people.

Ejemplo

Juego con mi perro.

I play with my dog.

hablar

to talk with

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb, takes preposition 'con' for companions.

Ejemplo

Hablo con el profesor.

I talk with the teacher.

soñar

to dream about

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb, stem-changing o>ue, takes preposition 'con'.

Ejemplo

Sueño con viajar.

I dream about traveling.

buscar

to look for

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb, does NOT take a preposition in Spanish (unlike English 'for').

Ejemplo

Busco las llaves.

I look for the keys.

mirar

to look at

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb, does NOT take a preposition in Spanish (unlike English 'at').

Ejemplo

Miro la televisión.

I look at the television.

escuchar

to listen to

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb, does NOT take a preposition in Spanish (unlike English 'to').

Ejemplo

Escucho música.

I listen to music.

esperar

to wait for

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb, does NOT take a preposition in Spanish (unlike English 'for').

Ejemplo

Espero el autobús.

I wait for the bus.

depender

to depend on

📌 Nota gramatical

-er verb, takes preposition 'de'.

Ejemplo

Depende del tiempo.

It depends on the weather. (de + el = del)

quedar

to meet up / to stay at

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb, takes preposition 'en' for meeting places or staying.

Ejemplo

Quedamos en el parque.

We meet at the park.

terminar

to finish of / to stop doing

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb, takes preposition 'de' before an infinitive to mean finishing an action.

Ejemplo

Termino de trabajar.

I finish working.

Configurar entrenamiento

Configura tu sesión de entrenamiento.
