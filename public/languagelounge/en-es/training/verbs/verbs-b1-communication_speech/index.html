argumentar

to argue / to reason

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; follows standard first-conjugation endings.

Ejemplo

El político supo argumentar muy bien sus propuestas durante el debate.

The politician knew how to argue his proposals very well during the debate.

charlar

to chat / to talk

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; used frequently for informal conversations.

Ejemplo

Nos gusta charlar un rato mientras tomamos un café por la tarde.

We like to chat for a while while we have a coffee in the afternoon.

comentar

to comment / to discuss

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; often introduces opinions on a specific topic.

Ejemplo

El profesor quiso comentar la noticia principal del periódico de hoy.

The teacher wanted to comment on the main news story of today's newspaper.

comunicar

to communicate

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; used for transmitting messages or information.

Ejemplo

La empresa decidió comunicar los cambios a todo el personal por correo.

The company decided to communicate the changes to all staff by email.

confesar

to confess / to admit

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb with an e>ie stem change in present forms (confieso, confiesas...).

Ejemplo

Te tengo que confesar que no entendí nada de la película.

I have to confess to you that I didn't understand anything about the movie.

convencer

to convince / to persuade

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb, though note the spelling change in yo (convenzo) to keep the /θ/ sound.

Ejemplo

Intenté convencer a mis padres para viajar juntos este verano.

I tried to convince my parents to travel together this summer.

criticar

to criticize

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; changes c to qu before 'e' in preterite (critiqué).

Ejemplo

Es muy fácil criticar el trabajo de los demás sin intentarlo.

It is very easy to criticize other people's work without trying it.

declarar

to declare / to state

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; often used in formal or legal contexts.

Ejemplo

El testigo tuvo que declarar ante el juez todo lo que vio.

The witness had to declare everything he saw before the judge.

describir

to describe

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb; irregular participle is 'descrito'.

Ejemplo

El testigo pudo describir perfectamente al ladrón a la policía.

The witness was able to describe the thief perfectly to the police.

discutir

to argue / to discuss

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb; note that in Spanish it often means 'to argue' as well as 'to discuss'.

Ejemplo

Mis hermanos suelen discutir por tonterías todo el tiempo.

My brothers usually argue over silly things all the time.

explicar

to explain

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; c changes to qu before 'e' in preterite (expliqué).

Ejemplo

El profesor tuvo que explicar la lección dos veces para que todos entendieran.

The teacher had to explain the lesson twice so that everyone would understand.

expresar

to express

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; used for conveying thoughts, feelings, or opinions.

Ejemplo

A veces es difícil expresar con palabras lo que uno realmente siente.

Sometimes it is difficult to express in words what one really feels.

informar

to inform

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; typically takes 'sobre' or 'de' for topics.

Ejemplo

El telediario se encarga de informar sobre los últimos sucesos mundiales.

The TV news is responsible for reporting on the latest world events.

insistir

to insist

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb; commonly followed by the preposition 'en'.

Ejemplo

No tienes que insistir tanto; ya te dije que iré a la fiesta.

You don't have to insist so much; I already told you I'll go to the party.

mentir

to lie

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir verb with an e>ie stem change in present (miento...) and e>i in preterite 3rd person (mintió, mintieron).

Ejemplo

Un buen amigo nunca debe mentir sobre cosas importantes.

A good friend should never lie about important things.

negar

to deny

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb with an e>ie stem change in present forms (niego, niegas...) and g>gu spelling change in preterite (negué).

Ejemplo

El político intentó negar todas las acusaciones de corrupción.

The politician tried to deny all the corruption accusations.

opinar

to express an opinion / to think

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; often used to ask or state personal viewpoints.

Ejemplo

En esta reunión todos tienen derecho a opinar sobre el proyecto.

In this meeting everyone has the right to give their opinion on the project.

prometer

to promise

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb; used for commitments and assurances.

Ejemplo

Te prometo que terminaré el informe antes de que termine el día.

I promise you that I will finish the report before the day ends.

recomendar

to recommend

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb with an e>ie stem change in present forms (recomiendo, recomiendas...).

Ejemplo

El médico me recomendó descansar en casa durante un par de días.

The doctor recommended that I rest at home for a couple of days.

saludar

to greet

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; used for polite greetings and acknowledgments.

Ejemplo

Siempre saluda muy amablemente a todos los vecinos por la mañana.

He always greets all the neighbors very politely in the morning.
