Giving & Taking

Dar y Tomar • B1 • 20 tarjetas

dar

to give

📌 Nota gramatical

Third-conjugation verb (-ir historically, irregular -ar conjugation), highly irregular in presente (doy) and pretérito indefinido (di, diste...).

Ejemplo

¿Puedes darme tu opinión sobre el nuevo proyecto de la oficina?

Can you give me your opinion about the new office project?

ofrecer

to offer

📌 Nota gramatical

Second-conjugation verb (-er) with a 'zco' spelling change in the first-person singular of the present tense (ofrezco).

Ejemplo

La empresa decidió ofrecer un curso gratuito de inglés a todos los empleados.

The company decided to offer a free English course to all employees.

regalar

to give (as a gift) / to present

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular first-conjugation verb (-ar).

Ejemplo

Quiero regalar un libro interesante a mi hermana por su cumpleaños.

I want to give an interesting book as a gift to my sister for her birthday.

entregar

to hand over / to deliver

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular first-conjugation verb (-ar) with a spelling change in the preterite (yo entregé -> entregé is incorrect; it is entregé with a g/gu adjustment? Wait, spelling change is gu to g before e: entregué).

Ejemplo

Tienes que entregar el informe final antes de las cinco de la tarde.

You have to hand over the final report before five in the afternoon.

donar

to donate

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular first-conjugation verb (-ar).

Ejemplo

Decidí donar la ropa que ya no uso a una organización benéfica.

I decided to donate the clothes I no longer wear to a charitable organization.

contribuir

to contribute

📌 Nota gramatical

Third-conjugation verb (-uir) requiring a 'y' before endings that do not start with 'i' (contribuye, contribuyen).

Ejemplo

Cada miembro del equipo debe contribuir con nuevas ideas para el proyecto.

Each team member must contribute with new ideas for the project.

prestar

to lend

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular first-conjugation verb (-ar). Often used with indirect object pronouns (e.g., prestar atención, prestar dinero).

Ejemplo

¿Me puedes prestar tu paraguas porque parece que va a llover fuerte?

Can you lend me your umbrella because it looks like it's going to rain hard?

asignar

to assign / to allocate

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular first-conjugation verb (-ar).

Ejemplo

El profesor decidió asignar una tarea diferente a cada grupo de alumnos.

The teacher decided to assign a different task to each group of students.

compartir

to share

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular third-conjugation verb (-ir).

Ejemplo

Es una buena costumbre compartir los gastos cuando viajamos con amigos.

It is a good habit to share expenses when we travel with friends.

transmitir

to transmit / to pass on

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular third-conjugation verb (-ir).

Ejemplo

Los abuelos suelen transmitir sus historias y tradiciones a los más jóvenes.

Grandparents usually pass on their stories and traditions to the youngest ones.

tomar

to take / to have (food or drink)

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular first-conjugation verb (-ar). Very common for consuming food/drink or taking decisions/transport.

Ejemplo

Solemos tomar un café juntos todas las mañanas antes de empezar a trabajar.

We usually have a coffee together every morning before starting work.

coger

to take / to catch / to grab

📌 Nota gramatical

Second-conjugation verb (-er) with a spelling change in the first-person singular of the present tense (cojo, due to g -> j before o).

Ejemplo

Tengo que coger el autobús de las ocho si quiero llegar puntual a la cita.

I have to catch the eight o'clock bus if I want to arrive punctual to the appointment.

recibir

to receive

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular third-conjugation verb (-ir).

Ejemplo

Ayer recibí una carta muy importante de la universidad sobre mi beca.

Yesterday I received a very important letter from the university about my scholarship.

quitar

to remove / to take away

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular first-conjugation verb (-ar). Often used reflexively (quitarse) for taking off clothing.

Ejemplo

Por favor, puedes quitar los platos sucios de la mesa cuando termines de cenar.

Please, can you remove the dirty plates from the table when you finish dining.

aceptar

to accept

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular first-conjugation verb (-ar).

Ejemplo

Después de pensarlo mucho, Carlos decidió aceptar la oferta de trabajo en Madrid.

After thinking about it a lot, Carlos decided to accept the job offer in Madrid.

adoptar

to adopt

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular first-conjugation verb (-ar).

Ejemplo

Mis amigos decidieron adoptar un perro abandonado de la protectora de animales.

My friends decided to adopt an abandoned dog from the animal shelter.

heredar

to inherit

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular first-conjugation verb (-ar).

Ejemplo

Ana heredó una casa antigua y hermosa en el campo de sus abuelos.

Ana inherited an old and beautiful house in the countryside from her grandparents.

apropiarse

to appropriate / to take possession of

📌 Nota gramatical

First-conjugation reflexive verb (-ar). Requires reflexive pronouns (me, te, se, nos, os, se) and preposition 'de'.

Ejemplo

Alguien se apropió de mi paraguas en la entrada de la biblioteca.

Someone took possession of my umbrella at the library entrance.

devolver

to return / to give back

📌 Nota gramatical

Second-conjugation stem-changing verb (o -> ue: devuelvo, devuelves...).

Ejemplo

Recuerda devolver el libro a la biblioteca antes de que termine la semana.

Remember to return the book to the library before the week ends.

aprovechar

to take advantage of / to make the most of

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular first-conjugation verb (-ar). Often used reflexively as 'aprovecharse de' to take unfair advantage.

Ejemplo

Debes aprovechar esta oportunidad única para estudiar un año en el extranjero.

You must take advantage of this unique opportunity to study a year abroad.
