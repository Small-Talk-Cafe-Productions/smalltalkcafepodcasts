Social Change

reivindicar

to claim, to demand rights

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb used heavily in socio-political contexts to demand rights or vindicate causes.

Ejemplo

Los manifestantes salieron a la calle para reivindicar mejores condiciones laborales y sociales.

The protesters took to the street to demand better working and social conditions.

concienciar

to raise awareness

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; often takes the preposition 'sobre' when introducing the topic of awareness.

Ejemplo

Las ONG organizan talleres para concienciar a los jóvenes sobre el cambio climático.

NGOs organize workshops to raise young people's awareness about climate change.

marginar

to marginalize

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb used when discussing social exclusion and discrimination.

Ejemplo

La pobreza extrema suele marginar a los sectores más vulnerables de la población urbana.

Extreme poverty tends to marginalize the most vulnerable sectors of the urban population.

subvencionar

to subsidize, to fund

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb referring to financial support for social programs or public services.

Ejemplo

El gobierno decidió subvencionar los programas de integración para inmigrantes.

The government decided to subsidize integration programs for immigrants.

erradicar

to eradicate

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb with spelling change in preterite yo (c -> qu: erradiqué).

Ejemplo

La principal prioridad del nuevo plan de desarrollo es erradicar la pobreza extrema.

The main priority of the new development plan is to eradicate extreme poverty.

protestar

to protest

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; often used with 'contra' to denote opposition to policies.

Ejemplo

Cientos de ciudadanos salieron a protestar contra la subida de los impuestos.

Hundreds of citizens went out to protest against the tax increase.

cofinanciar

to co-finance

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; compound of financiar, used in institutional cooperation.

Ejemplo

La Unión Europea va a cofinanciar varios proyectos de desarrollo sostenible.

The European Union is going to co-finance several sustainable development projects.

promover

to promote

📌 Nota gramatical

-er verb with o>ue stem change in present (promuevo, promueves...). Regular past tenses.

Ejemplo

Las nuevas leyes buscan promover la igualdad de oportunidades en el ámbito laboral.

The new laws seek to promote equal opportunities in the workplace.

reinsertar

to reintegrate (socially/professionally)

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb used in contexts of prisoner rehabilitation and social reentry.

Ejemplo

Los centros penitenciarios deben esforzarse por reinsertar a los presos en la sociedad.

Penitentiary centers must strive to reintegrate prisoners into society.

manifestarse

to demonstrate, to protest (reflexive)

📌 Nota gramatical

Reflexive -ar verb; requires reflexive pronouns (me, te, se, nos, os, se).

Ejemplo

Miles de estudiantes se manifestaron pacíficamente frente al ministerio de educación.

Thousands of students demonstrated peacefully in front of the ministry of education.

apoyar

to support, to back

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb used to express backing for causes, groups, or candidates.

Ejemplo

La fundación apoya iniciativas locales para mejorar el acceso al agua potable.

The foundation supports local initiatives to improve access to drinking water.

desarrollar

to develop

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; often applied to communities, programs, or human capacities.

Ejemplo

El programa busca desarrollar las habilidades de liderazgo entre los jóvenes rurales.

The program seeks to develop leadership skills among rural youth.

integrar

to integrate

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb frequently used in discussions about multiculturalism and social inclusion.

Ejemplo

La escuela desempeña un papel clave para integrar a los niños de diversas procedencias.

The school plays a key role in integrating children from diverse backgrounds.

impulsar

to boost, to drive, to promote

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb used metaphorically to drive forward social initiatives or economic growth.

Ejemplo

El ayuntamiento quiere impulsar medidas ecológicas para reducir la contaminación.

The city council wants to drive ecological measures to reduce pollution.

repartir

to distribute, to share out

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb frequently used when talking about wealth redistribution or aid delivery.

Ejemplo

Los voluntarios pasaron la mañana a repartir alimentos entre las familias sin recursos.

The volunteers spent the morning distributing food among destitute families.

rebelarse

to rebel (reflexive)

📌 Nota gramatical

Reflexive -ar verb; often takes preposition 'contra' to denote rebellion against injustice.

Ejemplo

Los ciudadanos se rebelaron contra las injustas medidas de austeridad económica.

The citizens rebelled against the unfair economic austerity measures.

visibilizar

to make visible, to bring to light

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb very common in modern social movements to highlight hidden issues.

Ejemplo

Esta campaña pretende visibilizar los problemas de salud mental en el entorno laboral.

This campaign aims to bring mental health problems in the workplace to light.

financiar

to finance, to fund

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb with written accent on the 'i' in present/imperfect endings (financiáis, etc.).

Ejemplo

Un grupo de inversores privados va a financiar la creación de viviendas sociales.

A group of private investors is going to fund the creation of social housing.

reivindicarse

to vindicate oneself, to redeem oneself

📌 Nota gramatical

Reflexive -ar verb used when a group or individual asserts their dignity or worth.

Ejemplo

El sector cultural busca reivindicarse tras años de recortes presupuestarios.

The cultural sector seeks to redeem itself after years of budget cuts.

reclamar

to demand, to call for

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb frequently used in formal complaints and social demands.

Ejemplo

Los sindicatos salieron a reclamar mejores medidas de seguridad en las obras.

The unions came out to demand better safety measures at construction sites.
