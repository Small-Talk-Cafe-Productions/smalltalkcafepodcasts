comparar

to compare

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; takes the auxiliary 'haber' in compound tenses.

Ejemplo

Es fundamental comparar las dos propuestas antes de tomar una decisión definitiva.

It is essential to compare the two proposals before making a definitive decision.

analizar

to analyze

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; takes the auxiliary 'haber' in compound tenses.

Ejemplo

El comité necesita analizar todos los informes financieros antes de la reunión de mañana.

The committee needs to analyze all financial reports before tomorrow's meeting.

diferenciar

to differentiate

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; often used reflexively ('diferenciarse de').

Ejemplo

Es difícil diferenciar entre la información verídica y las noticias falsas en la red.

It is difficult to differentiate between true information and fake news on the web.

contrastar

to contrast

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; used to highlight differences between two elements.

Ejemplo

El periodista quiso contrastar las declaraciones del político con los hechos reales.

The journalist wanted to contrast the politician's statements with the actual facts.

evaluar

to evaluate

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb with a weak diphthong accentuation pattern in certain forms (evalúo).

Ejemplo

El profesor va a evaluar el progreso de los alumnos al final de cada trimestre.

The teacher is going to evaluate the students' progress at the end of each term.

examinar

to examine

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; used both for physical examination and detailed intellectual inspection.

Ejemplo

El especialista se tomó su tiempo para examinar la radiografía con detalle.

The specialist took his time to examine the X-ray in detail.

concluir

to conclude

📌 Nota gramatical

-uir verb; adds 'y' between root and vowel in certain forms (concluye, concluyen).

Ejemplo

Tras estudiar las pruebas, los investigadores pudieron concluir que el sistema era vulnerable.

After studying the evidence, the researchers were able to conclude that the system was vulnerable.

deducir

to deduce

📌 Nota gramatical

-cir verb with 'zc' in first-person singular present (deduzco); irregular preterite (deduje).

Ejemplo

Por las huellas en el suelo, pudimos deducir que alguien había entrado por la ventana.

From the footprints on the floor, we could deduce that someone had entered through the window.

inferir

to infer

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir verb with e>ie stem change in present (infieres) and e>i in preterite (infirió, infirieron).

Ejemplo

A partir de sus gestos, el psicólogo pudo inferir que el paciente estaba muy nervioso.

From his gestures, the psychologist was able to infer that the patient was very nervous.

asimilar

to assimilate / to absorb

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; used for concepts, data, or cultural integration.

Ejemplo

Los estudiantes necesitaron varios días para asimilar toda la información del seminario.

The students needed several days to assimilate all the information from the seminar.

categorizar

to categorize

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; orthographic change z>c before 'e' (categoricé).

Ejemplo

El especialista se encargó de categorizar los documentos según su nivel de urgencia.

The specialist was in charge of categorizing the documents according to their level of urgency.

sintetizar

to synthesize / to summarize

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; z>c orthographic change in preterite (sinteticé).

Ejemplo

El ponente logró sintetizar dos horas de conferencia en diez minutos muy claros.

The speaker managed to synthesize two hours of lecture into ten very clear minutes.

desglosar

to break down / to itemize

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; typically used for breaking down data, costs, or arguments.

Ejemplo

El director prefirió desglosar el presupuesto total en varias partidas más pequeñas.

The director preferred to break down the total budget into several smaller items.

correlacionar

to correlate

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; often used in statistics and scientific analysis.

Ejemplo

El investigador intentó correlacionar el nivel de estrés con los hábitos de sueño.

The researcher tried to correlate the stress level with sleeping habits.

contraponer

to pit against / to contrast / to juxtapose

📌 Nota gramatical

Compound of 'poner'; irregular 'go' verb in present (contrapongo), irregular preterite (contrapuse).

Ejemplo

El ensayo busca contraponer las ideas de dos filósofos del siglo dieciocho.

The essay seeks to juxtapose the ideas of two eighteenth-century philosophers.

equiparar

to equate / to compare

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; often used with 'a' or 'con' when equating two different things.

Ejemplo

Sería un grave error equiparar el crecimiento económico con el bienestar social.

It would be a serious mistake to equate economic growth with social well-being.

interpretar

to interpret

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; used for data, texts, artistic performance, or signs.

Ejemplo

Diferentes expertos pueden interpretar los mismos resultados de maneras muy distintas.

Different experts can interpret the same results in very different ways.

discernir

to discern

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb; often used in formal or intellectual contexts regarding subtle differences.

Ejemplo

A veces resulta complicado discernir la verdad entre tanta información contradictoria.

Sometimes it turns out complicated to discern the truth among so much contradictory information.

ponderar

to weigh / to ponder / to evaluate

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; used for carefully weighing pros, cons, or factors.

Ejemplo

El juez debe ponderar cuidadosamente todas las pruebas antes de dictar sentencia.

The judge must carefully weigh all the evidence before passing sentence.

verificar

to verify

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; orthographic change c>qu before 'e' (verifiqué).

Ejemplo

Es fundamental verificar la autenticidad de los datos antes de publicar el artículo.

It is essential to verify the authenticity of the data before publishing the article.
