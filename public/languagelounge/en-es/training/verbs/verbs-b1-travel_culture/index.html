alojarse

to stay / to lodge

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar) reflexive verb. Requires the preposition 'en' to specify the accommodation.

Ejemplo

¿Prefieres alojarte en un hotel céntrico o en un hostal rural?

Do you prefer to stay in a central hotel or a rural hostel?

explorar

to explore

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular first conjugation (-ar) verb.

Ejemplo

Mañana saldremos temprano para explorar el casco histórico de la ciudad.

Tomorrow we will leave early to explore the historical center of the city.

visitar

to visit

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular first conjugation (-ar) verb used frequently for tourist attractions and monuments.

Ejemplo

Visitamos varios monumentos antiguos durante nuestras vacaciones en Roma.

We visited several ancient monuments during our vacation in Rome.

fotografiar

to photograph

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular first conjugation (-ar) verb with an acute accent on the 'i' in certain forms to maintain the diphthong.

Ejemplo

Me encanta fotografiar los paisajes naturales cuando subo a la montaña.

I love to photograph natural landscapes when I climb the mountain.

degustar

to taste / to sample

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular first conjugation (-ar) verb, commonly used for tasting local cuisine or wine.

Ejemplo

Es una gran experiencia degustar platos típicos en los mercados locales.

It is a great experience to taste typical dishes in the local markets.

desplazarse

to get around / to travel (locally)

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar) reflexive verb used when moving from one place to another within a city or region.

Ejemplo

Se puede desplazar fácilmente en metro por toda la capital.

You can get around easily by metro throughout the capital.

hacerse

to become / to get (accustomed)

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular second conjugation (-er) reflexive verb (derived from hacer). Expresses a change in state or culture integration.

Ejemplo

Al principio cuesta adaptarse, pero pronto te haces a las costumbres locales.

At first it is hard to adapt, but soon you get used to local customs.

organizar

to organize / to plan

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular first conjugation (-ar) verb with a spelling change in preterite (c -> qu before e).

Ejemplo

Organizamos una excursión guiada para visitar las ruinas mayas.

We organized a guided excursion to visit the Mayan ruins.

apreciar

to appreciate

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular first conjugation (-ar) verb, ideal for expressing opinions on art and culture.

Ejemplo

Desde este mirador se puede apreciar toda la belleza del valle.

From this viewpoint you can appreciate all the beauty of the valley.

conocer

to know / to get to know / to meet

📌 Nota gramatical

Second conjugation (-er) verb with a 'zco' spelling change in the first-person singular present ('conozco').

Ejemplo

Quiero conocer las tradiciones de este país antes de regresar a casa.

I want to get to know the traditions of this country before returning home.

recomendar

to recommend

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar) stem-changing verb (e > ie in present forms except nosotros/vosotros).

Ejemplo

El recepcionista nos recomendó un restaurante excelente cerca de la plaza.

The receptionist recommended an excellent restaurant near the square.

costar

to cost / to be difficult

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar) stem-changing verb (o > uei / o > ue in present forms). Commonly used for prices and effort.

Ejemplo

Los billetes de avión para viajar en verano suelen costar mucho más.

Plane tickets to travel in summer usually cost a lot more.

durar

to last

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular first conjugation (-ar) verb used to describe the duration of trips, events, or guided tours.

Ejemplo

La visita guiada por el museo duró aproximadamente dos horas.

The guided tour of the museum lasted approximately two hours.

incluir

to include

📌 Nota gramatical

Third conjugation (-ir) verb with a yod spelling insertion (i -> y) in third-person singular/plural forms.

Ejemplo

El precio de la excursión incluye el transporte y la entrada al monumento.

The price of the excursion includes transport and the entrance to the monument.

cruzar

to cross

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular first conjugation (-ar) verb with a spelling change in preterite (z -> c before e).

Ejemplo

Cruzamos la frontera a pie para entrar en el país vecino.

We crossed the border on foot to enter the neighboring country.

perderse

to get lost

📌 Nota gramatical

Third conjugation (-ir) stem-changing reflexive verb (e > ie). Often used metaphorically for wandering aimlessly in travel.

Ejemplo

Me gusta perderse por las callejuelas estrechas de los pueblos antiguos.

I like to get lost through the narrow alleys of ancient towns.

disfrutar

to enjoy

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular first conjugation (-ar) verb. Usually takes the preposition 'de' when followed by a noun or activity.

Ejemplo

Disfrutamos muchísimo de las playas paradisíacas durante el verano.

We enjoyed the paradisiacal beaches very much during the summer.

apuntar

to note down / to point

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular first conjugation (-ar) verb used for writing down tips, addresses, or travel recommendations.

Ejemplo

Apunté la dirección del restaurante que me recomendaste en la libreta.

I noted down the address of the restaurant you recommended to me in the notebook.

probar

to try / to taste

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar) stem-changing verb (o > ue in present forms).

Ejemplo

Tienes que probar la paella tradicional cuando visites la costa española.

You have to try the traditional paella when you visit the Spanish coast.

recorrer

to travel around / to tour / to cover distance

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular second conjugation (-er) verb used for traveling across a country, region, or city.

Ejemplo

Recorrimos todo el norte de España en coche durante las vacaciones de verano.

We traveled all over northern Spain by car during the summer holidays.
