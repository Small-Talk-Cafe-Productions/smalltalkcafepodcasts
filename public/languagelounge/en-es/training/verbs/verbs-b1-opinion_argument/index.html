opinar

to think / to give an opinion

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Followed by 'que' for subordinate clauses of opinion.

Ejemplo

¿Qué opinas sobre la nueva ley de educación en nuestro país?

What is your opinion on the new education law in our country?

creer

to believe / to think

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb. Often used with the subjunctive in negative forms (no creo que...).

Ejemplo

Creo que este proyecto tendrá mucho éxito en el futuro.

I believe that this project will have a lot of success in the future.

pensar

to think / to intend

📌 Nota gramatical

Stem-changing -ar verb (e > ie). Note the spelling and stem shift.

Ejemplo

¿Qué piensas sobre la propuesta que presentaron ayer en la oficina?

What do you think about the proposal they presented yesterday at the office?

argumentar

to argue / to reason

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Essential for formal debates and structured arguments.

Ejemplo

El abogado supo argumentar muy bien para defender a su cliente.

The lawyer knew how to argue very well to defend his client.

defender

to defend / to uphold

📌 Nota gramatical

Stem-changing -er verb (e > ie). Used when defending a standpoint or argument.

Ejemplo

Siempre debes defender tus derechos cuando consideres que es injusto.

You must always defend your rights when you consider it is unfair.

rechazar

to reject / to turn down

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Used to express disagreement or rejection of an argument.

Ejemplo

Los trabajadores decidieron rechazar la oferta de la empresa.

The workers decided to reject the company's offer.

apoyar

to support / to back

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Used to show agreement or backing for someone's view.

Ejemplo

Apoyo totalmente la decisión de organizar más actividades culturales.

I totally support the decision to organize more cultural activities.

convenir

to be advisable / to suit

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -ir verb (conjugated like venir). Often used impersonally (conviene que + subjuntivo).

Ejemplo

Conviene que hablemos con el director antes de tomar una decisión.

It is advisable that we talk to the director before making a decision.

discutir

to discuss / to argue

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb. In Spanish, 'discutir' can mean both friendly academic discussion and heated arguing.

Ejemplo

Es importante discutir este asunto con calma en la reunión de mañana.

It is important to discuss this matter calmly in tomorrow's meeting.

demostrar

to demonstrate / to prove

📌 Nota gramatical

Stem-changing -ar verb (o > u). Used when providing proof for an argument.

Ejemplo

El científico demostró con datos que su teoría era correcta.

The scientist demonstrated with data that his theory was correct.

proponer

to propose / to suggest

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -er verb (conjugated like poner). Used to offer suggestions in arguments.

Ejemplo

Propongo que hagamos una pausa de diez minutos para reflexionar.

I propose that we take a ten-minute break to reflect.

dudar

to doubt

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Often triggers the subjunctive in negative forms (dudo que...).

Ejemplo

Dudo que terminen el informe antes del viernes próximo.

I doubt that they will finish the report before next Friday.

estar de acuerdo

to agree

📌 Nota gramatical

Idiomatic expression with 'estar'. Followed by 'con' or 'en que'.

Ejemplo

Estoy totalmente de acuerdo con lo que acabas de decir.

I totally agree with what you just said.

convencer

to convince / to persuade

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb, except for a spelling change in yo (convencer > convenzo) to preserve the /θ/ sound.

Ejemplo

Intenté convencer a mis padres de que el viaje era seguro.

I tried to convince my parents that the trip was safe.

reconocer

to recognize / to admit

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -er verb (conjugated like conocer, with -zc- in 1st person singular). Used to admit points in an argument.

Ejemplo

Debo reconocer que cometí un error en los cálculos del presupuesto.

I must admit that I made a mistake in the budget calculations.

parecer

to seem / to appear

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -er verb (conjugated like conocer, with -zc-). Often used with indirect objects (me parece que...).

Ejemplo

Me parece que esta estrategia no dará buenos resultados a largo plazo.

It seems to me that this strategy will not yield good long-term results.

insistir

to insist

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb. Usually followed by the preposition 'en' (insistir en que...).

Ejemplo

Insisto en que debemos revisar el contrato antes de firmarlo.

I insist that we must review the contract before signing it.

consistir

to consist

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb. Always followed by the preposition 'en' when explaining what something consists of.

Ejemplo

El problema principal consiste en la falta de comunicación entre departamentos.

The main problem consists of the lack of communication between departments.

suponer

to suppose / to assume

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -er verb (conjugated like poner). Used to express assumptions or logical deductions.

Ejemplo

Supongo que llegarán tarde debido al mal tiempo en la carretera.

I suppose they will arrive late due to the bad weather on the road.

depender

to depend

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb. Always followed by the preposition 'depender de'.

Ejemplo

Todo depende de cómo se desarrollen las negociaciones esta semana.

Everything depends on how the negotiations develop this week.
