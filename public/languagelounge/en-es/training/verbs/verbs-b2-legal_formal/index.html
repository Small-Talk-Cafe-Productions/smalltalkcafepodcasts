demandar

to sue / to demand

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; often used in civil litigation contexts.

Ejemplo

La empresa decidió demandar al proveedor por incumplimiento de contrato.

The company decided to sue the supplier for breach of contract.

firmar

to sign

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; used for treaties, agreements, and official documents.

Ejemplo

Ambas partes van a firmar el acuerdo definitivo mañana por la mañana.

Both parties are going to sign the final agreement tomorrow morning.

denunciar

to report / to denounce

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; used in legal reporting of crimes or regulatory violations.

Ejemplo

Los empleados decidieron denunciar las irregularidades laborales ante la inspección.

The employees decided to report the labor irregularities to the inspection office.

estipular

to stipulate

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; highly formal, used for contractual clauses.

Ejemplo

El contrato estipula claramente las condiciones de rescisión anticipada.

The contract clearly stipulates the conditions for early termination.

ratificar

to ratify

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb (spelling change c>qu in preterite: ratifiqué); formal contexts.

Ejemplo

El parlamento debe ratificar el tratado internacional antes de final de año.

Parliament must ratify the international treaty before the end of the year.

reclamar

to claim / to complain

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; used for asserting rights or demanding compensation.

Ejemplo

Los consumidores tienen el derecho de reclamar si el producto está defectuoso.

Consumers have the right to claim if the product is defective.

absolver

to acquit / to absolve

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -er verb; stem-changing o>ue (absuelvo). Used in criminal justice.

Ejemplo

El juez decidió absolver al acusado por falta de pruebas concluyentes.

The judge decided to acquit the defendant due to a lack of conclusive evidence.

condenar

to sentence / to condemn

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; frequently used in judicial sentencing and formal disapproval.

Ejemplo

El magistrado va a condenar al criminal a diez años de prisión.

The magistrate is going to sentence the criminal to ten years in prison.

relevar

to relieve / to exempt

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; used in formal contexts meaning to release from duty or obligation.

Ejemplo

El consejo de administración decidió relevar al director de sus funciones.

The board of directors decided to relieve the director of his duties.

apelar

to appeal

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; used in legal appeals against court decisions.

Ejemplo

La defensa anunció que va a apelar la sentencia dictada por el juez.

The defense announced that it is going to appeal the sentence handed down by the judge.

promulgar

to promulgate / to enact

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb (spelling change g>gu in preterite: promulgué); legislative context.

Ejemplo

El gobierno se dispone a promulgar una nueva ley de protección de datos.

The government is preparing to enact a new data protection law.

consagrar

to enshrine / to consecrate

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; used to describe rights protected by law or constitution.

Ejemplo

La constitución se encarga de consagrar los derechos fundamentales de los ciudadanos.

The constitution is responsible for enshrining the fundamental rights of citizens.

invalidar

to invalidate / to void

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; used for nullifying documents, contracts, or votes.

Ejemplo

Un error de procedimiento puede invalidar todo el proceso electoral.

A procedural error can invalidate the entire electoral process.

dictaminar

to rule / to issue a verdict

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; formal verb used by experts, committees, and judges.

Ejemplo

Los peritos médicos deben dictaminar sobre las secuelas del accidente.

The medical experts must issue a ruling on the aftermath of the accident.

remediar

to remedy / to redress

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; formal use regarding correcting a wrong or breach.

Ejemplo

La empresa dispuso de un mes para remediar el incumplimiento contractual.

The company had one month to remedy the contractual breach.

comparecer

to appear (in court)

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -er verb; -zco in first-person singular (comparezco). Formal legal context.

Ejemplo

El testigo fue citado para comparecer ante el juez el próximo lunes.

The witness was summoned to appear before the judge next Monday.

coaccionar

to coerce

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; used in legal definitions of undue pressure or force.

Ejemplo

Intentaron coaccionar a los trabajadores para que aceptaran las nuevas condiciones.

They tried to coerce the workers into accepting the new conditions.

confiscar

to confiscate / to seize

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb (spelling change c>qu in preterite: confisqué); administrative/legal seizure.

Ejemplo

La policía procedió a confiscar los bienes adquiridos de forma ilícita.

The police proceeded to confiscate the assets acquired illegally.

impugnar

to challenge / to contest

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; used in legal or administrative challenges against a decision or document.

Ejemplo

La oposición decidió impugnar los resultados electorales ante el tribunal.

The opposition decided to contest the electoral results before the court.

transigir

to compromise / to yield

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb; often used in negative constructions meaning not to compromise on legal principles.

Ejemplo

El sindicato dejó claro que no va a transigir en materia de seguridad laboral.

The union made it clear that it will not compromise on occupational safety.
