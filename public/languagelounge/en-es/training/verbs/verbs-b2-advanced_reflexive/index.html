adaptarse

to adapt oneself

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular reflexive verb; takes the preposition 'a' before nouns.

Ejemplo

Es fundamental adaptarse rápidamente a los nuevos cambios en la empresa.

It is essential to adapt quickly to the new changes in the company.

arrepentirse

to regret

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir stem-changing verb (e>ie) reflexive; takes the preposition 'de'.

Ejemplo

Ella se arrepiente profundamente de haber dicho esas palabras hirientes.

She deeply regrets having said those hurtful words.

aprovecharse

to take advantage of

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular reflexive verb; takes the preposition 'de'.

Ejemplo

Algunas personas se aprovechan de la amabilidad de los demás.

Some people take advantage of the kindness of others.

obsesionarse

to become obsessed

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular reflexive verb; takes the preposition 'con'.

Ejemplo

No te obsesiones con los pequeños detalles que nadie va a notar.

Do not obsess over the small details that no one is going to notice.

enfrentarse

to face / to confront

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular reflexive verb; takes the preposition 'a'.

Ejemplo

Hay que enfrentarse a los problemas con valentía y optimismo.

One must face problems with courage and optimism.

negarse

to refuse

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar stem-changing verb (e>ie) reflexive; takes the preposition 'a' before infinitives.

Ejemplo

El testigo se negó a declarar ante el juez por motivos de seguridad.

The witness refused to testify before the judge for security reasons.

quejarse

to complain

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular reflexive verb; takes the preposition 'de'.

Ejemplo

Los clientes se quejaron del mal servicio en el restaurante.

The customers complained about the poor service in the restaurant.

empeñarse

to insist / to be determined

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular reflexive verb; takes the preposition 'en'.

Ejemplo

Se empeñó en terminar el proyecto antes de las vacaciones.

He was determined to finish the project before the holidays.

esforzarse

to strive / to make an effort

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar stem-changing verb (o>ue) reflexive; takes the preposition 'en'.

Ejemplo

Si te esfuerzas lo suficiente, conseguirás aprobar el examen oficial.

If you strive enough, you will manage to pass the official exam.

sorprenderse

to be surprised

📌 Nota gramatical

-er regular reflexive verb; takes the preposition 'de'.

Ejemplo

Nos sorprendimos mucho al ver los resultados de la encuesta.

We were very surprised upon seeing the results of the survey.

equivocarse

to be mistaken / to make a mistake

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular reflexive verb; takes the preposition 'de' when specifying what was wrong.

Ejemplo

Cualquiera puede equivocarse al rellenar un impreso tan largo.

Anyone can make a mistake when filling out such a long form.

acostumbrarse

to get used to

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular reflexive verb; takes the preposition 'a'.

Ejemplo

Te costará acostumbrarte al nuevo horario de invierno.

It will take you a while to get used to the new winter schedule.

encargarse

to take charge of / to be in charge of

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular reflexive verb; takes the preposition 'de'.

Ejemplo

Ella se encarga de coordinar las reuniones semanales del departamento.

She is in charge of coordinating the department's weekly meetings.

fijarse

to notice / to pay attention

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular reflexive verb; takes the preposition 'en'.

Ejemplo

Fíjate bien en los detalles antes de firmar el contrato.

Pay close attention to the details before signing the contract.

atreverse

to dare

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular reflexive verb; takes the preposition 'a'.

Ejemplo

Nunca me atrevería a hablar en público sin prepararme antes.

I would never dare to speak in public without preparing beforehand.

implicarse

to get involved

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular reflexive verb; takes the preposition 'en'.

Ejemplo

Es necesario que todos los empleados se impliquen en este proyecto social.

It is necessary for all employees to get involved in this social project.

conformarse

to settle for / to be content with

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular reflexive verb; takes the preposition 'con'.

Ejemplo

No debes conformarte con resultados mediocres si puedes dar más.

You must not settle for mediocre results if you can give more.

obsesionarse

to become fixated / obsessed

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular reflexive verb; takes the preposition 'con'.

Ejemplo

Los analistas se obsesionan a menudo con las cifras trimestrales.

Analysts often get fixated on quarterly figures.

desentenderse

to wash one's hands of / to ignore responsibility

📌 Nota gramatical

-er irregular stem-changing reflexive verb (conjugates like 'entender' with prefix des-); takes 'de'.

Ejemplo

No puedes desentenderse de tus responsabilidades cuando surgen problemas.

You cannot wash your hands of your responsibilities when problems arise.

apasionarse

to become passionate about

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular reflexive verb; takes the preposition 'por'.

Ejemplo

Desde joven se apasionó por la literatura contemporánea y el arte.

Since his youth he became passionate about contemporary literature and art.
