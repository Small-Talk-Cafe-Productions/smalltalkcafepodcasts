conocer

to know / to meet

📌 Nota gramatical

-er verb. Irregular 'yo' form in present (conozco).

Ejemplo

¿Quieres conocer a mis nuevos amigos?

Do you want to meet my new friends?

saludar

to greet

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb. Uses auxiliary haber in compound tenses.

Ejemplo

Siempre saludo a mis vecinos por la mañana.

I always greet my neighbors in the morning.

invitar

to invite

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb. Often followed by 'a' + infinitive.

Ejemplo

Te invito a mi fiesta de cumpleaños.

I invite you to my birthday party.

charlar

to chat / to talk

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb. Used for informal conversations.

Ejemplo

Me gusta charlar con mis amigos en el café.

I like to chat with my friends at the cafe.

reunirse

to get together / to meet

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar reflexive verb. Stem change e>i in gerund/indefinido? No, regular -ar reflexive.

Ejemplo

Nos reunimos todos los fines de semana.

We get together every weekend.

despedirse

to say goodbye

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir reflexive verb with e>i stem change (despido, despides, etc.).

Ejemplo

Tengo que despedirme de mis padres.

I have to say goodbye to my parents.

agradecer

to thank / to be grateful

📌 Nota gramatical

-er verb with -zco spelling change in the first person singular (agradezco).

Ejemplo

Te agradezco mucho tu gran ayuda.

I thank you very much for your great help.

disculpar

to excuse / to forgive

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb. Often used reflexively as 'disculparse' or imperatively ('disculpa').

Ejemplo

Te pido que me disculpes por llegar tarde.

I ask you to excuse me for arriving late.

felicitar

to congratulate

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb. Used for birthdays, achievements, and holidays.

Ejemplo

Quiero felicitarte por tu nuevo trabajo.

I want to congratulate you on your new job.

apoyar

to support

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb. Used both physically and emotionally for people or ideas.

Ejemplo

Mis amigos siempre me apoyan en todo.

My friends always support me in everything.

confiar

to trust

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb with an accented 'i' in present/imperfect endings (confío, confías).

Ejemplo

Confío plenamente en mi mejor amiga.

I fully trust my best friend.

discutir

to argue / to discuss

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir regular verb. In Spanish, 'discutir' often means to argue or quarrel.

Ejemplo

Mis hermanos siempre discuten por tonterías.

My brothers always argue over silly things.

perdonar

to forgive / to excuse

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb. Used to forgive someone or ask for pardon.

Ejemplo

Es fácil perdonar a un buen amigo.

It is easy to forgive a good friend.

bromear

to joke

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb. Used to describe making jokes or kidding around.

Ejemplo

A mi hermano le encanta bromear siempre.

My brother loves to joke around all the time.

relacionarse

to socialize / to interact

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar reflexive verb. Often followed by the preposition 'con'.

Ejemplo

Es importante relacionarse con gente nueva.

It is important to socialize with new people.

acompañar

to accompany / to go with

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb. Frequently used when going somewhere with someone.

Ejemplo

¿Me acompañas al supermercado?

Will you accompany me to the supermarket?

enfadarse

to get angry / to get upset

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar reflexive verb. Synonym of 'enojarse' (common in Latin America).

Ejemplo

No te enfades por una tontería.

Don't get angry over a silly thing.

coincidir

to coincide / to agree / to meet by chance

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir regular verb. Often used when schedules align or people meet unexpectedly.

Ejemplo

Nuestras opiniones coinciden en este tema.

Our opinions coincide on this topic.

compartir

to share

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir regular verb. Used for sharing objects, moments, or feelings.

Ejemplo

Me gusta compartir tiempo con mi familia.

I like to share time with my family.

festejar

to celebrate

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb. Synonym of 'celebrar', common in many Spanish-speaking regions.

Ejemplo

Vamos a festejar tu cumpleaños este sábado.

We are going to celebrate your birthday this Saturday.
