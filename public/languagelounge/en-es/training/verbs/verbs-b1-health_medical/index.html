curar

to cure / to heal

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb belonging to the first conjugation group.

Ejemplo

El médico espera curar la infección con este nuevo antibiótico.

The doctor hopes to cure the infection with this new antibiotic.

recetar

to prescribe

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb belonging to the first conjugation group.

Ejemplo

La doctora me recetó unas pastillas para calmar el dolor de cabeza.

The female doctor prescribed some pills for me to calm the headache.

operar

to operate (surgery)

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb belonging to the first conjugation group.

Ejemplo

Van a operar a mi abuela de la rodilla la próxima semana.

They are going to operate on my grandmother's knee next week.

examinar

to examine

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb belonging to the first conjugation group.

Ejemplo

El pediatra examinó al niño con mucho cuidado para ver su garganta.

The pediatrician examined the child very carefully to see his throat.

vacunar

to vaccinate / to immunize

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb belonging to the first conjugation group.

Ejemplo

Es obligatorio vacunar a los bebés contra varias enfermedades graves.

It is mandatory to vaccinate babies against several serious illnesses.

sanar

to heal / to recover

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb belonging to the first conjugation group.

Ejemplo

El corte en mi mano tardó varios días en sanar por completo.

The cut on my hand took several days to heal completely.

descansar

to rest

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb belonging to the first conjugation group.

Ejemplo

El médico me recomendó descansar en casa durante al menos tres días.

The doctor recommended that I rest at home for at least three days.

lesionarse

to injure oneself

📌 Nota gramatical

Reflexive -ar verb requiring reflexive pronouns. Stem belongs to first group.

Ejemplo

El delantero se lesionó la pierna derecha durante el último partido.

The striker injured his right leg during the last match.

recuperarse

to recover / to get better

📌 Nota gramatical

Reflexive -ar verb requiring reflexive pronouns. Regular conjugation.

Ejemplo

Mi abuelo se está recuperando lentamente de la operación de cadera.

My grandfather is recovering slowly from the hip surgery.

enfermarse

to get sick

📌 Nota gramatical

Reflexive -ar verb. (Note: 'enfermar' can be used without pronouns, but 'enfermarse' is very common for getting sick).

Ejemplo

Mucha gente suele enfermarse durante los meses más fríos del año.

Many people tend to get sick during the coldest months of the year.

marearse

to feel dizzy / to get motion sickness

📌 Nota gramatical

Reflexive -ar verb. Describes dizziness or motion sickness.

Ejemplo

Empecé a marearme porque el autobús se movía demasiado en la curva.

I started to feel dizzy because the bus was moving too much on the curve.

quejarse

to complain

📌 Nota gramatical

Reflexive -ar verb. Usually followed by the preposition 'de' ('quejarse de').

Ejemplo

El paciente no dejaba de quejarse de un fuerte dolor en el pecho.

The patient kept complaining about a sharp pain in his chest.

duele

it hurts (from doler)

📌 Nota gramatical

Defective/impersonal stem-changing verb (o>ue), mostly used in 3rd person singular ('duele') and plural ('duelen'). Belongs to -er group.

Ejemplo

Me duele la cabeza desde esta mañana porque no he bebido suficiente agua.

My head has hurt since this morning because I haven't drunk enough water.

proteger

to protect

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb, with a spelling change in the first person singular present (protejo) to maintain the sound.

Ejemplo

Es fundamental usar crema solar para proteger la piel contra los rayos ultravioleta.

It is essential to use sunscreen to protect the skin against ultraviolet rays.

prevenir

to prevent

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir verb, compound of 'venir', highly irregular (stem change e>ie in present, etc.).

Ejemplo

Lavarse las manos con frecuencia ayuda a prevenir muchas enfermedades infecciosas.

Washing hands frequently helps to prevent many infectious diseases.

nutrir

to nourish / to feed

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb belonging to the third conjugation group.

Ejemplo

Es necesario nutrir el cuerpo con alimentos frescos y variados todos los días.

It is necessary to nourish the body with fresh and varied foods every day.

sufrir

to suffer / to undergo

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb. Often used with 'de' for illnesses or chronic conditions.

Ejemplo

Mi abuela sufre de hipertensión desde hace más de diez años.

My grandmother has suffered from high blood pressure for more than ten years.

sentir

to feel

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir verb with stem change e>ie in present (siento, sientes...) and e>i in preterit 3rd person (sintió, sintieron).

Ejemplo

Desde ayer por la tarde siento un dolor muy extraño en el abdomen.

Since yesterday afternoon I have been feeling a very strange pain in my abdomen.

medir

to measure

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir verb with stem change e>i in present (mido, mides...) and preterit 3rd person (midió, midieron).

Ejemplo

La enfermera me midió la presión arterial antes de entrar en la consulta.

The nurse measured my blood pressure before entering the consultation room.

dormir

to sleep

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir verb with stem change o>ue in present (duermo, duermes...) and o>u in preterit 3rd person (durmió, durmieron).

Ejemplo

Los especialistas aconsejan dormir al menos ocho horas diarias para mantener una buena salud.

Specialists advise sleeping at least eight hours a day to maintain good health.
