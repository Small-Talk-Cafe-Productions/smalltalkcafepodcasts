argumentar

to argue / to debate

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Follows standard first-conjugation endings.

Ejemplo

El político supo argumentar su postura con datos muy sólidos durante el debate.

The politician knew how to argue his position with very solid data during the debate.

refutar

to refute

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Used frequently in academic and formal debate contexts.

Ejemplo

La científica logró refutar la teoría anterior con un experimento innovador.

The female scientist managed to refute the previous theory with an innovative experiment.

consensuar

to reach a consensus / to agree upon

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Highly useful for professional negotiation and collaborative debate.

Ejemplo

Los directivos tuvieron que consensuar nuevas medidas laborales con los sindicatos.

The directors had to reach a consensus on new labor measures with the unions.

exponer

to present / to outline / to expose

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -er verb. Follows the same pattern as poner (g-stem in present yo, irregular preterit).

Ejemplo

El ponente supo exponer sus ideas principales de forma muy clara y concisa.

The speaker managed to outline his main ideas in a very clear and concise way.

matizar

to nuance / to qualify / to fine-tune (a statement)

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Orthographic change in preterite yo form (c > qu).

Ejemplo

El periodista quiso matizar sus palabras para evitar malentendidos públicos.

The journalist wanted to nuance his words to avoid public misunderstandings.

contradecir

to contradict

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -ir verb. Compounds of decir. Stem-changing e>i and irregular yo form.

Ejemplo

En su discurso, el candidato tendió a contradecirse en varias ocasiones.

In his speech, the candidate tended to contradict himself on several occasions.

persuadir

to persuade

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb. Often followed by 'a + infinitive'.

Ejemplo

El publicista intentó persuadir al público mediante un mensaje muy emocional.

The advertiser tried to persuade the public through a very emotional message.

interrumpir

to interrupt

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb. Essential vocabulary for managing interactive discussions.

Ejemplo

Por favor, dejen terminar al orador antes de interrumpir con preguntas.

Please, let the speaker finish before interrupting with questions.

cuestionar

to question / to call into question

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Useful for expressing skepticism and critical thinking.

Ejemplo

El periodista empezó a cuestionar la veracidad de los informes oficiales.

The journalist began to question the truthfulness of the official reports.

justificar

to justify

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Orthographic change in preterite yo form (c > qu).

Ejemplo

El autor tuvo que justificar cada una de las fuentes citadas en su ensayo.

The author had to justify each of the sources cited in his essay.

rebatir

to rebut / to counter

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb. Very common in structured debates and formal discussions.

Ejemplo

La abogada supo rebatir las acusaciones del fiscal con gran elocuencia.

The female lawyer knew how to rebut the prosecutor's accusations with great eloquence.

concertar

to arrange / to coordinate / to schedule

📌 Nota gramatical

Stem-changing -ar verb (e > ie). Used in professional communication to set meetings.

Ejemplo

La secretaria logró concertar una entrevista con el director general.

The secretary managed to arrange an interview with the general manager.

divulgar

to disseminate / to popularize (information/science)

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Orthographic change in preterite yo form (g > gu).

Ejemplo

La universidad se encarga de divulgar los resultados de sus investigaciones científicas.

The university is responsible for disseminating the results of its scientific research.

expresarse

to express oneself

📌 Nota gramatical

Reflexive regular -ar verb. Requires reflexive pronouns (me, te, se, nos, os, se).

Ejemplo

Aprender un nuevo idioma te permite expresarte con mayor riqueza conceptual.

Learning a new language allows you to express yourself with greater conceptual richness.

convenir

to be advisable / to suit / to agree

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -ir verb. Conjugates like venir. Often used impersonally ('conviene').

Ejemplo

Conviene aclarar estos puntos antes de firmar el contrato definitivo.

It is advisable to clarify these points before signing the final contract.

enfatizar

to emphasize / to stress

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Orthographic change in preterite yo form (c > qu).

Ejemplo

El orador quiso enfatizar la importancia de la educación en el desarrollo social.

The speaker wanted to emphasize the importance of education in social development.

insistir

to insist

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb. Usually followed by 'en + infinitive' or 'en que + subjunctive'.

Ejemplo

A pesar de las críticas, el director insistió en defender su propuesta.

Despite the criticism, the director insisted on defending his proposal.

apoyar

to support / to back

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Used for backing arguments, initiatives, or colleagues.

Ejemplo

Muchos diputados decidieron apoyar la nueva ley de comunicación.

Many deputies decided to support the new communication law.

criticar

to criticize

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Orthographic change in preterite yo form (c > qu).

Ejemplo

Los analistas se dedicaron a criticar la falta de transparencia del proyecto.

The analysts dedicated themselves to criticizing the project's lack of transparency.

coincidir

to coincide / to agree (in opinion)

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb. Often followed by 'con' (someone) or 'en' (on a point).

Ejemplo

En este aspecto fundamental, tengo que coincidir plenamente con tu punto de vista.

On this fundamental aspect, I have to fully agree with your point of view.
