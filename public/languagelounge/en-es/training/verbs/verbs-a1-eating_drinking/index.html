Eating & Drinking

Comer y Beber • A1 • 20 tarjetas

Estructura

Frases de ejemplo

Etiquetas:

← → Flechas para navegar · A para escuchar

comer

to eat

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb. Takes 'haber' in compound tenses.

Ejemplo

Como una manzana cada día.

I eat an apple every day.

beber

to drink

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb. Takes 'haber' in compound tenses.

Ejemplo

Bebo agua fresca en verano.

I drink fresh water in summer.

beberse

to drink up

📌 Nota gramatical

Reflexive variant of beber, often implying completion.

Ejemplo

Me bebo todo el zumo.

I'm drinking up all the juice.

desayunar

to have breakfast

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Takes 'haber' in compound tenses.

Ejemplo

Desayuno pan con aceite.

I have bread with oil for breakfast.

almorzar

to have lunch

📌 Nota gramatical

Stem-changing o>ue verb (-ar). Note the spelling change c>qu in pretérito indefinido (yo almorcé).

Ejemplo

Almorzamos a las dos.

We have lunch at two.

cenar

to have dinner

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Takes 'haber' in compound tenses.

Ejemplo

cenamos pollo con patatas.

We have chicken with potatoes for dinner.

probar

to taste, to try (food)

📌 Nota gramatical

Stem-changing o>ue verb (-ar).

Ejemplo

Prueba esta tarta de chocolate.

Try this chocolate cake.

tomar

to have, to take (food/drink)

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Often used for food and drinks in Spain.

Ejemplo

Tomo un café con leche.

I have a coffee with milk.

gustar

to like (food/drink)

📌 Nota gramatical

Special construction (indirect object pronoun + gusta/gustan). Regular -ar verb.

Ejemplo

Me gusta la fruta fresca.

I like fresh fruit.

encantar

to love (food/drink)

📌 Nota gramatical

Similar structure to gustar. Regular -ar verb.

Ejemplo

Me encanta el chocolate negro.

I love dark chocolate.

querer

to want (food/drink)

📌 Nota gramatical

Stem-changing e>ie verb (-er). Irregular in indefinite past.

Ejemplo

Quiero un vaso de agua.

I want a glass of water.

preferir

to prefer

📌 Nota gramatical

Stem-changing e>ie verb (-ir). Also e>i in 3rd person preterite.

Ejemplo

Prefiero té con limón.

I prefer tea with lemon.

pedir

to order, to ask for

📌 Nota gramatical

Stem-changing e>i verb (-ir). e>i in 3rd person preterite.

Ejemplo

Pido una pizza grande.

I'm ordering a large pizza.

cortar

to cut

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb used in food preparation.

Ejemplo

Corto el pan con el cuchillo.

I cut the bread with the knife.

cocinar

to cook

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb.

Ejemplo

Cocino pasta para la cena.

I cook pasta for dinner.

preparar

to prepare (food)

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb.

Ejemplo

Preparo una ensalada rica.

I prepare a tasty salad.

comprar

to buy (food/groceries)

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb.

Ejemplo

Compro manzanas en el mercado.

I buy apples at the market.

tomarse

to have/consume (a drink/snack)

📌 Nota gramatical

Reflexive form of tomar, often used for consuming drinks or tapas.

Ejemplo

Me tomo una caña con mis amigos.

I'm having a small beer with my friends.

merendar

to have an afternoon snack

📌 Nota gramatical

Stem-changing e>ie verb (-ar).

Ejemplo

Meriendo pan con chocolate.

I have bread with chocolate for my afternoon snack.

comerse

to eat up completely

📌 Nota gramatical

Reflexive variant of comer, emphasizing eating everything.

Ejemplo

Me como toda la verdura.

I'm eating up all the vegetables.
