informar

to report / to inform

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Often used with the preposition 'sobre' when reporting on a specific topic in the news.

Ejemplo

El corresponsal informó sobre la situación política desde la capital esta mañana.

The correspondent reported on the political situation from the capital this morning.

declarar

to declare / to state

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb frequently used in press releases and official statements.

Ejemplo

El portavoz declaró a la prensa que no habrá más comentarios hasta el lunes.

The spokesperson stated to the press that there will be no further comments until Monday.

difundir

to broadcast / to spread / to disseminate

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb. Used for spreading news, broadcasting programs, or circulating information.

Ejemplo

La cadena de televisión decidió difundir el reportaje investigativo en horario estelar.

The television network decided to broadcast the investigative report during prime time.

cuestionar

to question / to call into question

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Useful for expressing critical analysis or journalistic skepticism.

Ejemplo

Varios periodistas decidieron cuestionar la veracidad de los datos oficiales presentados.

Several journalists decided to question the veracity of the official data presented.

desmentir

to deny / to refute / to debunk

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir verb with stem change e > ie in present forms (desmiento, desmientes...). Compound of mentir.

Ejemplo

El ministerio tuvo que desmentir los rumores sobre el supuesto cierre de fronteras.

The ministry had to deny the rumors about the alleged border closure.

revelar

to reveal / to disclose

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Commonly used in investigative journalism when exposing hidden facts or leaks.

Ejemplo

Una investigación periodística reveló detalles inéditos sobre la trama de corrupción.

An investigative journalism report revealed unprecedented details about the corruption plot.

asegurar

to claim / to assert / to assure

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. In media contexts, often means 'to claim' or 'to state with certainty'.

Ejemplo

El rotativo aseguró que el acuerdo comercial se firmará a finales de mes.

The daily newspaper claimed that the trade agreement will be signed at the end of the month.

reseñar

to review / to outline / to mention

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Used frequently in cultural journalism, literary reviews, or media summaries.

Ejemplo

El crítico literario se dispuso a reseñar la última novela del autor premiado.

The literary critic set out to review the latest novel by the award-winning author.

vaticinar

to predict / to forecast

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Often found in economic and political reporting when discussing future projections.

Ejemplo

Los analistas económicos vaticinan una notable desaceleración del crecimiento para el próximo año.

Economic analysts forecast a noticeable slowdown in growth for next year.

retransmitir

to broadcast / to relay (live)

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb. Used specifically for live broadcasts of events, sports, or news programs.

Ejemplo

La emisora de radio va a retransmitir el debate electoral en directo para todo el país.

The radio station is going to broadcast the electoral debate live for the whole country.

subrayar

to emphasize / to highlight

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Commonly used in journalism and rhetoric to emphasize key points or arguments.

Ejemplo

El editorialista subrayó la necesidad de proteger la libertad de expresión en todo momento.

The editorial writer emphasized the need to protect freedom of expression at all times.

ocultar

to conceal / to hide / to withhold

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Frequently used when media reports accuse authorities or individuals of withholding information.

Ejemplo

La investigación demostró que la compañía intentó ocultar los datos sobre el impacto ambiental.

The investigation showed that the company tried to conceal the data on the environmental impact.

manipular

to manipulate / to rig

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Used in media discussions regarding biased information or public opinion manipulation.

Ejemplo

Muchos críticos argumentan que algunos canales de televisión intentan manipular la opinión pública.

Many critics argue that some television channels try to manipulate public opinion.

contrastar

to check / to cross-reference / to contrast

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Essential journalistic practice: cross-referencing and verifying sources.

Ejemplo

Un buen periodista siempre debe contrastar la información antes de redactar la noticia.

A good journalist must always cross-reference the information before writing the news story.

divulgar

to divulge / to publicize / to popularize

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Orthographic change in preterite: divulgué (g > gu before e). Used for publishing findings or making knowledge public.

Ejemplo

La revista científica decidió divulgar los resultados del estudio a nivel internacional.

The scientific magazine decided to publicize the study results internationally.

alegar

to allege / to claim

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb with orthographic change in preterite: alegé (g > gu before e). Used in legal and journalistic contexts for assertions.

Ejemplo

Los abogados defensores alegan que su cliente no estuvo presente en el lugar del delito.

The defense attorneys allege that their client was not present at the crime scene.

censurar

to censor / to condemn

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Used both for governmental censorship of media and for public condemnation of actions.

Ejemplo

Varias organizaciones internacionales condenaron firmemente la decisión de censurar el diario independiente.

Several international organizations strongly condemned the decision to censor the independent newspaper.

ampliar

to expand / to elaborate (on news)

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb with a written accent on the 'i' in certain forms (ampliamos, amplías). Common in news anchors' phrasing.

Ejemplo

En unos minutos vamos a ampliar esta noticia con la última hora desde nuestros enviados especiales.

In a few minutes we will elaborate on this news story with the latest update from our special envoys.

atribuir

to attribute

📌 Nota gramatical

-uir verb with y spelling change in 3rd person singular and plural (atribuyó, atribuyeron) and accent marks on i.

Ejemplo

Algunos expertos atribuyen el fenómeno meteorológico al cambio climático global.

Some experts attribute the meteorological phenomenon to global climate change.

recalcar

to stress / to reiterate / to emphasize

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb with orthographic change in preterite: recalqué (c > qu before e). Very common in interviews and formal reporting.

Ejemplo

La directora del periódico recalcó la importancia de la veracidad en el periodismo moderno.

The director of the newspaper stressed the importance of truthfulness in modern journalism.
