cambiar

to change

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb that uses 'haber' for compound tenses.

Ejemplo

Quiero cambiar mi estilo de vida porque necesito más salud.

I want to change my lifestyle because I need better health.

convertirse

to become / to turn into

📌 Nota gramatical

Stem-changing -ir reflexive verb (e>ie) requiring the preposition 'en'.

Ejemplo

El pequeño pueblo se ha convertido en una gran ciudad turística.

The small village has turned into a major tourist city.

desarrollar

to develop

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb frequently used in professional and academic contexts.

Ejemplo

La empresa quiere desarrollar nuevas tecnologías para el sector verde.

The company wants to develop new technologies for the green sector.

mejorar

to improve

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb used when talking about progress and betterment.

Ejemplo

Practico español todos los días porque quiero mejorar mi fluidez.

I practice Spanish every day because I want to improve my fluency.

crecer

to grow / to mature

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb with a 'zco' spelling change in the first-person singular present (crezco).

Ejemplo

La empresa ha crecido mucho desde que abrieron la nueva sucursal.

The company has grown a lot since they opened the new branch.

empeorar

to worsen

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb, antonym of 'mejorar'.

Ejemplo

El clima empeoró rápidamente por la tarde y tuvimos que volver.

The weather worsened quickly in the afternoon and we had to return.

avanzar

to advance / to make progress

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb with a spelling change in the preterite yo form (avancé) to keep the /θ/ sound.

Ejemplo

Tenemos que avanzar con el proyecto antes de que llegue el plazo.

We have to advance with the project before the deadline arrives.

progresar

to progress

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb used for gradual improvement and personal or professional growth.

Ejemplo

Para progresar en tu carrera, es fundamental seguir aprendiendo siempre.

To progress in your career, it is essential to keep learning always.

adaptarse

to adapt

📌 Nota gramatical

Reflexive -ar verb taking the preposition 'a' when indicating what one adapts to.

Ejemplo

Nos costó mucho adaptarnos al nuevo sistema de trabajo remoto.

It was very hard for us to adapt to the new remote work system.

transformar

to transform

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb frequently used in both literal and metaphorical contexts of deep change.

Ejemplo

Aquel viaje transformó completamente su forma de ver el mundo.

That trip completely transformed his way of seeing the world.

mudarse

to move house

📌 Nota gramatical

Reflexive -ar verb used specifically for changing one's residence.

Ejemplo

Mis amigos se mudarán a otro barrio más cerca del centro la próxima semana.

My friends are going to move to another neighborhood closer to downtown next week.

evolucionar

to evolve

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb describing gradual biological, technological, or personal development.

Ejemplo

Los teléfonos móviles han evolucionado de forma impresionante en dos décadas.

Mobile phones have evolved impressively in two decades.

renovar

to renew / to update

📌 Nota gramatical

Stem-changing -ar verb (o>ue) used for documents, contracts, and updating things.

Ejemplo

Necesito renovar mi pasaporte antes de planificar las vacaciones de verano.

I need to renew my passport before planning the summer holidays.

modernizar

to modernize

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb formed from the adjective moderno.

Ejemplo

El ayuntamiento quiere modernizar el transporte público de la ciudad.

The city council wants to modernize the city's public transport.

ampliar

to expand / to widen

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb with a written accent on the 'i' in certain forms to maintain hiatus (amplías, amplía).

Ejemplo

La universidad decidió ampliar sus instalaciones para recibir a más estudiantes.

The university decided to expand its facilities to welcome more students.

reducir

to reduce / to decrease

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb with a 'zco' spelling change in the first-person singular present (reduzco).

Ejemplo

Tenemos que reducir el consumo de plástico para proteger el medio ambiente.

We have to reduce plastic consumption to protect the environment.

acostumbrarse

to get used to

📌 Nota gramatical

Reflexive -ar verb requiring the preposition 'a' before nouns or infinitives.

Ejemplo

Me costó mucho acostumbrarme al horario de desayunar tan temprano.

It was hard for me to get used to eating breakfast schedule so early.

substituir

to substitute / to replace

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb with a 'y' spelling change in certain forms (sustituye, sustituyen).

Ejemplo

Van a sustituir las viejas bombillas por luces LED para ahorrar energía.

They are going to replace the old light bulbs with LED lights to save energy.

variar

to vary / to fluctuate

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb with a written accent on the 'i' in present tense singular and 3rd plural (varía, varían).

Ejemplo

Los precios de la fruta varían mucho dependiendo de la temporada del año.

Fruit prices vary a lot depending on the time of year.

consolidar

to consolidate / to strengthen

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb used for strengthening a position, relationship, or project.

Ejemplo

La startup logró consolidar su posición en el mercado internacional.

The startup managed to consolidate its position in the international market.
