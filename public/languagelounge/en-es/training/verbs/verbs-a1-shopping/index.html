Shopping

comprar

to buy

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb belonging to the first conjugation group.

Ejemplo

Yo quiero comprar una camisa.

I want to buy a shirt.

pagar

to pay

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb with a spelling change in the preterite (g > gu before e).

Ejemplo

Prefiero pagar con tarjeta.

I prefer to pay by card.

costar

to cost

📌 Nota gramatical

Stem-changing -ar verb (o > ue). Used mainly in 3rd person singular and plural.

Ejemplo

¿Cuánto cuesta este vestido?

How much does this dress cost?

vender

to sell

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb belonging to the second conjugation group.

Ejemplo

Aquí venden fruta fresca.

Here they sell fresh fruit.

buscar

to look for

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Note that 'buscar' already means 'to look for', no preposition needed.

Ejemplo

Busco una talla más grande.

I am looking for a larger size.

llevar

to wear / to carry

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb used for wearing clothes or carrying items.

Ejemplo

Llevo una chaqueta roja.

I am wearing a red jacket.

probarse

to try on

📌 Nota gramatical

Reflexive stem-changing -ar verb (o > ue). Requires reflexive pronouns.

Ejemplo

Quiero probarme estos pantalones.

I want to try on these trousers.

gastar

to spend (money)

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb used specifically for spending money.

Ejemplo

No quiero gastar mucho dinero.

I don't want to spend a lot of money.

valer

to be worth

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -er verb with 'go' irregularity in the first person singular (valgo).

Ejemplo

¿Cuánto vale este sombrero?

How much is this hat worth?

preferir

to prefer

📌 Nota gramatical

Stem-changing -ir verb (e > ie). Useful for stating shopping choices.

Ejemplo

Prefiero el color azul.

I prefer the blue color.

necesitar

to need

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb used to state needs while shopping.

Ejemplo

Necesito una bolsa, por favor.

I need a bag, please.

encontrar

to find

📌 Nota gramatical

Stem-changing -ar verb (o > ue).

Ejemplo

No encuentro mis llaves.

I can't find my keys.

llevarse

to take (an item)

📌 Nota gramatical

Reflexive -ar verb used when deciding to take/buy an item from a store.

Ejemplo

Me llevo esta camisa.

I'll take this shirt.

mirar

to look at / to watch

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb used when browsing store windows or items.

Ejemplo

Solo estoy mirando, gracias.

I'm just looking, thanks.

quedar

to fit / to remain

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb often used impersonally with indirect objects (e.g., 'me queda').

Ejemplo

Los pantalones me quedan bien.

The trousers fit me well.

abrir

to open

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb. Useful for store opening hours.

Ejemplo

La tienda abre a las nueve.

The shop opens at nine.

cerrar

to close

📌 Nota gramatical

Stem-changing -ar verb (e > ie). Useful for store closing hours.

Ejemplo

El supermercado cierra temprano.

The supermarket closes early.

cambiar

to change / to exchange

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb used when exchanging an item for a different size or product.

Ejemplo

Quiero cambiar esta camisa.

I want to exchange this shirt.

gustar

to like

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb used with indirect object pronouns to express preferences.

Ejemplo

Me gusta este vestido.

I like this dress.

poder

to be able to / can

📌 Nota gramatical

Modal stem-changing -er verb (o > ue). Frequently used for polite requests in shops.

Ejemplo

¿Puedo ayudarle?

Can I help you?
