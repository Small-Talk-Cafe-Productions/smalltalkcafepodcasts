trabajar

to work

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; takes auxiliary 'haber' for compound tenses.

Ejemplo

Trabajo en una oficina grande.

I work in a big office.

ganar

to earn, to win

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; used for earning money or winning prizes.

Ejemplo

Gano un buen sueldo este año.

I earn a good salary this year.

cobrar

to get paid, to charge

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; commonly used in job contexts for receiving a paycheck.

Ejemplo

Cobramos el sueldo los viernes.

We get paid our salary on Fridays.

firmar

to sign

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; frequently used when signing a work contract.

Ejemplo

Firmé el contrato ayer por la tarde.

I signed the contract yesterday afternoon.

contratar

to hire

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; used by employers when taking on new staff.

Ejemplo

La empresa va a contratar nuevos empleados.

The company is going to hire new employees.

organizar

to organize

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; note spelling change in pretérito indefinido yo form (-cé).

Ejemplo

Organizo reuniones cada lunes por la mañana.

I organize meetings every Monday morning.

mandar

to send, to boss around

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; used for sending emails/messages or giving orders.

Ejemplo

Mando correos electrónicos a los clientes.

I send emails to the clients.

descansar

to rest, to take a break

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; used for taking lunch breaks or resting at work.

Ejemplo

Descansamos treinta minutos a mediodía.

We rest thirty minutes at midday.

jubilarse

to retire

📌 Nota gramatical

Reflexive -ar verb; requires reflexive pronouns (me, te, se, nos, os, se).

Ejemplo

Mi padre se jubila el próximo año.

My father is retiring next year.

entrevistar

to interview

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; used in hiring processes.

Ejemplo

El gerente entrevista a tres candidatos hoy.

The manager is interviewing three candidates today.

responder

to answer, to reply

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb; often used for answering work calls or emails.

Ejemplo

Respondo a los mensajes rápidamente.

I answer messages quickly.

vender

to sell

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb; common in commercial and retail jobs.

Ejemplo

Vendemos productos tecnológicos en la tienda.

We sell technological products in the store.

aprender

to learn

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb; used when acquiring new professional skills.

Ejemplo

Aprendo un nuevo programa informático en el trabajo.

I am learning a new computer program at work.

poder

to be able to, can

📌 Nota gramatical

Stem-changing o>ue -er verb; modal verb used before infinitives.

Ejemplo

No puedo asistir a la reunión de hoy.

I cannot attend today's meeting.

hacer

to do, to make

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -er verb; irregular yo form (hago) and irregular participle (hecho).

Ejemplo

Hago muchas llamadas durante el turno.

I make a lot of calls during the shift.

reunirse

to meet, to get together

📌 Nota gramatical

Reflexive -ir verb; used for holding meetings with colleagues.

Ejemplo

Nos reunimos con el director cada lunes.

We meet with the director every Monday.

escribir

to write

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb; used for writing reports and emails.

Ejemplo

Escribo informes para el departamento.

I write reports for the department.

abrir

to open

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb; used for opening businesses or stores.

Ejemplo

Abrimos la tienda a las nueve en punto.

We open the store at nine sharp.

subir

to go up, to upload, to promote

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb; can mean uploading files or getting a promotion/raise.

Ejemplo

Subo los documentos a la nube del trabajo.

I upload the documents to the work cloud.

decidir

to decide

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb; used when making professional choices.

Ejemplo

Decidimos cambiar de estrategia este trimestre.

We decided to change strategy this quarter.
