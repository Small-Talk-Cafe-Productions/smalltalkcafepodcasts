Influence & Persuasion

persuadir

to persuade

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb. Often followed by 'a' + infinitive when persuading someone to do something.

Ejemplo

El director logró persuadir al consejo de administración para aprobar el nuevo presupuesto.

The director managed to persuade the board of directors to approve the new budget.

convencer

to convince

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb, with a spelling change in the first-person singular present (convenzo) to maintain the /θ/ or /s/ sound.

Ejemplo

Sus sólidos argumentos lograron convencer a los inversores escépticos durante la reunión.

Her solid arguments managed to convince the skeptical investors during the meeting.

influir

to influence

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -ir verb with y-insertion in present, preterite, and subjunctive forms (influye, influyen, influyó, influyeron).

Ejemplo

Los medios de comunicación digitales pueden influir notablemente en la opinión pública actual.

Digital media can significantly influence current public opinion.

manipular

to manipulate

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Frequently used in psychological, political, and technical contexts.

Ejemplo

Algunos políticos intentan manipular la información para ocultar sus propios errores de gestión.

Some politicians try to manipulate information to hide their own management errors.

incitar

to incite / to urge

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Generally takes the preposition 'a' before an infinitive or noun phrase.

Ejemplo

El discurso del líder radical incitó a la multitud a cometer actos de vandalismo.

The radical leader's speech incited the crowd to commit acts of vandalism.

motivar

to motivate

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Frequently used in professional and educational leadership contexts.

Ejemplo

El gerente supo cómo motivar a su equipo de ventas para superar los objetivos trimestrales.

The manager knew how to motivate his sales team to exceed quarterly targets.

disuadir

to dissuade

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb. Typically used with the preposition 'de' before an infinitive or noun phrase.

Ejemplo

Intentamos disuadir a nuestro amigo de tomar una decisión tan precipitada sobre su carrera.

We tried to dissuade our friend from making such a hasty decision about his career.

coaccionar

to coerce / to pressure

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Refers to exercising unlawful pressure or force to compel someone's action.

Ejemplo

La empresa fue acusada de coaccionar a sus empleados para que aceptaran contratos peores.

The company was accused of coercing its employees to accept worse contracts.

seducir

to seduce / to charm

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb (note: root is seduc-, unlikeducir compounds like conducir which have -zc- in present yo).

Ejemplo

La nueva campaña publicitaria busca seducir a los consumidores más jóvenes con un enfoque ecológico.

The new advertising campaign seeks to charm younger consumers with an ecological approach.

imponer

to impose

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -er verb, following the pattern of 'poner' (yo impongo, pretérito impuse).

Ejemplo

El entrenador intentó imponer su táctica defensiva sin consultar primero con los jugadores clave.

The coach tried to impose his defensive tactic without consulting the key players first.

sugerir

to suggest

📌 Nota gramatical

Stem-changing -ir verb (e>ie in present, e>i in preterite 3rd person: sugirió, sugirieron).

Ejemplo

La consultora sugirió cambiar la estrategia de marketing para llegar a un público más amplio.

The consultant suggested changing the marketing strategy to reach a wider audience.

aconsejar

to advise / to counsel

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Frequently triggers the subjunctive mood in subordinate clauses ('Te aconsejo que vayas').

Ejemplo

Los expertos aconsejan mantener la calma cuando te enfrentas a una situación de negociación difícil.

Experts advise keeping calm when you face a difficult negotiation situation.

presionar

to pressure / to push

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Often followed by 'a' + infinitive when pressuring someone to act.

Ejemplo

Los manifestantes salieron a la calle para presionar al gobierno y exigir cambios inmediatos.

The protesters took to the street to pressure the government and demand immediate changes.

arrastrar

to drag / to sweep along / to pull

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Used both literally and metaphorically (e.g., to sweep people along in a movement or trend).

Ejemplo

El carismático orador consiguió arrastrar a la multitud con su mensaje apasionado.

The charismatic speaker managed to sweep the crowd along with his passionate message.

inclinar

to incline / to bias / to persuade (towards)

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Often used reflexively ('inclinarse a/hacia') to indicate leaning towards an opinion or decision.

Ejemplo

Los últimos acontecimientos lograron inclinar la balanza a favor de nuestra propuesta comercial.

Recent events managed to tip the scales in favor of our commercial proposal.

condicionar

to condition / to influence strongly

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Frequently used in academic, sociological, and psychological contexts.

Ejemplo

Nuestras experiencias pasadas suelen condicionar la forma en que tomamos decisiones hoy.

Our past experiences tend to condition the way we make decisions today.

atraer

to attract / to draw

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -er verb, following the pattern of 'traer' (yo atraigo, pretérito atraje).

Ejemplo

La innovadora propuesta de la startup logró atraer la atención de numerosos inversores internacionales.

The startup's innovative proposal managed to attract the attention of numerous international investors.

apelar

to appeal (to)

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Usually followed by the preposition 'a' (e.g., 'apelar a la emoción/la razón').

Ejemplo

Durante su discurso electoral, el candidato decidió apelar a la solidaridad de los ciudadanos.

During his election speech, the candidate decided to appeal to the solidarity of the citizens.

sugerirse

to be suggested / to arise (reflexive nuance of suggestion)

📌 Nota gramatical

Reflexive stem-changing -ir verb (e>ie). Used when a possibility or idea naturally presents itself.

Ejemplo

Se sugiere llegar con media hora de antelación para facilitar el proceso de acreditación.

It is suggested to arrive half an hour early to facilitate the accreditation process.

orientar

to guide / to steer / to orient

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Frequently used when steering someone towards a specific decision or course of action.

Ejemplo

El orientador profesional ayudó a los estudiantes a orientar sus carreras hacia el sector tecnológico.

The career counselor helped the students steer their careers toward the tech sector.
