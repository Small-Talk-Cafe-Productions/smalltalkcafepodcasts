poder

to be able to / can

📌 Nota gramatical

-er group, stem-changing o>ue. Modal verb used to express ability.

Ejemplo

Puedo hablar español.

I can speak Spanish.

querer

to want

📌 Nota gramatical

-er group, stem-changing e>ie. Modal verb used to express desire.

Ejemplo

Quiero un café.

I want a coffee.

deber

to must / should

📌 Nota gramatical

-er group. Modal verb used to express obligation or duty.

Ejemplo

Debes estudiar más.

You must study more.

saber

to know (a fact) / to know how to

📌 Nota gramatical

-er group. Irregular first person singular in present. Followed by infinitive to express knowing how to do something.

Ejemplo

Sé hablar inglés.

I know how to speak English.

necesitar

to need

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar group. Used to express necessity, often followed by a noun or infinitive.

Ejemplo

Necesito agua, por favor.

I need water, please.

preferir

to prefer

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir group, stem-changing e>ie. Used to express preference between options.

Ejemplo

Prefiero el té.

I prefer tea.

gustar

to like

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar group. Used with indirect object pronouns (me, te, le...) to express liking.

Ejemplo

Me gusta la música.

I like music.

poder

to be allowed to / may

📌 Nota gramatical

-er group, stem-changing o>ue. Also used for permission in questions.

Ejemplo

¿Puedo pasar?

May I come in?

intentar

to try / to attempt

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar group. Used to express trying to do an action.

Ejemplo

Intento hablar despacio.

I try to speak slowly.

desear

to desire / to wish

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar group. Formal or polite way to express a wish or desire.

Ejemplo

¿Desea algo más?

Do you wish anything else?

esperar

to hope / to wait

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar group. Used to express hope or waiting.

Ejemplo

Espero que estés bien.

I hope you are well.

soler

to usually do something

📌 Nota gramatical

-er group, stem-changing o>ue. Defective verb, mostly used in present and imperfect to express habitual actions.

Ejemplo

Suelo desayunar a las ocho.

I usually have breakfast at eight.

aprender

to learn

📌 Nota gramatical

-er group. Often followed by 'a' + infinitive.

Ejemplo

Aprendo a hablar español.

I am learning to speak Spanish.

decidir

to decide

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir group. Used to express making a choice or decision.

Ejemplo

Decido quedarme en casa.

I decide to stay at home.

pensar

to think / to intend

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar group, stem-changing e>ie. Followed by an infinitive means 'to intend to'.

Ejemplo

Pienso viajar mañana.

I intend to travel tomorrow.

empezar

to begin / to start

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar group, stem-changing e>ie. Followed by 'a' + infinitive.

Ejemplo

Empiezo a trabajar a las nueve.

I start working at nine.

terminar

to finish / to end

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar group. Often followed by 'de' + infinitive to mean finish doing something.

Ejemplo

Termino de trabajar temprano.

I finish working early.

probar

to try (food) / to test / to try on

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar group, stem-changing o>ue. Used for trying food or clothes.

Ejemplo

Prueba esta comida.

Try this food.

imaginar

to imagine

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar group. Often used reflexively as 'imaginarse'.

Ejemplo

No me lo imagino.

I can't imagine it.

conseguir

to get / to obtain / to manage to

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir group, stem-changing e>i. Used to express obtaining or managing to achieve something.

Ejemplo

Consigo entradas para el concierto.

I get tickets for the concert.

Configurar entrenamiento

Configura tu sesión de entrenamiento.
