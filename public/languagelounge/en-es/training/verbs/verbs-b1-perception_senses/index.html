observar

to observe

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; takes auxiliary 'haber' in compound tenses.

Ejemplo

Me gusta sentarme en el parque para observar a la gente pasar.

I like to sit in the park to observe people passing by.

mirar

to look at

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; takes auxiliary 'haber' in compound tenses.

Ejemplo

Estaba mirando por la ventana cuando empezó a llover intensamente.

I was looking out the window when it started to rain heavily.

ver

to see

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -er verb; irregular first-person present (veo), irregular imperfect (veía).

Ejemplo

Desde la cima de la montaña se puede ver todo el valle.

From the top of the mountain you can see the entire valley.

oír

to hear

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -ir verb; spelling and stem changes in present (oy- root).

Ejemplo

No puedo oír bien lo que dices porque hay mucho ruido aquí.

I cannot hear well what you are saying because there is a lot of noise here.

escuchar

to listen to

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; implies active attention compared to 'oír'.

Ejemplo

Me encanta escuchar música clásica mientras trabajo en mi oficina.

I love listening to classical music while I work in my office.

tocar

to touch / to play an instrument

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; spelling change in preterite (c -> qu before e: toqué).

Ejemplo

No debes tocar las obras de arte expuestas en el museo.

You must not touch the works of art exhibited in the museum.

oler

to smell

📌 Nota gramatical

Stem-changing o>ue verb with an initial h added before o in present forms (huelo).

Ejemplo

La comida en la cocina huele increíblemente bien esta noche.

The food in the kitchen smells incredibly good tonight.

saborear

to taste / to savor

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; takes auxiliary 'haber' in compound tenses.

Ejemplo

Es importante saborear cada plato lentamente para apreciar los ingredientes.

It is important to savor each dish slowly to appreciate the ingredients.

sentir

to feel

📌 Nota gramatical

Stem-changing e>ie verb (-ir group); also used reflexively for emotions.

Ejemplo

Siento un gran alivio después de terminar todos mis exámenes finales.

I feel a great relief after finishing all my final exams.

notar

to notice

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; expresses noticing a physical sensation or change.

Ejemplo

Noté que hacía mucho frío en cuanto salí a la calle sin abrigo.

I noticed that it was very cold as soon as I went out without a coat.

parecer

to seem / to appear

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -er verb;zc verb in present (parezco), used often with indirect objects.

Ejemplo

La película me pareció demasiado larga y un poco aburrida al final.

The movie seemed too long and a bit boring to me at the end.

contemplar

to contemplate / to gaze at

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; often used for deep visual perception and appreciation.

Ejemplo

Nos quedamos en silencio para contemplar la puesta de sol en la playa.

We stayed in silence to gaze at the sunset on the beach.

distinguir

to distinguish / to discern

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb; -guir spelling in 1st person present (distingo).

Ejemplo

Con poca luz en la habitación era difícil distinguir las caras.

With low light in the room it was difficult to distinguish faces.

ignorar

to ignore / to be unaware of

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; used when lacking perception or intentionally disregarding something.

Ejemplo

Ignoraba por completo que hubiera una reunión tan importante hoy.

I was completely unaware that there was such an important meeting today.

imaginar

to imagine

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; often used reflexively (imaginarse) to suppose or picture mentally.

Ejemplo

No te puedes imaginar lo difícil que fue organizar este evento cultural.

You cannot imagine how difficult it was to organize this cultural event.

suponer

to suppose / to assume

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -er verb; patterned after 'poner' (supongo, supondré).

Ejemplo

Supongo que llegarán tarde porque siempre hay mucho tráfico a esta hora.

I suppose they will arrive late because there is always heavy traffic at this time.

apreciar

to appreciate / to value

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; relates to perceiving value or fine details.

Ejemplo

Desde este mirador se puede apreciar la belleza de todo el paisaje.

From this viewpoint you can appreciate the beauty of the entire landscape.

descubrir

to discover

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb; irregular participle (descubierto).

Ejemplo

Los científicos descubrieron una nueva especie de planta en la selva.

The scientists discovered a new plant species in the jungle.

reconocer

to recognize / to admit

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -er verb; -zc verb in present (reconozco), patterned after 'conocer'.

Ejemplo

Apenas reconocí a mi viejo amigo después de tantos años sin vernos.

I scarcely recognized my old friend after so many years without seeing each other.

experimentar

to experience

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; used for undergoing sensory or psychological experiences.

Ejemplo

La ciudad experimentó un gran crecimiento económico durante la última década.

The city experienced great economic growth during the last decade.
