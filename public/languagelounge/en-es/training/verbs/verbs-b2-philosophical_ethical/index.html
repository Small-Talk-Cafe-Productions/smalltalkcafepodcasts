Philosophical & Ethical

cuestionar

to question / to call into question

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Followed by a direct object when challenging ideas or authority.

Ejemplo

Es fundamental cuestionar las normas sociales establecidas para lograr un verdadero progreso moral.

It is essential to question established social norms in order to achieve true moral progress.

reflexionar

to reflect / to ponder

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Frequently used with the preposition 'sobre' (sobre la vida, sobre el bien).

Ejemplo

Debemos reflexionar profundamente sobre las consecuencias éticas de nuestros actos cotidianos.

We must reflect deeply on the ethical consequences of our daily actions.

justificar

to justify

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb with orthographic spelling change in the preterite: c -> qu before 'é' (justifiqué).

Ejemplo

El fin no siempre justifica los medios en una sociedad regida por principios morales.

The end does not always justify the means in a society governed by moral principles.

argumentar

to argue / to reason

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Used to present logical reasons or philosophical viewpoints.

Ejemplo

El ponente supo argumentar con solidez por qué la empatía es la base de la ética.

The speaker managed to argue solidly why empathy is the basis of ethics.

asumir

to assume / to take on (responsibility)

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb. Often used in ethical contexts regarding responsibility or premises.

Ejemplo

Todo ciudadano maduro debe asumir la responsabilidad de sus propias decisiones morales.

Every mature citizen must take on the responsibility of their own moral decisions.

coexistir

to coexist

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb. Common in discussions about tolerance, diversity, and society.

Ejemplo

Diferentes corrientes de pensamiento pueden coexistir pacíficamente en una democracia pluralista.

Different currents of thought can coexist peacefully in a pluralistic democracy.

contradecir

to contradict

📌 Nota gramatical

Compound -ir verb following the pattern of decir (e->i stem change in present, irregular preterite: contradije).

Ejemplo

Sus acciones en la vida real parecen contradecir totalmente sus bellos discursos éticos.

His actions in real life seem to totally contradict his beautiful ethical speeches.

desafiar

to defy / to challenge

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb with an written accent on the 'i' in certain forms (desafía), though standard conjugation applies.

Ejemplo

Los pensadores ilustrados supieron desafiar el dogmatismo religioso de su época.

Enlightenment thinkers knew how to defy the religious dogmatism of their era.

discernir

to discern / to distinguish

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb. Used when separating right from wrong or abstract concepts.

Ejemplo

La madurez intelectual nos ayuda a discernir entre lo superficial y lo verdaderamente valioso.

Intellectual maturity helps us to discern between the superficial and the truly valuable.

interpretar

to interpret / to construe

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Essential for discussing texts, actions, and subjective experiences.

Ejemplo

Cada lector puede interpretar de manera diferente los símbolos ocultos en esta obra filosófica.

Each reader can interpret the hidden symbols in this philosophical work in a different way.

justipreciar

to appraise / to evaluate fairly

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Formal/literary register, used for giving fair value or critical assessment.

Ejemplo

Los críticos intentan justipreciar el impacto cultural de la teoría existencialista.

Critics attempt to fairly evaluate the cultural impact of existentialist theory.

postular

to postulate / to propose

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Frequently used in academic, scientific, and philosophical theses.

Ejemplo

Muchos filósofos contemporáneos suelen postular que la conciencia es un misterio sin resolver.

Many contemporary philosophers often postulate that consciousness is an unsolved mystery.

reivindicar

to reclaim / to vindicate / to demand rights

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb with orthographic spelling change in the preterite: c -> qu before 'é' (reivindiqué).

Ejemplo

Los movimientos sociales luchan por reivindicar los derechos de las minorías oprimidas.

Social movements fight to reclaim the rights of oppressed minorities.

subyacer

to underlie

📌 Nota gramatical

Compound -ir verb following the pattern of yacer (yo subyazgo in present, irregular present subjunctive subyazga).

Ejemplo

Un profundo sentimiento de culpa suele subyacer en las conductas excesivamente rígidas.

A deep sense of guilt usually underlies excessively rigid behaviors.

trascender

to transcend / to go beyond

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb (sometimes conjugated with e->ie stem change in traditional grammars, but standard reg -er is widely accepted and common).

Ejemplo

Las grandes obras de arte logran trascender las barreras del tiempo y del espacio.

Great works of art manage to transcend the barriers of time and space.

venerar

to venerate / to revere

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Used in discussions concerning religious or ideological devotion.

Ejemplo

En algunas culturas antiguas solían venerar las fuerzas de la naturaleza como divinidades.

In some ancient cultures they used to revere the forces of nature as divinities.

rebatir

to refute / to rebut

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir verb. Frequently used in debates, philosophical essays, and arguments.

Ejemplo

El filósofo logró rebatir los principales argumentos del empirismo tradicional.

The philosopher managed to refute the main arguments of traditional empiricism.

dilucidar

to elucidate / to clarify

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Used for clearing up complex philosophical or ethical dilemmas.

Ejemplo

La comisión ética se reunió para dilucidar los límites de la investigación genética.

The ethics committee met to elucidate the limits of genetic research.

consensuar

to reach a consensus on / to agree upon

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Essential in socio-political and ethical negotiation contexts.

Ejemplo

Es muy difícil consensuar un código ético universal en un mundo tan diverso.

It is very difficult to reach a consensus on a universal ethical code in such a diverse world.

condicionar

to condition / to determine

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb. Often used regarding social environment or determinism.

Ejemplo

Muchos argumentan que el entorno social y económico llega a condicionar nuestra libertad.

Many argue that the social and economic environment ends up conditioning our freedom.
