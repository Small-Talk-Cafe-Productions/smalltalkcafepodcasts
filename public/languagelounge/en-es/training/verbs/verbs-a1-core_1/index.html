ser

to be

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -er verb. Used for permanent characteristics, identity, and origin.

Ejemplo

Yo soy estudiante.

I am a student.

estar

to be

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -ar verb. Used for temporary states, emotions, and locations.

Ejemplo

Estoy muy feliz hoy.

I am very happy today.

tener

to have

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -er verb with a 'go' in the first-person singular and e>ie stem change.

Ejemplo

Tengo un gato pequeño.

I have a small cat.

hablar

to speak

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar conjugation group.

Ejemplo

Hablo español un poco.

I speak Spanish a little.

comer

to eat

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er conjugation group.

Ejemplo

Como una manzana roja.

I eat a red apple.

vivir

to live

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ir conjugation group.

Ejemplo

Vivo en una casa grande.

I live in a big house.

ir

to go

📌 Nota gramatical

Highly irregular -ir verb. Often followed by 'a' + place.

Ejemplo

Voy al supermercado hoy.

I am going to the supermarket today.

hacer

to do, to make

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -er verb with 'go' in the first-person singular.

Ejemplo

Hago los deberes ahora.

I am doing the homework now.

tener

to have

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular stem-changing verb (yo tengo, tú tienes); auxiliary 'haber'.

Ejemplo

Tengo sed.

I am thirsty.

querer

to want, to love

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -er verb with e>ie stem change.

Ejemplo

Quiero un café, por favor.

I want a coffee, please.

poder

to be able to, can

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -er modal verb with o>ue stem change.

Ejemplo

Puedo hablar español bien.

I can speak Spanish well.

saber

to know (facts, information)

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -er verb with 'sé' in the first-person singular.

Ejemplo

Sé la respuesta.

I know the answer.

ver

to see

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -er verb with 'veo' in the first-person singular.

Ejemplo

Veo una película buena.

I see a good movie.

dar

to give

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -ar verb with 'doy' in the first-person singular.

Ejemplo

Doy un regalo a María.

I give a gift to María.

venir

to come

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -ir verb with 'go' in the first-person singular and e>ie stem change.

Ejemplo

Vengo a la fiesta hoy.

I am coming to the party today.

decir

to say, to tell

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -ir verb with 'go' in the first-person singular and e>i stem change.

Ejemplo

Digo la verdad siempre.

I always tell the truth.

pensar

to think

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar ending with e>ie stem change.

Ejemplo

Pienso en mis vacaciones.

I think about my vacation.

encontrar

to find

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar ending with o>ue stem change.

Ejemplo

Encuentro mis llaves fácilmente.

I find my keys easily.

salir

to go out, to leave

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -ir verb with 'go' in the first-person singular.

Ejemplo

Salgo con mis amigos.

I go out with my friends.

leer

to read

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb with spelling change in 3rd-person past (i to y).

Ejemplo

Leo un libro interesante.

I read an interesting book.
