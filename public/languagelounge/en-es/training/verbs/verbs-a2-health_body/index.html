doler

to hurt / to ache

📌 Nota gramatical

-er verb, stem-changing (o>ue). Used primarily impersonally like 'gustar' (e.g., duele, duelen).

Ejemplo

¿Te duele la cabeza hoy?

Does your head hurt today?

sentir

to feel

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir verb, stem-changing (e>ie). Often used reflexively as 'sentirse' to describe physical or emotional states.

Ejemplo

Me siento muy cansado esta tarde.

I feel very tired this afternoon.

enfermarse

to get sick

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar reflexive verb. Regular conjugation patterns with reflexive pronouns.

Ejemplo

Mucha gente se enferma en invierno.

Many people get sick in winter.

descansar

to rest

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb. Essential for health and recovery contexts.

Ejemplo

Tienes que descansar para recuperar fuerzas.

You have to rest to regain strength.

respirar

to breathe

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb. Common during medical checkups or relaxation.

Ejemplo

El médico me dijo que respire hondo.

The doctor told me to breathe deeply.

toser

to cough

📌 Nota gramatical

-er regular verb. Typical symptom of a cold or flu.

Ejemplo

Empiezo a toser cuando hace frío.

I start to cough when it is cold.

estornudar

to sneeze

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb. Common allergy or cold symptom.

Ejemplo

El polvo me hace estornudar mucho.

Dust makes me sneeze a lot.

cuidar

to take care of / to look after

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb. Often used reflexively as 'cuidarse' to mean take care of oneself.

Ejemplo

Debes cuidarte cuando estás enfermo.

You must take care of yourself when you are sick.

tomar

to take (medicine) / to drink

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb. Used for taking medication or vitamins.

Ejemplo

Tienes que tomar esta medicina cada ocho horas.

You have to take this medicine every eight hours.

mejorar

to improve / to get better

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb. Often used reflexively as 'mejorarse' to recover health.

Ejemplo

Espero que te mejores muy pronto.

I hope you get better very soon.

recuperar

to recover

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb. Frequently used reflexively ('recuperarse') in medical recovery contexts.

Ejemplo

Necesito unos días para recuperarme de la operación.

I need a few days to recover from the operation.

examinar

to examine / to check

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb. Used by doctors when checking patients.

Ejemplo

El doctor quiere examinarte la garganta.

The doctor wants to examine your throat.

adelgazar

to lose weight / to slim down

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb. Common topic in physical health and diet.

Ejemplo

Quedo adelgazar un poco para el verano.

I want to lose a little weight for the summer.

engordar

to gain weight / to get fat

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb. Opposite of adelgazar.

Ejemplo

No quiero engordar durante las vacaciones.

I do not want to gain weight during the holidays.

relajar

to relax

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb. Frequently used reflexively as 'relajarse'.

Ejemplo

Es importante relajarse después de trabajar.

It is important to relax after working.

ejercitar

to exercise / to practice

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb. Often used reflexively as 'ejercitarse' for working out.

Ejemplo

Me gusta ejercitarme en el gimnasio.

I like to exercise at the gym.

herirse

to injure oneself / to get hurt

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir reflexive verb, stem-changing (e>ie).

Ejemplo

Ten cuidado para no herirte con eso.

Be careful not to hurt yourself with that.

sangrar

to bleed

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb. Common in injury and first aid contexts.

Ejemplo

La nariz me empezó a sangrar de repente.

My nose started to bleed suddenly.

marearse

to feel dizzy / to get motion sick

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar reflexive verb. Regular conjugation with reflexive pronouns.

Ejemplo

Me mareo cuando leo en el coche.

I get dizzy when I read in the car.

despertar

to wake up

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar verb, stem-changing (e>ie). Frequently used reflexively as 'despertarse' for daily routine.

Ejemplo

Me despierto temprano todas las mañanas.

I wake up early every morning.
