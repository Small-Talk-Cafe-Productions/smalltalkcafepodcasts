bajar

to download

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb. Also used for uploading when paired with 'subir'.

Ejemplo

¿Puedes bajar esta aplicación al móvil?

Can you download this app to the phone?

conectar

to connect

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb. Often used with the preposition 'a'.

Ejemplo

Necesito conectar el ordenador a internet.

I need to connect the computer to the internet.

apagar

to turn off

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb with spelling change in preterite (g > gu before e).

Ejemplo

Apaga la televisión, por favor.

Turn off the television, please.

encender

to turn on

📌 Nota gramatical

-er verb with e>ie stem change in present forms. Irregular preterite stem.

Ejemplo

Enciende la pantalla del ordenador.

Turn on the computer screen.

guardar

to save / to store

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb. Used for saving files or documents digitally.

Ejemplo

Debes guardar el documento antes de salir.

You must save the document before leaving.

imprimir

to print

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir regular verb, though it has an irregular participle ('impreso').

Ejemplo

Quiero imprimir este artículo de la revista.

I want to print this magazine article.

navegar

to surf / to navigate (the web)

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb with spelling change in preterite (g > gu before e). Used with 'por'.

Ejemplo

Me gusta navegar por internet por la noche.

I like to surf the internet at night.

mandar

to send

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb. Synonym of 'enviar' for messages and emails.

Ejemplo

Mándame un mensaje cuando llegues a casa.

Send me a message when you arrive home.

enviar

to send

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb with written accent variations in present forms (envío).

Ejemplo

Voy a enviar unas fotos por WhatsApp.

I am going to send some photos via WhatsApp.

chatear

to chat (online)

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb. Frequently used with 'con'.

Ejemplo

Chateo con mis amigos todos los días.

I chat with my friends every day.

compartir

to share

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir regular verb. Used for sharing links, posts, and media.

Ejemplo

Quiero compartir este vídeo en mis redes sociales.

I want to share this video on my social networks.

buscar

to search / to look up

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb with spelling change in preterite (c > qu before e).

Ejemplo

Busco información en Google para el proyecto.

I look up information on Google for the project.

subir

to upload / to post

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir regular verb. Used for uploading files or posting photos.

Ejemplo

Voy a subir mis fotos a Instagram.

I am going to upload my photos to Instagram.

funcionar

to work / to function

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb. Used for appliances, devices, and technology.

Ejemplo

Mi teléfono móvil nuevo ya funciona bien.

My new mobile phone works well now.

llamar

to call

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb. Also reflexive ('llamarse') for names.

Ejemplo

Te llamo más tarde por teléfono.

I'll call you later by phone.

contestar

to answer / to reply

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb. Used for answering calls or messages.

Ejemplo

Nadie contesta al teléfono.

Nobody is answering the phone.

bloquear

to block

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb. Used for blocking users or numbers on devices.

Ejemplo

Tuve que bloquear ese número desconocido.

I had to block that unknown number.

borrar

to delete / to erase

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb. Used for removing files, messages, or apps.

Ejemplo

Voy a borrar las fotos viejas del teléfono.

I am going to delete the old photos from the phone.

grabar

to record

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; auxiliary 'haber'. Used for recording audio, video, or data.

Ejemplo

Quiero grabar un vídeo para mi canal.

I want to record a video for my channel.

instalar

to install

📌 Nota gramatical

-ar regular verb. Used for software, apps, and hardware.

Ejemplo

Voy a instalar un programa nuevo en el portátil.

I am going to install a new program on the laptop.
