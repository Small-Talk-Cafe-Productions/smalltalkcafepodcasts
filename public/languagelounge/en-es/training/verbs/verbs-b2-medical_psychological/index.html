aconsejar

to advise / to counsel

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar) regular verb; often takes the subjunctive mood in subordinate clauses when giving professional recommendations.

Ejemplo

El especialista me aconsejó que descansara al menos una semana después de la intervención quirúrgica.

The specialist advised me to rest for at least a week after the surgical procedure.

agobiar

to overwhelm / to weigh down

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar) regular verb; frequently used reflexively ('agobiarse') to describe feeling overwhelmed or stressed.

Ejemplo

La acumulación de trabajo y las exigencias diarias pueden agobiar la salud mental del paciente.

The accumulation of work and daily demands can overwhelm the patient's mental health.

atenuar

to alleviate / to mitigate / to tone down

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar) verb with a written accent on the weak vowel in certain present forms (atenúo, atenúa) to maintain hiatus, though standard conjugation patterns apply.

Ejemplo

Este nuevo fármaco analgésico ayuda a atenuar el dolor crónico sin causar efectos secundarios graves.

This new analgesic drug helps to alleviate chronic pain without causing severe side effects.

carecer

to lack / to be devoid of

📌 Nota gramatical

Third conjugation (-er/-ir group pattern, specifically -ecer) irregular verb with a 'zc' mutation in the first-person singular present (carezco) and present subjunctive.

Ejemplo

Muchos pacientes con trastornos depresivos graves suelen carecer de motivación para realizar actividades cotidianas.

Many patients with severe depressive disorders tend to lack motivation to carry out daily activities.

cohibirse

to inhibit oneself / to feel awkward

📌 Nota gramatical

Third conjugation (-ir) reflexive verb; requires reflexive pronouns and features written accents on the 'i' in various present and imperfect subjunctive forms to indicate hiatus.

Ejemplo

La paciente tendía a cohibirse durante las primeras sesiones de terapia debido a su timidez extrema.

The female patient tended to inhibit herself during the first therapy sessions due to her extreme shyness.

diagnosticar

to diagnose

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar) regular verb; orthographic change in the preterite yo form (diagnostiqué) to preserve the hard /k/ sound before 'e'.

Ejemplo

El neurólogo le diagnosticó una migraña crónica tras analizar exhaustivamente todos sus síntomas.

The neurologist diagnosed him with chronic migraine after exhaustively analyzing all his symptoms.

descompensarse

to become destabilized / to decompensate

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar) reflexive verb; commonly used in medicine when chronic conditions (like diabetes or hypertension) lose stability.

Ejemplo

Si el paciente diabético no sigue la dieta estricta, su nivel de glucosa en sangre puede descompensarse rápidamente.

If the diabetic patient does not follow the strict diet, their blood glucose level can quickly become destabilized.

disminuir

to decrease / to diminish / to reduce

📌 Nota gramatical

Third conjugation (-ir) verb with a yod spelling change (adding 'y' between vowels in certain forms: disminuye, disminuyen) and written accents.

Ejemplo

Los analgésicos recetados lograron disminuir la inflamación en las articulaciones del paciente reumático.

The prescribed painkillers managed to decrease the inflammation in the rheumatic patient's joints.

enfrentarse

to face / to confront

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar) reflexive verb; typically used with the preposition 'a' ('enfrentarse a') when confronting difficulties or psychological traumas.

Ejemplo

Muchos veteranos de guerra tienen que enfrentarse al trastorno de estrés postraumático al volver.

Many war veterans have to face post-traumatic stress disorder upon returning.

fallecer

to pass away / to die

📌 Nota gramatical

Third conjugation (-ecer) irregular verb; uses the 'zc' mutation in the first-person singular present (fallezco) and throughout the present subjunctive.

Ejemplo

El anciano lamentablemente falleció en el hospital tras una larga enfermedad degenerativa.

The elderly man unfortunately passed away in the hospital after a long degenerative illness.

inhibir

to inhibit / to block

📌 Nota gramatical

Third conjugation (-ir) regular verb; often used in pharmacology regarding neurotransmitters or enzyme actions.

Ejemplo

Este medicamento está diseñado para inhibir la recaptación de serotonina en el sistema nervioso central.

This medication is designed to inhibit serotonin reuptake in the central nervous system.

internalizar

to internalize

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar) regular verb; frequently applied in psychological contexts regarding emotions, behaviors, or social norms.

Ejemplo

Durante la infancia, las personas tienden a internalizar las expectativas críticas de sus padres de manera inconsciente.

During childhood, people tend to internalize the critical expectations of their parents unconsciously.

medicarse

to medicate oneself / to take medication

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar) reflexive verb; used when individuals take drugs for health issues, sometimes carrying a warning against self-medication ('automedicarse').

Ejemplo

Los médicos advierten que nunca se debe medicarse sin la supervisión previa de un profesional sanitario.

Doctors warn that one must never medicate oneself without the prior supervision of a health professional.

paralizarse

to become paralyzed / to freeze up

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar) reflexive verb; used both literally (neurological paralysis) and metaphorically (psychological freezing under extreme fear or shock).

Ejemplo

Ante una situación de peligro extremo, algunas personas tienden a paralizarse por completo debido al pánico.

Faced with a situation of extreme danger, some people tend to freeze up completely due to panic.

paliar

to alleviate / to palliate / to ease

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar) regular verb; frequently used in palliative care ('cuidados paliativos') to describe easing symptoms without curing the root cause.

Ejemplo

Los cuidados paliativos buscan paliar el sufrimiento físico y emocional de los enfermos terminales.

Palliative care seeks to ease the physical and emotional suffering of terminally ill patients.

prescribir

to prescribe

📌 Nota gramatical

Third conjugation (-ir) regular verb; shares the exact same participle irregular form history as escribir ('prescrito'), though 'prescripto' is also found in some regions.

Ejemplo

El psiquiatra le prescribió un tratamiento farmacológico innovador para regular sus cambios de humor.

The psychiatrist prescribed him an innovative pharmacological treatment to regulate his mood swings.

recaer

to relapse / to fall back

📌 Nota gramatical

Second conjugation (-er) irregular verb; compound of 'caer', adding an extra 'y' in the preterite 3rd person (recayó, recayeron) and present subjunctive.

Ejemplo

Desafortunadamente, el paciente volvió a consumir sustancias adictivas y experimentó una fuerte recaída; es decir, volvió a recaer.

Unfortunately, the patient started using addictive substances again and experienced a strong relapse; that is, he relapsed again.

reprimir

to repress / to suppress

📌 Nota gramatical

Third conjugation (-ir) regular verb; frequently used in psychoanalysis regarding emotions or memories kept out of conscious awareness.

Ejemplo

Según la teoría freudiana, reprimir los traumas infantiles puede generar trastornos psicológicos graves en la edad adulta.

According to Freudian theory, repressing childhood traumas can generate serious psychological disorders in adulthood.

somatizar

to somatize

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar) regular verb; takes a spelling change in the preterite 'yo' form (somatisé -> somaticé) to preserve pronunciation.

Ejemplo

Muchas personas estresadas suelen somatizar sus problemas emocionales sufriendo dolores físicos inexplicables.

Many stressed people tend to somatize their emotional problems by suffering inexplicable physical pains.

superar

to overcome / to get over

📌 Nota gramatical

First conjugation (-ar) regular verb; extensively used in psychological recovery, coping mechanisms, and successfully dealing with clinical disorders.

Ejemplo

Con la ayuda de un buen terapeuta, logró superar sus ataques de pánico en menos de un año.

With the help of a good therapist, she managed to overcome her panic attacks in less than a year.
