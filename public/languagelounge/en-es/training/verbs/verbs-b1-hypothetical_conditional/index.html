Verbs for Hypotheses & Wishes

desear

to desire / to wish

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; takes the subjunctive mood in subordinate clauses when expressing wishes for others.

Ejemplo

Deseo que tengas mucho éxito en tu nuevo proyecto profesional.

I wish that you have a lot of success in your new professional project.

esperar

to hope / to expect

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; key verb for expressing expectations and future hopes.

Ejemplo

Espero que el clima mejore antes de nuestra excursión del sábado.

I hope that the weather improves before our Saturday excursion.

imaginar

to imagine

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; often used reflexively ('imaginarse') to express mental hypotheses or scenarios.

Ejemplo

Imagino que terminarás el informe antes de la reunión de mañana.

I imagine that you will finish the report before tomorrow's meeting.

suponer

to suppose / to assume

📌 Nota gramatical

-er verb with a 'g' in the yo present form (supongo) and irregular future/conditional stems (supondr-).

Ejemplo

Supongo que ellos llegarán tarde debido al tráfico en la autopista.

I suppose that they will arrive late due to the traffic on the highway.

creer

to believe / to think

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -er verb; spelling change in pretérito indefinido (ió -> yó).

Ejemplo

Creo que ganaremos el partido si jugamos con tanta energía como ayer.

I believe we will win the match if we play with as much energy as yesterday.

sospechar

to suspect

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; used to express cautious hypotheses or intuitions.

Ejemplo

Sospecho que alguien ha entrado en la oficina durante el fin de semana.

I suspect that someone has entered the office during the weekend.

dudar

to doubt

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; triggers the subjunctive in affirmative sentences ('dudo que...') because it denotes uncertainty.

Ejemplo

Dudo que este método funcione tan bien como prometen los anuncios.

I doubt that this method works as well as the advertisements promise.

parecer

to seem / to appear

📌 Nota gramatical

-cer verb requiring 'zc' in the yo present form (parezco); often used impersonally ('parece que').

Ejemplo

Parece que lloverá esta tarde, así que deberíamos llevar paraguas.

It seems that it will rain this afternoon, so we should bring umbrellas.

calcular

to calculate / to estimate

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; used for quantitative hypotheses and estimations.

Ejemplo

Calculo que tardaremos unas tres horas en llegar a la cima de la montaña.

I estimate that we will take about three hours to reach the top of the mountain.

considerar

to consider

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; used when evaluating alternatives or forming hypotheses.

Ejemplo

Considero que sería prudente revisar el contrato antes de firmarlo.

I consider that it would be prudent to review the contract before signing it.

preferir

to prefer

📌 Nota gramatical

Stem-changing -ir verb (e > ie in present, e > i in preterite 3rd person).

Ejemplo

Prefiero que me avises con antelación si vas a cambiar los planes.

I prefer that you notify me in advance if you are going to change plans.

apetecer

to feel like / to fancy

📌 Nota gramatical

-cer verb with 'zc' in the yo form; typically used like 'gustar' with indirect object pronouns (e.g., 'me apetece').

Ejemplo

Me apetecería mucho tomar un helado ahora que hace tanto calor.

I would really feel like having an ice cream now that it is so hot.

gustar

to like

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb used with indirect object pronouns; extremely common for expressing personal desires and preferences.

Ejemplo

Me gustaría viajar a Japón el próximo año si ahorro suficiente dinero.

I would like to travel to Japan next year if I save enough money.

ansiar

to yearn for / to long for

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; often requires an accent on the 'i' in certain conjugations (ansío), expressing intense wishes.

Ejemplo

Ansío que llegue el verano para poder descansar en la playa.

I long for summer to arrive so I can rest on the beach.

ambicionar

to aspire to / to crave

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; used for ambitious wishes regarding career or status.

Ejemplo

Ambiciona conseguir un puesto directivo en la multinacional antes de los treinta.

He aspires to get a management position in the multinational before thirty.

planear

to plan / to scheme

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; used for future projects and conditional setups.

Ejemplo

Planeamos hacer un viaje largo por Sudamérica si conseguimos tres semanas libres.

We plan to make a long trip through South America if we get three free weeks.

prever

to foresee / to anticipate

📌 Nota gramatical

Irregular -er verb, conjugated similarly to 'ver' with the prefix 'pre-'.

Ejemplo

Preveo que habrá complicaciones técnicas durante la transmisión en directo.

I foresee that there will be technical complications during the live broadcast.

conjeturar

to conjecture / to guess

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; formal verb used for making educated guesses or hypotheses.

Ejemplo

Los investigadores conjeturan que el manuscrito data del siglo quince.

The researchers conjecture that the manuscript dates from the fifteenth century.

aspirar

to aspire

📌 Nota gramatical

Regular -ar verb; typically followed by the preposition 'a' when expressing career or life goals.

Ejemplo

Aspiro a conseguir una beca para estudiar un máster en el extranjero.

I aspire to get a scholarship to study a master's degree abroad.

deducir

to deduce / to infer

📌 Nota gramatical

-ir verb with 'zc' in the yo present form (deduzco) and irregular preterite stem (deduj-).

Ejemplo

Deduje que estabas enfadado porque no respondiste a mis mensajes.

I deduced that you were angry because you didn't answer my messages.
