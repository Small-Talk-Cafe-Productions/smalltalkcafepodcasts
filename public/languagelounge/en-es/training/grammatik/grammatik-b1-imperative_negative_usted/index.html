Formación del imperativo negativo (tú)

Formation of the negative imperative (tú)

📌 Nota gramatical

To form the negative tú command, take the present subjunctive (yo) form of the verb, drop the -o, and add the opposite vowel: -es for -ar verbs, and -as for -er/-ir verbs. For example, hablar becomes no hables.

Verbos con cambios ortográficos en el imperativo negativo (tú)

Verbs with spelling changes in the negative imperative (tú)

📌 Nota gramatical

Verbs ending in -car, -gar, and -zar undergo spelling changes in the negative tú command to maintain the correct pronunciation before the 'e' of the ending: -car becomes -ces (tocar -> no toques), -gar becomes -gues (llegar -> no llegues), and -zar becomes -ces (empezar -> no empieces).

Ejemplo

Verbos con cambios de raíz (stem-changing) en el imperativo negativo (tú)

Stem-changing verbs in the negative imperative (tú)

📌 Nota gramatical

Verbs that undergo stem changes in the present subjunctive (e.g., e>ie, o>ue, e>i) maintain those exact same stem changes in the negative tú commands because they are derived from the present subjunctive yo form.

Ejemplo

Verbos irregulares en el imperativo negativo (tú)

Irregular verbs in the negative imperative (tú)

📌 Nota gramatical

Verbs with irregular yo forms in the present indicative (like tener, poner, salir, venir, decir, hacer, ser, ir, ver) use their irregular yo subjunctive stems for the negative tú command. For example: tener -> tenga -> no tengas.

Formación de los mandatos formales de usted

Formation of formal usted commands

📌 Nota gramatical

The usted command (both affirmative and negative) is derived from the present subjunctive yo form. Drop the -o and add -e for -ar verbs, and -a for -er/-ir verbs. For negative commands, simply place 'no' before the verb.

Ejemplo

Formación de los mandatos formales de ustedes

Formation of formal ustedes commands

📌 Nota gramatical

The ustedes command follows the same subjunctive root as the usted command, but adds an -n to make it plural: -en for -ar verbs, and -an for -er/-ir verbs. Negative ustedes commands also simply require 'no' in front.

Ejemplo

Colocación de los pronombres con mandatos negativos (tú, usted, ustedes)

Pronoun placement with negative commands (tú, usted, ustedes)

📌 Nota gramatical

Unlike affirmative commands where pronouns are attached to the end, pronouns in ALL negative commands (tú, usted, ustedes, vosotros) MUST be placed before the conjugated verb, directly between 'no' and the verb.

Ejemplo

Mandatos formales con cambios ortográficos (usted / ustedes)

Formal commands with spelling changes (usted / ustedes)

📌 Nota gramatical

Just like the tú form, verbs ending in -car, -gar, and -zar maintain phonetic consistency in usted and ustedes commands by changing to -c-, -gu-, and -c- respectively before the endings (-e/-en or -a/-an).

Ejemplo

Verbos irregulares comunes en usted y ustedes

Common irregular verbs in usted and ustedes

📌 Nota gramatical

Important irregular verbs like ser, ir, dar, estar, and saber have completely unique forms derived from their subjunctive paradigms: ser (no sea / no sean), ir (no vaya / no vayan), dar (no dé / no deuden? No, no den), estar (no esté / no estén), saber (no sepa / no sepan).

Ejemplo

Mandatos negativos con el pronombre reflexivo

Negative commands with reflexive pronouns

📌 Nota gramatical

When dealing with reflexive verbs in negative commands, the reflexive pronoun (te, se) precedes the verb, positioned between 'no' and the conjugated subjunctive verb form.

Ejemplo

Uso del imperativo negativo en consejos médicos y profesionales

Use of the negative imperative in medical and professional advice

📌 Nota gramatical

At level B1, you will frequently use usted/ustedes negative commands in professional settings, such as giving health instructions, workplace safety guidelines, or service recommendations.

Combinación de pronombres de objeto directo e indirecto (le/lo)

Combination of direct and indirect object pronouns (le/lo)

📌 Nota gramatical

When both indirect and direct object pronouns appear with a negative command, the indirect pronoun comes first, followed by the direct pronoun (ID: Indirect-Direct). If both start with 'l' (like le lo), change the first 'le/les' to 'se'.
