Introducción a la nominalización

Introduction to nominalisation: transforming verbs into nouns

📌 Nota gramatical

Nominalisation is the process of turning verbs or adjectives into nouns. In formal Spanish, this shifts focus from actions to concepts, creating a more objective and concise tone typical of reports, academic writing, and official correspondence. ⚠️ Using personal verb clauses in formal essays where a nominalised phrase would be more concise and objective (e.g., writing 'cuando decidimos' instead of 'en el momento de la decisión').

Ejemplo

La reducción de los costes operativos mejoró los beneficios de la empresa.

The reduction of operational costs improved the company's profits.

Sufijos nominales comunes: -ción / -sión

Common noun suffixes: -ción / -sión for action nouns

📌 Nota gramatical

Verbs ending in -ar, -er, or -ir frequently form feminine abstract nouns using the suffixes -ción or -sión. These are essential for converting dynamic processes into static concepts in formal texts. ⚠️ Incorrectly applying the suffix or changing the gender of the resulting noun (e.g., writing 'el aprobación' instead of 'la aprobación').

Ejemplo

La aprobación del presupuesto se retrasó debido a varios desacuerdos.

The approval of the budget was delayed due to several disagreements.

Sufijos nominales: -miento y -ncia

Noun suffixes: -miento and -ncia for states and actions

📌 Nota gramatical

Verbs often take the suffix -miento (derived from verbs like crecer -> crecimiento) or -ncia/ -anza (derived from adjectives or verbs like existir -> existencia) to denote states, qualities, or prolonged actions. ⚠️ Confusing -miento with -mienta or misspelling words ending in -ncia with -nsia.

Ejemplo

El ayuntamiento anunció el mantenimiento de las zonas verdes este verano.

The city council announced the maintenance of the green areas this summer.

Eliminación de sujetos personales (Se impersonal / Pasiva refleja)

Elimination of personal subjects using impersonal 'se' or reflexive passive

📌 Nota gramatical

Formal writing avoids personal pronouns ('yo', 'tú', 'nosotros') to maintain objectivity. Instead, the impersonal 'se' or the passive refleja is used to state facts, rules, or general observations. ⚠️ Failing to make the verb agree with the direct object in the passive reflexive structure (e.g., writing 'Se vende piso' instead of the plural 'Se venden pisos' when multiple apartments are sold).

Ejemplo

Se requiere experiencia previa en la gestión de proyectos informáticos.

Previous experience in IT project management is required.

Uso de la primera persona del plural (Plural de modestia)

Use of the first-person plural ('plural de modestia') in formal discourse

📌 Nota gramatical

When an author needs to include themselves in an academic or professional text, they use 'nosotros' (first-person plural) instead of 'yo'. This inclusive/modest plural diffuses personal responsibility and engages the reader. ⚠️ Switching inconsistently between the impersonal 'se', the 'plural de modestia', and singular 'yo' within the same paragraph.

Ejemplo

A continuación, analizaremos los resultados obtenidos en el estudio de mercado.

Next, we will analyse the results obtained in the market research study.

Construcciones con infinitivo en lugar de oraciones subordinadas

Infinitive constructions replacing subordinate clauses

📌 Nota gramatical

Formal Spanish frequently condenses subordinate clauses ('cuando', 'porque', 'para que') into prepositional infinitive phrases (e.g., al + infinitive, tras + infinitive, con el fin de + infinitive). ⚠️ Using an incorrect preposition before the infinitive (e.g., using 'para' instead of 'con el fin de' or confusing 'al + inf' with 'cuando').

Ejemplo

Tras finalizar la reunión, el director firmó los nuevos acuerdos comerciales.

After finishing the meeting, the director signed the new trade agreements.

Sustitución léxica: Verbos comodín por verbos precisos

Lexical substitution: replacing generic verbs with precise formal verbs

📌 Nota gramatical

In formal writing, generic verbs like tener, hacer, poner, or decir are replaced by precise equivalents such as disponer de, realizar, establecer, or manifestar. ⚠️ Relying on conversational verbs like 'hacer' or 'tener' in professional documents instead of 'efectuar' or 'contar con'.

Ejemplo

La empresa realizó una inversión significativa en investigación y desarrollo.

The company made a significant investment in research and development.

Conectores discursivos formales (Causa y Consecuencia)

Formal discourse markers for cause and consequence

📌 Nota gramatical

To link complex ideas formally, standard connectors like porque or así que are replaced by advanced equivalents such as debido a, a causa de, por lo tanto, en consecuencia, or por consiguiente. ⚠️ Using causal connectors incorrectly (e.g., placing 'porque' at the beginning of a standalone formal sentence instead of 'debido a que').

Ejemplo

Las ventas disminuyeron considerablemente; por lo tanto, se redujo el presupuesto.

Sales decreased considerably; therefore, the budget was reduced.

Conectores discursivos formales (Oposición y Concesión)

Formal discourse markers for contrast and concession

📌 Nota gramatical

Expressing formal contrast requires shifting from basic words like pero to advanced connectors and transition phrases such as sin embargo, no obstante, en cambio, a pesar de, or por el contrario. ⚠️ Using 'aunque' with indicative when a subjunctive or a nominalised structure like 'a pesar de' is required for formal nuance.

Ejemplo

El plan inicial parecía viable; no obstante, surgieron complicaciones técnicas imprevistas.

The initial plan seemed viable; however, unforeseen technical complications arose.

Evitar el tuteo: Fórmulas de cortesía (Usted / Ustedes)

Avoiding familiar address: formal courtesy formulas (Usted / Ustedes)

📌 Nota gramatical

Formal correspondence demands the third-person pronouns usted (singular) and ustedes (plural in Spain, universal in Latin America) alongside third-person verb conjugations to maintain professional distance and respect. ⚠️ Mixing 'tú' and 'usted' forms within the same professional email or letter.

Ejemplo

Le rogamos que nos envíe su currículum actualizado a la mayor brevedad.

We kindly request that you send us your updated CV as soon as possible.

Estructuras enfáticas y de relieve (Cleft sentences formales)

Formal cleft sentences and focalising structures

📌 Nota gramatical

To highlight specific information in formal texts without sounding colloquial, writers use cleft structures such as lo que + verb + es que, or prepositional phrases like es por ello por lo que. ⚠️ Translating English cleft structures literally (e.g., trying to use literal 'es... que' constructions without adjusting Spanish prepositions and relative pronouns).

Ejemplo

Lo que resulta verdaderamente preocupante es la caída constante de la productividad.

What is truly worrying is the constant drop in productivity.

Redacción sintética: Evitar la redundancia y la hiperexpresividad

Synthetic writing: avoiding redundancy and hyper-expressivity

📌 Nota gramatical

Formal register prizes economy of language. Adverbs ending in -mente can often be condensed, redundant adjectives removed, and overly emotional modifiers replaced with measured, factual terminology. ⚠️ Overloading sentences with too many adverbs ending in '-mente' in a single sentence, which disrupts the formal rhythm of Spanish.

Ejemplo

El informe demuestra claramente la viabilidad del proyecto propuesto.

The report clearly demonstrates the feasibility of the proposed project.

Contrast rule and common errors: Formal Written Register & Nominalisation

📌 Nota gramatical

Key contrast in Formal Written Register & Nominalisation: Pay special attention to distinguishing core forms (Introducción a la nominalización, Sufijos nominales: -miento y -ncia, Eliminación de sujetos personales, Uso de la primera persona del plural) from their false friends or overlapping structures. Primary pitfall: Using personal verb clauses in formal essays where a nominalised phrase would be more concise and objective (e.g., writing 'cuando decidimos' instead of 'en el momento de la decisión'). ⚠️ Avoid the common error: Using personal verb clauses in formal essays where a nominalised phrase would be more concise and objective (e.g., writing 'cuando decidimos' instead of 'en el momento de la decisión').

Ejemplo

Posición sintáctica y orden de las palabras: El registro escrito formal y la nominalización

Word order and sentence structure: Formal Written Register & Nominalisation

📌 Nota gramatical

Word order and sentence structure rules for Formal Written Register & Nominalisation (B2): In main clauses, subordinate clauses, and with negation or adverbial phrases, ensure Introducción a la nominalización, Sufijos nominales: -miento y -ncia, Eliminación de sujetos personales, Uso de la primera persona del plural occupy their strict grammatical position. ⚠️ Misplacing elements relative to the conjugated verb, negation particle, or clause boundary.

Ejemplo

La aprobación del presupuesto se retrasó debido a varios desacuerdos.

The approval of the budget was delayed due to several disagreements.

Registro formal vs. uso coloquial: El registro escrito formal y la nominalización

Formal register vs. colloquial spoken usage: Formal Written Register & Nominalisation

📌 Nota gramatical

Register and usage nuance (B2): While everyday spoken communication frequently uses conversational shortcuts, formal written texts and CEFR B2 exams require precise adherence to Formal Written Register & Nominalisation. ⚠️ Using overly colloquial shortcuts in formal written contexts or stiff bookish phrasing where natural flow is required.

Ejemplo

El ayuntamiento anunció el mantenimiento de las zonas verdes este verano.

The city council announced the maintenance of the green areas this summer.

Regla de decisión y síntesis práctica: El registro escrito formal y la nominalización

Decision framework and practical summary: Formal Written Register & Nominalisation

📌 Nota gramatical

Decision framework for Formal Written Register & Nominalisation (B2): Before producing a sentence, verify 1) grammatical agreement (gender, number, person), 2) tense/mood triggers, and 3) exact word order. ⚠️ Producing clauses without checking case, mood triggers, or subject-verb agreement.

Ejemplo

Se requiere experiencia previa en la gestión de proyectos informáticos.

Previous experience in IT project management is required.
