Connectors & Discourse Markers

Además (Addition)

Furthermore / Moreover / In addition

📌 Nota gramatical

Use 'además' or 'además de' to add information to a previous statement. 'Además' stands alone as an introductory adverb, while 'además de' functions as a preposition followed by a noun or an infinitive verb ('además de trabajar').

Ejemplo

Sin embargo (Contrast)

However / Nevertheless

📌 Nota gramatical

'Sin embargo' introduces an adversative contrast to the preceding idea, equivalent to 'however' in English. It is almost always set off by commas when placed within or at the beginning of a sentence.

Ejemplo

Por lo tanto (Cause & Effect)

Therefore / Consequently

📌 Nota gramatical

'Por lo tanto' expresses a logical consequence or conclusion resulting from the preceding clause. It is formally equivalent to 'therefore' or 'consequently' and requires a comma or pause in speech.

Ejemplo

Por un lado... por otro lado (Structuring arguments)

On the one hand... on the other hand

📌 Nota gramatical

This correlative marker is used to present two contrasting aspects, perspectives, or arguments of a topic. Variations include 'por una parte... por otra parte'.

Ejemplo

Por eso (Cause & Effect / Informal)

That's why / Therefore

📌 Nota gramatical

'Por eso' is a very common, slightly less formal connector used to introduce a consequence or reason. It links clauses dynamically without needing heavy punctuation.

Ejemplo

En primer lugar / En segundo lugar (Sequencing)

Firstly / Secondly

📌 Nota gramatical

Discourse markers for enumeration help organize ideas chronologically or logically in a speech or essay. They guide the listener smoothly from one point to the next.

Ejemplo

En cambio / Por el contrario (Opposition)

Instead / On the contrary / By contrast

📌 Nota gramatical

These markers indicate a direct opposition or alternative between two facts or subjects. 'En cambio' is slightly softer ('conversely' or 'meanwhile'), while 'por el contrario' emphasizes direct contradiction.

Ejemplo

A pesar de (Concession)

Despite / In spite of

📌 Nota gramatical

'A pesar de' is a prepositional phrase expressing concession. It must be followed by a noun, a pronoun, or an infinitive verb (e.g., 'a pesar de tener...'). For clauses with conjugated verbs, use 'a pesar de que'.

Ejemplo

Aunque (Concession / Contrast)

Although / Even though / Even if

📌 Nota gramatical

'Aunque' introduces a concessive clause. When it means 'although' (fact), it takes the indicative if the fact is known, or the subjunctive if it introduces a hypothetical condition ('even if').

Ejemplo

O sea / Es decir (Clarification)

In other words / That is to say

📌 Nota gramatical

These markers are used to rephrase, explain, or clarify a preceding statement. 'Es decir' is somewhat more formal, while 'o sea' is very common in spoken conversational Spanish.

Ejemplo

Por ejemplo (Illustration)

For example

📌 Nota gramatical

Use 'por ejemplo' to introduce an illustrative example of a general category, concept, or rule just mentioned. It is usually set off with commas.

Ejemplo

Por último / Finalmente (Conclusion)

Finally / Lastly

📌 Nota gramatical

These discourse markers signal the final point in a list of arguments, a speech, or a set of instructions, equivalent to 'finally' or 'lastly' in English.

Ejemplo

Configurar entrenamiento

Configura tu sesión de entrenamiento.
