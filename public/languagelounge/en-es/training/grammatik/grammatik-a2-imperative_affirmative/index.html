Imperativo de tú (verbos regulares)

Affirmative imperative for 'tú' (regular verbs)

📌 Nota gramatical

To give an affirmative command to someone you address as 'tú', use the third-person singular (él/ella) form of the present indicative. For -ar verbs, use -a; for -er and -ir verbs, use -e. ⚠️ Using the present subjunctive form (hables, comas) instead of the third-person indicative form (habla, come) for affirmative tú commands.

Ejemplo

Habla más despacio, por favor.

Speak more slowly, please.

Imperativo irregular de tú (8 verbos principales)

Irregular 'tú' imperative (8 main verbs)

📌 Nota gramatical

Eight common verbs have short, irregular forms for the affirmative 'tú' command: decir (di), hacer (haz), ir (ve), poner (pon), salir (sal), ser (sé), tener (ten), venir (ven). Memory aid: 'Pon Sal Haz Ve Di Ten Sé Ven'. ⚠️ Applying regular conjugation rules to these common verbs (e.g., saying hace or ponne instead of haz and pon).

Ejemplo

Haz la tarea esta tarde.

Do the homework this afternoon.

Imperativo de usted (cortesía)

Affirmative imperative for 'usted' (formal)

📌 Nota gramatical

To command someone politely using 'usted', take the first-person singular (yo) of the present indicative, drop the -o, and add the opposite vowel: -e/-en for -ar verbs, and -a/-an for -er/-ir verbs. ⚠️ Using the infinitive or the 'tú' command form when trying to speak formally to a client or stranger.

Ejemplo

pase por aquí, por favor.

Step this way, please.

Imperativo de vosotros (peninsular)

Affirmative imperative for 'vosotros' (plural informal)

📌 Nota gramatical

To address a group informally in Spain, take the infinitive of the verb, drop the final -r, and add a -d. Example: hablar -> hablad, comer -> comed, vivir -> vivid. ⚠️ Dropping the final -d and mistakenly adding an -r, or using the infinitive (e.g., hablar instead of hablad).

Ejemplo

Escuchad la música con atención.

Listen to the music carefully.

Imperativo de ustedes (plural formal/América Latina)

Affirmative imperative for 'ustedes' (formal plural)

📌 Nota gramatical

The 'ustedes' command uses the third-person plural of the present subjunctive. It is formed by taking the 'yo' form of the present indicative, dropping the -o, and adding -en (for -ar verbs) or -an (for -er/-ir verbs). ⚠️ Using the infinitive verb form to give orders to a group instead of the subjunctive-derived imperative.

Ejemplo

Siéntense en la mesa cinco.

Sit down at table five.

Pronombres pegados (enclíticos) en afirmativo

Attached pronouns (enclitics) in affirmative commands

📌 Nota gramatical

In affirmative commands, direct, indirect, and reflexive pronouns must be attached directly to the end of the verb. Count back syllables to determine if a written accent mark (tilde) is needed. ⚠️ Placing object pronouns before the affirmative imperative verb (e.g., lo compra instead of cómpralo).

Ejemplo

Lávate las manos antes de comer.

Wash your hands before eating.

Verbos con cambio vocálico (e->ie, o->ue)

Stem-changing verbs in the imperative

📌 Nota gramatical

Verbs that undergo stem changes in the present indicative (e->ie, o->ue, e->i) keep these same vowel changes in the 'tú', 'usted', 'vosotros', and 'ustedes' affirmative imperative forms. ⚠️ Keeping the stem unchanged in verbs like volver or entender (volve instead of vuelve).

Ejemplo

Enciende la luz, por favor.

Turn on the light, please.

Verbos con cambio ortográfico (-car, -gar, -zar)

Spelling-change verbs (-car, -gar, -zar) in formal commands

📌 Nota gramatical

In formal commands (usted/ustedes) and negative forms, verbs ending in -car, -gar, and -zar change their spelling before 'e' to preserve the pronunciation: c->qu, g->gu, z->c. ⚠️ Maintaining the original letter before 'e' (e.g., writing buse instead of busque), altering the pronunciation.

Ejemplo

Busque su pasaporte en la maleta.

Look for your passport in the suitcase.

Acentuación con pronombres enclíticos

Accentuation with attached pronouns

📌 Nota gramatical

When pronouns are attached to affirmative commands, the length of the word increases. To keep the original stress, a written accent mark (tilde) is added if the word ends in a vowel, -n, or -s and violates natural stress rules. ⚠️ Forgetting to add the written accent mark when attaching one or two pronouns to the verb.

Ejemplo

Dámelo, necesito ese libro.

Give it to me, I need that book.

El imperativo con verbos reflexivos (vosotros)

Reflexive verbs in the 'vosotros' imperative

📌 Nota gramatical

When attaching the reflexive pronoun 'os' to the 'vosotros' affirmative imperative, drop the final -d of the verb (except with 'irirse', which becomes 'idos'). Example: levantaros -> levantaos. ⚠️ Keeping the final -d when attaching 'os' (e.g., levantadros instead of levantaos).

Ejemplo

Levantaos temprano mañana por favor.

Get up early tomorrow please.

Verbos irregulares de usted y ustedes

Irregular verbs for 'usted' and 'ustedes'

📌 Nota gramatical

Verbs that are irregular in the 'yo' present indicative (like tener, poner, salir) maintain that irregularity in formal commands because these commands derive from the 'yo' form. Irregular subjunctives like ser (sea), ir (vaya), saber (sepa), dar (dé) have special forms. ⚠️ Applying regular -ar/-er endings to verbs like ser or ir when making formal commands (seje instead of sea).

Ejemplo

Sea paciente con los niños.

Be patient with the children.

Uso práctico del imperativo en instrucciones

Practical use of the imperative in instructions

📌 Nota gramatical

The affirmative imperative is frequently used at level A2 for recipes, giving directions, classroom instructions, and friendly advice. Tone changes depending on whether you use tú or usted. ⚠️ Mixing tú and usted forms inconsistently within the same set of instructions or directions.

Ejemplo

Corta las patatas y añade sal.

Cut the potatoes and add salt.

Regla de contraste y errores frecuentes: El imperativo afirmativo

Contrast rule and common errors: The Affirmative Imperative

📌 Nota gramatical

Key contrast in The Affirmative Imperative: Pay special attention to distinguishing core forms (Imperativo de tú, Imperativo irregular de tú, Imperativo de usted, Imperativo de vosotros) from their false friends or overlapping structures. Primary pitfall: Using the present subjunctive form (hables, comas) instead of the third-person indicative form (habla, come) for affirmative tú commands. ⚠️ Avoid the common error: Using the present subjunctive form (hables, comas) instead of the third-person indicative form (habla, come) for affirmative tú commands.

Ejemplo

Habla más despacio, por favor.

Speak more slowly, please.

Posición sintáctica y orden de las palabras: El imperativo afirmativo

Word order and sentence structure: The Affirmative Imperative

📌 Nota gramatical

Word order and sentence structure rules for The Affirmative Imperative (A2): In main clauses, subordinate clauses, and with negation or adverbial phrases, ensure Imperativo de tú, Imperativo irregular de tú, Imperativo de usted, Imperativo de vosotros occupy their strict grammatical position. ⚠️ Misplacing elements relative to the conjugated verb, negation particle, or clause boundary.

Ejemplo

Haz la tarea esta tarde.

Do the homework this afternoon.

Registro formal vs. uso coloquial: El imperativo afirmativo

Formal register vs. colloquial spoken usage: The Affirmative Imperative

📌 Nota gramatical

Register and usage nuance (A2): While everyday spoken communication frequently uses conversational shortcuts, formal written texts and CEFR A2 exams require precise adherence to The Affirmative Imperative. ⚠️ Using overly colloquial shortcuts in formal written contexts or stiff bookish phrasing where natural flow is required.

Ejemplo

pase por aquí, por favor.

Step this way, please.

Regla de decisión y síntesis práctica: El imperativo afirmativo

Decision framework and practical summary: The Affirmative Imperative

📌 Nota gramatical

Decision framework for The Affirmative Imperative (A2): Before producing a sentence, verify 1) grammatical agreement (gender, number, person), 2) tense/mood triggers, and 3) exact word order. ⚠️ Producing clauses without checking case, mood triggers, or subject-verb agreement.

Ejemplo

Escuchad la música con atención.

Listen to the music carefully.
