Le féminin régulier des adjectifs

Regular feminine formation of adjectives

📌 Note de grammaire

To form the feminine of most French adjectives, add an unvoiced 'e' to the masculine singular form. If the masculine form already ends in an 'e', it remains unchanged in the feminine. ⚠️ Forgetting to add the 'e' to feminine nouns (e.g., La table est petit), ignoring gender agreement entirely.

Exemple

La table est petite.

The table is small.

Le pluriel régulier des adjectifs

Regular plural formation of adjectives

📌 Note de grammaire

To form the plural of most French adjectives, add an 's' to the singular form (both masculine and feminine). The 's' is typically silent when spoken. ⚠️ Writing singular adjectives with plural nouns or adding 's' to the noun only while leaving the adjective singular.

Exemple

Les chats sont petits.

The cats are small.

Adjectifs en -e au masculin

Adjectives ending in -e in the masculine

📌 Note de grammaire

Adjectives that already end in an unstressed 'e' in the masculine singular do not change their spelling in the feminine singular. They take only an 's' for the plural. ⚠️ Adding an extra 'e' to adjectives that already end in 'e' (e.g., writing jeunee).

Exemple

Le garçon est jeune.

The boy is young.

Adjectifs en -eux

Adjectives ending in -eux

📌 Note de grammaire

Adjectives ending in '-eux' in the masculine singular change their ending to '-euse' in the feminine singular. The plural forms add 's' (masculine remains '-eux', feminine becomes '-euses'). ⚠️ Applying the regular rule by simply adding an 'e' to the masculine form (e.g., heureuxe) instead of changing '-eux' to '-euse'.

Exemple

Il est très heureux.

He is very happy.

Adjectifs en -f

Adjectives ending in -f

📌 Note de grammaire

Adjectives ending in '-f' in the masculine singular change their ending to '-ve' in the feminine singular. Plural forms add an 's' to these respective bases. ⚠️ Writing sportiffe by treating '-f' like a standard consonant and simply adding '-e'.

Exemple

Mon frère est sportif.

My brother is athletic.

Adjectifs doublant la consonne

Adjectives doubling the final consonant (-el, -en, -et, -on)

📌 Note de grammaire

Many adjectives ending in '-el', '-en', '-et', or '-on' double their final consonant before adding the feminine 'e' (e.g., bon becomes bonne, ancien becomes ancienne). ⚠️ Failing to double the final consonant in the feminine form (e.g., writing bone instead of bonne).

Exemple

Le café est bon.

The coffee is good.

La règle générale de position (après le nom)

General adjective position (after the noun)

📌 Note de grammaire

Unlike English, most French adjectives come after the noun they modify. This includes colors, nationalities, shapes, and religious or physical descriptions. ⚠️ Placing color or nationality adjectives before the noun (e.g., une rouge voiture), transferring English word order directly.

Exemple

J'aime une voiture rouge.

I like a red car.

Les adjectifs avant le nom (BANGS)

Adjectives before the noun (BANGS)

📌 Note de grammaire

Common, short, high-frequency adjectives usually go before the noun. Remember the acronym BANGS: Beauty, Age, Number, Goodness, Size (e.g., beau, jeune, vieux, bon, grand, petit). ⚠️ Placing adjectives like grand or petit after the noun (un chien grand), which sounds unnatural in standard French.

Exemple

C'est une belle maison.

It is a beautiful house.

Formes spéciales au masculin singulier (beau, nouveau, vieux)

Special masculine singular forms (beau, nouveau, vieux)

📌 Note de grammaire

Before masculine singular nouns starting with a vowel sound or silent 'h', adjectives like beau, nouveau, and vieux change to bel, nouvel, and vieil to ease pronunciation. ⚠️ Using beau or nouveau directly before a vowel-starting masculine noun (e.g., un beau ami instead of un bel ami).

Exemple

C'est un bel appartement.

It is a beautiful apartment.

L'accord avec les pluriels mixtes

Agreement with mixed-gender plurals

📌 Note de grammaire

When an adjective modifies multiple nouns of different genders, the masculine plural form takes precedence over the feminine. ⚠️ Using a feminine plural adjective when even one masculine noun is present in the coordinated subject group.

Exemple

Le chat et la table sont petits.

The cat and the table are small.

Le pluriel des adjectifs en -al

Plural of adjectives ending in -al

📌 Note de grammaire

Most adjectives ending in '-al' in the masculine singular change their plural masculine ending to '-aux' (e.g., général becomes généraux, national becomes nationaux). ⚠️ Simply adding an 's' to words ending in '-al' (e.g., writing traditionnals instead of traditionnels).

Exemple

Ce sont des plats traditionnels.

These are traditional dishes.

Adjectifs invariables en couleur (orange, marron)

Invariable color adjectives (orange, marron)

📌 Note de grammaire

Adjectives derived from fruits or items, such as orange and marron, do not change for gender or number. They remain identical regardless of the noun they modify. ⚠️ Adding an 's' or feminine 'e' to orange or marron (e.g., des chaussures marrones), treating them like regular adjectives.

Exemple

J'ai des chaussures marron.

I have brown shoes.

Ordre des mots et position syntaxique: Accord et position des adjectifs

Word order and sentence structure: Adjective Agreement & Position

📌 Note de grammaire

Word order and sentence structure rules for Adjective Agreement & Position (A1): In main clauses, subordinate clauses, and with negation or adverbial phrases, ensure Le féminin régulier des adjectifs, Le pluriel régulier des adjectifs, Adjectifs en -e au masculin, Adjectifs en -eux occupy their strict grammatical position. ⚠️ Misplacing elements relative to the conjugated verb, negation particle, or clause boundary.

Exemple

Les chats sont petits.

The cats are small.

Registre formel vs usage courant: Accord et position des adjectifs

Formal register vs. colloquial spoken usage: Adjective Agreement & Position

📌 Note de grammaire

Register and usage nuance (A1): While everyday spoken communication frequently uses conversational shortcuts, formal written texts and CEFR A1 exams require precise adherence to Adjective Agreement & Position. ⚠️ Using overly colloquial shortcuts in formal written contexts or stiff bookish phrasing where natural flow is required.

Exemple

Le garçon est jeune.

The boy is young.

Règle de décision et synthèse pratique: Accord et position des adjectifs

Decision framework and practical summary: Adjective Agreement & Position

📌 Note de grammaire

Decision framework for Adjective Agreement & Position (A1): Before producing a sentence, verify 1) grammatical agreement (gender, number, person), 2) tense/mood triggers, and 3) exact word order. ⚠️ Producing clauses without checking case, mood triggers, or subject-verb agreement.

Exemple

Il est très heureux.

He is very happy.
