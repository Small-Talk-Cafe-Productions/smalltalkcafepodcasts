Health & Medicine

ordonnance

prescription

📌 Note de grammaire

Feminine noun; often used with the verb 'rédiger' or 'donner'.

Exemple

Le médecin m'a rédigé une ordonnance pour des antibiotiques.

The doctor wrote me a prescription for antibiotics.

symptôme

symptom

📌 Note de grammaire

Masculine noun; note the spelling with a circumflex accent.

Exemple

Si vos symptômes persistent pendant plus de trois jours, consultez un médecin.

If your symptoms persist for more than three days, consult a doctor.

fièvre

fever

📌 Note de grammaire

Feminine noun; used with the partitive article 'de la' when expressing 'to have a fever'.

Exemple

Mon fils a de la fièvre, alors je vais lui prendre sa température.

My son has a fever, so I am going to take his temperature.

toux

cough

📌 Note de grammaire

Feminine noun; invariable in spelling for the plural, though 'les toux' is rarely used.

Exemple

Cette toux sèche m'empêche de dormir correctement la nuit.

This dry cough prevents me from sleeping properly at night.

allergie

allergy

📌 Note de grammaire

Feminine noun; starts with a mute h/vowel requiring the elided article 'l'.

Exemple

Elle souffre d'une grave allergie aux arachides depuis son enfance.

She has been suffering from a severe peanut allergy since childhood.

traitement

treatment

📌 Note de grammaire

Masculine noun derived from the verb 'traiter'.

Exemple

Le nouveau traitement médical donne de très bons résultats sur les patients.

The new medical treatment is yielding very good results on the patients.

infirmier

nurse

📌 Note de grammaire

Masculine form; the feminine equivalent is 'l'infirmière'.

Exemple

L'infirmier est venu pour me faire une prise de sang ce matin.

The nurse came to take my blood test this morning.

urgences

emergency room

📌 Note de grammaire

Normally used in the plural form when referring to the hospital department.

Exemple

En cas de douleur thoracique intense, vous devez vous rendre aux urgences.

In case of severe chest pain, you must go to the emergency room.

guérir

to heal / to cure

📌 Note de grammaire

Second-group regular verb (-ir); can be used transitively or intransitively.

Exemple

Il a fallu deux semaines pour guérir complètement de cette mauvaise grippe.

It took two weeks to completely recover from this bad flu.

soulager

to relieve / to soothe

📌 Note de grammaire

First-group verb with a cedilla (ç) before 'a' and 'o' to keep the soft 's' sound.

Exemple

Ce médicament m'aide à soulager la douleur dans le bas du dos.

This medicine helps me relieve the pain in my lower back.

consulter

to consult (a doctor)

📌 Note de grammaire

Regular -er verb; directly takes the person consulted as a direct object without preposition.

Exemple

Il est important de consulter un spécialiste si la douleur persiste.

It is important to consult a specialist if the pain persists.

blesser

to injure / to hurt

📌 Note de grammaire

Often used reflexively ('se blesser') when hurting oneself.

Exemple

Il s'est blessé au genou en jouant au football avec ses amis.

He injured his knee while playing soccer with his friends.

fatigué

tired / fatigued

📌 Note de grammaire

Past participle used as an adjective; agrees in gender and number with the subject.

Exemple

Je me sens très fatigué ces derniers temps à cause du stress au travail.

I have been feeling very tired lately because of stress at work.

médicament

medicine / medication

📌 Note de grammaire

Masculine noun; commonly used in plural to refer to one's regimen.

Exemple

N'oubliez pas de prendre vos médicaments avec un grand verre d'eau.

Do not forget to take your medication with a large glass of water.

pharmacie

pharmacy / drugstore

📌 Note de grammaire

Feminine noun; uses preposition 'à la' for locations ('aller à la pharmacie').

Exemple

Je vais passer à la pharmacie pour acheter des pastilles pour la gorge.

I am going to stop by the pharmacy to buy some throat lozenges.

mutuelle

supplementary health insurance

📌 Note de grammaire

Feminine noun; a crucial concept in the French healthcare system.

Exemple

Ma mutuelle rembourse une grande partie des frais dentaires et optiques.

My supplementary health insurance reimburses a large part of dental and optical costs.

rendez-vous

appointment

📌 Note de grammaire

Masculine compound noun; invariable in the plural ('les rendez-vous').

Exemple

J'ai pris un rendez-vous chez le dentiste pour un contrôle annuel.

I made an appointment at the dentist's office for an annual check-up.

pouls

pulse

📌 Note de grammaire

Masculine noun with a silent 's' in both singular and plural.

Exemple

L'infirmière a pris mon pouls pour vérifier que tout allait bien.

The nurse took my pulse to check that everything was fine.

tension

blood pressure / tension

📌 Note de grammaire

Feminine noun; 'tension artérielle' specifically means blood pressure.

Exemple

Le médecin m'a mesuré la tension artérielle pendant la consultation.

The doctor measured my blood pressure during the consultation.

gorge

throat

📌 Note de grammaire

Feminine noun; used in the idiom 'avoir mal à la' + body part.

Exemple

J'ai mal à la gorge quand j'essaie de avaler quelque chose.

My throat hurts when I try to swallow something.

opération

surgery / operation

📌 Note de grammaire

Feminine noun starting with a vowel; uses the verb 'subir' for undergoing surgery.

Exemple

Il devra subir une opération chirurgicale la semaine prochaine à l'hôpital.

He will have to undergo surgical surgery next week at the hospital.

cabinet

doctor's office / practice

📌 Note de grammaire

Masculine noun; refers to a professional office.

Exemple

Le cabinet du médecin généraliste se trouve au premier étage de l'immeuble.

The general practitioner's office is located on the first floor of the building.

en bonne santé

healthy / in good health

📌 Note de grammaire

Fixed expression using the feminine noun 'santé'.

Exemple

Pour rester en bonne santé, il est conseillé de faire de l'exercice régulièrement.

To stay in good health, it is recommended to exercise regularly.

grippe

flu

📌 Note de grammaire

Feminine noun; used with the verbs 'attraper' or 'avoir'.

Exemple

Chaque hiver, beaucoup de gens attrapent la grippe et restent alités.

Every winter, many people catch the flu and stay bedridden.

malade

sick / ill

📌 Note de grammaire

Adjective that remains the same for masculine and feminine ('un homme malade', 'une femme malade').

Exemple

Je suis tombé malade après avoir mangé quelque chose de suspect.

I fell ill after eating something suspicious.
