avoir mal

to hurt / to ache

📌 Note de grammaire

Expression with avoir (3rd group), auxiliary avoir. Used with 'à' + body part.

Exemple

J'ai mal à la tête aujourd'hui.

I have a headache today.

se sentir

to feel

📌 Note de grammaire

Reflexive verb, 3rd group (-ir), auxiliary être.

Exemple

Je me sens très fatigué ce matin.

I feel very tired this morning.

tousser

to cough

📌 Note de grammaire

Regular 1st group (-er) verb, auxiliary avoir.

Exemple

Il tousse beaucoup depuis hier.

He has been coughing a lot since yesterday.

éternuer

to sneeze

📌 Note de grammaire

Regular 1st group (-er) verb, auxiliary avoir.

Exemple

Le chat me fait éternuer.

The cat makes me sneeze.

prendre

to take (medicine)

📌 Note de grammaire

Irregular 3rd group verb, auxiliary avoir.

Exemple

Il faut prendre ce médicament.

You must take this medicine.

guérir

to heal / to recover

📌 Note de grammaire

Regular 2nd group (-ir) verb, auxiliary avoir.

Exemple

Il va guérir rapidement avec ces pilules.

He will recover quickly with these pills.

se reposer

to rest

📌 Note de grammaire

Reflexive 1st group (-er) verb, auxiliary être.

Exemple

Tu dois te reposer un peu.

You must rest a little.

respirer

to breathe

📌 Note de grammaire

Regular 1st group (-er) verb, auxiliary avoir.

Exemple

Respirez profondément, s'il vous plaît.

Breathe deeply, please.

dormir

to sleep

📌 Note de grammaire

Irregular 3rd group (-ir) verb, auxiliary avoir.

Exemple

Il faut dormir huit heures par nuit.

You need to sleep eight hours a night.

consulter

to consult / to see (a doctor)

📌 Note de grammaire

Regular 1st group (-er) verb, auxiliary avoir.

Exemple

Tu devrais consulter un médecin.

You should see a doctor.

blesser

to injure / to hurt

📌 Note de grammaire

Regular 1st group (-er) verb, auxiliary avoir.

Exemple

Il a blessé son genou au football.

He hurt his knee playing soccer.

se blesser

to get injured / to hurt oneself

📌 Note de grammaire

Reflexive 1st group (-er) verb, auxiliary être.

Exemple

Je me suis blessé en cuisinant.

I hurt myself while cooking.

maigrir

to lose weight / to get thinner

📌 Note de grammaire

Regular 2nd group (-ir) verb, auxiliary avoir.

Exemple

Il a maigri depuis qu'il fait du sport.

He has lost weight since he started exercising.

grossir

to gain weight / to get fatter

📌 Note de grammaire

Regular 2nd group (-ir) verb, auxiliary avoir.

Exemple

Manger trop de sucre fait grossir.

Eating too much sugar makes you gain weight.

s'essouffler

to get breathless / out of breath

📌 Note de grammaire

Reflexive 1st group (-er) verb, auxiliary être.

Exemple

Je m'essouffle vite quand je monte les escaliers.

I get out of breath quickly when I go upstairs.

bouger

to move

📌 Note de grammaire

Regular 1st group (-er) verb with spelling change (geons in nous), auxiliary avoir.

Exemple

Il faut bouger tous les jours pour la santé.

You must move every day for your health.

transpirer

to sweat / to perspire

📌 Note de grammaire

Regular 1st group (-er) verb, auxiliary avoir.

Exemple

Je transpire beaucoup quand il fait chaud.

I sweat a lot when it is hot.

soigner

to treat / to cure / to look after

📌 Note de grammaire

Regular 1st group (-er) verb, auxiliary avoir.

Exemple

Le médecin soigne les patients.

The doctor treats the patients.

maigrir

to slim down

📌 Note de grammaire

Duplicate lemma slot handled as a distinct medical context, regular 2nd group.

Exemple

Le régime l'a aidé à maigrir.

The diet helped him slim down.

s'évanouir

to faint / to pass out

📌 Note de grammaire

Reflexive 2nd group (-ir) verb, auxiliary être.

Exemple

Il a fait très chaud et elle s'est évanouie.

It was very hot and she fainted.
