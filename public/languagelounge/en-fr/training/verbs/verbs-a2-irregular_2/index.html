Irregular Verbs, Part 2

boire

to drink

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group verb, takes avoir, stem changes between singular (boi-) and plural (buv-).

Exemple

Je bois un café le matin.

I drink a coffee in the morning.

connaître

to know (someone/a place)

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group verb, takes avoir, features a circumflex accent on the 'i' (connaît) in the third person singular.

Exemple

Tu connais bien cette ville ?

Do you know this city well?

croire

to believe

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group verb, takes avoir, stem shifts to croy- in the plural present.

Exemple

Je crois que tu as raison.

I believe that you are right.

devoir

to have to, must

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group modal verb, takes avoir, expresses obligation or probability.

Exemple

Je dois partir maintenant.

I have to leave now.

dire

to say, to tell

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group verb, takes avoir, irregular 'vous dites' form in the present tense.

Exemple

Qu'est-ce que tu dis ?

What are you saying?

écrire

to write

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group verb, takes avoir, adds a 'v' in the plural present and other tenses (écriv-).

Exemple

J'écris un message à ma mère.

I am writing a message to my mother.

lire

to read

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group verb, takes avoir, adds an 's' sound spelled 'lison/lisez' in plural present (lis-).

Exemple

Je lis un livre passionnant.

I am reading an exciting book.

mettre

to put, to put on

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group verb, takes avoir, drops the double 't' in the singular present.

Exemple

Je mets mon manteau.

I am putting on my coat.

ouvrir

to open

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group verb, takes avoir, conjugates in the present like regular -er verbs (-e, -es, -e...).

Exemple

Tu peux ouvrir la fenêtre ?

Can you open the window?

offrir

to offer, to give (a gift)

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group verb, takes avoir, follows the same pattern as 'ouvrir'.

Exemple

Je veux t'offrir un cadeau.

I want to offer you a gift.

partir

to leave, to depart

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group verb, takes être, drops the 't' in singular present forms (par-).

Exemple

Je pars en vacances demain.

I am leaving on vacation tomorrow.

pouvoir

to be able to, can

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group modal verb, takes avoir, expresses ability or permission.

Exemple

Je peux t'aider avec ça ?

Can I help you with that?

prendre

to take

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group verb, takes avoir, drops the 'd' in plural present (prenn-) with doubled 'n' for ils/elles.

Exemple

Je prends le bus pour aller au travail.

I take the bus to go to work.

répondre

to answer, to reply

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group regular -re verb, takes avoir, takes a preposition 'à' before an object.

Exemple

Tu réponds à mon message ?

Are you replying to my message?

sortir

to go out, to exit

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group verb, takes être (or avoir when meaning to take something out), like 'partir'.

Exemple

Je sors avec mes amis ce soir.

I am going out with my friends tonight.

venir

to come

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group verb, takes être, highly irregular stem changes (ven-, viend-, vien-).

Exemple

Tu viens chez moi ce soir ?

Are you coming to my place tonight?

vivre

to live

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group verb, takes avoir, uses the stem viv- in present plural and other tenses.

Exemple

Je vis dans une grande ville.

I live in a big city.

voir

to see

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group verb, takes avoir, irregular future/conditional stem (ver-).

Exemple

Je vois un chat dans le jardin.

I see a cat in the garden.

vouloir

to want

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group modal verb, takes avoir, expresses desire or request.

Exemple

Je veux un café, s'il vous plaît.

I want a coffee, please.

attendre

to wait for

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group regular -re verb, takes avoir, takes a direct object without preposition.

Exemple

J'attends le bus depuis dix minutes.

I have been waiting for the bus for ten minutes.
