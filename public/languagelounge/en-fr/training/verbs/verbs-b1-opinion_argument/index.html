penser

to think

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb; uses auxiliary 'avoir'.

Exemple

Je pense que ce projet est très important pour notre avenir.

I think that this project is very important for our future.

croire

to believe

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group irregular verb; uses auxiliary 'avoir'.

Exemple

Je crois qu'il a raison concernant cette situation économique.

I believe that he is right concerning this economic situation.

estimer

to estimate, to reckon

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb; uses auxiliary 'avoir'.

Exemple

Le gouvernement estime que le chômage va diminuer.

The government reckons that unemployment is going to decrease.

trouver

to find (to consider)

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb; uses auxiliary 'avoir'.

Exemple

Je trouve que cette décision est injuste pour les employés.

I find this decision to be unfair to the employees.

soutenir

to support, to maintain (an argument)

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group irregular verb (conjugated like tenir); uses auxiliary 'avoir'.

Exemple

Il soutient que la liberté d'expression est fondamentale.

He maintains that freedom of speech is fundamental.

argumenter

to argue, to reason

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb; uses auxiliary 'avoir'.

Exemple

Il faut bien argumenter pour convaincre le public.

One must argue well to convince the public.

convaincre

to convince

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group irregular verb (-aindre/-indre/-convaincre pattern); uses auxiliary 'avoir'.

Exemple

J'essaie de convaincre mes parents de changer d'avis.

I am trying to convince my parents to change their minds.

persuader

to persuade

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb; uses auxiliary 'avoir'.

Exemple

Je suis persuadé qu'il a agi avec de bonnes intentions.

I am persuaded that he acted with good intentions.

défendre

to defend

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group (-re) regular verb; uses auxiliary 'avoir'.

Exemple

Il est important de défendre ses convictions avec calme.

It is important to defend one's convictions calmly.

contester

to dispute, to contest, to challenge

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb; uses auxiliary 'avoir'.

Exemple

Plusieurs experts contestent les résultats de cette étude.

Several experts contest the results of this study.

refuser

to refuse

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb; uses auxiliary 'avoir'.

Exemple

Elle refuse d'admettre qu'elle a commis une erreur.

She refuses to admit that she made a mistake.

approuver

to approve

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb; uses auxiliary 'avoir'.

Exemple

Le conseil municipal approuve la construction du nouveau parc.

The municipal council approves the construction of the new park.

douter

to doubt

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb; takes preposition 'de'; uses auxiliary 'avoir'.

Exemple

Je doute que cette solution soit vraiment efficace à long terme.

I doubt that this solution is truly effective in the long run.

prétendre

to claim, to assert

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group (-re) regular verb; uses auxiliary 'avoir'.

Exemple

Il prétend avoir résolu le problème tout seul.

He claims to have solved the problem all by himself.

admettre

to admit

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group irregular verb (conjugated like mettre); uses auxiliary 'avoir'.

Exemple

Il faut admettre que la situation est plus complexe que prévu.

It must be admitted that the situation is more complex than expected.

nier

to deny

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb; uses auxiliary 'avoir'.

Exemple

Il est inutile de nier l'évidence dans cette affaire.

It is useless to deny the obvious in this matter.

partager

to share (an opinion)

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) spelling change verb (nous partageons); uses auxiliary 'avoir'.

Exemple

Je partage entièrement votre avis sur ce sujet.

I entirely share your opinion on this topic.

juger

to judge, to consider

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb; uses auxiliary 'avoir'.

Exemple

Je juge utile de rappeler ces règles de sécurité.

I consider it useful to recall these safety rules.

rester

to remain (to hold an opinion/position)

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) verb; uses auxiliary 'être'.

Exemple

Je reste convaincu que nous avons pris la bonne décision.

I remain convinced that we made the right decision.

sembler

to seem, to appear

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb; uses auxiliary 'avoir'.

Exemple

Il me semble que cette proposition mérite d'être étudiée.

It seems to me that this proposal deserves to be studied.
