penser

to think

📌 Note de grammaire

1st group verb (-er), uses 'avoir' as auxiliary. Often followed by 'que' or 'à'.

Exemple

Je pense que nous devrions changer notre stratégie cette année.

I think that we should change our strategy this year.

réfléchir

to reflect, to think over

📌 Note de grammaire

2nd group verb (-ir with -issant present participle), uses 'avoir' as auxiliary. Takes preposition 'à'.

Exemple

Elle réfléchit longuement avant de prendre une décision importante.

She reflects for a long time before making an important decision.

croire

to believe

📌 Note de grammaire

Irregular 3rd group verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary. Stem changes in plural present and subjunctive.

Exemple

Je crois qu'il a raison concernant ce sujet délicat.

I believe that he is right regarding this delicate subject.

comprendre

to understand

📌 Note de grammaire

Irregular 3rd group verb (conjugated like prendre), uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Je ne comprends pas pourquoi tu refuses cette belle opportunité.

I don't understand why you are refusing this great opportunity.

se souvenir

to remember

📌 Note de grammaire

Reflexive verb, conjugated with 'être' as auxiliary. Followed by 'de'.

Exemple

Je ne me souviens plus du nom de cette rue.

I no longer remember the name of that street.

oublier

to forget

📌 Note de grammaire

1st group verb (-er), uses 'avoir' as auxiliary. Can take an infinitive with 'de'.

Exemple

J'ai oublié d'acheter du pain en rentrant du travail.

I forgot to buy bread on my way back from work.

imaginer

to imagine

📌 Note de grammaire

1st group verb (-er), uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Peux-tu imaginer vivre dans un pays tropical toute l'année ?

Can you imagine living in a tropical country all year round?

deviner

to guess

📌 Note de grammaire

1st group verb (-er), uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Essaie de deviner ce que j'ai préparé pour le dîner.

Try to guess what I prepared for dinner.

se douter

to suspect, to have an inkling

📌 Note de grammaire

Reflexive verb, conjugated with 'être' as auxiliary. Followed by 'de'.

Exemple

Je me doutais bien qu'il y aurait des problèmes avec ce projet.

I suspected there would be problems with this project.

douter

to doubt

📌 Note de grammaire

1st group verb (-er), uses 'avoir' as auxiliary. Followed by 'de'.

Exemple

Je doute qu'il vienne à la réunion si tard.

I doubt that he will come to the meeting so late.

considérer

to consider

📌 Note de grammaire

1st group verb (-er) with stem change (é -> è in most forms), uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Je considère cette proposition comme une excellente opportunité.

I consider this proposal to be an excellent opportunity.

juger

to judge

📌 Note de grammaire

1st group verb (-ger), retains 'e' after 'g' before 'a' and 'o' (nous jugeons), uses 'avoir'.

Exemple

Il ne faut pas juger les gens sur leur apparence.

You must not judge people by their appearance.

supposer

to suppose, to assume

📌 Note de grammaire

1st group verb (-er), uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Je suppose que tu as déjà fini ton rapport ?

I suppose you have already finished your report?

se rendre compte

to realize

📌 Note de grammaire

Reflexive expression (rendre compte is idiomatic), conjugated with 'être' as auxiliary. Followed by 'de'.

Exemple

Je me suis rendu compte de mon erreur trop tard.

I realized my mistake too late.

réaliser

to realize (make/achieve or become aware of)

📌 Note de grammaire

1st group verb (-er), uses 'avoir' as auxiliary. (Note: in modern French, also used for to become aware, though 'se rendre compte' is traditionally preferred).

Exemple

Il a réalisé son rêve de voyager autour du monde.

He realized his dream of traveling around the world.

remarquer

to notice

📌 Note de grammaire

1st group verb (-er), uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

As-tu remarqué le nouveau comportement de notre collègue ?

Did you notice our colleague's new behavior?

savoir

to know (a fact)

📌 Note de grammaire

Irregular 3rd group verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary. Used for facts, information, or knowing how to do something.

Exemple

Je sais que tu as beaucoup de travail en ce moment.

I know that you have a lot of work right now.

connaître

to know (someone/somewhere/something well)

📌 Note de grammaire

Irregular 3rd group verb (-aître), uses 'avoir' as auxiliary. Used for familiarity with people, places, or things.

Exemple

Je connais très bien cette région pour y avoir habité.

I know this region very well for having lived there.

hésiter

to hesitate

📌 Note de grammaire

1st group verb (-er), uses 'avoir' as auxiliary. Followed by 'à' + infinitive.

Exemple

N'hésite pas à me contacter si tu as besoin de conseils.

Don't hesitate to contact me if you need advice.

se tromper

to be mistaken, to make a mistake

📌 Note de grammaire

Reflexive verb, conjugated with 'être' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Je me suis trompé de chemin et je suis arrivé en retard.

I took the wrong road and arrived late.
