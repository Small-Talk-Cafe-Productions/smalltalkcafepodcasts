télécharger

to download / to upload

📌 Note de grammaire

First-group regular verb (-er), uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Je dois télécharger cette application avant notre réunion de demain.

I must download this application before our meeting tomorrow.

partager

to share

📌 Note de grammaire

First-group regular verb (-er), uses 'avoir' as auxiliary. Takes a soft 'g' sound before 'a' and 'o' (nous partageons).

Exemple

Tu peux partager ton écran pour que nous puissions voir ta présentation.

You can share your screen so that we can see your presentation.

imprimer

to print

📌 Note de grammaire

First-group regular verb (-er), uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Est-ce que tu peux imprimer ce rapport avant l'arrivée du directeur ?

Can you print this report before the director's arrival?

sauvegarder

to save (files, data)

📌 Note de grammaire

First-group regular verb (-er), uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

N'oublie pas de sauvegarder ton travail toutes les dix minutes.

Don't forget to save your work every ten minutes.

effacer

to delete / to erase

📌 Note de grammaire

First-group regular verb (-er) with a cedilla (ç) before 'a' and 'o' to maintain the soft 'c' sound (nous effaçons). Uses 'avoir'.

Exemple

Il faut effacer ces anciens fichiers pour libérer de l'espace sur le disque.

You must delete these old files to free up space on the disk.

brancher

to plug in / to connect

📌 Note de grammaire

First-group regular verb (-er), uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Tu dois brancher ton ordinateur portable car la batterie est presque vide.

You need to plug in your laptop because the battery is almost empty.

débrancher

to unplug

📌 Note de grammaire

First-group regular verb (-er), uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

N'oublie pas de débrancher la prise avant de nettoyer l'écran.

Don't forget to unplug the socket before cleaning the screen.

allumer

to turn on / to switch on

📌 Note de grammaire

First-group regular verb (-er), uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Allume ta webcam pour que nous puissions commencer la visioconférence.

Turn on your webcam so we can start the video conference.

éteindre

to turn off / to shut down

📌 Note de grammaire

Third-group irregular verb ending in -indre, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary. Conjugation pattern mirrors 'peindre'.

Exemple

Pense à éteindre ton téléphone portable pendant la réunion importante.

Remember to turn off your mobile phone during the important meeting.

fonctionner

to function / to work

📌 Note de grammaire

First-group regular verb (-er), uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Mon nouveau smartphone fonctionne très bien depuis la dernière mise à jour.

My new smartphone has been working very well since the latest update.

naviguer

to surf / to navigate (the internet)

📌 Note de grammaire

First-group regular verb (-er), uses 'avoir' as auxiliary. Retains the 'e' before 'a' and 'o' (nous naviguons) to keep the hard 'g'.

Exemple

J'aime naviguer sur Internet pour trouver des tutoriels de programmation.

I like to surf the internet to find programming tutorials.

cliquer

to click

📌 Note de grammaire

First-group regular verb (-er), uses 'avoir' as auxiliary. Followed by the preposition 'sur'.

Exemple

Il faut cliquer sur ce lien pour valider votre inscription en ligne.

You must click on this link to validate your online registration.

installer

to install

📌 Note de grammaire

First-group regular verb (-er), uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Un technicien va installer la nouvelle connexion Internet haut débit demain.

A technician is going to install the new high-speed internet connection tomorrow.

mettre à jour

to update

📌 Note de grammaire

Compound verb based on 'mettre' (third group, irregular), uses 'avoir' as auxiliary. Participle is 'mis à jour'.

Exemple

Il est important de mettre à jour son système d'exploitation régulièrement.

It is important to update one's operating system regularly.

se connecter

to log in / to connect

📌 Note de grammaire

First-group reflexive verb (-er), uses 'être' as auxiliary. Requires reflexive pronouns (me, te, se, nous, vous, se).

Exemple

Tu dois te connecter avec ton identifiant et ton mot de passe sécurisé.

You must log in with your ID and secure password.

se déconnecter

to log out / to disconnect

📌 Note de grammaire

First-group reflexive verb (-er), uses 'être' as auxiliary. Requires reflexive pronouns.

Exemple

N'oublie pas de te déconnecter de ta session avant de quitter l'ordinateur.

Don't forget to log out of your session before leaving the computer.

créer

to create

📌 Note de grammaire

First-group regular verb (-er) with a root ending in 'e' (cré-), uses 'avoir' as auxiliary. Retains written accents on the 'e' (créé).

Exemple

Je veux créer un nouveau compte pour accéder à cette plateforme numérique.

I want to create a new account to access this digital platform.

participer

to participate / to take part (in online forums, etc.)

📌 Note de grammaire

First-group regular verb (-er), uses 'avoir' as auxiliary. Followed by the preposition 'à'.

Exemple

Tous les salariés peuvent participer à cette formation sur la sécurité informatique.

All employees can participate in this training on IT security.

numériser

to digitize / to scan

📌 Note de grammaire

First-group regular verb (-er), uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

L'administration demande de numériser tous les justificatifs administratifs.

The administration asks to digitize all administrative supporting documents.

redémarrer

to restart / to reboot

📌 Note de grammaire

First-group regular verb (-er), uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Si l'application plante, tu dois redémarrer ton téléphone complètement.

If the application crashes, you need to restart your phone completely.
