arroser

to water

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Tu dois arroser les fleurs du jardin.

You must water the flowers in the garden.

planter

to plant

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Nous allons planter des arbres ce weekend.

We are going to plant trees this weekend.

recycler

to recycle

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Il faut recycler le plastique et le papier.

We must recycle plastic and paper.

protéger

to protect

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) spelling-change verb (-ger takes an 'e' before 'a' and 'o'), uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Nous devons protéger la nature et les animaux.

We must protect nature and the animals.

polluer

to pollute

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Les voitures polluent l'air en ville.

Cars pollute the air in the city.

gaspiller

to waste

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Il ne faut pas gaspiller l'eau potable.

We must not waste drinking water.

économiser

to save / to conserve

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Nous essayons d'économiser l'énergie chez nous.

We try to save energy at home.

trier

to sort (waste)

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

J'apprends à trier mes déchets correctement.

I am learning to sort my waste correctly.

jeter

to throw away

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) stem-changing verb (doubles 't' before mute 'e'), uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Il ne faut pas jeter les papiers par terre.

We must not throw paper on the ground.

fleurir

to bloom / to flower

📌 Note de grammaire

Second group (-ir) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Les roses commencent à fleurir au printemps.

Roses begin to bloom in spring.

pousser

to grow (plants)

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Les légumes poussent bien dans ce sol riche.

Vegetables grow well in this rich soil.

ramasser

to pick up / to collect

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Nous allons ramasser les déchets sur la plage.

We are going to pick up trash on the beach.

découvrir

to discover

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group (-rir) irregular verb conjugated like 'ouvrir', uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

J'aime découvrir de nouveaux paysages en randonnée.

I like discovering new landscapes while hiking.

menacer

to threaten

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-cer) spelling-change verb (ç before a/o), uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

La pollution menace la vie marine.

Pollution threatens marine life.

réduire

to reduce

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group (-re) irregular verb ending in -duire, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Nous devons réduire notre consommation d'énergie.

We must reduce our energy consumption.

réchauffer

to warm up / to heat up

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Le soleil réchauffe la terre en été.

The sun warms up the earth in summer.

nourrir

to feed / to nourish

📌 Note de grammaire

Second group (-ir) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Il ne faut pas nourrir les animaux sauvages.

We must not feed wild animals.

éteindre

to turn off / to extinguish

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group (-re) irregular verb ending in -indre, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Pense à éteindre la lumière en sortant.

Remember to turn off the light when leaving.

respecter

to respect

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Nous devons respecter l'environnement.

We must respect the environment.

disparaître

to disappear

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group (-re) irregular verb ending in -aître, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Plusieurs espèces animales risquent de disparaître.

Several animal species risk disappearing.
