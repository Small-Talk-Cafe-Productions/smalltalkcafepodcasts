se réveiller

to wake up

📌 Note de grammaire

1st group (-er) reflexive verb, uses 'être' as auxiliary in compound tenses.

Exemple

Je me réveille tôt.

I wake up early.

se lever

to get up

📌 Note de grammaire

1st group (-er) reflexive verb with stem change (è), uses 'être' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Je me lève vite.

I get up quickly.

se laver

to wash oneself

📌 Note de grammaire

1st group (-er) reflexive verb, uses 'être' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Je me lave les mains.

I wash my hands.

to get dressed

📌 Note de grammaire

1st group (-er) reflexive verb with elision, uses 'être' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Exemple
