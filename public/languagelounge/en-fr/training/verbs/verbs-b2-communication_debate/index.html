argumenter

to argue / to reason

📌 Note de grammaire

First-group (-er) verb, conjugated with avoir.

Exemple

Il a su argumenter pour convaincre le jury de sa bonne foi.

He knew how to argue to convince the jury of his good faith.

affirmer

to assert / to affirm

📌 Note de grammaire

First-group (-er) verb, conjugated with avoir.

Exemple

Elle affirme que le projet sera rentable dès la première année.

She asserts that the project will be profitable from the first year.

soutenir

to support / to maintain (an argument)

📌 Note de grammaire

Third-group (-ir) verb, conjugated with avoir; irregular model like tenir.

Exemple

Il est difficile de soutenir une telle thèse sans preuves solides.

It is difficult to maintain such a thesis without solid proof.

contredire

to contradict

📌 Note de grammaire

Third-group verb conjugated with avoir, irregular 'vous contredisez' form.

Exemple

Il ne faut jamais contredire le client sans lui proposer d'alternative.

One must never contradict the client without offering an alternative.

nuancer

to nuance / to qualify (a statement)

📌 Note de grammaire

First-group (-cer) verb requiring a cedilla (ç) before 'a' and 'o'.

Exemple

Il est sage de nuancer ses propos lors d'un débat public.

It is wise to qualify one's remarks during a public debate.

objecter

to object / to counter

📌 Note de grammaire

First-group (-er) verb, conjugated with avoir.

Exemple

L'avocat a objecté que les preuves présentées étaient irrecevables.

The lawyer objected that the evidence presented was inadmissible.

persuader

to persuade

📌 Note de grammaire

First-group (-er) verb, conjugated with avoir; takes 'de' before an infinitive.

Exemple

Il a réussi à persuader l'assemblée de voter la nouvelle motion.

He managed to persuade the assembly to vote for the new motion.

discuter

to discuss / to debate

📌 Note de grammaire

First-group (-er) verb, conjugated with avoir.

Exemple

Les syndicats discutent âprement des conditions de travail.

The unions are bitterly discussing working conditions.

formuler

to formulate / to express

📌 Note de grammaire

First-group (-er) verb, conjugated with avoir.

Exemple

Le député a formulé une critique constructive sur le budget.

The deputy formulated a constructive criticism on the budget.

convaincre

to convince

📌 Note de grammaire

Third-group (-re) verb, conjugated with avoir; stem changes to convainc- / convainqu-.

Exemple

Son allocution a su convaincre les plus sceptiques de l'assistance.

His speech managed to convince the most skeptical members of the audience.

interrompre

to interrupt

📌 Note de grammaire

Third-group (-re) verb, conjugated with avoir; conjugated like rompre.

Exemple

Il est impoli d'interrompre quelqu'un au milieu de sa phrase.

It is rude to interrupt someone in the middle of their sentence.

prétendre

to claim / to maintain

📌 Note de grammaire

Third-group (-re) verb, conjugated with avoir; regular -re pattern.

Exemple

Il prétend posséder des informations exclusives sur cette affaire.

He claims to possess exclusive information on this matter.

démontrer

to demonstrate / to prove

📌 Note de grammaire

First-group (-er) verb, conjugated with avoir.

Exemple

L'orateur a brillamment démontré l'efficacité de sa méthode.

The speaker brilliantly demonstrated the effectiveness of his method.

réfuter

to refute / to debunk

📌 Note de grammaire

First-group (-er) verb with stem change (é -> è) in most forms except nous/vous, conjugated with avoir.

Exemple

Le scientifique a réfuté les fausses rumeurs avec des données précises.

The scientist refuted the false rumors with precise data.

interpeller

to challenge / to call out / to question

📌 Note de grammaire

First-group (-eler) verb doubling the 'l' (ll) before mute 'e', conjugated with avoir.

Exemple

La journaliste a interpellé le ministre sur sa politique environnementale.

The journalist challenged the minister regarding his environmental policy.

exposer

to outline / to explain / to expose

📌 Note de grammaire

First-group (-er) verb, conjugated with avoir.

Exemple

Le conférencier a exposé son point de vue avec beaucoup de clarté.

The speaker outlined his point of view with great clarity.

partager

to share (an opinion/view)

📌 Note de grammaire

First-group (-ger) verb retaining 'e' after 'g' before 'a' and 'o' (nous partageons).

Exemple

Je ne partage pas votre avis concernant cette décision stratégique.

I do not share your opinion regarding this strategic decision.

concéder

to concede / to grant

📌 Note de grammaire

First-group (-er) verb with stem change é -> è in most forms, conjugated with avoir.

Exemple

L'opposition a dû concéder que les résultats économiques étaient positifs.

The opposition had to concede that the economic results were positive.

reconnaître

to acknowledge / to recognize

📌 Note de grammaire

Third-group (-re) verb conjugated with avoir, irregular pattern like connaître.

Exemple

Il a fini par reconnaître ses torts au cours de la discussion.

He ended up acknowledging his mistakes during the discussion.

souligner

to emphasize / to highlight / to underline

📌 Note de grammaire

First-group (-er) verb, conjugated with avoir.

Exemple

L'expert a souligné l'importance d'une transition énergétique rapide.

The expert emphasized the importance of a rapid energy transition.
