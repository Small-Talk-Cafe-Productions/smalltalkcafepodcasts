analyser

to analyze

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Il faut analyser les données avant de rédiger la conclusion du rapport.

It is necessary to analyze the data before writing the conclusion of the report.

démontrer

to demonstrate / to prove

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Cette étude vise à démontrer l'impact de la pollution sur la biodiversité.

This study aims to demonstrate the impact of pollution on biodiversity.

hypothéser

to hypothesize

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Les sociologues hypothèsent que le niveau d'éducation influence le comportement électoral.

Sociologists hypothesize that the level of education influences voting behavior.

publier

to publish

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Le professeur compte publier ses recherches dans une revue internationale.

The professor intends to publish his research in an international journal.

synthétiser

to synthesize

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Il est difficile de synthétiser un livre de cinq cents pages en dix minutes.

It is difficult to synthesize a five-hundred-page book in ten minutes.

approfondir

to deepen / to explore further

📌 Note de grammaire

Second group (-ir) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Ce doctorant souhaite approfondir sa thèse sur l'histoire contemporaine.

This doctoral student wishes to deepen his thesis on contemporary history.

redéfinir

to redefine

📌 Note de grammaire

Second group (-ir) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Les nouvelles découvertes obligent les scientifiques à redéfinir ce concept.

New discoveries force scientists to redefine this concept.

conclure

to conclude

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group irregular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

L'auteur conclut son ouvrage en ouvrant sur de nouvelles perspectives.

The author concludes his work by opening up new perspectives.

exclure

to exclude

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group irregular verb, conjugated like 'conclure', uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Il ne faut pas exclure cette hypothèse avant d'avoir examiné toutes les preuves.

We must not exclude this hypothesis before having examined all the evidence.

démontrer

to demonstrate (alternative academic context)

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Le thésard doit démontrer sa capacité à mener une recherche autonome.

The PhD candidate must demonstrate his ability to conduct autonomous research.

étayer

to support / to substantiate

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) verb with stem change (y -> i before mute e), uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Il est indispensable d'étayer son argumentation avec des sources fiables.

It is essential to substantiate one's argument with reliable sources.

corroborer

to corroborate

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Ces nouveaux documents viennent corroborer la thèse initiale de l'historien.

These new documents corroborate the historian's initial thesis.

infirmer

to invalidate / to refute

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

L'expérience a infirmé la théorie qui faisait l'unanimité jusqu'alors.

The experiment invalidated the theory that had been unanimously accepted until then.

extrapoler

to extrapolate

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Il est imprudent d'extrapoler des résultats d'un échantillon aussi restreint.

It is unwise to extrapolate results from such a small sample.

postuler

to postulate

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Ce modèle économique postule que les agents sont parfaitement rationnels.

This economic model postulates that agents are perfectly rational.

conceptualiser

to conceptualize

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Le philosophe a mis des années à conceptualiser cette nouvelle approche.

The philosopher spent years conceptualizing this new approach.

cartographier

to map / to chart

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Cette étude cartographie les zones à risque en cas de catastrophe naturelle.

This study maps areas at risk in the event of a natural disaster.

contextualiser

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

It is crucial to contextualize these events to understand their significance.

to quantify

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Il est difficile de quantifier précisément les retombées économiques de ce projet.

Exemple
