Giving & Taking

donner

to give

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Tu devrais donner ton vieux manteau à une association caritative.

You should give your old coat to a charitable association.

prendre

to take

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group (-re) irregular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Il est important de prendre le temps de réfléchir avant de décider.

It is important to take the time to think before deciding.

offrir

to offer / to give (a gift)

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group verb conjugated like -er verbs, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Mes collègues m'ont offert un beau cadeau pour mon départ à la retraite.

My colleagues offered me a nice gift for my retirement.

recevoir

to receive

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group (-oir) irregular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Elle a reçu une promotion après avoir réussi ses projets importants.

She received a promotion after succeeding in her important projects.

accepter

to accept

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Il a accepté l'offre d'emploi malgré la distance avec son domicile.

He accepted the job offer despite the distance from his home.

refuser

to refuse

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Le propriétaire a refusé de baisser le loyer de l'appartement.

The landlord refused to lower the apartment rent.

partager

to share

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-ger) verb, takes a 'e' after 'g' before 'a' and 'o' (nous partageons), uses 'avoir'.

Exemple

Nous aimons partager nos expériences de voyage avec nos amis.

We like to share our travel experiences with our friends.

rapporter

to bring back / to yield

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

N'oublie pas de rapporter les livres à la bibliothèque municipale.

Don't forget to bring the books back to the municipal library.

emmener

to take along / to lead away

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) verb with stem change (e -> è before a silent e), uses 'avoir'.

Exemple

Je vais t'emmener découvrir un restaurant formidable ce soir.

I'm going to take you along to discover a wonderful restaurant tonight.

transmettre

to transmit / to pass on

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group (-tre) irregular verb, conjugated like 'mettre', uses 'avoir'.

Exemple

Le professeur a transmis le message important à tous les étudiants.

The teacher passed on the important message to all the students.

emprunter

to borrow

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Puis-je emprunter ton dictionnaire pour préparer mon examen de français ?

Can I borrow your dictionary to prepare for my French exam?

prêter

to lend

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Mon voisin m'a prêté son perceuse pour réparer une étagère.

My neighbor lent me his drill to fix a shelf.

voler

to steal / to fly

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Quelqu'un a volé mon vélo alors qu'il était attaché dans la rue.

Someone stole my bike while it was locked up in the street.

saisir

to seize / to grasp

📌 Note de grammaire

Second group (-ir) regular verb with -issant participle, uses 'avoir'.

Exemple

Il faut saisir cette opportunité professionnelle avant qu'il ne soit trop tard.

We must seize this career opportunity before it is too late.

abandonner

to abandon / to give up

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Malgré les difficultés, elle n'a jamais voulu abandonner ses études.

Despite the difficulties, she never wanted to give up her studies.

fournir

to provide / to supply

📌 Note de grammaire

Second group (-ir) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

L'entreprise doit fournir tous les documents nécessaires avant vendredi.

The company must provide all the necessary documents before Friday.

récupérer

to retrieve / to pick up / to recover

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) verb with stem change (é -> è before silent e), uses 'avoir'.

Exemple

Peux-tu récupérer les enfants à l'école en rentrant du bureau ?

Can you pick up the kids from school on your way back from the office?

confier

to entrust / to confide

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-ier) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Elle m'a confié son chat pendant qu'elle était en voyage d'affaires.

She entrusted her cat to me while she was on a business trip.

distribuer

to distribute / to hand out

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-uer) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Des bénévoles distribuent des repas chauds aux sans-abri en hiver.

Volunteers distribute warm meals to the homeless in winter.

soustraire

to subtract / to withdraw

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group irregular verb, conjugated like 'traire', uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Pour résoudre ce problème de mathématiques, il faut soustraire ce montant.

To solve this math problem, you must subtract this amount.
