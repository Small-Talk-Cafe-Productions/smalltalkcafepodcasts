exprimer

to express

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Il est important de s'exprimer clairement pendant un entretien.

It is important to express oneself clearly during an interview.

discuter

to discuss / to talk over

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Nous devons discuter des conditions de travail avec le directeur.

We must discuss the working conditions with the manager.

expliquer

to explain

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Le professeur a expliqué la leçon de grammaire deux fois.

The teacher explained the grammar lesson twice.

répondre

to answer / to reply

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group (-re) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary. Takes preposition 'à'.

Exemple

Je n'ai pas encore répondu au courriel du client.

I haven't answered the client's email yet.

raconter

to tell (a story) / to relate

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Mon grand-père nous racontait souvent des histoires de sa jeunesse.

My grandfather often used to tell us stories of his youth.

conseiller

to advise / to recommend

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary. Followed by indirect object or subjunctive.

Exemple

Je te conseille de lire ce livre avant de voir le film.

I advise you to read this book before seeing the movie.

convaincre

to convince / to persuade

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group irregular verb (-indre/-vincre pattern), uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Il a essayé de me convaincre d'accepter cette offre d'emploi.

He tried to convince me to accept this job offer.

protester

to protest

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Les citoyens sont descendus dans la rue pour protester contre la hausse des prix.

Citizens took to the street to protest against the price hike.

prétendre

to claim / to assert

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group (-re) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary. False friend: means to claim, not to pretend.

Exemple

Il prétend tout savoir sur l'intelligence artificielle.

He claims to know everything about artificial intelligence.

avouer

to confess / to admit

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Le suspect a fini par avouer son crime à la police.

The suspect ended up confessing his crime to the police.

promettre

to promise

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group irregular verb (conjugated like 'mettre'), uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Il m'a promis de m'aider à déménager ce week-end.

He promised me to help me move this weekend.

mentir

to lie

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group (-ir) irregular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Il est inacceptable de mentir à ses meilleurs amis.

It is unacceptable to lie to one's best friends.

négocier

to negotiate

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) verb with stem change (-ier / -iions spelling in nous/vous imparfait), uses 'avoir'.

Exemple

Le syndicat a négocié une augmentation de salaire avec la direction.

The union negotiated a salary increase with management.

critiquer

to criticize

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Il est facile de critiquer le travail des autres sans agir.

It is easy to criticize other people's work without taking action.

annoncer

to announce

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb with cedilla (ç) before 'a' and 'o' to keep soft /s/ sound, uses 'avoir'.

Exemple

Le gouvernement a annoncé de nouvelles mesures économiques ce matin.

The government announced new economic measures this morning.

murmurer

to murmur / to whisper

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Elle lui a murmuré un secret à l'oreille pendant le concert.

She whispered a secret in his ear during the concert.

recommander

to recommend

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Un ami m'a recommandé ce petit restaurant italien du centre-ville.

A friend recommended this small Italian restaurant downtown.

interrompre

to interrupt

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group irregular verb (conjugated like 'rompre'), uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Il est impoli d'interrompre quelqu'un pendant qu'il parle.

It is rude to interrupt someone while they are speaking.

traduire

to translate

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group irregular verb ending in -uire, uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Ce document important doit être traduit en trois langues différentes.

This important document must be translated into three different languages.

avancer

to put forward / to suggest / to advance

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) regular verb with cedilla (ç) before 'a' and 'o', uses 'avoir' as auxiliary.

Exemple

Il a avancé une hypothèse intéressante lors de la réunion.

He put forward an interesting hypothesis during the meeting.
