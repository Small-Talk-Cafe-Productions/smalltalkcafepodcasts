← → Flèches pour naviguer · A pour écouter

aboutir

to lead to, to result in

📌 Note de grammaire

Second group (-ir verb), auxiliary avoir, followed by the preposition 'à'.

Exemple

Ces négociations laborieuses vont enfin aboutir à un accord satisfaisant.

These laborious negotiations will finally lead to a satisfactory agreement.

compromettre

to compromise, to jeopardize

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group (-re verb, conjugated like mettre), auxiliary avoir.

Exemple

Un tel comportement irresponsable risque de compromettre sa carrière professionnelle.

Such irresponsible behavior risks jeopardizing his professional career.

entreprendre

to undertake, to initiate

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group (-re verb, conjugated like prendre), auxiliary avoir.

Exemple

Elle a décidé d'entreprendre des études approfondies en sciences politiques.

She decided to undertake in-depth studies in political science.

favoriser

to favor, to foster, to promote

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er verb), auxiliary avoir.

Exemple

Cette nouvelle mesure fiscale vise à favoriser l'investissement vert.

This new tax measure aims to foster green investment.

freiner

to slow down, to curb, to hinder

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er verb), auxiliary avoir.

Exemple

La bureaucratie excessive tend à freiner l'innovation technologique.

Excessive bureaucracy tends to hinder technological innovation.

générer

to generate, to produce

📌 Note de grammaire

First group stem-changing verb (-érer changes to -è- before mute e), auxiliary avoir.

Exemple

Ce projet innovant va générer de nombreux emplois dans la région.

This innovative project will generate numerous jobs in the region.

inciter

to encourage, to prompt, to incite

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er verb), auxiliary avoir, followed by preposition 'à'.

Exemple

Le gouvernement cherche à inciter les citoyens à trier leurs déchets.

The government seeks to encourage citizens to sort their waste.

mettre en œuvre

to implement, to carry out

📌 Note de grammaire

Compound expression based on mettre (third group), auxiliary avoir.

Exemple

La direction doit mettre en œuvre une stratégie plus efficace.

Management must implement a more effective strategy.

nuire

to harm, to damage

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group (-re verb), auxiliary avoir, takes indirect object with 'à'.

Exemple

Une mauvaise communication interne peut nuire gravement à la cohésion d'équipe.

Poor internal communication can seriously harm team cohesion.

optimiser

to optimize

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er verb), auxiliary avoir.

Exemple

L'entreprise cherche à optimiser ses coûts de production pour rester compétitive.

The company seeks to optimize its production costs to remain competitive.

persister

to persist, to persevere

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er verb), auxiliary avoir (or être in literary contexts).

Exemple

Si ces difficultés économiques persistent, il faudra revoir notre budget.

If these economic difficulties persist, we will have to review our budget.

prôner

to advocate, to preach

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er verb), auxiliary avoir.

Exemple

Le directeur prône une gestion transparente et participative.

The director advocates transparent and participatory management.

remédier

to remedy, to fix

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er verb), auxiliary avoir, followed by preposition 'à'.

Exemple

Il est urgent de remédier à cette situation critique.

It is urgent to remedy this critical situation.

restituer

to restore, to return, to reproduce

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er verb), auxiliary avoir.

Exemple

Le comité doit restituer ses conclusions à la fin du mois.

The committee must submit its findings at the end of the month.

se détacher

to stand out, to detach oneself

📌 Note de grammaire

First group reflexive verb, auxiliary être.

Exemple

Son profil se détache nettement grâce à son expérience internationale.

Her profile clearly stands out thanks to her international experience.

se pencher

to look into, to examine closely

📌 Note de grammaire

First group reflexive verb, auxiliary être, idiomatically used with 'sur' to mean examine.

Exemple

Le conseil d'administration va se pencher sur cette question délicate.

The board of directors is going to look into this delicate issue.

s'efforcer

to strive, to endeavor

📌 Note de grammaire

First group reflexive verb, auxiliary être, followed by preposition 'de'.

Exemple

Nous nous efforçons de répondre à toutes vos attentes professionnelles.

We strive to meet all your professional expectations.

s'interroger

to wonder, to question oneself

📌 Note de grammaire

First group reflexive verb, auxiliary être.

Exemple

Face à ces mutations rapides, on peut légitimement s'interroger sur l'avenir.

Faced with these rapid changes, one can legitimately wonder about the future.

subvenir

to provide for

📌 Note de grammaire

Third group (-ir verb, conjugated like venir), auxiliary avoir, takes indirect object with 'à'.

Exemple

Il travaille dur pour subvenir aux besoins de sa famille.

He works hard to provide for his family's needs.

valoriser

to value, to enhance, to promote

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er verb), auxiliary avoir.

Exemple

Ce stage permet de valoriser les compétences acquises pendant la formation.

This internship makes it possible to enhance the skills acquired during training.
