Reporting & Media

Reportage et Médias • B2 • 20 cartes

Exemples

← → Flèches pour naviguer · A pour écouter

diffuser

to broadcast

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) verb, conjugated with avoir.

Exemple

La chaîne va diffuser un reportage exclusif sur la corruption.

The channel is going to broadcast an exclusive report on corruption.

interviewer

to interview

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) verb, conjugated with avoir.

Exemple

Le journaliste a interviewé le ministre pendant le journal télévisé.

The journalist interviewed the minister during the evening news.

relayer

to relay / to forward

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) verb with stem change (è instead of é before a mute e), conjugated with avoir.

Exemple

Les réseaux sociaux relayent rapidement les fausses informations.

Social networks quickly relay fake news.

censurer

to censor

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) verb, conjugated with avoir.

Exemple

Le gouvernement tente de censurer les articles critiques.

The government is trying to censor critical articles.

publier

to publish

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) verb, conjugated with avoir.

Exemple

Le quotidien a publié une enquête approfondie sur la crise.

The daily newspaper published an in-depth investigation into the crisis.

rédiger

to write / to draft

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) verb with spelling change (ge -> geons in nous form), conjugated with avoir.

Exemple

Le journaliste rédige un article incisif pour l'édition du soir.

The journalist is drafting an incisive article for the evening edition.

enquêter

to investigate

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) verb, conjugated with avoir. Often takes 'sur'.

Exemple

La rédaction enquête sur les pratiques frauduleuses de l'entreprise.

The newsroom is investigating the company's fraudulent practices.

divulguer

to disclose / to leak

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) verb, conjugated with avoir.

Exemple

Le lanceur d'alerte a divulgué des documents confidentiels.

The whistleblower disclosed confidential documents.

manipuler

to manipulate

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) verb, conjugated with avoir.

Exemple

Certains sites d'information manipulent l'opinion publique.

Some news sites manipulate public opinion.

revendiquer

to claim (responsibility) / to assert

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) verb, conjugated with avoir.

Exemple

Un groupe extrémiste a revendiqué l'attentat sur Internet.

An extremist group claimed responsibility for the attack on the internet.

diffamer

to defame / to slander

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) verb, conjugated with avoir.

Exemple

L'homme politique a poursuivi le magazine pour l'avoir diffamé.

The politician sued the magazine for defaming him.

contester

to contest / to challenge

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) verb, conjugated with avoir.

Exemple

L'opposition conteste les chiffres officiels du chômage.

The opposition is challenging the official unemployment figures.

confirmer

to confirm

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) verb, conjugated with avoir.

Exemple

Une source officielle a confirmé la démission du ministre.

An official source confirmed the minister's resignation.

démentir

to deny / to refute

📌 Note de grammaire

Second/Third group pattern (conjuated like sentir), conjugated with avoir.

Exemple

La direction a immédiatement démenti ces accusations de corruption.

Management immediately denied these allegations of corruption.

persuader

to persuade

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) verb, conjugated with avoir. Followed by de + infinitive.

Exemple

L'éditorial tente de persuader les lecteurs de l'urgence climatique.

The editorial tries to persuade readers of the climate emergency.

influencer

to influence

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) verb, conjugated with avoir.

Exemple

Les campagnes publicitaires influencent le comportement des consommateurs.

Advertising campaigns influence consumer behavior.

saisir

to grasp / to seize (an opportunity or court case)

📌 Note de grammaire

Second group (-ir) verb, conjugated with avoir.

Exemple

Il est parfois difficile de saisir toute la complexité d'une actualité internationale.

It is sometimes difficult to grasp the full complexity of international news.

amplifier

to amplify / to blow out of proportion

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) verb, conjugated with avoir.

Exemple

Les médias ont tendance à amplifier les faits divers.

The media tend to blow minor news items out of proportion.

occulter

to conceal / to black out / to gloss over

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) verb, conjugated with avoir.

Exemple

Le reportage occulte une partie importante de la vérité.

The report conceals an important part of the truth.

témoigner

to testify / to bear witness

📌 Note de grammaire

First group (-er) verb, conjugated with avoir. Often followed by de.

Exemple

Un ancien employé a accepté de témoigner à visage couvert.

A former employee agreed to testify with a concealed face.
