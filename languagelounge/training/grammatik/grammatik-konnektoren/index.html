Beispielsätze

Tags:

weil

because

📐 Word Order

Verb moves to the END of the clause

Beispielsatz

Ich lerne Deutsch, weil ich in Deutschland arbeiten moechte.

I'm learning German because I want to work in Germany.

denn

because / for (more formal)

📐 Word Order

No word order change - keeps Verb in position 2

Beispielsatz

Ich lerne Deutsch, denn ich moechte in Deutschland arbeiten.

I'm learning German, for I want to work in Germany.

da

since / as (already-known reason)

📐 Word Order

Verb moves to the END; often used at the beginning

Beispielsatz

Da es regnet, nehmen wir einen Regenschirm.

Since it's raining, we're taking an umbrella.

deshalb

therefore / that's why

📐 Word Order

deshalb pushes the subject AFTER the verb (inversion)

Beispielsatz

Es regnet. Deshalb bleiben wir zu Hause.

It's raining. That's why we're staying home.

daher

therefore / hence

📐 Word Order

Same as deshalb - causes subject-verb inversion

Beispielsatz

Das Wetter ist schlecht. Daher fahren wir nicht ans Meer.

The weather is bad. Hence we're not going to the sea.

deswegen

because of this / for this reason

📐 Word Order

Causes subject-verb inversion

Beispielsatz

Sie hat keine Lust. Deswegen kommt sie nicht.

She doesn't feel like it. Because of this she isn't coming.

folglich

consequently / as a result

📐 Word Order

Causes subject-verb inversion

Beispielsatz

Er hat nicht geubt. Folglich hat er die Pruefung nicht bestanden.

He didn't practice. Consequently he didn't pass the exam.

obwohl

although / even though

📐 Word Order

Verb moves to the END of the clause

Beispielsatz

Obwohl er muede ist, arbeitet er weiter.

Although he is tired, he keeps working.

trotzdem

nevertheless / despite that

📐 Word Order

Causes subject-verb inversion

Beispielsatz

Es regnet. Trotzdem gehen wir spazieren.

It's raining. Nevertheless we go for a walk.

aber

but / however

📐 Word Order

No change - keeps V2 in both clauses

Beispielsatz

Ich moechte kommen, aber ich habe keine Zeit.

I'd like to come, but I don't have time.

jedoch

however / yet

📐 Word Order

Can appear in different positions; causes inversion if first

Beispielsatz

Das war schwierig. Jedoch haben wir es geschafft.

That was difficult. However we managed it.

dennoch

nonetheless / still

📐 Word Order

Causes subject-verb inversion

Beispielsatz

Die Aufgabe ist schwer. Dennoch versuchen wir es.

The task is hard. Nonetheless we try it.

obgleich

even though / although (formal)

📐 Word Order

Verb moves to the END

Beispielsatz

Obgleich er Erfahrung hat, macht er Fehler.

Even though he has experience, he makes mistakes.

damit

so that / in order that (different subjects)

📐 Word Order

Verb moves to the END of the clause

Beispielsatz

Ich erklaere es langsam, damit du alles verstehst.

I explain it slowly so that you understand everything.

um ... zu + Infinitiv

in order to (same subject)

📐 Word Order

um at start, zu + infinitive at end of clause

Beispielsatz

Er lernt Deutsch, um in Deutschland zu arbeiten.

He learns German in order to work in Germany.

als

when (single past event)

📐 Word Order

Verb moves to the END; used for SINGLE past events

Beispielsatz

Als ich Kind war, wohnte ich in Hamburg.

When I was a child, I lived in Hamburg.

wenn

when / whenever / if

📐 Word Order

Verb moves to the END; used for present, future, repeated events

Beispielsatz

Wenn ich Zeit habe, lese ich ein Buch.

When I have time, I read a book.

waehrend

while / during

📐 Word Order

Verb moves to the END

Beispielsatz

Waehrend er kochte, hoerte er Musik.

While he was cooking, he listened to music.

nachdem

after (an action is completed)

📐 Word Order

Verb at END; tense must shift (Plusquamperfekt in the nachdem-clause)

Beispielsatz

Nachdem er gegessen hatte, machte er einen Spaziergang.

After he had eaten, he went for a walk.

bevor

before

📐 Word Order

Verb moves to the END

Beispielsatz

Bevor er schlaeft, putzt er die Zaehne.

Before he sleeps, he brushes his teeth.

seitdem

since (a point in time)

📐 Word Order

Verb moves to the END

Beispielsatz

Seitdem er in Muenchen wohnt, spricht er besser Deutsch.

Since he has been living in Munich, he speaks German better.

sobald

as soon as

📐 Word Order

Verb moves to the END

Beispielsatz

Sobald der Zug ankommt, steigen wir ein.

As soon as the train arrives, we get on.

sondern

but rather / but instead (after negation)

📐 Word Order

Used after negation (nicht / kein); no word order change

Beispielsatz

Er ist nicht gross, sondern klein.

He is not tall, but rather short.

waehrend (adversativ)

while / whereas (contrast)

📐 Word Order

Verb moves to the END

Beispielsatz

Er mag Kaffee, waehrend sie lieber Tee trinkt.

He likes coffee, whereas she prefers tea.

sodass

so that (as a result)

📐 Word Order

Verb moves to the END

Beispielsatz

Er sprach zu leise, sodass wir ihn nicht hoeren konnten.

He spoke too quietly, so that we couldn't hear him.

so ... dass

so ... that

📐 Word Order

so + adjective in main clause, dass + verb at END

Beispielsatz

Das Buch ist so interessant, dass ich es nicht weglegen konnte.

The book is so interesting that I couldn't put it down.

falls

if / in case (conditional)

📐 Word Order

Verb moves to the END

Beispielsatz

Falls es regnet, nehmen wir ein Taxi.

If it rains, we'll take a taxi.

sofern

provided that / as long as

📐 Word Order

Verb moves to the END

Beispielsatz

Sofern du puenktlich bist, koennen wir das schaffen.

Provided you are on time, we can manage it.

indem

by doing / by (method)

📐 Word Order

Verb moves to the END; same subject as main clause

Beispielsatz

Er lernt Deutsch, indem er jeden Tag Vokabeln uebst.

He learns German by practising vocabulary every day.

anstatt ... zu + Infinitiv

instead of (doing something)

📐 Word Order

anstatt at start, zu + infinitive at end

Beispielsatz

Anstatt zu lernen, schaut er Fernsehen.

Instead of studying, he watches TV.
